# Scene Plan: DeShawn

## Summary

A meeting with DeShawn Cole, now 26 and running his own venture. The dynamic between them has shifted: DeShawn is no longer the mentee Kevin Zhou guided during Jerome Washington's son's coding adolescence, but a peer, perhaps more. They meet at a cafe or co-working space, the conversation professional on the surface but personal beneath. Kevin Zhou sees in DeShawn both his younger self and the correction to his younger self - Jerome's influence visible in DeShawn's questions about impact, about responsibility, about what technology should do rather than merely can do. The scene marks Kevin Zhou's recognition that wisdom might come from those he once taught.
