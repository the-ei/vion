# Scene Plan: The Message

## Summary

Kevin Zhou composes his message to Yusuf Hassan. The consciousness interface project he is developing could use Yusuf's voice - literally, for audio components, but also metaphorically as someone who understands experience from below rather than above. Kevin Zhou struggles with the composition: how to explain without explaining, how to ask without demanding, how to bridge the gulf between his world and Yusuf's. The message is awkward and sincere, lacking the polish of professional communication. He sends it. Then waits. The threshold Kevin Zhou crosses is not grand but small: asking for connection from someone who owes him nothing, admitting that his technical brilliance needs what Yusuf carries.
