# Scene Plan: Parents at Distance

## Summary

Kevin Zhou considers calling his parents in Shenzhen. He opens the video app, looks at their contact information, contemplates the call. Political tensions between the US and China have made these communications harder - not just technically but emotionally. They ask when he is coming home; he does not know where home is. The distance is not just geographic but cultural, generational, aspirational. His parents wanted success for him; he achieved it but lost something they might have valued more. The scene ends with Kevin Zhou not making the call - closing the app without dialing, the silence that follows. Carson compression: the moment of withdrawal, the connection unmade.
