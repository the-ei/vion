# Scene Plan: The Architecture of Solitude

## Summary

Kevin Zhou in his San Francisco apartment, which is both beautiful and empty - the material success that accompanied spiritual vacancy. The scene follows him through a morning routine performed with the precision of someone who has optimized even solitude. Every object in this space represents achievement: the view, the furniture, the technology. But no object represents connection. His screens glow with the project he is building - not for profit this time, not for achievement, but for something he cannot quite name. The consciousness interface project that may finally give his technical brilliance purpose beyond extraction. Extended Knausgaard attention to the space, the objects, the silence, the way morning light moves through an apartment designed for living but inhabited like an office.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's apartment as isolation made architectural
- Material wealth as spiritual vacancy
- Morning routine as optimization, not pleasure
- The consciousness interface project on his screens
- San Francisco view: success visible, connection absent
- Nine years of trajectory since Prometheus
- The question of what he is building and why
- Objects that represent achievement, none that represent love
- The silence of a space inhabited alone
