# Scene Plan: The System from Inside

## Summary

Unable to sleep at home, Elena returns to the hospice during a night shift. She has been this staff - the exhausted nurses, the compassionate aides, the overworked doctors making rounds. Now she watches them from the other side. The scene follows her through a night of observation: the small kindnesses that exceed job descriptions (a nurse repositioning abuela with extra gentleness, an aide bringing Elena coffee without being asked) and the small brutalities that emerge from overwork (a rushed interaction, a forgotten request). Elena's threshold is not about judgment but recognition: these are her people, doing impossible work in broken systems. The healthcare system that has shaped her career appears here not as abstraction but as living organism - flawed, essential, human.

## Key Elements

- Night shift atmosphere: different rhythm, different light
- The hospice staff observed by one of their own
- Small kindnesses that exceed professional requirements
- Small failures that emerge from systemic exhaustion
- Elena's recognition: she has been both competent and overwhelmed
- Conversations with night staff who know she is a nurse
- The quiet of the building at 3 AM
- Elena's wakefulness as both vigil and professional observation
- The system seen from patient-family side
