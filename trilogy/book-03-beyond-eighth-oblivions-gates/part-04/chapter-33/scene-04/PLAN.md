# Scene Plan: Amina's Visit

## Summary

Yusuf's sister Amina arrives during a break in recording. She has never heard his music like this - not fragments from his phone but full production taking shape through professional speakers. Her reaction cuts through Yusuf's self-doubt: surprise, pride, something like recognition of a brother she didn't fully know. They talk briefly about their father, about what he might have thought hearing this, about the warehouse that took his life and the warehouse that might give Yusuf's art a chance. Carson compression: the sibling exchange that says more than the words. Amina sees her brother transformed, still himself but more visible.

## Key Elements

- Amina visiting the session: crossing into his world
- Hearing his music through professional production

- Brief conversation about their father
- Two warehouses: death and art
- Sibling recognition

- What the successful sibling sees in the artistic one
