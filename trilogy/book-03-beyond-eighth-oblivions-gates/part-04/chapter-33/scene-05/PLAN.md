# Scene Plan: Night Session

## Summary

As Minneapolis darkens, the recording continues. Yusuf finds the zone - that space where self-consciousness dissolves and the music moves through rather than from him. The song he has been working on for years finally completes itself, not because he forced it but because he finally allowed it. The night session has the quality of trance: exhaustion and exhilaration blurred, time losing normal meaning. The producer knows when it is happening, stays quiet, lets the capture proceed. By early morning, something real has been recorded - not perfect, not polished, but true. The chapter ends with the session wrapped, Yusuf standing in the parking lot in the spring night, changed.
