# Scene Plan: First Takes

## Summary

Recording begins. The strange experience of committing voice to permanent capture - the pressure of the red light, the silence before singing, the producer's voice through the headphones. False starts, frustration, moments when the song almost arrives and then retreats. Yusuf realizes his years of voice memos and car humming were preparation - he has been training without knowing it, developing a relationship with his own voice that now reveals its depth. The producer's patience guides without directing. The scene captures the frustration and occasional grace of first takes, the struggle to let something that has always been private become fixed and shareable.
