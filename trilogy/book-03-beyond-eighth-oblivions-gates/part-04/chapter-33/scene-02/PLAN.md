# Scene Plan: Sound Check

## Summary

The producer and engineer work with Yusuf on the technical preparation for recording: levels, microphone placement, the translation of voice into waveform. Kevin Zhou's audio interface is part of the setup - technology designed for transmission rather than extraction, the product of their unlikely connection. Yusuf is uncomfortable with being attended to, with professionals taking his work seriously. His years of solitary voice memos and humming in his car feel suddenly relevant - training he didn't know was training. The scene captures the strange intimacy of sound check: hearing yourself through professional monitoring for the first time, the voice both yours and not yours.
