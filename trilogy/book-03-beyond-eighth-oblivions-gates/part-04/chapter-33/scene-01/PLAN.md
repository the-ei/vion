# Scene Plan: The Warehouse

## Summary

Yusuf arrives at the recording studio - a converted warehouse in Minneapolis that triggers immediate memory of his father's death. He has never recorded in a professional space like this. The equipment, the acoustic treatment, the quality of everything suggests permanence, suggests his music being taken seriously. He is out of place in every way that matters on the surface, and exactly where he belongs in every way that matters beneath. The scene unfolds as Yusuf walks through the space, encountering its physical reality while memory of his father's warehouse accident shadows everything. The building that killed his father; the building that might let his voice finally be heard. The parallel is not lost on him.
