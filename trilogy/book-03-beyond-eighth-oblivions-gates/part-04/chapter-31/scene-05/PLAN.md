# Scene Plan: Threshold Crossed

## Summary

Evening has fallen completely. Ananya prepares to leave but pauses. What she says to Delphine in this moment - about Priya, about what she wants her daughter to know - constitutes the threshold's crossing. The public testimony in the documentary matters, but the private threshold is what she must cross first: she will tell Priya herself, before any footage is released. The alliance between these two women has produced not public truth but private courage. Ananya leaves with a commitment made, not to Delphine's cameras but to her daughter. The chapter ends with departure, the door closing on the conversation but opening toward what comes next.
