# Scene Plan: The Archive

## Summary

Delphine shows Ananya the materials she has gathered for the documentary. Footage from the Eighth Oblivion years - news coverage, interviews, internal corporate documents obtained through leaks and litigation. Ananya appears in some of it: younger, more certain, defending positions she no longer holds with a confidence that now looks like denial. Extended attention to watching oneself in the past, the strange archaeology of recorded history. The screens show versions of Ananya she barely recognizes and completely remembers. Delphine has been building this archive for years, assembling the narrative of what happened. Now Ananya must decide what to add.
