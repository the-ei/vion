# Scene Plan: The Documentary's Shape

## Summary

What will Ananya contribute to the documentary? Delphine explains her vision: not expose designed for outrage, not redemption narrative designed for comfort, but honest accounting of what happened from multiple perspectives. She wants Ananya's testimony not for public confession but because the record requires someone who was inside the machine to explain how it looked from there. What decisions seemed reasonable at the time. What rationalizations enabled continuation. Ananya begins to understand what she might say - not justification, not self-laceration, but something else: the texture of complicity from within.

## Key Elements

- Delphine's documentary vision: honest accounting, not expose or redemption
- What Ananya's testimony would contribute
- The view from inside the machine
- Decisions that seemed reasonable in context
- Rationalizations that enabled continuation
- The documentary's purpose: understanding, not judgment
- What Ananya might actually say
- The difference between public confession and honest testimony
- Planning without promising
