# Scene Plan: What They Made

## Summary

The real conversation. Delphine and Ananya sit with drinks as the light shifts toward evening, the archive screens now dark. They talk about complicity - not confessing but analyzing, understanding. Delphine made content that shaped perception of the Eighth Oblivion, gave it the narrative frames through which millions understood what was happening. Ananya provided ethical cover for technological development that proceeded despite her objections. They are not the same kind of complicit, but they recognize each other across the difference. Carson mode punctuates the Knausgaard flow: moments of stark admission that hang in the air between two women who have stopped pretending.
