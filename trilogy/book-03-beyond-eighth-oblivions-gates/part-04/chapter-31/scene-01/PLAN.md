# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Ananya arrives at Delphine Okafor-Barnes's home in the hills above Los Angeles. The geography is strange to her - different from San Francisco's fog and vertical density, all this horizontal light and space. Delphine greets her with the warmth of someone who has been waiting for this conversation across years of developing alliance. A brief domestic scene: Jessie offering tea with easy hospitality, Theo visible in a doorway watching the visitor with adolescent curiosity, the life Delphine has made visible in every surface. Ananya thinks of her own apartment's comparative emptiness - not loneliness exactly, but the absence of this layered family presence. The scene establishes the space where truth will be spoken.
