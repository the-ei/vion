# Scene Plan: The Invitation

## Summary

Ananya finally reads Delphine Okafor-Barnes's message - the one that arrived last night and kept her from sleeping. Delphine's documentary project about the Eighth Oblivion has reached a stage where she needs Ananya's participation: not as a talking head offering corporate perspective, but as someone willing to speak honestly about what happened inside Prometheus Systems. The message is carefully worded, respecting their friendship while making clear what is being asked. Ananya reads it multiple times, each reading revealing different implications. Memory surfaces of how their alliance developed across the years of Book 3 - two women at different nodes of the system who recognized each other's complicity. The phrases of the message repeat in her mind like a Carson poem: fragmented, weighted, impossible to escape.

## Key Elements

- Delphine's message: the specific request for honest testimony
- Multiple readings revealing layers of implication
- Memory of how the Ananya-Delphine friendship developed
- The implicit accusation in being asked for honesty
- The documentary as both opportunity and exposure
- Ananya's professional reputation at stake
- Recognition between two women who understand complicity
- The phrase "what really happened" and what it demands
