# Scene Plan: Threshold Recognition

## Summary

Ananya dresses for her meeting with Delphine. The scene compresses toward decision - the long interior morning finally resolving into movement. Looking in the mirror, she sees herself at 47: the face she has earned through a decade of choices. The physical details of preparation - selecting clothes, applying minimal makeup, gathering what she needs - become ritual, the body preparing before the mind has finished deliberating. She has not decided what to say to Delphine, but she has decided to go. The chapter closes with her leaving the apartment, the door closing behind her with a sound that suggests finality without explaining it. White space surrounds the choice to move. The threshold is not crossed in this scene - it is recognized as crossable.

## Key Elements

- Mirror scene: Ananya seeing her 47-year-old face
- The body at a threshold: physical aging as psychological marker
- Selection of clothes as decision-making by other means
- The apartment left behind: what stays when she goes
- The door closing: finality suggested but not explained
- Movement replacing deliberation
- San Francisco outside the window: where she is going
- Carson compression: white space around the choice
