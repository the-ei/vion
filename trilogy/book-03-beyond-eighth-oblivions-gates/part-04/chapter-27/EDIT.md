# Edit Notes: Chapter 27 - The Return

## Prose Characteristics

**Knausgaard Elements (Strong)**
- Pre-dawn insomnia rendered with full sensory attention: ceiling gray, street glow, car headlights as searchlights
- Morning inventory performed unconsciously: "Chief Ethics Officer at Prometheus Systems for eleven years"
- Physical aging detailed: body generating heat, thermostat of middle age
- Apartment's silence as "particular quality of stillness"

**Carson Elements (Moderate)**
- "Ananya Ramaswamy. Forty-seven years old." - declarative identity statements
- Delphine's message following into dreams "about standing in front of a door"
- Choices "layer upon layer of choices that had seemed reasonable at the time"

**Voice Consistency**
- Ananya's analytical mind even in half-waking state
- Self-examination without self-pity
- Solitude catalogued as geographic fact: Vikram left, Priya moved, years accumulated
