# Scene Plan: Morning Archaeology

## Summary

Ananya Ramaswamy wakes early in her San Francisco apartment, the pre-dawn light moving across objects accumulated over years of her career at Prometheus Systems. The scene follows her through the ritualized motions of morning preparation - making coffee, moving through familiar spaces - while her mind excavates the sediment of decisions she has justified to herself over a decade. Each object carries weight: a framed photo from an ethics conference, a gift from a colleague who later testified against the company, a view of the city that has transformed around her. The morning feels different from other mornings without her being able to articulate why. The Delphine invitation waits on her phone, unread since last night, shaping the air around everything.

## Key Elements

- Pre-dawn San Francisco light as psychological atmosphere
- Ananya's apartment as museum of professional choices
- Coffee preparation as meditation and avoidance
- Objects triggering memory fragments from Prometheus years
- The body at 47: physical awareness of aging
- The unread message from Delphine creating background pressure
- San Francisco geography through her window: what has changed
- The deliberate slowness of someone postponing confrontation with meaning

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (47): POV character, waking into the weight of her professional life's meaning
- **Prometheus colleagues**: Present through objects and memory fragments - unnamed but felt

## Setting

- Ananya's San Francisco apartment, Pacific Heights or similar neighborhood
- Early morning, January/February 2041
- Interior spaces: bedroom, kitchen, living room with city view

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 90 minutes (4:30 AM to 6:00 AM)
- Memory sequences span 2032-2040, triggered by objects

## Page Target

7 pages (~1,925 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Extended Knausgaard mode: exhaustive interiority, mundane elevated to profound
- Long flowing sentences following the movement of consciousness
- Physical detail paramount - the texture of morning routine
- Time moves slowly - each small action receives attention
- Minimal dialogue (internal monologue only)
- Light as character: the way dawn changes surfaces
