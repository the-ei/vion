# Scene Plan: Priya's Silence

## Summary

Ananya recalls her last conversation with Priya, now 23 and working in climate policy. The memory surfaces with painful clarity: practical arrangements about an upcoming family event, no mention of work, the careful choreography of two people who love each other and cannot speak about the thing between them. Priya stopped asking about her mother's work years ago - not from disinterest but from exhaustion with answers that were always too complicated, too qualified, too defensive. The threshold Ananya faces is not public confession but intimate truth: can she say to her daughter what she has never been able to say? The scene moves between the memory of their last conversation and Ananya's present meditation on what Priya would even want to hear. The silence between them is not empty - it is crowded with unspoken questions.

## Key Elements

- Memory of the last Ananya-Priya conversation: practical, evasive
- Priya at 23: working in climate policy, her own relationship to systemic harm
- The choreography of avoidance between mother and daughter
- Questions Priya stopped asking and why
- What Ananya has never been able to articulate
- The gap between professional self-justification and parental truth
- Priya's silence as more damning than accusation
- Ananya imagining possible conversations that haven't happened
