# Scene Plan: The Patients

## Summary

The shift begins and the clinic fills. Extended attention to individual patient encounters - each one a story of systemic failure meeting individual need. A diabetic woman whose insurance lapsed when her employer cut hours to avoid providing coverage. A child with asthma exacerbated by air quality that no individual choices can improve. An elderly man whose medication costs force choices between pills and groceries. Elena provides care while seeing the larger machinery that creates these wounds. She has always seen this; now she sees it without the protective layer of burnout. The rage and care she integrated at her grandmother's deathbed move together through each encounter.

- Multiple patient encounters: specific, detailed
- Systemic failure embodied in individuals

- The rhythm of a clinic shift: continuous demand
