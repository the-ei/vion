# Scene Plan: Daniel's Call

## Summary

During a brief break late in the shift, Elena calls Daniel. He is working a construction job in New Mexico, weeks away from home, their marriage running on logistics and occasional phone calls. The conversation is practical - the kids, the house, when he might be back, the small decisions of a shared life maintained at distance. Underneath runs the current of their marriage: tested by years of strain, still holding. Elena does not tell him about the decision forming in her mind, not yet. Some thresholds must be crossed alone before they can be shared. The call is ordinary and essential - the maintenance work of a relationship that has survived by continuous repair.
