# Scene Plan: Before the Doors Open

## Summary

Elena arrives at the community health center early, before patients. The clinic in silence is a rare gift - a moment to see the space without the bodies that will fill it within the hour. She walks through empty exam rooms with their paper-covered tables, supply closets that are never quite full enough, the waiting room where people spend hours that their employers don't pay them for. The funeral was weeks ago; grief has settled into something quieter but still present. She is not the same person who walked these halls before her grandmother's death. Something has shifted in her relationship to this space - not what she does here, but what it means. The scene establishes Elena's changed perspective before the shift begins.

## Key Elements

- The clinic in pre-opening silence
- Empty exam rooms, waiting room, supply areas
- Elena walking through familiar space with new eyes
- Post-funeral grief: settled but present
- What has shifted: meaning, not action
- The resources that are never enough
- Phoenix morning light entering the building
- The pause before the day's demands begin
- Elena's changed relationship to her work
