# Scene Plan: The Colleagues

## Summary

Break room conversation with fellow nurses. They are all exhausted, all dedicated, all burning out at different rates. The conversation is ordinary - complaints about scheduling, dark humor about impossible cases, the mutual support of people who understand what the work actually costs. But Elena sees them differently now: not fellow victims of the system but potential carriers of what she knows. The idea begins to form - not escape from this work, but transmission within it. What if she trained the next generation to see what she sees? What if she documented the workarounds, the small acts of resistance, the ways to provide care despite the system?

## Key Elements

- Break room as refuge and gathering place
- Fellow nurses: exhausted, dedicated, burning out
- Ordinary conversation: complaints, humor, support
- Elena seeing colleagues as potential carriers
- The idea forming: training, transmission, documentation
- Not escape but multiplication
- What experienced nurses know that isn't taught
- The informal culture of resistance in healthcare
- Community among the overworked
