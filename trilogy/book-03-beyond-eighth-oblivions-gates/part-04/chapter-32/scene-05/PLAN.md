# Scene Plan: The Decision

## Summary

Late in the shift, Elena encounters a young nursing student shadowing one of her colleagues. Something about the student's combination of idealism and ignorance sparks the decision. The student asks questions that reveal both genuine care and vast gaps in understanding - gaps that nursing school doesn't address, that only experience fills. Elena realizes she can be the bridge. She will develop a training program. She will document the cases, the workarounds, the strategies for providing care despite the system. She will become a threshold for others to cross. The chapter ends with Elena writing notes on her phone - the beginning of something new. Carson compression: fragments of clarity emerging from exhaustion.
