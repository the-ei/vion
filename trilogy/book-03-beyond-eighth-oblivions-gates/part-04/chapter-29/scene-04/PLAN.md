# Scene Plan: Kevin Zhou's Voice

## Summary

An unexpected message arrives from Kevin Zhou, whom Yusuf met during the Book 2 crisis - thrown together by circumstance in a way that bridged their vastly different worlds. Kevin Zhou has been developing a technology interface project and wants Yusuf's involvement. The message is awkward and sincere, the communication style of someone more comfortable with code than people. It is not directly about music, but about the possibility of Yusuf's voice reaching beyond the constraints of his circumstances - literal voice for audio components, metaphorical voice as transmission of experience. The scene compresses two people from different worlds recognizing something in each other across digital distance. Carson mode: sparse exchange, weighted meaning.
