# Scene Plan: Before Dawn

## Summary

Yusuf sits in his car as the sky begins to lighten over Minneapolis. The winter dawn arrives slowly - grey becoming less grey, the parking lot emerging from darkness. He opens a voice memo on his phone and records himself: not polished, not ready, but real. The song he has been working on for years begins to take shape through his voice and the cold air visible in his breath. When he finishes, he responds to the label email. Yes. The word itself. He does not know what will happen, cannot promise success, cannot even promise completion. But he refuses to wait any longer for conditions that will never arrive. The threshold crossed is not about certainty but about refusal of eternal preparation. He drives home, humming, the melody finally moving toward completion.

## Key Elements

- Winter dawn over Minneapolis: slow emergence of light
- The voice memo: raw recording, not performance
- The song taking shape after years of fragments
- The "yes" sent to the label: simple, irreversible
- Refusal to wait for perfect conditions
- Breath visible in cold as he sings
- The car as studio, threshold space
- Driving home with changed interior state
- The melody completing itself because he allowed it
