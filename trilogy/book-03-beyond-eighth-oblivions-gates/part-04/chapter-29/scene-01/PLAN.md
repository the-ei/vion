# Scene Plan: Night Shift

## Summary

Yusuf Hassan on a delivery shift in Minneapolis winter. The familiar choreography of gig work unfolds: phone pinging with assignments, car heater struggling against the cold, packages transferred from vehicle to doorstep, the brief encounters with strangers who barely see him. But Yusuf's mind is elsewhere - it has always been elsewhere. He hums while driving, working on the song he has been building for years without admitting to himself that he is building anything. The city at night, the cold that seeps through everything, the strange intimacy of bringing things to strangers' doors. The scene establishes the texture of his precarious work while revealing the music that lives beneath it, the private self that gig work has never been able to touch.

## Key Elements

- Gig work choreography: the rhythm of app-based labor
- Minneapolis winter night: cold as constant physical presence
- The car as workspace, refuge, practice space
- Humming and melody-working while driving
- The anonymity of delivery: seen but not seen
- Years of accumulated musical fragments
- Economic precarity as lived experience, not abstraction
- The city at night: familiar routes, weather variations
- The contrast between external work and interior creative life
