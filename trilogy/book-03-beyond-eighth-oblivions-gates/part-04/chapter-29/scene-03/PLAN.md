# Scene Plan: Mother and Father

## Summary

Yusuf stops at his mother Halima's apartment. She is awake despite the late hour, her chronic health issues disrupting her sleep. They sit in the kitchen together, the conversation dancing around what matters - her health, his work, Amina's success, the bills. Memory of his father surfaces: the warehouse accident that killed him when Yusuf was 12, the absence that shaped everything after. In this kitchen, with his mother's hands wrapped around a cup of tea, Yusuf realizes he has been waiting to be safe before risking anything, and safety will never come. His mother's hands - like Elena's abuela's hands in the previous chapter - represent labor, love, and mortality. They will not hold him forever. The scene crystallizes what the offer demands: not certainty, but willingness to act without it.

## Key Elements

- Late-night kitchen conversation: mother and son
- Halima's health: chronic issues, sleeplessness
- Dancing around the real subjects
- Memory of father's warehouse death
- The warehouse as site of labor, danger, absence
- What Yusuf owes his family vs. what he owes himself
- Mother's hands as image (connecting to Elena's chapter)
- The impossibility of ever being "ready" or "safe"
- Amina mentioned: the successful sibling, the family's other hope
