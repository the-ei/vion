# Scene Plan: Preparation

## Summary

Kevin Zhou in the space he has prepared for the consciousness interface test - a renovated loft in San Francisco, clinical but not cold. He checks equipment, reviews protocols, performs the rituals of technical preparation that have always been his comfort zone. But this preparation is different from all the ones before: he is not only the builder but a participant. The threshold for Kevin Zhou is not whether the technology works but whether he can allow it to work on him - whether he can permit himself to be seen by others through the interface he created. Extended attention to his interior state as the test moment approaches, the builder becoming the bridge.

## Key Elements

- The renovated loft: prepared for the test
- Equipment checks as ritual and comfort
- Kevin Zhou as both builder and participant
- The fear beneath the technical preparation
- Protocols reviewed: consent, safety, withdrawal options
- The space designed for connection, usually empty
- San Francisco summer light through windows
- The transition from builder to user
- Interior fear of being seen, known
