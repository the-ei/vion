# Scene Plan: The Session

## Summary

The consciousness interface test begins. Extended attention to what shared awareness actually feels like - the strange sensation of one's emotional state being witnessed, of witnessing others'. Yusuf's warmth arriving through the interface, his open-heartedness become palpable. Kevin Zhou's own loneliness suddenly visible to others, the isolation he has maintained for years becoming impossible to hide. The threshold for Kevin Zhou is not technical success but allowing himself to be known. Other participants' experiences woven through - what they feel from each other, the shock of genuine empathic connection. Carson mode for the peak moments of recognition, when the interface achieves what it was designed for.
