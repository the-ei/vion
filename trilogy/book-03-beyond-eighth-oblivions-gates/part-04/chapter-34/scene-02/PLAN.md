# Scene Plan: Arrivals

## Summary

The participants gather for the interface test. Yusuf arrives from Minneapolis, awkward in this tech space but curious, carrying the confidence of his recently completed recording. Others arrive: carefully selected individuals who represent different relationships to technology - a former colleague who questioned the ethics, a community organizer skeptical of tech solutions, perhaps DeShawn Cole. Kevin Zhou greets them with the stilted social manner that has always marked him - technically fluent, interpersonally awkward. The contrast is not played for comedy but understood as the cost of his particular brilliance. The group assembles in the prepared space, diverse in background but united by willingness to try something unprecedented.
