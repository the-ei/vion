# Scene Plan: The Interface Explained

## Summary

Kevin Zhou presents what the participants are about to experience. The consciousness interface allows sharing of emotional and sensory states - not thoughts, not mind-reading, but something like expanded awareness of others' inner experience. He explains the ethics built into the design: consent at every level, the ability to withdraw at any moment, the commitment that no data is stored. Ananya Ramaswamy's influence is visible in these safeguards even though she is not present - the rival who pushed him toward ethical consideration now vindicated in his design choices. The explanation is technical but accessible, the builder translating his creation for those who will inhabit it.
