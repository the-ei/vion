# Scene Plan: Yusuf - The EP Released

## Summary

Yusuf's EP has gone live. A modest release, barely visible in the noise of streaming platforms, but real - his voice out in the world, no longer private. He is at his mother's apartment, her health stable for now but always precarious. Amina is there. The family listens to the songs together, the first time they have heard this part of Yusuf in full. His father's absence is present in the room - the man who would have heard this, who never will. The gate Yusuf crossed leads here: not to fame or fortune, but to expression. What was private is now public, and that is enough. The EP exists. He exists through it.
