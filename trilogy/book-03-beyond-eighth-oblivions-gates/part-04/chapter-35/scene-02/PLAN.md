# Scene Plan: Elena - Training Begins

## Summary

Elena in Phoenix, her training program taking tangible shape. A small group of nursing students and young nurses gather in her living room for an informal session - the knowledge the healthcare system does not teach, the wisdom only experience provides. Daniel is home from the construction job; Sofia helps with drinks and snacks; the ordinary domestic scene is inflected with new purpose. Elena's clarity from her grandmother's death has not solved the system's problems - it has given her a way to pass something on. The scene shows the beginning of transmission: what Elena knows becoming what others can carry forward.
