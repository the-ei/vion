# Scene Plan: Kevin Zhou - After Connection

## Summary

Kevin Zhou in the aftermath of the interface test. Something has changed in how he moves through the world - the experience of shared consciousness, brief as it was, has made his isolation feel like a choice rather than a fate. Choices can be changed. He calls his parents in Shenzhen, the conversation he has been avoiding for months. The call is awkward, full of unresolved tension about his life choices, their distance, the political gulf between their worlds. But he made it. The gate of connection has opened slightly wider. The scene captures Kevin Zhou beginning to participate in human life rather than merely building technology for it.

## Key Elements

- Aftermath of the interface test
- Isolation now feeling like a choice, not fate

- Shenzhen and San Francisco: distance and connection
- Political tension between US and China as backdrop

- The call not redemptive but beginning
