# Scene Plan: Ruth - The Gate Approaching

## Summary

News arrives: Ruth Abramson is in the hospital. A stroke, or something like it - the details unclear, relayed through the network of relationships the POV characters have built across the trilogy. This scene does not narrate Ruth's experience directly (she is a supporting POV in Part 4) but shows how the news arrives to the other characters. Ananya receives a call. Elena hears through the network of healthcare professionals who know Ruth's work. The eldest of the POV characters is approaching her own gate - the threshold of mortality that no clarity or wisdom can negotiate. The scene ends with the weight of this news settling over the dispersed characters, binding them through their shared care for Ruth as Part 4 closes.
