# Scene Plan: Ananya - Aftermath of Honesty

## Summary

Ananya has told Priya. The scene opens in the days after that conversation - not the conversation itself, which remains private between mother and daughter, but its aftermath. Priya's response was neither what Ananya feared (rejection, accusation) nor what she hoped (absolution, understanding). Something between: the beginning of understanding, not its completion. Ananya prepares to participate in Delphine's documentary, but the public testimony now feels secondary. The private threshold was the real gate. The conversation with Priya has not healed everything, but it has made everything more honest. Ananya moves through her San Francisco apartment with a different relationship to the objects of her professional life.

## Key Elements

- After the conversation with Priya: what remains
- Priya's response: neither feared nor hoped, something between
- Public documentary now secondary to private truth
- The private threshold as the real gate
