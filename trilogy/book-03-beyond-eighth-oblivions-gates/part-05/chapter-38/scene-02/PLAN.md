# Scene Plan: The Premiere

## Summary

The theater fills with industry people, critics, friends, the audience for a documentary about the Eighth Oblivion that refuses to explain its subject. Delphine finds Ananya before the lights dim - their friendship expressed in shorthand, their shared history compressed into a glance. Then the film begins, and Delphine watches her work through the audience's reactions. The documentary itself is glimpsed through her experience of showing it: fragmentary, non-narrative, beautiful in ways that resist commercial calculation. Memories of making specific scenes surface - the ethical choices, the honest choices, the interviews that couldn't be included. The moment when she knows whether it works.
