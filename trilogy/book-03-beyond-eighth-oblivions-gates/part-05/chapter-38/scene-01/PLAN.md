# Scene Plan: The Morning Before

## Summary

Delphine wakes on the morning of her documentary premiere, the culmination of two years' work and decades of career. The domestic morning unfolds around her: Jessie getting Theo ready for school, the ordinary rituals of family that continue regardless of professional milestones. Delphine video-calls her mother in London, trying to explain what she hopes for the film, what it means to her. Her father's death years ago surfaces - what would he have thought of what she made? The fear of being seen, of having made something honest in an industry built on performance, compresses into Carson fragments.

## Key Elements

- The premiere morning, domestic normalcy alongside professional culmination
- Video call with her mother in London, across time zones and distance
- Trying to articulate what the documentary means, failing, trying again
- Jessie and Theo as grounding presence, the family continuing regardless
- Memory of her father's death, what he might have seen in this film
- The fear of exposure - this is not a commercial film, it's a personal one
- The attention economy Delphine has critiqued now receiving her critique
- Theo at 12, on the threshold of adolescence, his own person emerging
- Carson compression: the fear of being seen, really seen
