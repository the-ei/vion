# Scene Plan: The Reception

## Summary

After the screening, the performance of reception begins. Industry people offer praise that misses the point, critique that accidentally hits it, the currency of attention exchanged. Delphine circulates, no longer performing as she once would have, but observing. Jessie finds her with the truth that only a spouse can offer. Ananya connects, their friendship expressed in the shorthand of years. Theo is bored by adult film talk, reminding Delphine that non-professional life exists and matters. The question of what comes next - what Delphine does after this, whether she continues - hangs unspoken in every conversation.
