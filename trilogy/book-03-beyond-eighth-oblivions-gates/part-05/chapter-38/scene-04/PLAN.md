# Scene Plan: After Everyone Leaves

## Summary

Home again after the premiere, Theo asleep, Delphine and Jessie finally have the real conversation about what the film means, what's next, whether Delphine continues in this industry or finds another gate. The complicity Delphine has carried throughout the trilogy - the knowledge that her work feeds an attention economy she can critique but not escape - reaches resolution. Not absolution, but understanding, and the decision to leave this behind. The scene ends with Delphine deleting her professional profiles, a literal crossing, a gate passed through. What comes next is unknown, and that's the resolution.
