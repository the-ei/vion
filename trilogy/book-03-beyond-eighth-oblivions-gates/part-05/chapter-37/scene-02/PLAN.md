# Scene Plan: The Family Gathers

## Summary

Denise arrives from work, having navigated these crises before. Jerome's sister comes, carrying the weight of having done more of the daily caregiving over the years. And then DeShawn arrives from San Francisco, the tech worker son who entered the world Jerome spent his career warning against. The three generations of Washington family gather around the dying woman, and the tensions that normally define them - Jerome and DeShawn's ideological distance, the siblings' caregiving imbalance, the marriage tested by decades - recede in the presence of the ending that waits. The grandmother becomes the figure who connects them all.

## Key Elements

- Denise's arrival, the wife who has been through everything with Jerome
- Jerome's sister's perspective: she did more of the daily care, her particular exhaustion
- DeShawn's arrival from San Francisco, representing the tech world Jerome distrusts
- Father and son's careful dance around their differences, muted by the occasion
- The family dynamics visible in how they position themselves in the room
- Jerome's mother drifting in and out of lucidity as family arrives
- The shared memories they trade, constructing the dying woman together
- The marriage examined through glances between Jerome and Denise

## Characters
