# Scene Plan: After

## Summary

The immediate aftermath of death: the calls to make, the body to attend to, the bureaucracy of ending. Jerome steps outside into Baltimore winter air, needing to breathe something that isn't the death-room. He reflects on what his mother wanted for him versus what he became, whether she would have understood his choices, whether her simple questions contained wisdom his complex answers lacked. DeShawn joins him outside, and father and son share a moment of reconciliation that needs no words. The truth mattering or not mattering resolves: it mattered to him, and that has to be enough. Jerome returns inside to his family, to the continuation.
