# Scene Plan: Delphine - The Reception

## Summary

After the panels, the reception unfolds with its performances of connection and status. Delphine circulates as observer, no longer performing as she once would have - her documentary done, her career paused. Conversations overheard reveal how narratives form about what happened, what gets emphasized and what gets lost. She finds Ananya, their alliance visible to others. Amina Hassan approaches - Yusuf's sister, the political voice of her generation - and the next generation's perspective becomes clear: they inherit this history, they'll revise it. The gap between Delphine's documentary and what the symposium says about the same events.
