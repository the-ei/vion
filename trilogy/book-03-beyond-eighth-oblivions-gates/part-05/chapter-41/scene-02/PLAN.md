# Scene Plan: Jerome - The Audience

## Summary

Jerome sits in the audience, notebook open, observing the symposium as a journalist - the role he's never fully shed. His own journalism is being cited by academics now, the investigations he conducted becoming source material. Strange to hear his work transformed into official history. He watches Ananya speak, remembering when she was a source, then a friend, the relationship that crossed professional boundaries into genuine care. DeShawn is somewhere in the crowd, the tech world representative who is also his son. The truth Jerome told is becoming the official story, and he must decide whether that's victory.
