# Scene Plan: Ananya - Before the Panel

## Summary

Ananya prepares for her panel at the symposium on the Eighth Oblivion, with Priya beside her. The event is historicizing what Ananya lived - the crisis, the response, her role at Prometheus and after. She reflects on what she'll say about ethical AI, complicity, the limits of working from within. Delphine finds her backstage, their friendship visible in shorthand. Mother-daughter dynamic emerges: Priya's views differ from Ananya's, the next generation already revising what the previous one built. What it means to be historicized while still living, to become a source rather than an actor.
