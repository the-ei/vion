# Scene Plan: In Transit

## Summary

Characters traveling to their respective homes, the liminal space of transit. Jerome on a flight to Baltimore, thinking about his mother, about truth, about what remains. Ananya in an airport, thinking about Chennai, about Priya grown and going her own way. Yusuf driving back to Minneapolis after attending the symposium, the road as thought-space. Delphine in a car toward Los Angeles, thinking about silence, about what comes after making. Ruth at home, not traveling, a brief glimpse of her continuation. Transit as the in-between, the space where reflection is possible.

## Key Elements

- Characters in transit, between the symposium and their lives
- Jerome on a flight: mother, truth, continuation
- Ananya at an airport: Chennai, Priya, the ethical question continuing
- Yusuf driving to Minneapolis: the road, music, survival with dignity
- Delphine in a car: silence, what comes after a career in narrative
- Ruth at home: not traveling, her continuation glimpsed
- The liminal space of transit as site of reflection
- Each character's resolution held during movement
- The return to life, the ordinary continuing
