# Scene Plan: Beyond

## Summary

The trilogy's final scene: brief final images for the POV characters, some longer, some just a glimpse. Jerome writing something, perhaps not for publication, the practice continuing. Ananya in a moment of solitude, the ethical questions still present but held differently. Delphine with Theo, watching him become himself, the future. Ruth felt through the others, wisdom in absence. Yusuf with music, survival, continuation with dignity. The last paragraph doesn't name the Eighth Oblivion - it's simply the world now, lived. The final sentence echoes and transforms the trilogy's opening, a gate that is not a wall, life continuing beyond the page.
