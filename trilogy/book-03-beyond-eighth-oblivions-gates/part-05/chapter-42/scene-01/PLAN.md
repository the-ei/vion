# Scene Plan: Morning - The Departures

## Summary

The morning after the symposium, characters depart through hotel lobbies and airports. Brief exchanges occur between people who may not see each other again - or may, the future uncertain but not threatening. Ananya and Jerome share wordless understanding, old friends who became allies. Delphine and Ananya's alliance is complete, their friendship the continuation. The young people - Priya, DeShawn, Amina - are glimpsed in conversation, the future. Each character heads to a gate, literal airport or figurative life, the dispersal from convergence gentle rather than dramatic.
