# Scene Plan: The Decision

## Summary

Ananya and Priya finally have the honest conversation they have perhaps never had - about what Ananya chose when she left Chennai, what she gave up, what she gained, and what she passed on to Priya. The question of Ananya's father hangs between them: should he come to America? Should Ananya stay? The resolution comes not as answer but as clarity about the question itself - Ananya understanding that the ethical dilemmas she has spent her career wrestling with began here, in the first departure, in the first choice between worlds. The scene ends with departure for the airport, Chennai receding, a gate crossed.

## Key Elements

- Honest conversation between Ananya and Priya, perhaps the first in years
- What Ananya chose when she left Chennai for America - examined directly
- Priya's own perspective: what she inherited, what she resisted, who she is becoming
- The question of Ananya's father's future - stay or relocate?
- Resolution as clarity rather than answer - the questions remain, held differently
- Ananya's career in ethics seen from the vantage of this original departure
- The gap between Ananya's public role and private uncertainties
- Departure for the airport, the physical leaving
- Chennai receding through car windows, the gate of departure crossed
