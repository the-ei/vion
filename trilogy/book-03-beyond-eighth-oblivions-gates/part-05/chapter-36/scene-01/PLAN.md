# Scene Plan: Arrival in Chennai

## Summary

Ananya and Priya arrive in Chennai after the long international flight, depleted and disoriented by grief and travel. The sensory assault of the city - humidity, traffic noise, the particular quality of South Indian light - floods Ananya with memories her body holds but her mind has suppressed. Relatives meet them at the airport, their grief both performative and genuine, the ritual of receiving a grieving daughter enacted with precision. Ananya sees her mother's body briefly before the cremation preparations, and something in her that has been held tightly for decades begins to crack.

## Key Elements

- The physical experience of arriving in Chennai after decades away
- Sensory immersion: heat, humidity, sounds, smells of Chennai airport and roads
- Relatives greeting them with stylized grief and genuine sorrow intermingled
- Priya as witness to her mother's displacement, seeing Ananya as stranger to herself
- The first glimpse of Ananya's mother's body, laid out for preparation
- Ananya's body remembering what her conscious mind has forgotten
- The linguistic shift - Tamil emerging, English receding
- Priya's alienation from this world she came from but never knew

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (47): POV character, arriving as grieving daughter, American visitor to birthplace
- **Priya Ramaswamy** (23): Ananya's daughter, seeing her mother's childhood world for the first time
- **Relatives (aunts, uncles, cousins)**: The receiving party at the airport, figures from Ananya's past
- **Ananya's mother** (deceased): Present as body, absence made material

## Setting

- Chennai, India - November 2041
- International airport arrivals hall, then the drive through the city
- Family home where the body is laid out
- Time: early morning local time, the disorientation of crossed time zones

## Timeline

- Late November 2041
- Scene spans approximately 4-5 hours: arrival, drive, reaching the family home
- Jet lag blurring time perception

- The body's memory vs. conscious memory as organizing tension
- Tamil words woven through without translation, the linguistic displacement
