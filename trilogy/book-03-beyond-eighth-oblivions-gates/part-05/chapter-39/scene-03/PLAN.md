# Scene Plan: Questions and Responses

## Summary

The Q&A session after Ruth's lecture becomes its own scene - the hard questions from students, the softball questions from admirers, and one question that cuts to the heart: did your framework matter? Ruth's honest answer neither defends nor defeats, simply acknowledging the gap between aspiration and achievement, between what law promised and what it delivered. Former clerks approach afterward, embodying what she passed on. Brief exchanges with David and Rebecca reveal the family layer beneath the professional performance - the mother beneath the judge.
