# Scene Plan: Afterward

## Summary

The lecture done, Ruth walks Yale's campus in the evening, alone with her thoughts. The physical place holds memory - where she walked as a student, where Susan would have walked with her if Susan had lived. Reflection on what she's passing on versus what she takes with her: the law as living thing that survives its practitioners. She's not dying in this scene, but she's accepting mortality - the work is done, not complete, but done as far as she can take it. Ruth sits on a bench Susan would have liked, at peace with incompleteness, the law as gate she passes through while it remains.

- Ruth alone, walking Yale's campus in evening
- The physical campus holding memory: student days, Susan, the past
- Reflection on what's passed on vs. what dies with her
- The law as living thing, surviving its practitioners

- Peace achieved not through resolution but through acceptance
