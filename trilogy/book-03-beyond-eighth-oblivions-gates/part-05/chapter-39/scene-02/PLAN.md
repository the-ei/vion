# Scene Plan: The Lecture

## Summary

Ruth stands at the podium in a packed room - former clerks, colleagues, opponents, students, her children David and Rebecca. The lecture on law, technology, and human dignity unfolds in excerpts, the formal language breaking into honesty when Ruth departs from her notes. She assesses the legal response to the Eighth Oblivion directly: partial success, partial failure, the law straining but holding. Watching her children's faces, she sees David's pragmatic accommodation and Rebecca's idealistic struggle - the two paths she recognizes in herself. Memory is triggered by phrases: cases she decided, arguments she lost, the accumulation of a life in law.

## Key Elements

- Ruth at the podium, the filled room, the weight of final address
- The lecture in excerpts: law, technology, dignity, institutional limits
- Departing from notes to say something true, unscripted honesty

- The audience's reception - some hearing, some not
