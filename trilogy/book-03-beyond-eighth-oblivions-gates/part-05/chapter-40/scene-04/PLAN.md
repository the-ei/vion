# Scene Plan: After

## Summary

Backstage after the concert, Yusuf receives his family. His mother's response is simple and profound - not about the music but about him, about what he has become. Amina offers pride in her brother alongside her own calling - her organizing work, her political voice, the different path she's chosen. Kevin Zhou's presence is felt through their ongoing connection, whether he's there or referenced. The question of what success means when you never expected to survive: Yusuf finds it's not triumph but continuation with dignity. Final image: Yusuf alone with his instrument, the music his regardless of audience.
