# Scene Plan: Soundcheck

## Summary

Yusuf Hassan stands on stage during soundcheck, testing equipment, running through technical preparations for his first real concert - not a gig, not background music, but a performance where people paid to hear him. The venue fills slowly as he works. Memory surfaces: the gig work years, the algorithm's management, the deliveries and rideshare shifts that funded survival while music was escape. Now music is becoming something else - public, shared, potentially a career - and Yusuf isn't sure he trusts this transformation. His Minneapolis musician collaborators surround him, the community that made this possible.

## Key Elements

- Yusuf at the venue, soundcheck underway
- The physical details of pre-performance preparation
- Memory of gig work: driving, delivering, the algorithm's management
- The transformation from escape to career, distrust of good fortune
- Minneapolis music community present - collaborators, supporters
- The venue filling: who comes to hear him?
- Music as private survival becoming public expression
- The specific sounds of soundcheck - fragments, adjustments, levels
- Precarity mindset not fully shed, waiting for this to be taken away
