# Scene Plan: The Performance

## Summary

Yusuf on stage, the concert itself. The songs emerge from their history - precarity, survival, dignity - but in performance they become something shared rather than private. The experience of performing: vulnerability and power simultaneously, giving something away while holding it. Yusuf reads the audience, finds his mother's face, feels the connection that performance at its best creates. Memory weaves through songs - each one triggers what created it, the gig work, the algorithmic management, the survival that became art. The moment when performance transcends self-consciousness and becomes simply itself.
