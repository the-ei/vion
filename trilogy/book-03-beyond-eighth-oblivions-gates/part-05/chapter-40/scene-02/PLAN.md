# Scene Plan: Before the Lights

## Summary

Backstage, waiting for the concert to begin, Yusuf watches his mother arrive in the venue, Amina helping her to her seat. His mother's health has stabilized but remains fragile - she has survived to see this, and that fact carries weight Yusuf can barely hold. Memory of his father's death surfaces: the warehouse accident when Yusuf was 12, the event that shaped everything after. A text arrives from Kevin Zhou - "Break a leg" - the unlikely friendship from Book 2's crisis maintained across years and worlds. The moment before going on stage, what Yusuf carries with him: precarity, survival, his family watching.
