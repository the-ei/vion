# Scene Plan: The Offer

## Summary

Yusuf receives the formal job offer from Cooperative Logistics Network, a worker-owned delivery cooperative that emerged from the organizing he was part of. He reads through the details with Amina present: salary, healthcare, retirement contributions, paid leave. It's not wealth but it's stability - the thing his family has never had. Amina is excited for him, already talking about what this means for their mother. But Yusuf feels something like dread. Later, he visits his mother, who cries when he tells her - not sad tears but something she can't name. That night, Yusuf finds job postings his father saved before he died, printouts never followed up on, dreams deferred until they became impossible.

## Key Elements

- The offer letter: physical document, specific numbers, the weight of stability
- Reading it with Amina: her excitement, her relief, her projection forward
- The specifics: $52,000 salary, full health insurance, 401k matching
- What this would mean: his mother could stop one of her jobs, Amina's loans could be paid faster
- Yusuf's dread: Why? The question he can't answer
- The visit to his mother: her apartment, her exhaustion, her tears
- What those tears contain: hope, fear, the weight of all the years
- That night alone: finding his father's old job applications
- The printouts: warehouse supervisor positions, delivery manager, things that required stability he never had
- The inherited pattern of aspiration deferred, now offered to Yusuf

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (30):** POV character, facing the possibility of what his family never had
- **Amina Hassan (22):** His sister, excited, already planning
- **Yusuf's mother (late 50s):** Her reaction to the news
- **Yusuf's father (in artifacts):** Present through the saved job applications

## Setting

- Yusuf's apartment, Minneapolis, where the offer arrives
- His mother's apartment nearby
- Fall 2040, a Thursday
- The physical documents as weight

## Timeline

- The offer arrives Thursday morning
- Reading it with Amina that afternoon
- Visiting his mother that evening
- Finding his father's applications that night
- Present-time action spans approximately 12 hours

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-mode: the offer letter rendered in detail, its language, its numbers, what they represent. His mother's apartment described with the exhaustion visible in its arrangement. The father's job applications given close attention - the paper quality, the handwriting, the dates. Long paragraphs about material reality: rent, healthcare, retirement, the vocabulary of stability. Yusuf's dread is physical, located in the body. The scene should feel like weight accumulating, like the offer is heavier than it should be.
