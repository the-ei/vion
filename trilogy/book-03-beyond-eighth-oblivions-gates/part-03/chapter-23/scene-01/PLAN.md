# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Jerome Washington enters the Austin Convention Center for TechForward 2040, a major industry conference where his son DeShawn will present. Everything in the space signals a world Jerome doesn't belong to - the branding, the vocabulary, the young people networking with fierce casualness. He's here because DeShawn invited him, a peace offering after their conflict at the memory care facility. Jerome moves through the conference margins, watching, remembering his own professional confidence in rooms where he once belonged. He sees journalists present too - but they're not investigators like him; they're content producers indistinguishable from the PR people they're supposedly covering.

## Key Elements

- The convention center as alien territory: banners, booths, swag, corporate aesthetics
- Jerome's displacement: wrong clothes, wrong age, wrong vocabulary
- Young founders and engineers networking - the language of disruption
- The conference schedule showing DeShawn's session, the company name Jerome doesn't recognize
- Journalists Jerome observes: not watchdogs but ecosystem participants
- Memories of conferences where Jerome was the one people sought out
- A brief interaction with a young reporter who doesn't recognize Jerome's name
- Kevin Zhou's company mentioned on signage - the shadow presence
- Jerome's interior negotiation: why he came, what he hopes for, what he fears
- Finding a seat at the back of the main stage area, waiting for DeShawn

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (57-58):** POV character, displaced witness in unfamiliar territory
- **Young conference attendees:** Background characters, representing what tech is now
- **Young journalist:** Brief interaction, doesn't know who Jerome is
- **Kevin Zhou (mentioned/present in signage):** Shadow presence, DeShawn's mentor

## Setting

- Austin Convention Center, TechForward 2040 conference
- Spring 2040, morning to early afternoon
- Corporate conference aesthetics: screens, booths, branded everything
- Air-conditioned interior, Texas heat outside

## Timeline

- Conference day, approximately 9am to 1pm
- Present-time action spans 4 hours of wandering and waiting
- Flashbacks to Jerome's journalism career, conferences where he mattered

## Page Target

7 pages (pages 1-7 of chapter)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-mode: the texture of a tech conference rendered exhaustively. Jerome's alienation detailed in long paragraphs - the vocabulary he doesn't know, the assumptions he doesn't share, the confidence of youth. The scene should feel like anthropology, Jerome observing a tribe he once studied but now finds unrecognizable. His own memories intrude as contrast - conferences where sources sought him out, where his presence meant something to people with power.
