# Scene Plan: The Presentation

## Summary

DeShawn takes the stage in one of the conference's featured sessions. His presentation is about building accountability systems into AI development - not the ethics-washing the industry has learned to perform, but something structural, embedded, harder to circumvent. Jerome is surprised. DeShawn cites historical precedents, investigative methodologies, systems thinking. He never names his father, but Jerome hears his own principles translated into a different language - accountability through architecture rather than exposure. Afterward, DeShawn is surrounded by admirers, investors, fellow engineers. Jerome waits at the edge, watching his son inhabit a world Jerome didn't build but somehow influenced.

## Key Elements

- DeShawn at the podium: confident, prepared, speaking the industry's language fluently
- The presentation content: accountability systems, structural integrity, design not oversight
- Historical references that echo Jerome's work without citing him
- Jerome's interior surprise: this is not what he expected, not the sellout he feared
- The methodology: investigative thinking applied to system design
- The audience response: engaged, some skeptical, some excited
- Kevin Zhou's influence visible in the technical sophistication
- But something else visible too - values that came from somewhere Kevin Zhou didn't provide
- After the presentation: DeShawn surrounded, networking, success visible
- Jerome waiting, processing, the gap between expectation and reality

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (57-58):** POV character, watching his son exceed and confound his expectations
- **DeShawn Cole (22-23):** Presenter, fully an adult in his professional element
- **Conference audience:** Receiving DeShawn's ideas, validating his presence
- **Kevin Zhou (referenced/possibly present):** Mentor whose influence is visible but not total

## Setting

- Main stage area, the conference's featured session space
- Midday, DeShawn's session one of the conference highlights
- Professional presentation setup: screens, slides, audience of several hundred

## Timeline

- The presentation spans approximately 45 minutes plus Q&A

- References to DeShawn's development, Kevin Zhou's mentorship

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-mode for the presentation itself: fragments of DeShawn's speech, Jerome's interior responses in the gaps. What Jerome expected versus what he hears. Short paragraphs punctuated by specific moments from the talk. The surprise rendered through rhythm - pauses in Jerome's internal monologue where recalibration happens. Some Knausgaard expansion for Jerome's key realization: this is his work, transformed, made into something that might actually function differently in the world.
