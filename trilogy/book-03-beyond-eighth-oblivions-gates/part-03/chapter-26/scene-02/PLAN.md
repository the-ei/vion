# Scene Plan: The Gathering

## Summary

Weekend family gathering at Daniel's parents' house in Tempe. Multiple generations present - the grandparents, Daniel's siblings and their children, the cousins running between rooms. Elena watches inheritance happening in real time: Sofia mimics her grandmother's hand gestures, Mateo has Daniel's father's laugh, the cousins carry fragments of aunts and uncles they barely know. She sees other families' inheritances too: what Daniel's sister has transmitted to her children, the patterns of her brother-in-law's anxiety visible in his son. A private moment with Daniel where they discuss what they're trying to build, what they hope their children carry, what they fear they've already passed on without meaning to.

## Key Elements

- The gathering space: Daniel's parents' house, yard, the familiar chaos of extended family
- Multiple generations visible: grandparents, parents, children, the flow between
- Sofia's gestures echoing her grandmother: inheritance visible, unconscious
- Mateo's laugh as his grandfather's: genetic or learned, unclear
- The cousins as contrast: different parents, different patterns
- Conversations overheard: climate, economy, politics, the future worried aloud
- Daniel's sister's children: what they've absorbed, what it reveals
- The private moment with Daniel: their partnership in transmission
- What they consciously want to pass on
- What they fear they've already transmitted without choice

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (40):** POV character, observer at the gathering
- **Daniel Varga:** Her husband, present in family context
- **Sofia Varga (11):** Visible carrying inheritance
- **Mateo Varga (8-9):** Also visible, different patterns
- **Daniel's parents:** The previous generation, still transmitting
- **Extended family:** Aunts, uncles, cousins, the family ecosystem

## Setting

- Daniel's parents' house, Tempe, Arizona
- A Sunday afternoon
- Backyard, kitchen, living room - the spaces of family gathering
- Late 2040, pleasant weather

## Timeline

- Sunday gathering, approximately 1pm to 6pm
- Present-time action spans 5 hours
- Flashbacks to earlier gatherings, what's changed

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-mode for the gathering's fragments: glimpses of conversations, gestures noted, inheritance observed in quick cuts. Short paragraphs moving between family members, between generations. The private conversation with Daniel gets Knausgaard expansion - longer, more interior, their partnership examined. The scene should feel like kaleidoscope, family as system, each person carrying pieces of others. The observation mode makes Elena slightly separate even in the midst of family.
