# Scene Plan: Preparation

## Summary

Sound check in The Threshold's performance space. Yusuf meets Mr. Tadesse, his father's former coworker from the warehouse days, now retired, who came for the opening. The man tells stories about Yusuf's father that don't match Yusuf's memories - a different person at work than at home, someone who joked constantly, who helped others with their shifts, who talked about his children's futures. Amina challenges Yusuf about why he stopped making music, what he's afraid of. Their conversation reveals how differently they've processed their father's death and their family's precarity - she escaped through education, he escaped through survival, neither escape complete.

## Key Elements

- Sound check: Yusuf's guitar, the sound system, the acoustic of the new space
- Mr. Tadesse's arrival: an old man who knew Yusuf's father differently
- Stories from the warehouse: the man Yusuf's father was among coworkers
- The gap between the father Yusuf knew and the coworker Mr. Tadesse knew
- Amina's challenge: "You stopped making music because you were afraid of wanting things"
- Their different paths out of precarity - hers academic, his survival-mode
- The songs Yusuf wrote years ago, never performed, hidden in his phone
- His mother's absence - she couldn't face this space, what it represents
- The community gathering: people arriving, some who remember the accident
- The approaching performance as threshold (echoing the space's name)

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (29-30):** POV character, preparing to do something he's avoided for years
- **Amina Hassan (21-22):** Challenging him as only a sibling can
- **Mr. Tadesse (late 60s):** His father's former coworker, bearing a different version
- **Community members:** Arriving for the opening, some with their own memories

## Setting

- The Threshold's performance area (former loading dock)
- Late afternoon into early evening
- The tech setup: microphone, speakers, stage lights
- People beginning to gather in the space

## Timeline

- Same day, approximately 6pm to 8pm
- Present-time action spans 2 hours
- Stories reference the father's working life, 14+ years ago

7 pages (pages 8-14 of chapter)

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-mode dominates: Mr. Tadesse's stories in fragments, Yusuf's responses brief. The sibling argument rendered in quick exchanges with silences between. What's said and unsaid about their different survival strategies. Some Knausgaard expansion when Yusuf processes the gap between the father he knew and the coworker Mr. Tadesse describes - a long paragraph of recalibration. The sound check provides physical anchors: the guitar's sound in the space, the levels being adjusted.

## Connections

- **Parent (Chapter 21):** Complications of inheritance - whose version of the father is true?
- **Previous (Scene 1):** Space established; now filled with other voices, other memories
- **Next (Scene 3):** The performance as release of what's been held
- **Part Theme:** We inherit multiple versions; the person we knew was not the only person they were
