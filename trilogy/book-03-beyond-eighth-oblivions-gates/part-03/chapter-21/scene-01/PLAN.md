# Scene Plan: Return

## Summary

Yusuf and Amina arrive at the transformed warehouse in Minneapolis - the place where their father died fourteen years ago, now converted into a community arts space called The Threshold. Yusuf had advocated for this transformation during his organizing work, had given testimony at city council meetings, had helped write grant applications. But he's never been inside since the renovation was completed. The space is unrecognizable - bright, open, filled with murals and installations - but Yusuf keeps seeing ghost images of what it was. He shows Amina where things used to be, where the accident happened. She's heard the story but never seen the place. He tells her things about their father she didn't know.

## Key Elements

- Arrival at The Threshold: the building's exterior, the new sign, the glass doors where loading bays used to be
- The interior transformation: murals, suspended sculptures, natural light where fluorescents once buzzed
- Yusuf's double vision: seeing both the arts space and the warehouse underneath
- Showing Amina the loading dock (now a performance stage), where the accident happened
- Stories about their father that Yusuf has never told: his jokes, his exhaustion, his thwarted dreams
- Amina's responses - she was only 7 when he died, her memories few
- Other people setting up for tonight's opening, the bustle of community event preparation
- Yusuf's role in making this space exist - advocacy as inheritance
- The question of whether transformation honors or erases
- His guitar in the car, the performance ahead that terrifies him

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (29-30):** POV character, returning to the site of formative trauma
- **Amina Hassan (21-22):** His sister, now adult, with her own relationship to their father's death
- **Event organizers (background):** Community members preparing for the opening
- **Yusuf's father (in memory):** Present through the space, through stories

## Setting

- The Threshold arts space, Minneapolis
- Fall 2039, late afternoon
- The former warehouse now bright and transformed
- The loading dock stage, gallery walls, community gathering area

## Timeline

- The day of the opening
- Present-time action spans approximately 2 hours (4pm to 6pm)
- Flashbacks to fourteen years ago and to Yusuf's childhood

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-mode: exhaustive attention to the physical space in its doubled nature - what it is now and what it was. Long paragraphs describing specific locations with both their current and past states layered. The labor of transformation detailed: the advocacy meetings, the grant writing, the community organizing that Yusuf was part of. Memory triggered by architecture. Amina's presence provides dialogue breaks, but the dominant mode is Yusuf's interiority navigating space and time.
