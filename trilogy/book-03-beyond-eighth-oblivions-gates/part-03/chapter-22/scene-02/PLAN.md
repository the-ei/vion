# Scene Plan: Sofia's Turn

## Summary

Sofia takes the stage for her presentation. What follows is partly what Elena expected and partly deeply surprising. Sofia talks about her great-grandmother (abuela), about Elena's work as a nurse practitioner, about Daniel's construction work. But she also talks about things Elena didn't know she'd absorbed - Elena's anger at the healthcare system, her political transformation, things Sofia must have overheard or intuited without being directly told. There's a moment where Sofia says something about "why my mom fights for people" that makes Elena's eyes fill. The presentation reveals that inheritance isn't controlled - children take what they witness, interpret it through their own understanding, and make it theirs.

## Key Elements

- Sofia at the microphone, her poster board with photographs behind her
- The expected content: family photos, abuela's story, Elena's nursing work
- The unexpected content: Sofia's interpretation of Elena's politics, her fury
- A specific phrase that cuts: "My mom fights for people who the system forgets"
- Elena's physical reaction: tears, Daniel's hand finding hers
- The audience's response: polite attention, some parents recognizing themselves
- What Sofia gets "right" and "wrong" - the gaps in her understanding
- The photographs Sofia chose, including one Elena didn't know she had
- Teachers watching, the institutional framing of family pride
- The end of the presentation, applause, Sofia looking for her parents' faces

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (39-40):** POV character, being seen through her daughter's eyes
- **Sofia Varga (10-11):** Presenter, interpreter of her mother's life
- **Daniel Varga:** Witness beside Elena, his own place in Sofia's story
- **The audience:** Collective witness to the Varga family as Sofia presents them
- **Mateo (visible):** Watching his sister, too young to fully understand

## Setting

## Timeline

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-mode dominates: Sofia's presentation rendered in fragments, Elena's interior response in the gaps between. Short paragraphs, the presentation's words arriving like interruptions. What Elena hears Sofia saying versus what Sofia might mean - the interpretation of interpretation. Some Knausgaard expansion for the most significant moments: when Sofia says "why my mom fights," Elena's response detailed in long sentences of interior experience. The tears, the hand-holding, the recognition.
