# Scene Plan: Understanding

## Summary

At home after the presentation. Sofia wants to know how she did - not the grade, but whether she got the family story right. Elena and Daniel must navigate this carefully, having conversations they've avoided. Sofia asks directly about Elena's politics, her anger, what she wants to change. Elena tries to explain without burdening her daughter with adult fury. Daniel offers his own version, different from Elena's. The chapter ends with Elena realizing that Sofia will carry her own version of the story regardless of what Elena intends - that transmission is never clean, and trying to control it only adds another layer to what children must sort through.

## Key Elements

- The kitchen table, lunch after the presentation
- Sofia's question: "Did I get it right?"
- The difficulty of answering - what is "right" about family stories?
- Elena trying to explain her politics without loading her daughter
- Daniel's version: different emphasis, different understanding of Elena's transformation
- Mateo present but less engaged, his own simpler relationship to family story
- What Elena actually wanted to transmit versus what she has
- Sofia's follow-up questions that reveal what she still doesn't understand
- The acceptance that inheritance will be Sofia's version, not Elena's
- Ending on Elena watching Sofia move on to something else, the story already hers

## Stylistic Notes

Blended mode: the family conversation moves between Knausgaard expansion (when Elena processes her responses, thinks through what to say) and Carson compression (the actual exchanges, the gaps, the things not said). The dialogue rhythm of family life - interruptions, subject changes, circles back. Elena's realization at the end rendered in longer paragraphs, the acceptance that transmission isn't controlled. The ending quiet, domestic - Sofia moving on to homework, Elena watching her carry the story forward in her own way.
