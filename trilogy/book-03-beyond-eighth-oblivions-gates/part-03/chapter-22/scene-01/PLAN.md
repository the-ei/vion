# Scene Plan: Assembly

## Summary

Elena and Daniel arrive at Desert Vista Elementary School for the annual Heritage Presentation Day. The gymnasium fills with families, children preparing to present their family histories to the community. Elena is nervous - she helped Sofia with research but Sofia insisted on writing the final script herself. Mateo waves from the primary school section where he'll present later. Elena watches other children present before Sofia: immigrant stories, working-class stories, professional-class stories. Each presentation reveals what families have chosen to transmit and how children have received it. The waiting becomes a meditation on inheritance - what all these families are giving their children, knowingly and unknowingly.

## Key Elements

- The school gymnasium: folding chairs, a temporary stage, children nervous backstage
- Elena and Daniel sitting together, their co-parenting visible to the community
- Other parents: the Garcias, the Thompsons, the Nguyens - families Elena knows
- Children's presentations: some polished, some awkward, all revealing
- What different families have transmitted: pride, shame, aspiration, trauma
- Elena's anxiety about what Sofia will say, what she's absorbed without being taught
- Daniel's calm versus Elena's tension - different relationships to being seen
- Mateo in the younger section, his own simpler presentation ahead
- Teachers managing the flow, the institutional framing of family stories
- Elena remembering her own childhood presentations, her mother's reactions

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (39-40):** POV character, anxious witness to her own legacy interpreted
- **Daniel Varga:** Her husband, calmer, different relationship to exposure
- **Sofia Varga (10-11):** Backstage, preparing to present
- **Mateo Varga (8):** In the younger section, visible across the gymnasium
- **Other families:** Background characters, each carrying their own inheritance patterns

## Setting

- Desert Vista Elementary School gymnasium, Phoenix
- Winter 2039-2040, a Saturday morning
- Folding chairs, temporary stage, motivational banners
- The community gathered, the institutional space transformed

## Timeline

- Heritage Presentation Day, approximately 9am to 10:30am
- Present-time action spans approximately 90 minutes of waiting and watching
- Flashbacks to Elena's own school days

## Page Target

6 pages (pages 1-6 of chapter)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-mode: the social texture of the school community rendered in detail. Long paragraphs about specific families, their presentations, what they reveal. Elena's internal commentary on what each child has absorbed from their parents. The waiting as accumulating anticipation. Some Carson compression in quick glimpses of other families, observations that cut. The gymnasium as microcosm of how communities transmit and receive across generations.
