# Scene Plan: The Solstice Dawn - Yusuf

## Summary

Yusuf wakes on the winter solstice for his first day at Kevin Zhou's company. His Minneapolis apartment is unchanged - the same bed, the same view of the street - but he dresses differently, not for gig work but for something that feels like a future. His mother has made breakfast, the way she did when he was a child going to school, the ritual of her care marking this as significant. Amina has texted encouragement from her dorm. He logs onto his new computer, meets his team via video, begins the strange work of translating precarity into data points that might actually change something. Between meetings, in his headphones, he plays the track he recorded at the studio. The music hasn't gone anywhere. He's just found another way to carry it.

## Key Elements

- The first day at a new job as transformation marker
- The unchanged apartment: same space, different trajectory
- His mother's breakfast: maternal care marking significance
- Amina's text: sibling support across distance
- The video calls with new colleagues: entering a different world
- The work itself: translating lived experience into actionable analysis
- The music in his headphones: what hasn't been lost
- The solstice dawn: light returning after the longest night
