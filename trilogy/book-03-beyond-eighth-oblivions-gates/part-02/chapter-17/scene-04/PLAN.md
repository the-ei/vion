# Scene Plan: The Solstice Evening - Ananya

## Summary

Ananya arrives at Elena's home in Phoenix for the wake - they have become friends through Delphine, through the kinship of reckoning, through the strange ways crisis draws people together. She brings food, sits with the family, listens to stories about abuela from relatives who have gathered. Elena introduces her to Sofia and Mateo. The Prometheus lawsuit consequences have begun - aggressive legal responses, former colleagues cutting contact - but here, in this house of mourning, corporate machinations feel remote. Ananya holds Elena's hand during a quiet moment on the patio. Elena asks: "How do you do it?" Ananya asks: "Do what?" Elena says: "Keep going after the reckoning." Ananya doesn't have an answer. But she's here. They're all here. The longest night ends with the promise of returning light.

## Key Elements

- Ananya traveling to Phoenix for the wake: friendship made visible in presence
- The gathering of Elena's extended family: the rituals of mourning
- Stories about abuela: the deceased made present through memory
- Ananya meeting Sofia and Mateo: the children who are inheriting
- The lawsuit consequences mentioned but distant: real but not central
- Elena's question about continuing after reckoning
- Ananya's lack of answer: presence as the only response available
- The solstice ending: darkness at its peak, light returning
