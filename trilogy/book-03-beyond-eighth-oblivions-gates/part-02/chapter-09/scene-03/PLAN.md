# Scene Plan: The Daughter's Questions

## Summary

Priya Ramaswamy, now 17, comes home from school to work on her college application essay. She has chosen to write about "someone who influenced your values" and has selected her mother as the subject. What begins as an innocent exercise transforms into an interrogation that parallels and deepens the legal complaint. Priya's questions - Did you ever stop anything bad from happening? Did you stay because you believed or because it was comfortable? - echo what the lawsuit alleges but carry emotional weight the legal documents cannot. Ananya must decide what to tell her daughter about the gap between intention and effect, knowing that whatever answer she gives will shape how Priya understands her mother and, by extension, herself.

## Key Elements

- College essay interview as framework for deeper confrontation
- Priya's questions escalating from admiring to probing to pointed
- The parallel between legal complaint and daughter's inquiry
- Ananya's struggle to explain institutional reality to someone who hasn't experienced it
- The difference between what Ananya told Priya over the years and what was true
- Priya beginning to see her mother as flawed rather than heroic
- The impending separation (college) adding urgency to understanding
- No clean resolution - the conversation opens wounds rather than healing them

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character facing her daughter's judgment, more devastating than any lawsuit
- **Priya Ramaswamy** (17): High school senior, intellectually precocious, emotionally intuitive; her questions come from genuine need to understand, not malice
- **Vikram** (referenced): Priya's father, whose venture capital work goes unquestioned in contrast to Ananya's scrutinized ethics role
- **Prometheus and its systems** (referenced): The subject of Priya's growing understanding

## Setting

- Ananya's apartment, late afternoon into evening
- The kitchen table where they sit, Priya's laptop open for notes
- The living room where the conversation migrates as it deepens
- Priya's overnight bag by the door (she shuttles between parents)
- Outside: San Francisco evening descending, lights appearing in windows
- Food going cold on the table as conversation overtakes dinner

## Timeline

- Late 2037, three days after the summons
- Scene spans one evening: 4 PM through 9 PM
- Priya stays overnight (her week at Ananya's)
- The conversation continues in fragments over subsequent days, but this evening is the core
- Application deadline creates external pressure

## Page Target

7-8 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Begin in domestic register: mother helping daughter with essay, familiar routine
- Shift gradually toward intensity as questions deepen
- Dialogue-heavy but interspersed with Ananya's interior response to each question
- Carson-style fragments when Ananya cannot answer coherently
- Knausgaard attention to Priya's body language, the small signals of judgment forming
- End on uncertainty rather than reconciliation - the scene should feel unfinished, as real conversations do
