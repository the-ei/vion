# Scene Plan: What Was Known

## Summary

Extended flashback scene tracking key moments in Ananya's decade at Prometheus Systems (2028-2036). Triggered by reading the complaint, Ananya moves between present-day examination of saved files and memory of the meetings, presentations, and compromises that defined her tenure as Chief Ethics Officer. The scene reveals the texture of institutional complicity: the meeting where she first raised concerns about engagement optimization algorithms; the board presentation where her recommendations received praise and quiet burial; the performance review where her effectiveness was measured by how few projects she blocked rather than how many harms she prevented. In the present timeline, she searches her archived files for evidence - of resistance, of complicity, of the truth she can no longer clearly see.

## Key Elements

- Flashback structure alternating between past meetings and present-day file review
- The 2029 meeting where she flagged engagement optimization concerns
- The 2031 board presentation - "Ethics as Competitive Advantage"
- The 2033 performance review conversation with the CEO
- Her saved files as archaeological record of moral compromise
- The gap between what she believed she was doing and what the record shows
- The gradual normalization she recognized, resisted, and ultimately surrendered to
- Evidence that could help plaintiffs mixed with evidence that condemns her

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character in present; also 34-41 in flashbacks - tracking her evolution from hopeful reformer to exhausted accommodator
- **David Park** (memory): Prometheus CEO, charismatic and ruthless, skilled at making refusal feel like failure
- **Ethics team colleagues** (memory): The other voices in meetings, some sympathetic, most silent
- **Board members** (memory): Faces across the table who praised her work while ignoring its substance
- **HR director** (memory): The performance review voice defining success as non-obstruction

## Setting

- Present: Ananya's home office, laptop open, archived files spreading across screen
- Past (2029): Glass-walled conference room, early morning, her first major ethics intervention
- Past (2031): Board room, executive floor, the presentation that should have mattered
- Past (2033): CEO's office, the review that revealed what her role actually was
- Past (2035): Her own office at night, the moment she knew she should leave
- The contrast between Prometheus's gleaming spaces and the cramped apartment where she now reviews them

## Timeline

- Present: Late 2037, same day as summons receipt, afternoon into evening
- Flashback span: 2028-2036, key moments across eight years
- The structure moves chronologically through the flashbacks while Ananya's present-day search proceeds
- Night falls during the scene, marking the passage of hours

## Page Target

7-8 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard mode for the flashbacks: exhaustive detail of meetings, the specific words exchanged, the light in rooms
- Present-day sequences more Carson-compressed: staccato file names, fragments of documents
- The contrast between elaborate past scenes and sparse present reinforces loss
- Internal monologue should blur the line between explanation and self-justification
- No resolution - the scene ends with Ananya no closer to certainty about her own complicity
