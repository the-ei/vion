# Scene Plan: The Summons

## Summary

Ananya Ramaswamy receives a legal summons at her San Francisco apartment, delivered on an ordinary Tuesday morning. The complaint names her as a defendant alongside Prometheus Systems in a civil suit brought by former employees and users alleging that AI systems she approved for deployment caused demonstrable psychological harm. As she reads through the pages, she encounters her own memos quoted back at her - words meant as warnings now reframed as evidence of knowledge. The scene establishes the chapter's central tension: Ananya's decade of attempted ethics work now weaponized as proof of complicity. She calls Delphine Okafor-Barnes, the only person she can be fully honest with about what this means.

## Key Elements

- Legal summons arriving at her home, breaking the membrane between professional and personal
- The complaint's specific allegations about engagement optimization systems
- Ananya's own words quoted as evidence against her
- Her initial response moving from shock to recognition to dread
- The phone call with Delphine as emotional anchor
- Her apartment in the Marina District, smaller than before, reflecting post-Prometheus finances
- The ordinary morning interrupted by extraordinary consequences
- The question forming: cooperate, defend, or remain silent?

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character receiving the summons that catalyzes her reckoning; her identity as "the ethical one" now under legal examination
- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes** (41): Voice on phone, Ananya's closest friend, the one relationship that survived her professional transformation; provides perspective without judgment
- **Process server** (briefly): Impersonal delivery of the document that changes everything
- **Prometheus colleagues** (memory): Faces that surface as she reads names in the complaint

## Setting

- Ananya's apartment in San Francisco's Marina District, a two-bedroom she moved to after leaving Prometheus
- Late morning, Tuesday, late 2037
- Autumn light through bay windows, the ordinary beauty of the city indifferent to crisis
- Her home office corner where she does consulting work, the legal documents spreading across her desk
- Later, the balcony where she calls Delphine, overlooking the street below

## Timeline

- Late 2037, six months after Part 1
- Scene spans approximately three hours: from summons delivery (10 AM) through phone call with Delphine (early afternoon)
- First day of the chapter's week-long span
- The complaint references events from 2028-2036 (her Prometheus years)

## Page Target

6-7 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with Knausgaard-style attention to ordinary morning details before the summons arrives
- The reading of the complaint should slow time, each paragraph landing with physical weight
- Carson-style compression for the moments of recognition - when Ananya sees her own words weaponized
- Interior monologue blending legal language with emotional processing
- The phone call with Delphine shifts register to dialogue, providing relief from interiority
- End with open uncertainty rather than decision
