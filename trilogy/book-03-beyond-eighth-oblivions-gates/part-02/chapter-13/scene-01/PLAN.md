# Scene Plan: The Folder

## Summary

Priya Ramaswamy arrives at her mother's San Francisco apartment with a folder - printouts, notes, a bibliography. She has spent the summer between high school graduation and college doing independent research on her mother's career with the rigor of a thesis project. The confrontation that begins is clinical, almost academic: "I want to understand the decision-making process. Walk me through how the ethics review functioned in practice." But beneath the research methodology is something rawer. Ananya realizes her daughter has found documents she herself had forgotten - internal memos that leaked during the lawsuit, interview clips with former employees, academic papers analyzing Prometheus's ethics-washing. Priya's questions reveal the shape of her conclusion: that Ananya was complicit, that her presence legitimized harm, that the ethics role was performance.

## Key Elements

- The folder as physical object: Priya's research made tangible
- The clinical framing of the confrontation: methodology as emotional distance
- Ananya's shock at the thoroughness of Priya's research
- Documents Ananya had forgotten now weaponized by her daughter
- The questions escalating from process to judgment
- Priya's conclusion visible in her line of questioning
- The mother realizing her daughter has become her most rigorous critic
- No resolution - the scene ends with the confrontation just beginning

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character facing her daughter's documented judgment; her identity as "the ethical one" now subject to her child's analysis
- **Priya Ramaswamy** (18): Intellectually formidable, emotionally controlled, conducting this confrontation like research - but the emotion is there beneath the methodology
- **Prometheus colleagues** (in documents): Names and quotes from Priya's research, people Ananya worked alongside
- **Former employees and users** (in documents): Voices from interviews and lawsuits that Priya has compiled

## Setting

- Ananya's San Francisco apartment, late summer afternoon
- The living room where they sit, Priya's folder spreading across the coffee table
- The kitchen visible, dinner preparations abandoned
- Light changing through the windows as hours pass
- Priya's bags by the door - she's about to leave for college

## Timeline

- Late summer 2038, days before Priya leaves for college
- Scene spans approximately three hours: 4 PM (Priya's arrival) through 7 PM
- Priya's research conducted over the summer months
- Documents reference events from 2028-2036 (Ananya's Prometheus years)
- College departure creates deadline pressure

## Page Target

6-7 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with Priya's arrival: the folder visible immediately, Ananya's recognition of what's coming
- Priya's questions rendered precisely: she has prepared them, the language careful
- Ananya's interior response layered: recognition, defensiveness, curiosity about what Priya found
- The documents summarized rather than quoted at length - their existence matters more than their content
- Dialogue heavy but punctuated by Ananya's processing
- End mid-confrontation: Priya has laid out her case, Ananya has not yet responded substantially
