# Scene Plan: The Call

## Summary

Ananya calls Priya at college - not for permission, not for advice, but for witness. She tells her daughter what she has decided: to cooperate fully with the plaintiffs, to provide testimony, to accept whatever consequences follow. Priya is quiet for a long moment, then says: "I know this is hard. But I think it's right." It's not absolution - Priya is still processing her own judgment from Chapter 13 - but it's something like respect. After the call, Ananya signs the cooperation agreement, making the decision formal and irrevocable. The chapter ends with her alone in her apartment, not triumphant but clear. The reckoning is not complete - there will be depositions, testimony, fallout - but the choice has been made. She is no longer merely knowing; she is acting.

## Key Elements

- The phone call as witness, not consultation
- Priya's response: not forgiveness but respect
- The distance between them made audible: college sounds in the background
- Ananya announcing her decision rather than seeking approval
- Priya's "I think it's right" as validation from the person who matters most
- Signing the cooperation agreement: the legal act that makes choice irrevocable
- Ananya alone afterward: the solitude of moral decision
- Clarity rather than triumph: the feeling of alignment, not victory

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character completing her decision arc
- **Priya Ramaswamy** (18): Voice on phone, college freshman, offering witness to her mother's choice
- **Sarah Okonkwo** (referenced): The attorney who will receive the signed agreement
- **The cooperation agreement**: The document that formalizes the decision
- **Ananya's future self** (implied): The person who will face depositions, testimony, consequences

## Setting

- Ananya's San Francisco apartment, evening
- The living room where she makes the call, sitting on the couch where she and Priya talked in Chapter 13
- Her desk where the cooperation agreement waits, already printed
- Priya's college dorm room audible through the phone: voices, music, the sound of young life
- The December night outside, the city's lights
- The apartment after the call: quiet, her own breath, the document on the desk

## Timeline

- Winter 2038, the evening before the deposition deadline
- Scene spans approximately two hours: 8 PM (the call) through 10 PM (alone afterward)
- Priya is finishing her first semester of college
- The cooperation agreement has been prepared by Sarah Okonkwo's office

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with Ananya preparing to call: the phone in her hand, what she plans to say
- The call itself in dialogue, with Priya's voice and background sounds conveying her new world
- Priya's response rendered simply: no elaborate language, the power in plainness
- The signing of the agreement in physical detail: the pen, the paper, her signature
- Carson-style compression for the moment after signing: brief, white space, the weight of done
- Ananya alone at the end: Knausgaard interiority about what clarity feels like
- End with forward motion: the choice made, the consequences not yet arrived
