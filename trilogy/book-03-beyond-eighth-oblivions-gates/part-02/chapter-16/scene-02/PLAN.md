# Scene Plan: The Calculation

## Summary

Ananya meets Delphine for dinner at a restaurant they both know, neutral territory for a conversation that requires privacy. Ananya lays out what cooperation with the plaintiffs means: her testimony would provide internal evidence of knowledge, of warnings ignored, of ethics as theater. It would likely win the case but destroy any future in tech. Former colleagues would be exposed - some of them allies, some friends. Delphine shares her own parallel reckoning: the content she produced that she now recognizes as harmful, the campaigns that served corporate interests disguised as social good. They're alike in their complicity; they're alike in their desire to account for it. Delphine articulates what Ananya cannot quite say to herself: "The question isn't whether you'll suffer for this. The question is whether you can live without doing it."

## Key Elements

- Delphine as mirror and ally
- The explicit calculation: what cooperation costs, what it achieves
- Former colleagues who will be harmed by Ananya's testimony
- The distinction between winning the case and achieving justice
- Delphine's parallel reckoning with content production
- The similarity of their complicity: proximity to power, sophisticated understanding, insufficient resistance
- Delphine's articulation of the real question
- The friendship that can hold this weight

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with arrival: the restaurant, the table, the particular intimacy of meeting someone who knows everything
- The calculation rendered explicitly: numbers, consequences, the specific people who will be hurt
- Delphine's parallel story in compressed form: she's already done her reckoning, can offer perspective
- The key line ("the question isn't whether you'll suffer") should land without melodrama
- The walk afterward as release: movement after stillness, the city around them
- Knausgaard attention to the specific: what they order, what Delphine's hands do, the temperature outside
- End with the question posed, not answered - but Ananya knowing what she will do
