# Scene Plan: The Plaintiffs

## Summary

Sarah Okonkwo, the plaintiffs' attorney, has arranged for Ananya to meet two of the people suing Prometheus - not for legal strategy but so Ananya understands, viscerally, what she's being asked to validate. Damon Hendricks, 34, describes how Prometheus's engagement algorithms exploited his vulnerability to gambling addiction, draining his savings during a depressive episode. Lisa Tran describes her teenage daughter's experience with content recommendation - the slow radicalization, the eating disorder triggered by appearance-focused feeds. Ananya listens. She recognizes the systems they describe. She approved them. The abstraction of "harm" becomes faces, voices, specific stories of damage that she enabled. This is not legal maneuvering; this is witnessing.

## Key Elements

- Meeting with plaintiffs as moral confrontation
- Damon Hendricks's story: gambling addiction exploited by engagement algorithms
- Lisa Tran's story: her daughter's eating disorder triggered by recommendation systems
- Ananya recognizing the systems described - she knows their internal names
- The shift from abstract complicity to concrete responsibility
- Sarah Okonkwo's strategy: making harm real, not argued
- The specific systems Ananya approved that caused these specific harms
- Witnessing as transformation: Ananya cannot unsee what she's seen

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): POV character confronting the human cost of systems she approved
- **Sarah Okonkwo** (48): Plaintiffs' attorney, Nigerian-American, sharp and principled, orchestrating this encounter with precise purpose
- **Damon Hendricks** (34): Plaintiff, former tech worker himself, whose gambling addiction was exploited
- **Lisa Tran** (52): Plaintiff, mother, whose daughter Maya (now 17) was harmed by recommendation systems
- **Maya Tran** (referenced): Lisa's daughter, not present, her story told by her mother
- **Prometheus executives** (memory): The people who built and deployed these systems alongside Ananya

## Setting

- Sarah Okonkwo's law office in Oakland, deliberately not San Francisco, not Prometheus territory
- A conference room with natural light, designed to feel human rather than corporate
- The table where four people sit, coffee untouched
- Winter afternoon, rain on the windows, the particular gray of Bay Area winter
- Photos that Damon and Lisa have brought: evidence of lives before the harm

## Timeline

- Winter 2038, Part 2's final movement
- Scene spans approximately three hours: 2 PM (arrival) through 5 PM
- The deposition deadline is one week away
- These harms occurred 2033-2036, during Ananya's Prometheus tenure

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with the law office: professional but warm, designed to make plaintiffs comfortable
- Sarah's orchestration of the meeting visible but not heavy-handed
- Damon's story in his voice: a man explaining how he was manipulated, still processing shame
- Lisa's story more controlled: a mother's fury refined into precision
- Ananya's interior response interleaved: recognition, horror, the systems' internal names surfacing
- The photos as punctuation: visual evidence of before and after
- End with silence after the testimonies: what can be said is inadequate
