# Scene Plan: The Meeting

## Summary

Yusuf Hassan meets Kevin Zhou at a coffee shop in Minneapolis - Kevin has traveled from San Francisco specifically for this conversation. The dynamic is awkward: they shared a crisis during Book 2 but have had no ongoing relationship, know each other in the particular way that shared extremity creates but don't know each other's daily lives. Kevin explains his company's problem: they build systems that affect gig workers, and focus groups don't capture what it actually feels like to be on the receiving end of algorithmic management. They need someone embedded, someone who can identify harms before deployment. It's a real job - salary, benefits, stock options - not charity, not consultation. Yusuf's instinct is refusal; Kevin adds: "I'm not offering you a favor. I'm offering you a chance to make these systems less terrible. You'd have actual power." Yusuf asks for time to decide.

## Key Elements

- Kevin traveling to Minneapolis as demonstration of seriousness
- The awkward dynamic of crisis connection without ongoing relationship
- Kevin's explanation of the company's problem: systems without ground truth
- The job offer specifics: salary, benefits, equity - real, not performative
- Yusuf's instinct to refuse: pride, suspicion, fear of co-optation
- Kevin's reframe: not favor but opportunity to exercise power
- The question of whether working within the system can change it
- Yusuf asking for time rather than answering

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan** (27): POV character weighing an offer that challenges his understanding of resistance and accommodation
- **Kevin Zhou** (32): Engineer, startup founder, offering opportunity from complicated motives - guilt, genuine need, and something like friendship
- **The company** (referenced): Kevin's venture, what it does, why it needs Yusuf's perspective
- **The gig workers** (referenced): The people Yusuf would ostensibly be helping, including himself

## Setting

- A coffee shop in Northeast Minneapolis, gentrified neighborhood, uncomfortable for both of them
- A corner table chosen for privacy, exposed brick behind them
- Late fall afternoon, gray Minnesota sky through the windows
- The menu that Kevin doesn't know how to read, the ordering that marks class difference
- Yusuf's phone on the table, work notifications pinging even now

## Timeline

- Late fall 2038, approximately two months after Chapter 11
- Scene spans approximately two hours: 2 PM (Kevin arrives) through 4 PM
- Kevin flew in this morning, will fly back tonight
- The offer has a one-week decision window

## Page Target

6-7 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with Kevin's arrival: the way he enters the space, the markers of his different life
- The awkwardness should be physical: where to sit, what to order, how to begin
- Kevin's explanation of the company in dialogue that includes Yusuf's clarifying questions
- The job offer rendered specifically: numbers, titles, what the work would actually involve
- Yusuf's interior response layered: pride, suspicion, curiosity, fear
- Kevin's reframe ("actual power") should land as challenge, not seduction
- End with Yusuf asking for time, Kevin accepting, the handshake that means something uncertain
