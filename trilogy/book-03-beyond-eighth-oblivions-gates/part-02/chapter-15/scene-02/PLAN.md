# Scene Plan: The Deliberation

## Summary

Yusuf talks with Amina, home for fall break. He has laid out Kevin Zhou's offer, and she immediately sees the opportunity - it's everything they worked toward, a way out that doesn't require abandoning where he came from. But she also sees his hesitation: "You don't want to become him." Yusuf articulates what he fears: taking this job means admitting the system won, means trading his anger for comfort, means becoming someone who benefits from the very precarity that ground him down. Amina pushes back with the perspective of someone who escaped differently: "Or it means using what you learned to help people still stuck. Not everyone who succeeds is selling out." They argue, not bitterly, but with the intensity of siblings who care too much to be polite.

## Key Elements

- Amina as sounding board and challenger
- Her perspective: the college student who escaped via education
- Yusuf's fear of co-optation articulated clearly
- The question of what "selling out" actually means
- Amina's counter: success can be a tool, not just a capitulation
- The siblings' different escape routes: her education, his proposed inside work
- The argument that is also love
- No resolution from the conversation itself

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan** (27): POV character deliberating, using his sister to think out loud
- **Amina Hassan** (19): Sister, college student, who escaped precarity through scholarships and can see both the opportunity and the fear
- **Mrs. Hassan** (background): Present in the apartment, her silent hope for Yusuf's escape palpable
- **Kevin Zhou** (referenced): The person Yusuf fears becoming

## Setting

- The Hassan family apartment in Minneapolis
- The small living room where the siblings sit, their mother's presence elsewhere in the space
- Fall break timing: Amina home for a week
- The particular light of November afternoon in Minnesota: gray, early dark
- The sounds of the apartment: their mother in the kitchen, the neighbors' television through thin walls

## Timeline

- Late fall 2038, two days after the meeting with Kevin
- Scene spans one evening: 6 PM (after family dinner) through 10 PM
- Amina's fall break is one week; she leaves in four days
- Decision deadline is five days away

7-8 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with the aftermath of family dinner: dishes, the rhythm of household
- The conversation beginning casually, escalating naturally
- Yusuf's fears articulated in his voice: the particular rhythms of someone who reads widely but didn't finish college
- Amina's challenges matching his register: she knows him, doesn't soften
- The argument should feel like argument, not debate - interruptions, raised voices, then quiet
- Mrs. Hassan's presence felt through small intrusions: offering tea, asking a question, retreating
- End without answer: the siblings have clarified the question but not resolved it
