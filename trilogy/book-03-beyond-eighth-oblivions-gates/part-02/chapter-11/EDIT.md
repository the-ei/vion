# Edit Notes: Chapter 11 - The Ledger

## Prose Characteristics

**Knausgaard Elements (Strong)**
- Financial precarity rendered through algorithmic detail (rating drops, shift access)
- Jamal and Ahmad as alternate futures - the paths not taken
- Interior monologue calculating debt repayment timeline
- The strip mall parking lot as space for existential accounting

**Carson Elements (Strong)**
- "Two paths Yusuf had not taken. But his own path didn't feel like escape."
- Compressed narrative of Jamal's death (fentanyl, funeral, mother weeping)
- Ahmad's imprisonment distilled to commissary prices

**Voice Consistency**
- Yusuf's working-class perspective maintained through concrete detail
- Mathematical thinking about time and money
- Anger at systems without self-pity
- Twenty-seven, "still in the room where he had been a child" - devastating specificity

## Deviations from Plan

- **Scene 1 (The Cascade):** Collections notice, rating drop, cascade effect all present; Kevin Zhou call framework established
- **Scene 2 (Amina Comes Home):** Sibling dinner and honest conversation to be verified
- **Scene 3 (The Studio):** Music recording and Kevin's callback to be verified

## Editorial Decisions

- Rating drop from 4.5 to 4.2 - specific enough to feel real, devastating enough to matter
- Jamal's death: "fentanyl in something that was supposed to be something else" - the casualness of crisis-era death
- Ahmad "the smart one" now memorizing commissary prices - class mobility as myth
- Math made explicit: one year to pay down debt, credit destroyed, no apartment
