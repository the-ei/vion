# Scene Plan: Amina Comes Home

## Summary

Amina Hassan arrives in Minneapolis for spring break, carrying the visible markers of her transformation: a college sweatshirt, a new laptop, the confidence of someone whose future feels open. She and Yusuf have dinner with their mother in the apartment where both siblings grew up but only one still lives. The conversation is loving but charged with everything unsaid: Amina's awareness that she escaped what Yusuf did not, Yusuf's pride mixed with something that might be envy, their mother's joy at one child's success shadowed by worry for the other. After their mother goes to bed, the siblings talk honestly. Amina asks the question Yusuf has been avoiding: why did he stop making music?

## Key Elements

- Amina's arrival as marker of divergent trajectories
- The family dinner as performance of normalcy amid strain
- Mrs. Hassan's presence: still working despite health issues, still dependent on Yusuf's contributions
- The physical markers of Amina's new life: clothes, technology, vocabulary
- The unspoken economics: Amina's scholarships versus Yusuf's support payments
- The late-night sibling conversation after their mother sleeps
- Amina's question about music as probe of Yusuf's abandoned self
- The absence of satisfying answer - Yusuf cannot explain even to himself

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan** (27): POV character confronting his sister's escape alongside his own stagnation
- **Amina Hassan** (19): College sophomore at University of Minnesota, scholarship student, the family's achieved hope; her success both vindicates and complicates Yusuf's sacrifice
- **Mrs. Hassan**: Their mother, working two jobs despite chronic fatigue, her health precarious; loves both children but cannot hide her differential worry
- **Yusuf's music** (absence): The abandoned dream present in its absence throughout the scene

## Setting

- The Hassan family apartment in a working-class Minneapolis neighborhood
- The kitchen table where generations of family meals have occurred
- The small living room where Amina sleeps on the pull-out when visiting
- Yusuf's childhood bedroom, unchanged, where he still lives
- Spring evening in Minneapolis, snow finally melting, promise of warmth

## Timeline

- Spring 2038, one week after the cascade (Scene 1)
- Scene spans one evening: 5 PM (Amina's arrival) through midnight (sibling conversation ends)
- Amina's spring break is one week; this is her first night
- The music question refers to Yusuf's abandoned pursuit from 2033-2034

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with Amina's arrival: physical description detailed, Yusuf's perception of her transformation
- The family dinner in Knausgaard mode: specific foods, specific words, the texture of ordinary family
- Mrs. Hassan's presence conveyed through small gestures: how she serves food, watches her children, hides her exhaustion
- The shift to sibling conversation after mother sleeps: more intimate, more direct
- Amina's question about music should land hard - it's the question Yusuf won't ask himself
- End with the question unanswered but opened: something possible in the silence
