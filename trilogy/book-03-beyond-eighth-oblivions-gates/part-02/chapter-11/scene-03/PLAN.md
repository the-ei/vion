# Scene Plan: The Studio

## Summary

Yusuf returns to a recording studio he used to visit - a small, scrappy operation in Northeast Minneapolis that has survived the neighborhood's gentrification. He hasn't touched his music in four years. The session is awkward: the rust is audible, the equipment has changed, his voice and hands remember patterns his mind has forgotten. But something remains beneath the rust. The scene is about confronting abandoned possibility: did Yusuf leave music because circumstances demanded it, or because it was easier to blame circumstances than risk failure? The session doesn't produce finished work, but it produces a question he can no longer avoid. And at scene's end, Kevin Zhou's call comes back with something more specific than sympathy - an offer connected to skills Yusuf developed during the crisis that have nothing to do with music, but might be his way back to a life that includes it.

## Key Elements

- The recording studio as space of abandoned dreams
- Yusuf's physical rust: fingers on keys, voice in microphone
- The studio owner, someone from the old days who remembers Yusuf's potential
- What remains beneath the rust: not skill but something harder to name
- The question crystallizing: circumstance or self-sabotage?
- Kevin Zhou's callback with a more concrete offer
- The connection between crisis survival skills and corporate need
- Music as the thing that can't be bought - separate from the offer, still calling

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan** (27): POV character confronting his abandoned creative self
- **Marcus Williams** (45): Studio owner, producer, remembers Yusuf from five years ago, offers space without judgment
- **Kevin Zhou** (32): Voice on phone at scene's end, articulating an offer that connects Yusuf's crisis experience to real opportunity
- **Yusuf's younger self** (memory): The 22-year-old who made music before everything fell apart
- **The music itself**: Present in fragments, unfinished beats, the ghost of what Yusuf didn't become

## Setting

- Recording studio in Northeast Minneapolis, industrial space converted to creative use
- The live room with its microphones and treated walls
- The control room with equipment Yusuf half-recognizes
- The neighborhood around: breweries, art spaces, the uncomfortable coexistence of old residents and new money
- Yusuf's phone, on silent during the session, lighting up with Kevin's call

## Timeline

- Spring 2038, three days after Amina's arrival
- Scene spans one afternoon: 2 PM (arrival at studio) through 6 PM (Kevin's call)
- Yusuf booked two hours of studio time; he stays longer
- The offer has emerged from Kevin's follow-up call after the initial conversation

## Stylistic Notes

- Open with the physical journey to the studio: the bus, the walk, the neighborhood changed and unchanged
- The studio itself in careful physical description: Yusuf's attention to equipment, to space, to the particular smell of treated rooms
- The session in close focus: specific musical choices, specific failures, the hands that remember
- Interior monologue on the question of why he stopped - no clear answer, but the question itself mattering
- Kevin's call in dialogue: specific enough to intrigue, vague enough to leave decision for later
- End with the tension between music (uncommodifiable) and the offer (economic salvation) - both real, both calling
