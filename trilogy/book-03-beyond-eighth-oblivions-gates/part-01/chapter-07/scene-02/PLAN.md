# Scene Plan: The Testimony

## Summary

Washington D.C. Ruth before Senator Miriam Oduya's subcommittee on AI governance. The theater of political testimony: grandstanding questions, performative outrage, occasionally serious engagement. Ruth delivers her prepared statement on the case's implications - why the liability question matters, what the post-crisis frameworks actually do and don't provide. She fields questions with judicial precision. One hostile senator tries to paint her as an activist judge; she responds with a precision that draws blood without raising her voice. Afterward, Benjamin Torres finds her in the hallway - his company has noticed, he's in a difficult position, but he wanted her to know she was effective. Ruth flies home exhausted but feeling something she hasn't felt in years: consequential.

## Key Elements

- Senate hearing room: the architecture of American political process
- Senator Oduya: ally with her own agenda, using Ruth strategically
- Ruth's prepared statement: the legal argument in public form
- The Q&A: various senators with various investments
- The hostile senator: attempting to discredit, receiving careful response
- Ruth's precision under pressure: the judge's training serving her
- Benjamin in the hallway: the awkward reunion
- His company's attention: the stakes becoming personal for him too
- The flight home: exhaustion mixed with purpose
- The feeling of mattering: something Ruth had missed
