# Scene Plan: The Crossing

## Summary

Delphine on the road from Los Angeles to Tucson - she's driving rather than flying, needing the transition time to prepare for what's coming. The I-10 corridor in 2037 reveals the changed American Southwest: massive solar installations stretching to the horizon, rest stops with water rationing signs, the desert showing its new extremity. During the drive, Delphine listens to her own documentary work, hearing it differently with Ananya's message fresh in her mind. Video calls with Theo from the car break up the solitude - he's fine, Jessie is managing, but the distance already weighs. As she approaches Tucson, the city emerges from heat shimmer, adapted and surviving.

## Key Elements

- The decision to drive: needing liminal time, the transition space
- The I-10 corridor's transformed landscape: industrial solar, drought markers, heat extremity
- Delphine's documentary work played through the car's system: hearing it with new ears
- The gap between what she made and what it may have obscured
- Rest stop encounter: brief human contact revealing 2037's ordinary life
- Video calls with Theo: seven-year-old's concerns, Jessie in the background
- The guilt of absence even for necessary work
- Tucson appearing through the heat: a city that has learned to survive
