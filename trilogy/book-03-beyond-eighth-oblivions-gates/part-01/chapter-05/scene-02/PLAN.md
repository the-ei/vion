# Scene Plan: The Relocation

## Summary

Delphine arrives at Ananya's rented house in a Tucson neighborhood of climate migrants and tech refugees. Ananya has aged visibly - the years since the crisis marked on her face - but she's also calmer, more focused than Delphine remembers from their previous brief encounters. They catch up: Ananya's departure from Prometheus (pushed out after the crisis when her internal warnings became corporate liability), her year of independent research, her choice of Tucson (cheaper, less surveilled, Priya enrolled at the university). Priya appears briefly, polite but guarded about her mother's work. Then the catching-up ends and the real conversation begins.

## Key Elements

- Tucson neighborhood: climate migrants, tech refugees, a new American demography
- Ananya's rented house: modest, functional, chosen for purposes not comfort
- The physical changes in Ananya: age, focus, something hardened
- Ananya's departure from Prometheus: pushed out when warnings became inconvenient
- The year of independent research: what Ananya discovered working outside institutions
- Tucson's appeal: cheaper, less surveilled, her daughter nearby
- Priya at 17: guarded, smart, aware of risks her mother has taken
- The pivot from catching up to the urgent matter

## Characters

- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes** (POV, 41): Arriving to learn what Ananya knows
- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (44): Former Chief Ethics Officer, now independent researcher
- **Priya Ramaswamy** (17): Ananya's daughter, University of Arizona student, wary

## Setting

- Tucson residential neighborhood (south side or midtown)
- Ananya's rented house: small, single-story, southwestern style
- Time: Late afternoon, approximately 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM
- Continuation of travel day

## Timeline

- Approximately 3 hours of story time
- Arrival, reunion, catching up
- Leads into evening and Scene 3

6-7 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- Tucson rendered as environment: heat, light, architecture, the feel of a different America
- Ananya's physical presence conveying what's changed since Books 1-2
- The reunion should balance warmth with tension - they're not quite friends yet
- Priya's appearance should plant seeds without over-explaining
- The transition from personal to urgent should feel organic, inevitable
- Interior monologue on Delphine processing what she sees and feels
