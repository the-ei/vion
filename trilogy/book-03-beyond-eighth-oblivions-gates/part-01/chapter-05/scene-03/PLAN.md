# Scene Plan: The Second Phase

## Summary

Evening and into night. Ananya lays out what she's documented over the past year: the post-crisis regulatory frameworks were designed to fail. The "safety" protocols built into AI systems created new surveillance capabilities. The consolidation that followed concentrated power in fewer hands than before the crisis. Everything looks managed from outside; from inside, it's a ratchet tightening. She has evidence - documents, recordings, internal communications - but can't release it alone; she'd be too easy to discredit, too vulnerable to retaliation. She wants Delphine to help her tell the story in a form that can't be ignored or absorbed. Delphine understands the scale of what's being asked. The chapter ends with both women on the porch, the desert night enormous around them, committing to something that will change both their lives.

## Key Elements

- Ananya's year of research: what she found, how she found it
- The "second phase": post-crisis consolidation disguised as reform
- Regulatory frameworks designed to fail: specific mechanisms
- AI safety protocols as surveillance expansion: the technical reality
- Power concentration greater than before: who gained, who lost
- The evidence Ananya has: documents, recordings, the corporate paper trail
- Why she can't release it alone: the vulnerability of single voices
- What she's asking Delphine to do: make it visible, make it undeniable
- The porch conversation: two women deciding to take a risk
- The desert night: vast, indifferent, beautiful
