# Scene Plan: The Bridge

## Summary

After Yusuf leaves, Kevin Zhou makes a decision. He finds Jerome Washington's contact information - not hard, Jerome is a public figure - and places a call. The conversation is awkward at first: Kevin Zhou explaining who he is, why he's calling, what he knows about Prometheus. Jerome is guarded but listening. When Kevin Zhou mentions DeShawn, Jerome's attention sharpens. Kevin Zhou explains what he sees: the acquisition pattern, the absorption strategy, the way talented young founders become complicit before they understand what's happened. He's not telling Jerome what to do - just giving information he wishes someone had given him. Jerome thanks him. The call ends. Kevin Zhou looks out at Oakland, feeling something shift. Part 1 closes with the sense that scattered threads are beginning to weave together, even as the characters don't yet see the pattern they're forming.

## Key Elements

- The decision to call: reaching outside his own sphere
- Finding Jerome: the public information of a notable journalist
- The call's awkward beginning: two strangers with a connection (DeShawn) between them
- Kevin Zhou's explanation: who he is, what he knows about Prometheus
- Jerome's guarded listening: journalist's skepticism, father's attention
- The Prometheus acquisition pattern: what Kevin Zhou can describe from experience
- How young founders become complicit: the mechanism of absorption
- Kevin Zhou's intention: information sharing, not instruction
- Jerome's thanks: genuine but uncertain
- The call's end: bridge built, outcome unknown
- Kevin Zhou at the window: Oakland's lights, the weight of having acted
- Part 1 closure: threads weaving without characters seeing the pattern
