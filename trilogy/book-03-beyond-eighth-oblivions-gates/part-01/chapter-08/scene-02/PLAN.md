# Scene Plan: The Visit

## Summary

Yusuf arrives in Oakland - their first in-person meeting since the crisis threw them together in Book 2. Their friendship still surprises both of them: formed in extremity, maintained through texts and occasional calls, now real in the same room. They walk through Oakland together, Yusuf seeing the Bay Area for the first time. Yusuf's life has stabilized: steady work, his sister Amina thriving at college, his mother's health managed. But he hasn't lost his clarity about systems - if anything, stability has sharpened his analysis. Their conversation ranges wide, and Kevin Zhou realizes Yusuf understands something about power that he's still learning. The visit gives Kevin Zhou perspective on the DeShawn situation and confirms what he needs to do.

## Key Elements

- Yusuf's arrival: the physical presence of an unlikely friend
- The friendship's origin: crisis in Book 2, maintained since
- Walking Oakland together: Yusuf's outsider view revealing what Kevin Zhou takes for granted
- Yusuf's life now: Minneapolis, steady work, family doing better
- Amina's success: the next generation on a different path
- Yusuf's analysis: ground-level clarity about systems and power
- Their conversation: technology, survival, what actually helps people
- Kevin Zhou learning from Yusuf: the reversal of assumed expertise
- The perspective gained: clarity about DeShawn's situation
- Yusuf's implicit counsel: sometimes you have to go around someone to help them

## Characters
