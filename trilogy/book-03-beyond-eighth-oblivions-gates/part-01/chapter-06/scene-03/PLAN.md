# Scene Plan: The Gap

## Summary

Three days later. DeShawn has returned to his startup, Denise's treatment plan is set, and Jerome receives an email from Malik Jeffries, a young journalist and researcher examining the Eighth Oblivion coverage for a book project. Malik has found something: evidence that certain corporate actors knew more than they revealed during Jerome's investigation - that the story had a hidden dimension. Jerome's first instinct is defensive; he was thorough, he was careful. But Malik's evidence is compelling. A video call where Malik lays out the findings and asks Jerome to collaborate. Jerome faces the choice: protect the record he built or pursue the truth it missed. Denise, from her chair, tells him she knows what he'll choose. The chapter ends with Jerome beginning to write again, looking backward to find what was hidden.

## Key Elements

- Three days of aftermath: Denise's treatment beginning, Jerome adjusting
- Malik's email arriving: a young journalist with new information
- Jerome's defensive response: his coverage was careful, thorough
- The evidence Malik presents: corporate actors who knew more than they revealed
- What the original coverage missed: not error but limitations of access
- Video call with Malik: the young researcher's earnestness, his findings
- The choice: legacy protection versus truth pursuit
- Denise's observation: she knows who Jerome is
- Jerome at his desk: beginning to write again with different purpose
