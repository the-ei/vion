# Scene Plan: The Return

## Summary

DeShawn comes home when he hears about his mother's diagnosis. The conflict of Chapter 2 is temporarily suspended as the family confronts something that transcends their disagreements. A night of genuine presence: Denise, Jerome, DeShawn together in the kitchen, talking about treatment plans, eating takeout, briefly laughing at old stories. DeShawn shows unexpected tenderness - this is still his mother, whatever distance has grown between him and his father. After DeShawn goes to sleep in his old room, Jerome and Denise talk in the dark about fear, about time, about what they've built together. The intimacy is painful and necessary.

## Key Elements

- DeShawn's arrival: the physical fact of the family together
- The suspended conflict: disagreements don't disappear but recede
- The kitchen as gathering place: takeout containers, familiar space
- Treatment plan discussion: the practical details of what's coming
- Old stories: the family's shared memory surfacing naturally
- DeShawn's tenderness toward his mother: seeing him differently
- Jerome watching his son care for his wife: complex gratitude
- After DeShawn sleeps: the parental conversation in the dark
- Jerome and Denise's marriage: thirty years of building something together
- The fear of loss making presence more acute

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington** (POV, 55): Watching his family, feeling love's vulnerability
- **Denise Washington** (54): The center of attention but deflecting toward normalcy
- **DeShawn Washington** (20): Son returning, conflict suspended, tenderness revealed

## Setting

- The Washington family home in Baltimore
- Kitchen where they gather
- Parents' bedroom where the final conversation occurs
- Time: Evening through late night, approximately 6:00 PM - midnight
- Day 2 of the chapter's week-long span

## Timeline

- Approximately 6 hours of story time
- Family dinner and conversation 3 hours
- Private conversation between Jerome and Denise 1 hour
- Evening deepening into night

## Page Target

7-8 pages

## Stylistic Notes

- The family gathering rendered with warmth and underlying tension
- Food as comfort: the specificity of what they eat, who ordered it
- Conversation should feel natural, not scripted
- DeShawn's tenderness should surprise Jerome (and the reader)
- The bedroom conversation in intimate Knausgaard mode
- Marriage as accumulation: thirty years present in small gestures
- Fear articulated between people who rarely articulate fear
