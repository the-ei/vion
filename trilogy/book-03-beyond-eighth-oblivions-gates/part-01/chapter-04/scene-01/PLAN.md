# Scene Plan: Morning Code

## Summary

Kevin Zhou wakes early in his Oakland apartment - small, clean, deliberately chosen as a step down from his San Francisco startup days. The morning routine establishes his new relationship to life: exercise (a practice from his Berlin period), careful breakfast (learning to cook as part of learning to live), then three hours of focused coding. His current project involves digital infrastructure for mutual aid networks - something that would have seemed beneath him at 25 but feels essential now. His small team of three people works asynchronously; they meet twice a week. The scene establishes Kevin Zhou's transformation while revealing its incompleteness. A message from DeShawn arrives, enthusiastic about his startup's latest milestone. Kevin Zhou's response is measured, supportive, worried.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's Oakland apartment: small, minimalist, overlooking a neighborhood street
- Morning routine as practice: the physicality of exercise, the attention of cooking
- The coding session: deep focus, familiar pleasure, but with different purpose
- The mutual aid infrastructure project: technical elegance serving human need
- His small team: three people, asynchronous collaboration, twice-weekly meetings
- DeShawn's message: excitement about a funding round, a product launch, momentum
- Kevin Zhou's internal response: recognition of his younger self, fear for DeShawn's trajectory
- The careful reply: support without endorsement, warning without preaching
