# Scene Plan: The Mirror

## Summary

Evening. Kevin Zhou has a longer video call with DeShawn - the mentorship taking shape as something between instruction and warning. DeShawn's startup is promising, moving fast, attracting attention from investors and larger companies. Kevin Zhou sees his own early trajectory replayed and doesn't know how to intervene without crushing ambition that might be healthy. They discuss what DeShawn's platform actually does - an AI-driven optimization system that Kevin Zhou can see both the potential and the dangers of. Kevin Zhou tries to share what he learned from his own rise and reckoning without being preachy; he mostly fails. After the call, Kevin Zhou cooks dinner alone and thinks about Yusuf - sends a text checking in. The chapter closes with Kevin Zhou at his window, looking at Oakland's lights, holding the tension between what he built, what it cost, and what he might build still.

## Key Elements

- The video call setup: two screens, two lives at different stages of the same trajectory
- DeShawn's excitement: the energy of building something that's working
- Kevin Zhou's recognition: seeing himself at 20, knowing what came after
- The startup's product: AI optimization for small business operations
- The tension between opportunity and capture that Kevin Zhou sees and DeShawn doesn't
- Kevin Zhou's attempt to warn without crushing: the difficulty of transmitting experience
- Cooking alone: the physical practice grounding the emotional weight
- The text to Yusuf: maintaining the unlikely friendship
- Final image: Kevin Zhou at the window, the city's lights, the weight of accumulated choices

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou** (POV, 31): Mentor who knows too much, unable to fully transmit his knowledge
- **DeShawn Cole** (20, via video): Ambitious, talented, unable to hear warnings as anything but fear
- **Yusuf Hassan** (27, via text): The friendship maintained through small touches
