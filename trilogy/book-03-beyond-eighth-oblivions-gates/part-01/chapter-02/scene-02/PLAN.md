# Scene Plan: The Record

## Summary

Back in his home office, Jerome records a podcast interview with a host eager to discuss his Eighth Oblivion coverage. The interviewer wants the triumphalist narrative: brave journalist exposed corruption, public learned the truth, change followed. But Jerome finds himself unable to deliver this story. Instead, he describes what he documented - systemic failures, corporate evasions, political complicity - and then honestly confronts the aftermath: reforms absorbed, narratives co-opted, understanding that changed nothing fundamental. The interview becomes uncomfortable. Afterward, Denise brings him lunch and they have a quiet conversation about purpose, about whether truth-telling has any power in a world that has learned to metabolize exposure.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's home studio: the physical setup of independent journalism in 2037
- The podcast host (audio only): pressing for the legacy narrative Jerome can't deliver
- Jerome's Eighth Oblivion coverage recalled through specific examples
- The honest reckoning: exposure that changed the conversation but not the outcome
- The interviewer's discomfort when Jerome refuses the hero framing
- Denise bringing lunch: the domestic intrusion that becomes the real conversation
- Jerome and Denise's marriage as long partnership, the shorthand of decades
- The question: what is journalism for if truth doesn't produce change?
