# Scene Plan: The Inheritor

## Summary

Evening. DeShawn arrives for a rare dinner visit. The meal is tense with unspoken conflict - DeShawn, now 20, has dropped out of college to run a successful AI-driven startup, embodying exactly the kind of optimization system Jerome spent his career warning about. The dinner conversation circles the disagreement before finally landing on it: DeShawn sees opportunity, democratization, the future; Jerome sees capture, consolidation, the same patterns repeating. The argument that erupts isn't loud but cuts deep. Denise mediates. DeShawn leaves early. Afterward, Jerome sits with the knowledge that his son has become what he fought, and the further knowledge that he can't stop loving him. The chapter ends with Jerome beginning to write - not an article, but something else. A reckoning.

## Key Elements

- DeShawn's arrival: the physical fact of the son Jerome can no longer fully reach
- The dinner table as arena of generational conflict
- DeShawn's startup: AI-driven optimization platform that Jerome sees as dangerous
- The argument: technology as liberation vs. technology as control
- Denise's mediation - her role as bridge that can't quite span the gap
- DeShawn's departure: early, frustrated, still loving despite distance
- The aftermath: Jerome and Denise alone, processing the failure to connect
- Jerome at his desk, beginning to write something personal for the first time

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington** (POV, 55): Father confronting the gap with his son
- **DeShawn Washington** (20): Tech founder, ambitious, unable to see his father's warnings as anything but fear
- **Denise Washington** (54): Mediating the conflict, holding space for both

## Setting

- Time: Evening, approximately 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM

## Stylistic Notes

- The dinner should build tension through Knausgaard attention to small gestures and avoided eye contact

- DeShawn should be sympathetic even as his position troubles Jerome
- The generational gap should feel real - both perspectives internally consistent
- After DeShawn leaves, shift to more interior Knausgaard mode
- Final image: Jerome writing something he doesn't yet understand, searching
