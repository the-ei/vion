# Edit Notes: Chapter 02 - What the Record Shows

## Prose Characteristics

**Knausgaard Elements (Strong)**
- Deep immersion in place: Baltimore rendered through sensory detail and personal history
- Caregiving scenes given full weight - the intimacy of dementia care
- Long sentences that accumulate meaning: the drive from Roland Park to Northwood becomes meditation
- Jerome's relationship to the city as extension of identity

**Carson Elements (Moderate)**
- Grace's dialogue compressed: "'She's asking for Daddy again,' Grace said. 'Since five this morning.'" - devastating in brevity
- White space used to mark transitions between locations
- The "ritual of arrival" rendered as fragmentary list of preparations

**Voice Consistency**
- Jerome's journalist's eye present: observation, categorization, analysis
- Underlying self-examination about whether truth-telling matters
- Warmth beneath the professional detachment, especially with family
