# Scene Plan: The Seminar

## Summary

Monday morning at the Berkeley Law building. Ruth leads her seminar "Technology and Constitutional Order" through a discussion of algorithmic governance - specifically, when automated decision-making requires due process protections. The students are sharp, their sharpness tactical in ways that unsettle Ruth. She finds herself departing from the syllabus to tell them about cases from the Eighth Oblivion crisis, watching their faces as she describes decisions she made that shaped outcomes. One student asks the question Ruth has been avoiding: "Did any of the legal responses actually work?" Her answer is more honest than she intended, revealing her own doubts about whether institutional reform was real or theater.

## Key Elements

- Berkeley Law building: glass and concrete, institutional modernism
- The seminar room: twelve students, oval table, natural light
- The discussion topic: algorithmic governance and due process
- Students representing different types: ambitious, idealistic, tactical, genuine
