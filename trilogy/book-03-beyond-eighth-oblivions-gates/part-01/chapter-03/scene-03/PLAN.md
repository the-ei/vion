# Scene Plan: The Former Clerk

## Summary

Friday evening. Benjamin Torres, who clerked for Ruth fifteen years ago and was one of her best, takes her to dinner in San Francisco. He now runs policy for a major tech company Ruth has criticized in her writing. The dinner is warm and tense - genuine affection cut with fundamental disagreement. Benjamin explains his theory of change from within; Ruth recognizes her younger self in his arguments and doesn't know whether to feel compassion or disappointment. As the evening deepens, Benjamin reveals something: a regulatory fight is coming, one that will test whether the post-crisis frameworks have any teeth. He's asking, without asking, for Ruth's help - or at least her abstention. Ruth returns to Berkeley uncertain what to do with the information, carrying the weight of an impossible request.

## Key Elements

- San Francisco restaurant: upscale but not ostentatious, chosen for privacy
- Benjamin's trajectory from idealistic clerk to corporate policy director
- The warmth of old mentorship mixed with professional estrangement
- Benjamin's "change from within" argument and Ruth's recognition of her own past rationalizations
- The specific regulatory fight: AI liability standards reaching the Supreme Court
- Benjamin's complicated position - he disagrees with his company but serves it
- The implicit request: Ruth's influence could matter, if she chooses to use it
- The drive back to Berkeley, Ruth alone with a decision she can't yet make

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson** (POV, 64): Former mentor confronting what her mentorship produced
- **Benjamin Torres** (38): Former clerk, now Director of Policy at major tech firm, caught between worlds
- **Susan** (deceased): Present through memory - she would have had sharp words about Benjamin's choices
