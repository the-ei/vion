# Edit Notes: Chapter 01 - The Arrangement of Light

## Prose Characteristics

**Knausgaard Elements (Strong)**
- Long, accumulative sentences establishing domestic atmosphere
- Deep psychological interiority: Delphine's consciousness of aging, time, mediation
- Mundane elevated to profound: morning routines, light quality, the texture of waking
- Time dilation in significant moments: the slow assembly of consciousness on waking

**Carson Elements (Present)**
- Compression in key moments: "Forty-one years old. Three years since everything changed."
- White space between paragraphs creating rhythm
- Fragmentary observations that require reader inference

**Voice Consistency**
- Delphine's perspective maintains documentarian's eye throughout
- Self-aware without being self-pitying
- Attention to visual composition (lighting, framing) consistent with her profession

## Deviations from Plan

- **Scene 1:** Plan calls for "domestic AI (a presence rather than a character)" - executed as ambient system rather than named AI, which feels more subtle and appropriate
- **Scene 2:** Miranda from Thalberg Foundation mentioned but actual call content abbreviated; foundation dynamics established through Delphine's internal commentary rather than full dialogue
- **Scene 3:** Mother's video call executes as planned; Ananya's message arrives as planned; Jessie's series described but not explicitly named as "about the Eighth Oblivion" in conversation with mother - revealed through Delphine's internal framing

## Editorial Decisions

- The opening sentence ("She woke to the house already awake") is deliberately simple before the complexity unfolds - effective hook
- Technology integration achieved through naturalistic detail rather than exposition (climate-adaptive glass, autonomous vehicles)
- Theo's presence established but his "difficult questions" are implied through Delphine's anxiety rather than explicit dialogue in sampled pages
- The metaphor of struggling roses acknowledged self-consciously ("The metaphor was too obvious") - postmodern self-awareness that fits the style
