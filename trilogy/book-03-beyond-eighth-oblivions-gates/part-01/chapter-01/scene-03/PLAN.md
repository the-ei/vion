# Scene Plan: Distances

## Summary

The scene weaves together three conversations that map the distances in Delphine's life. First, a video call with her mother Nkechi in London reveals the generational and geographical gap in understanding the Eighth Oblivion - from across the Atlantic, it registered as "American drama" rather than existential rupture. Discussion of her father's death (five years ago now) surfaces grief that hasn't finished its work. Then Jessie calls briefly from set; the conversation is loving but logistical, the intimacy of long partnership compressed into efficiency. Finally, a message arrives from Ananya Ramaswamy: "I need to talk to you about what Prometheus is planning. Not over any network. Can we meet?" The chapter closes with Delphine staring at the message, feeling the new arrangement of her life begin to shift.

## Key Elements

- Video call with Nkechi: London visible through her window, a different world's rhythm
- The Eighth Oblivion as viewed from Europe - historicized differently, less existentially threatening
- Five years since her father's death; the grief that resurfaces through her mother's aging
- Nkechi's questions about Theo, about Jessie, about whether Delphine is happy
- The brief Jessie call: love expressed through logistics, the vocabulary of established partnership
- Jessie's work on the Eighth Oblivion streaming series - the event becoming entertainment
- Ananya's message arriving: the specific wording, the urgency, the implication
- Final image: Delphine on the porch as afternoon light shifts, holding her phone, sensing change
