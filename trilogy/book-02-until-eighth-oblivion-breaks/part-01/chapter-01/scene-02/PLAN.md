# Scene Plan: Constitutional Shadows

## Summary

Ruth teaches her seminar at Berkeley Law on Fourth Amendment jurisprudence in the age of ubiquitous surveillance. The material is familiar - she's taught variations of this course for years - but now it feels like explaining chess rules while everyone plays a different game. Her students are bright, engaged, but oscillate between cynicism and naivete in ways that unsettle her. When a student asks directly about her testimony at the Senate Judiciary Committee hearings on the Prometheus revelations, Ruth's answer is honest but unsatisfying - she describes the process without defending its outcome. The scene captures Ruth at her professional best while revealing her private knowledge that the frameworks she teaches may already be obsolete. Brief interaction with Daniel Okonkwo, her teaching assistant, who represents the next generation's pragmatic adaptation to systems Ruth still believes should be reformed.

## Key Elements

- Berkeley Law seminar room: the architecture of legal education
- Ruth's teaching style: Socratic, rigorous, genuinely engaged with students
- Fourth Amendment jurisprudence as increasingly fictional in practice
- The direct question about Senate hearings: Ruth's moment of vulnerability
- Student generational divide: some cynical, some naive, none as certain as Ruth once was
- Daniel Okonkwo as bridge figure: Nigerian-American graduate student, pragmatic rather than idealistic
- The gap between Ruth's public expertise and private doubt
- The classroom as performance space Ruth can still inhabit
- First hint of "counter-narratives" - a student mentions conspiracy theories Ruth dismisses but notes

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** Central consciousness; teacher whose material feels increasingly abstract
- **Daniel Okonkwo:** Teaching assistant, brief but significant interaction
- **Law students (unnamed):** Several speak, representing generational perspectives
- **The questioning student:** One who asks about the Prometheus hearings directly
