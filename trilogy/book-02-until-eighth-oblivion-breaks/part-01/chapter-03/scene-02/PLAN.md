# Scene Plan: Parallel Lines

## Summary

Networking lunch at the summit. Kevin navigates the social dynamics - more sought after than before, which makes him isolated in a different way. The scene's center is an extended encounter with Ananya Ramaswamy, who serves on the same industry safety board where they've clashed. Their conversation is layered: professional courtesy, genuine intellectual engagement, unresolved tensions from Book 1. They discuss Prometheus's "pivot to safety" - Ananya defends it as real progress, Kevin questions whether progress within a fundamentally flawed system is progress at all. Neither wins; both are changed slightly by the exchange. The scene shows their mutual respect and mutual suspicion. Ananya mentions her daughter Priya is now 16 and increasingly independent; Kevin has nothing comparable to share - his life is the work. The conversation ends without resolution, both aware they'll continue to orbit each other.

## Key Elements

- Networking lunch: the social choreography of professional events
- Kevin's increased status: being sought after as new form of isolation
- The encounter with Ananya: rivals, colleagues, reluctant respecters
- Their Book 1 history: the tension they carry forward
- Ananya's defense of institutional reform
- Kevin's critique of reform within broken systems
- The intellectual engagement: both genuinely smart, neither fully right
- Priya mentioned: Ananya's life outside work
- Kevin's lack of comparable personal life: the loneliness beneath success
- Mutual respect and mutual suspicion: the complex relationship established
