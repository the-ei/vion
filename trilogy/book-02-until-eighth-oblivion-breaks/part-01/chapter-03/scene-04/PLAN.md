# Scene Plan: The Dinner

## Summary

Conference dinner at a venue in SOMA. The old guard and the new guard of AI development gathered together: VCs who funded the problematic systems, engineers who built them, regulators who failed to regulate, journalists who covered the crisis. Kevin circulates, performs the social role expected of him, drinks more than he should. A speech by Victor Okonkwo, the conference organizer, manages to say nothing while sounding significant - Kevin notes how expertly it's done. He has a conversation with a regulator who seems genuinely thoughtful, which somehow makes everything worse - even the people who understand are powerless. The scene captures the social exhaustion of success: being in the room, playing the game, wondering if the game is all there is.

## Key Elements

- SOMA venue: the geography of San Francisco tech wealth
- Old guard and new guard together: the ecosystem in one room
- Kevin's circulation: the performance of networking
- Drinking more than he should: self-medication visible
- Victor Okonkwo's speech: content-free eloquence
- The regulator conversation: genuine understanding, no power
- The exhaustion of success: being wanted, being empty
- The game and the players: Kevin's anthropological observation
- No answers found: the dinner as performance without purpose
- Kevin's isolation amid company
