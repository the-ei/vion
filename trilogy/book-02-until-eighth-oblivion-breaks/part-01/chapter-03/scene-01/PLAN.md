# Scene Plan: Safety Theater

## Summary

Kevin Zhou arrives at the Moscone Center for the AI Safety Summit, nine months after the crisis. The scene establishes the environment with acidic precision: corporate sponsorship banners, media coverage setup, the visual language of "responsibility" rendered in earth tones, organic coffee, and diversity statements. Kevin registers, receives his badge ("Speaker - Industry Expert"), and enters the first panel. His interiority dominates: the dissonance between the urgency he felt nine months ago and the procedural calm of the discussion now. He watches former skeptics deliver the approved messaging, watches people who should know better nod along. A brief moment of dark humor with an acquaintance from his Prometheus days. The scene establishes Kevin as inside the room but not of it - his expertise made him valuable to a system he's not sure he believes in anymore.

## Key Elements

- Moscone Center: the architecture of tech industry gatherings
- Visual language of "responsibility": earth tones, organic coffee, carefully diverse panels
- Kevin's badge: "Speaker - Industry Expert" - the credential he didn't seek
- The first panel: former adversaries speaking the same vocabulary
- The dissonance between urgency and procedure
- Kevin's intelligence as isolation: he sees the theater, can't unsee it
- The acquaintance from Prometheus: shared history, careful present
- Corporate sponsorship as subtext: who pays for "safety"
- Kevin's unexpected success: his warnings made him valuable
- The question of whether presence equals endorsement

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Central consciousness; the insider who can't believe he's inside
- **Tech industry figures (background):** Panelists, attendees, the ecosystem
- **Former Prometheus colleague:** Brief exchange, shared understanding
