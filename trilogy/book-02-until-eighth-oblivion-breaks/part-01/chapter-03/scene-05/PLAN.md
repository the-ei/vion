# Scene Plan: What We Build

## Summary

Carson mode - compressed, late night. Kevin leaves the dinner, walks through San Francisco. He ends up in a bar where he finds himself next to Maya Lindberg, a young engineer whose startup just got funded. She's brilliant, hungry, confident in ways he recognizes from his younger self. They talk about what they're building. Kevin finds himself offering both warnings and encouragement - he cannot decide which is genuine. She asks what he would do differently. He doesn't have an answer that satisfies him. He walks home alone, the city's fog rolling in, thinking about what the summit accomplished (nothing) and what his company is building (something that matters, or something that sells the appearance of mattering). The chapter ends with Kevin in his apartment, looking at the city lights, unable to sleep.

## Key Elements

- Walking through San Francisco: escape from the dinner's performance
- The bar encounter: accidental, authentic
- Maya Lindberg: young engineer, Kevin's reflection
- Her startup: just funded, full of possibility
- Their conversation: warnings and encouragement intertwined
- The question she asks: what would you do differently?
- Kevin's non-answer: the uncertainty he lives with
- Walking home alone: the fog, the city, the solitude
- What the summit accomplished: nothing
- What Kevin's company builds: uncertain significance
- The apartment, the city lights, the sleeplessness
