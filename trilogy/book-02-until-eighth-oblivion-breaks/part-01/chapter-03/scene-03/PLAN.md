# Scene Plan: The Afternoon Sessions

## Summary

Kevin attends afternoon panels on governance, technical alignment, and industry self-regulation. Knausgaard mode: the physical experience of conferences - bad chairs, recycled air, the particular exhaustion of sustained attention when you doubt the premises. His mind wanders: he thinks about his parents in China, about the WeChat message from his mother he hasn't answered, about the distance growing between them as political tensions make communication complicated. He thinks about the woman he dated briefly last year who wanted to know who he was outside of work - he didn't have an answer. He checks his startup's metrics on his phone: growth continues, investment interest is high, everything the system rewards is happening. He's not sure this is good news. The scene shows Kevin's success as hollow, his loneliness as chosen and also not chosen.

## Key Elements

- Afternoon panels: the content of "safety" discourse
- The physical toll of conferences: bad chairs, recycled air, attention fatigue
- Mind wandering: Kevin's private thoughts surfacing
- His parents in China: the unanswered WeChat message

- The woman from last year: the relationship that didn't work
- The question she asked: who is he outside of work?
- His startup's metrics: growth, investment, conventional success
- The hollow feeling: success that doesn't satisfy
- Kevin's isolation: both defense and prison
