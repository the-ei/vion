# Scene Plan: Before the Room

## Summary

Early morning in Ananya's office at Prometheus Systems headquarters. She reviews the presentation for the board meeting, rehearsing the language she's helped develop - the vocabulary of "responsible AI" that has become her professional dialect. Knausgaard mode: the physical details of corporate preparation (the coffee that's better than it used to be, the clothes chosen to project authority without intimidation, the view from her corner office window). Her interiority holds the arguments she makes to herself about why this matters, and the counter-arguments she cannot silence. A memory surfaces: a conversation with her father years ago in Chennai about compromise and working within systems - he was a civil servant, navigated bureaucracy his whole life, taught her that change happens from within. She is no longer sure she believes him. She takes a call from her assistant about the day's schedule. She looks at a photo of Priya on her desk, taken before the divorce, before everything became complicated.

## Key Elements

- The corner office: the architecture of corporate success
- Presentation rehearsal: the language of "responsible AI" as performance
- Physical preparation: clothes, coffee, the body as professional instrument
- Ananya's internal debate: the arguments and counter-arguments
- Memory of her father: the civil servant's philosophy of compromise
- Chennai in memory: the other life she might have had
- The assistant's call: the day's schedule as obstacle course
- Priya's photo: the daughter she's failing to know
- The divorce as shadow: before and after
- The gap between what Ananya believes and what she does

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Central consciousness; preparing for performance
- **Ananya's father (memory):** The civil servant's philosophy
- **Priya (photo/thought):** Daughter, 16, growing distant
- **Ananya's assistant (phone):** Brief, logistical
