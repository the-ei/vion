# Scene Plan: The Daughter Question

## Summary

Midday. Ananya takes a call from Vikram, her ex-husband, in an empty conference room. They discuss Priya's college plans - she's interested in environmental science, wants to attend a school in Colorado, far from both parents. The conversation is civil, efficient, and laden with subtext: their different visions for Priya, their different interpretations of the divorce, the ways their daughter has become a proxy for their unfinished arguments. Vikram mentions that Priya has been "asking questions about Prometheus" - about the company Ananya works for, about the crisis, about what her mother did. Ananya feels the accusation beneath the information. After the call, she has a brief moment alone: thinking about what she will tell Priya someday about these years, about the choices she made and why, about whether "working from within" is explanation or excuse.

## Key Elements

- The empty conference room: sterile privacy for personal conversation
- Vikram's voice: civil, efficient, carrying old wounds
- Priya's college plans: environmental science, Colorado, distance
- The co-parenting dynamics: different visions, different interpretations
- Priya as proxy: their unfinished arguments channeled through their daughter
- "Asking questions about Prometheus": Priya's judgment emerging
- The accusation beneath information: what Vikram doesn't say
- Ananya alone afterward: the weight of what she'll need to explain
- The daughter growing away: 16, independent, increasingly her father's child
- Future reckoning: what will Ananya tell Priya someday?
