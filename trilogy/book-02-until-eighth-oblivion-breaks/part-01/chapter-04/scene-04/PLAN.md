# Scene Plan: The Information

## Summary

Late afternoon. Ananya meets Sanjay Krishnamurthy, a junior researcher at Prometheus, at a coffee shop away from campus. He's nervous, young, reminds her of Kevin Zhou when she first encountered him - technically brilliant, ethically unformed, now suddenly confronted with a choice. He shows her documentation: Prometheus's safety monitoring tools, designed to detect problematic AI outputs, have been adapted and quietly licensed to three governments for "content moderation" - which in practice means surveillance. The contracts are hidden in subsidiary structures. Ananya's professional training kicks in: she asks the right questions, assesses the evidence, protects neither company nor whistleblower, just gathers information. Inside, she is reeling. The framework she just presented to the board is being weaponized. Her work is covering for the thing she opposed. The scene ends with Ananya promising nothing - she needs to think.

## Key Elements

- The off-campus coffee shop: neutral ground, borrowed privacy
- Sanjay Krishnamurthy: junior researcher, nervous, morally awakened
- The documentation: safety tools repurposed as surveillance
- Three governments: the scope of the problem
- Hidden subsidiary structures: how it was concealed
- Ananya's professional response: questions, assessment, gathering information
- Her internal reeling: the framework weaponized
- The parallel to Kevin Zhou: brilliance meeting ethics
- The impossible choice: protect the company, protect the whistleblower, protect herself
- No promises made: the scene ends in uncertainty
