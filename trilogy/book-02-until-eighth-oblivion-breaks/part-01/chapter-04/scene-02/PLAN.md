# Scene Plan: The Ethical AI Framework

## Summary

The board meeting where Ananya presents Prometheus's new "Ethical AI Framework." The scene establishes the room: expensive, deliberately warm (the architecture of trustworthiness), diverse in appearance if not in power. Ananya's presentation is genuinely sophisticated, built on real technical work - she's not a fraud, her framework has value. But the board's responses reveal the dynamics: who asks substantive questions, who performs engagement, who has already decided. Nathan Webb, the CEO, backs her publicly - but his framing subtly shifts her work from "moral imperative" to "competitive advantage." Dr. James Liu raises a technical objection she handles well. The scene shows Ananya at the height of her competence, commanding the room, winning approval for work that matters. And it shows how that competence serves purposes beyond her control - the framework will be licensed to competitors, will generate revenue, will provide cover.

## Key Elements

- The boardroom: expensive, diverse in appearance, homogeneous in power
- Ananya's presentation: technically sophisticated, genuinely valuable
- Board dynamics: who has power, who performs, who decides
- Nathan Webb's support: real backing, subtle reframing
- "Moral imperative" to "competitive advantage": the shift that changes everything
- Dr. James Liu's objection: technical, handled well
- Ananya's competence: real and on display
- The framework as product: ethics that generates revenue
- The applause: Ananya has won something she's not sure she wanted
- The gap between achievement and meaning
