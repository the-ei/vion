# Scene Plan: Responsible Innovation Award

## Summary

Carson mode - short, fragmented. The evening reception where Ananya receives an industry award for "leadership in responsible AI development." The room is full of people she has worked with, competed with, distrusted, admired. The award speech praises Prometheus's transformation. Ananya gives brief, gracious remarks, performs the role expected of her. She shakes hands, accepts congratulations, moves through the room as the person she was this morning before Sanjay's revelation. Then she is in the parking garage, sitting in her car, the award on the passenger seat, Sanjay's documentation open on her phone. The chapter ends there: she cannot go home, cannot go back to the reception, cannot unsee what she has seen. She sits in the silence, the weight of the choice she has not yet made pressing down.

## Key Elements

- The reception: industry celebration, networking, performance
- The award: "Leadership in Responsible AI Development"
- The speech praising Prometheus's transformation: irony now visible
- Ananya's gracious remarks: professional persona functioning on autopilot
- The hands shaken, congratulations accepted: the machinery of success
- The parking garage: alone, finally
- The award on the passenger seat: trophy for complicity
- Sanjay's documentation on her phone: evidence of what the award covers
- The paralysis: cannot go home, cannot go back, cannot unsee
- The weight of the choice: what will she do?
