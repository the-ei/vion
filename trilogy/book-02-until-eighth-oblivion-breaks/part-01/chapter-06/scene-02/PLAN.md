# Scene Plan: The Passenger

## Summary

Midday, Yusuf switches to rideshare. The scene starts with typical fares: airport runs, suburban shuttles, the performance of friendliness for rating purposes. Then an unexpected passenger: an older Somali woman who reminds him of his aunties, who asks him real questions. Their conversation is brief but genuine - she asks about his family, he asks about hers, they speak some Somali. She tips well, but more importantly, she sees him. The scene shows what human connection feels like against the backdrop of algorithmic mediation. After she leaves, Yusuf sits in his car for a moment, unexpectedly moved. Then the app pings, and he is back to work.

## Key Elements

- Switching to rideshare: the multi-app juggle of gig work
- Typical fares: airport, suburbs, the performance of friendliness
- Rating-driven behavior: the smile that is not a smile
- The unexpected passenger: older Somali woman
- Her questions: real, personal, about his family
- Their conversation in Somali: the language of home
- She sees him: the human connection amid algorithmic labor
- The good tip: money, but also acknowledgment
- Yusuf's unexpected emotion: moved by being seen
- The app pings: the algorithm calling him back

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Central consciousness; experiencing genuine connection
- **The Somali passenger:** Older woman, unnamed, catalyst for reflection
- **Other passengers (brief):** The typical fares before the meaningful one
- **The Algorithm (presence):** The ping that ends the moment

- **Atmosphere:** Transactional, then briefly warm, then transactional again

- **Interiority emphasis:** Yusuf's surprise at being moved, his recognition of the gap
- **Key techniques:** The one meaningful encounter framed by many meaningless ones
