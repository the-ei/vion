# Scene Plan: The Morning Run

## Summary

Yusuf Hassan wakes early in Minneapolis, checks his apps, sees what the day offers. The scene establishes his routine: the car that serves as both transportation and workspace, the coffee he cannot really afford, the calculation of which deliveries pay enough to be worth the gas. Knausgaard mode applied to gig work: the texture of loading groceries, finding apartments, dealing with customers who do not acknowledge him. His interiority is observational, wry, aware of the absurdity without being defeated by it. He recognizes other delivery workers, exchanges nods with some, competitive wariness with others. The algorithm assigns, tracks, rates; Yusuf complies, resists in small ways, performs the role. A delivery goes wrong (wrong address, no tip); his rating drops. The scene shows the powerlessness beneath the appearance of flexibility.

## Key Elements

- Early morning wake-up: the phone as alarm, the apps as first check
- The car as office: workspace, transportation, the center of gig life
- Coffee he cannot afford: the small luxuries that become necessities
- Calculation of which deliveries are worth it: the math of survival
- Loading groceries, finding apartments: the physical labor of delivery
- Customers who do not see him: the invisibility of service work
- Other delivery workers: nods, recognition, competition
- The algorithm: invisible boss, constant presence
- The wrong delivery: wrong address, no tip, rating drops
- Powerlessness beneath flexibility: the gig economy's false promise

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Central consciousness; navigating algorithmic management
- **Other gig workers (glimpsed):** The community of invisible labor
- **Customers (brief):** People who receive deliveries, often without acknowledgment
- **The Algorithm (presence):** The invisible boss
