# Scene Plan: The Sound

## Summary

Carson mode - late night. The apartment quiet, Fatima and Amina asleep. Yusuf in his room, headphones on, making music. This is the private self the algorithm does not see: creative, generative, real. He is working on a track with his friend Tariq (remote, sending files back and forth), something that sounds like Minneapolis and Somalia and the spaces between. The music is good - genuinely good - but the gap between this and any sustainable future feels insurmountable. He does not let himself think about that. He just creates. The chapter ends with Yusuf listening to what he has made, in the dark, the city outside his window, the algorithm silent for now.
