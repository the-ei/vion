# Scene Plan: Dawn

## Summary

Carson mode - compressed, luminous. The shift ends. Elena walks out to the parking lot as the sun rises over Phoenix. The heat is already building. She thinks about the year: what she has seen, what she has learned, what she cannot fix. She thinks about her patients - Sarah Kim now sleeping in a hospital bed, Fatima Hassan whose conditions she can treat but not cure. She thinks about the anniversary coverage, the experts who have moved on, the systems that remain unchanged. Something crystallizes: not a plan, not a solution, but a clarity. She cannot heal the system. She can only keep doing what she does, and keep seeing what she sees, and maybe - someday - that seeing will become action. Part 1 ends with Elena getting in her car, driving toward her children, the new day beginning.
