# Scene Plan: Sarah Kim

## Summary

Around 10 PM, Sarah Kim arrives. She is 32, a software engineer, presenting with an overdose on anxiety medication. The scene is clinical: Elena and the team stabilize her, assess the damage, make rapid decisions. Sarah survives - it was a cry for help more than a determined attempt, though that distinction is often overdrawn. The scene is Knausgaard mode at its most technical and most human: the procedures of crisis care rendered in precise detail, while Elena's interiority registers Sarah's face, her fear, the wedding ring she still wears. After Sarah is stabilized, Elena takes a moment alone. She has saved a life tonight - or delayed a death, or interrupted a story she does not know the ending of. The work means something. It also means she will go home exhausted to children she barely sees.
