# Scene Plan: What Bodies Remember

## Summary

Early shift at the clinic. Unusually busy - not dramatically so, but enough to notice. Elena begins to see a pattern: stress responses, anxiety presentations, somatic complaints that spike around trauma anniversaries even when patients do not consciously mark them. Bodies remember what minds might not acknowledge. A patient mentions the anniversary directly; most do not. Elena moves through examinations, prescriptions, referrals. Her competence is absolute; her exhaustion is chronic. A moment with Dr. Osei: they acknowledge the anniversary to each other, briefly, the camaraderie of people who were working through it a year ago. The scene shows the anniversary's texture - not as media event but as embodied memory, carried in the stress hormones and blood pressure readings of her patients.

- Unusually busy clinic: the anniversary's invisible effect
- The pattern Elena sees: stress responses, somatic complaints
- Bodies remembering: trauma anniversaries in physiology
- Patients not naming it: the anniversary present but unspoken

- Her chronic exhaustion: the cost of functioning
- Dr. Osei's moment: the camaraderie of shared experience
- Anniversary as embodied memory: the medical perspective
- The texture of the shift: examinations, prescriptions, referrals
