# Scene Plan: What Holds Us Together

## Summary

Around midnight. Daniel texts - he is still awake, cannot sleep. Elena takes her break, calls him. The conversation starts logistical and becomes something else. They talk about the marriage: the distance, the logistics, the ways they have become roommates managing a household. It is not an argument - they are too tired for arguments. It is an accounting. Elena admits she has been using the medication more than prescribed. Daniel admits he has been wondering if they should try therapy. They do not solve anything, but they name things that have been unnamed. The conversation ends with something that might be a commitment: to try, to talk again, to not let another year pass like this one. Elena returns to work slightly changed.

- Daniel's text: awake, unable to sleep, reaching out
