# Scene Plan: The Year

## Summary

Late afternoon, the day before Elena's night shift. Elena prepares for work, listening to anniversary coverage on the radio - one year since Book 1's crisis. Sofia and Mateo are with Gloria; Daniel is home from his job site for once. The media packages the anniversary neatly: experts reflect, timelines are displayed, lessons allegedly learned. Elena's interiority holds the gap between how the year has been narrated and how she has lived it - patient by patient, body by body. She thinks about what she remembers from the crisis itself, from her perspective in Book 1. The coverage feels both accurate and false simultaneously. Daniel watches with her, asks what she thinks. She does not have words yet. She drives to the clinic as the sun sets, the anniversary beginning.
