# Scene Plan: The Body Keeps Score

## Summary

Ruth experiences cardiac symptoms - tightness, shortness of breath, a frightening moment of dizziness. She sees Dr. Torres, her physician. The tests are inconclusive - stress, probably, but her heart shows some concerning patterns. Nothing immediately dangerous, but warnings. The scene is Knausgaard at its most physical: the medical office, the tests, the conversation with a doctor who knows her history, who knew Susan. Ruth thinks about mortality - not abstractly, as before, but concretely. She has work left to do, and her body might not cooperate. The doctor recommends reducing stress; Ruth almost laughs. After the appointment, she sits in her car in the medical center parking lot, thinking about Susan's final months, wondering how much time she has.

- Cardiac symptoms: tightness, shortness of breath, dizziness
- Dr. Torres: Ruth's physician, someone who knew Susan

- The parking lot: alone with her body's message
