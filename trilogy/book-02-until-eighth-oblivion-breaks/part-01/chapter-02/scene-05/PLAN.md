# Scene Plan: The Drive Home

## Summary

Carson mode - shorter, more compressed. Elena drives through dark Phoenix after her shift, listening to a podcast discussing the Prometheus hearings and the crisis anniversary approaching. The voices on the podcast speak of systemic risk, regulatory frameworks, AI governance - abstractions that seem to arrive from another planet. Elena's mind moves between the podcast's analysis and her patients: the bodies that held the day's accumulated suffering. A moment of clarity - or despair - crystallizes: she's treating symptoms of a disease she can't diagnose, let alone cure. The systems producing her patients' illnesses are the same systems the podcast discusses, but the connection feels impossible to make visible. She arrives home to find the house dark, Gloria asleep on the couch, the children long since put to bed. She stands in Sofia's doorway watching her daughter sleep. The chapter ends there - no resolution, just the exhaustion, the love, and the impossibility of the situation.

## Key Elements

- Dark Phoenix at night: the drive home as decompression
- The podcast: voices from the world of policy and abstraction
- Prometheus hearings mentioned: connecting to other chapters
- The gap between systemic analysis and embodied care
- Elena's clarity/despair: she treats symptoms, the disease continues
- The empty house: Gloria asleep, children in bed, the life Elena misses
- Sofia sleeping: the tenderness that makes everything worth it
- No resolution: the chapter ends in exhaustion and love
- The impossibility named: Elena cannot heal the system
