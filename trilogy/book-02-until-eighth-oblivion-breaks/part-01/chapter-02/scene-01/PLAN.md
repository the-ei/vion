# Scene Plan: Before the Sun

## Summary

Elena Varga wakes at 4:30 AM in her Phoenix home, moving through the dark house before anyone else stirs. The scene establishes her routine with Knausgaard specificity: the texture of exhaustion, the particular movements of a household with young children. She checks on Sofia (7) and Mateo (4) sleeping, makes breakfast she won't have time to eat. Her grandmother Gloria is already up - they have their brief morning exchange in a mix of English and Spanish, the domestic choreography of care passing between generations. Elena's interiority holds her love for her children shadowed by guilt about her constant absences, her anxiety about leaving them (medicated but ever-present). She drives through pre-dawn Phoenix, the city still cool before the brutal heat arrives, the clinic parking lot already filling with early arrivals who have nowhere else to go.

## Key Elements

- 4:30 AM waking: the body trained to insufficient sleep
- The dark house: moving quietly around sleeping family
- Checking on the children: the physical tenderness of watching them sleep
- Gloria's presence: grandmother as anchor, morning handoff of care
- Spanish and English mixing: the household's linguistic texture
- Elena's medications: taken quietly, efficiently, without examination
- The breakfast she won't eat: care for family with no time for self-care
- Pre-dawn Phoenix: the city between night and day, the geography of healthcare access
- The clinic parking lot: people already waiting, the visible need

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Central consciousness; the healer as exhausted caregiver
- **Gloria (Abuela):** Elena's grandmother, awake and ready for childcare
- **Sofia Varga (7):** Elena's daughter, sleeping
- **Mateo Varga (4):** Elena's son, sleeping
- **Daniel Varga (mentioned):** Elena's husband, away on a construction job

## Setting

- **Location:** The Varga home in Phoenix, Arizona, then the drive to the clinic
- **Physical details:** Small house, children's rooms, kitchen where two generations meet; Phoenix streets in pre-dawn darkness
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet, tender, underlaid with exhaustion and anxiety
