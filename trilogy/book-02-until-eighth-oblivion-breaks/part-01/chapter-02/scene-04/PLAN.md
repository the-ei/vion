# Scene Plan: What Comes Through the Door

## Summary

The second half of Elena's shift. The pace intensifies - an urgent case arrives, a difficult diagnosis challenges her, the teenager from the morning returns with worsening symptoms. Near the end of the shift, Fatima Hassan appears - Yusuf's mother, though Elena doesn't know the connection yet. Fatima presents with chronic conditions: hypertension, joint pain, the accumulated damage of hard labor and inadequate care. She lacks insurance, has been deferring treatment for months. Elena recognizes something in her - a woman worn down by circumstances, holding a family together through sheer will. They have a brief but genuine connection. Elena orders tests she's not entirely sure will be approved, gives Fatima medication samples she's probably not supposed to distribute. The scene shows Elena's small rebellions against the system she works within - the ways care workers bend rules to provide care.

## Key Elements

- Afternoon urgency: the shift intensifying rather than winding down
- The returning teenager: Jaylen Mitchell's anxiety worsening, inadequate options
- A difficult diagnosis: Elena's clinical skills tested
- Fatima Hassan's arrival: the introduction of Yusuf's mother
- Fatima's presentation: hypertension, chronic pain, deferred care
- The recognition between Elena and Fatima: two women carrying too much
- Elena's rule-bending: tests ordered, samples given, care provided outside the system
- The cost of caring: Elena's resources depleted by the effort
- Connection planted for Yusuf's chapter: Elena treating someone whose son we'll meet
