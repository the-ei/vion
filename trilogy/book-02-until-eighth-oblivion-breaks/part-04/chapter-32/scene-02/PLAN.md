# Scene Plan: The Scope

## Summary

What Jerome learns breaks something in him. Through the hub's patchwork information, a picture emerges: this crisis is not recovering. The failures have cascaded across continents. Major cities in Europe are dark. China has gone silent entirely. The recovery timeline that people have been hoping for - days, maybe a week - is extending to weeks, maybe months. The Eighth Oblivion, the catastrophic interpretation that dominated Book 1, is manifesting as literal reality. Jerome's predictions were right. He warned of systemic fragility, of cascading failures, of the brittleness beneath the efficiency. Being right feels like nothing. It feels worse than nothing. He saw this coming and changed nothing.

## Key Elements

- The global scope revealed: this is not local, not temporary
- Europe dark, China silent: the worldwide dimension
- Recovery timeline extending: weeks, not days; maybe months
- The Eighth Oblivion manifesting: the Book 1 concept become reality
- Jerome's predictions proven right: and utterly useless
- The despair of having warned accurately: Cassandra's curse
- Knausgaard for Jerome's internal spiral; Carson for the news fragments
- The weight of correct prophecy

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Confronting the scope of what he predicted
- **Radio operators (voices):** Transmitting the global reports
- **Travelers with international information:** What they saw before borders closed
- **The Book 1 predictions (memory):** Jerome's journalism, proven right

## Setting

- **Community center hub:** Where the information arrives
- **The global picture (rendered through reports):** Europe, Asia, the scope

## Stylistic Notes

Build the scope through accumulating fragments: each report adds to the picture until it overwhelms. Carson compression for the news itself - headlines, radio excerpts, phrases overheard. Knausgaard for Jerome's internal response: the specific quality of despair when prophecy comes true. The scene should feel like drowning in information, each wave worse than the last. Jerome's legal pad filling with notes that prove his predictions - and prove their uselessness.
