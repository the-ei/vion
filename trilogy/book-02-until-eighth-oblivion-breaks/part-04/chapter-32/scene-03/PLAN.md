# Scene Plan: DeShawn's Company

## Summary

The news gets personal. Through the hub's networks, information emerges about the tech company where DeShawn works. The company's systems may have been a vector for the cascade - not the only cause, but part of the infrastructure that failed catastrophically. Kevin Zhou's startup, the one DeShawn joined over his father's objections, is named in multiple reports. The campus is dark. There is no evacuation list. No one knows who was there when the systems failed. Jerome's professional instinct to investigate wars with his paternal terror. He wants to know what the company did. He doesn't want to know where his son was when it happened. The two desires are incompatible.

## Key Elements

- The company named: Kevin Zhou's startup as part of the cascade
- DeShawn inside: the son was working at the vector
- Jerome's conflict: journalist vs. father
- The campus dark: no information about who was there
- Possible complicity: what did DeShawn know?
- Possible danger: where is DeShawn now?
- The father-son rift's new dimension: Jerome warned him, DeShawn didn't listen
- Knausgaard psychological depth for the conflict

## Stylistic Notes

The scene should feel like vertigo: the global crisis becoming personal. Jerome's thoughts spiral between professional analysis (what did the company do?) and paternal fear (where is my son?). Memory sections of the father-son arguments about the tech industry - Jerome's criticism now vindicated but meaningless. The information about the company rendered in fragments; what's known is worse than what's suspected. End the scene with Jerome holding both possibilities: DeShawn complicit, DeShawn a victim. No resolution.
