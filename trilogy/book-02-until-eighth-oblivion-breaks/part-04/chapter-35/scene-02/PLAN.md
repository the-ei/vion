# Scene Plan: Habiba's Eyes (Yusuf)

## Summary

Yusuf returns to Minneapolis to find his mother alive. The journey to the enclave worked - they had dialysis equipment, they agreed to help, Nathaniel's connections opened doors that should have been closed. But the help wasn't charity; it came with knowledge. Yusuf now knows things about who prepared for this crisis and who was left to improvise. The tech executives who built their lifeboat while assuring everyone the ship was unsinkable. The class divide between preparation and abandonment. He walks into his family's apartment to find Habiba weak but conscious, Amina asleep in the chair beside her bed, exhausted from days of impossible caregiving. His mother's eyes find his. She is alive. That is everything. And Yusuf carries knowledge that will shape his Book 3 arc.

## Key Elements

- Yusuf's return: the journey succeeded
- Habiba alive: the goal achieved
- Amina exhausted but triumphant: she held everything together
- The enclave's help: dialysis equipment, medical care
- The knowledge gained: who prepared, who was abandoned
- Tech executives' lifeboat: the class dimension of survival
- His mother's eyes: the reunion's emotional center
- The cost Amina paid: aged beyond her years

## Setting

- **The enclave (through Yusuf's memory):** What he saw outside the city

## Stylistic Notes

Emotional scene after physical journey. The return should be rendered through bodies: Yusuf's exhaustion, Amina's collapsed sleep, Habiba's weakness and wakefulness. His mother's eyes as the focal point - describe what he sees in them. Brief flashbacks to the enclave: what it looked like, who was there, what he understood about who built it. The knowledge is bitter but the return is sweet. Hold both.
