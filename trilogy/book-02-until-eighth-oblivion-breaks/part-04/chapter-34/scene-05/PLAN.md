# Scene Plan: Elena's Fury, Elena's Hope

## Summary

Elena stands apart for a moment, at the edge of the firelight, processing. She still carries the rage from Mrs. Gutierrez's death, from every death. The system that let people die from treatable conditions. The corporations that abandoned supplies. The institutions that failed. That rage hasn't diminished. But now she also carries something else: proof that people can organize, can care for each other, can build. The community around the fire isn't a fantasy; it's real and fragile and possible. Elena's radicalization isn't softened - it's directed. She knows now what she'll fight for. Not the return of what was, but the survival of what has formed. The chapter ends with Elena rejoining the circle, carrying both fury and hope, ready for what comes.

- Standing apart: Elena processing at the edge
- Mrs. Gutierrez still present: the deaths not forgotten
- The rage undimmed: at systems, corporations, institutions

## Stylistic Notes

Quiet, compressed ending. Elena alone with her thoughts, then choosing to return. The prose should hold both registers: the rage rendered clearly, specifically (Mrs. Gutierrez's face, the locked warehouse, the failures), and the hope rendered just as specifically (Mr. Ochoa's fire, Mrs. Williams's vegetables, the network's function). End on action: Elena walking back toward the circle. Not redemption, not resolution - direction. She knows where she's going now.
