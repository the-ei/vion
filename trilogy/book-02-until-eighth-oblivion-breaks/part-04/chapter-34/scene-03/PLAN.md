# Scene Plan: What We Built

## Summary

Elena learns about the network that has formed. It didn't happen by plan; it happened by necessity and proximity. Someone knew how to fix solar panels. Someone knew which houses had wells. Someone had seeds. Someone had medical training - Elena, now integrated into a web of mutual need. Decisions are made by whoever shows up, argued out, arrived at by rough consensus. It's inefficient. It's fragile. It's human in ways the systems weren't. Elena watches the network operate and sees something that could survive. Not because it's perfect, but because it's flexible. Because it's made of people who need each other and know it.

## Key Elements

- The mutual aid network: how it formed organically
- Skill sharing: solar panels, wells, seeds, medicine
- Consensus decision-making: messy but functional
- Elena's integration: her medical skills as contribution
- Inefficient but flexible: what lets it survive
- People who need each other: the honest foundation

## Stylistic Notes

This scene should feel like documentation: how does mutual aid actually work? Specific examples of decisions made, skills exchanged, resources pooled. The arguing should be included - it's not all harmony. But the arguments get resolved because everyone knows the stakes. Elena's perspective is both impressed and analytical; she's seeing something she hasn't seen before, trying to understand how it functions. Carson compression for the principles emerging; Knausgaard for the examples.
