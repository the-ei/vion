# Scene Plan: The Narrative Ecosystem

## Summary

Delphine reviews what's being said about the crisis. Through fragmentary reports - radio, surviving internet nodes, word of mouth transcribed - she assembles a picture of the narrative ecosystem. Government conspiracy theories: the crisis is manufactured to justify martial law. Foreign attack narratives: China, Russia, pick your enemy. AI uprising stories: the machines have turned. Divine punishment interpretations: God's judgment on a sinful world. And scattered throughout, accurate information that no one can distinguish from the noise. Delphine recognizes the patterns from her career in media - she helped build this ecosystem, this attention economy where fear spreads faster than truth. Her past work looks different in this light.

## Key Elements

- Survey of competing narratives: conspiracy, foreign attack, AI, divine punishment
- The information warfare landscape during crisis
- Delphine's professional analysis: she knows how narrative spreads
- Her complicity recognized: she helped build this broken ecosystem
- Accurate information lost in noise: truth indistinguishable from rumor
- The question of what story can counter this
- Carson fragments for the narratives themselves: headlines, radio snippets, overheard phrases
- Knausgaard for Delphine's self-reckoning
