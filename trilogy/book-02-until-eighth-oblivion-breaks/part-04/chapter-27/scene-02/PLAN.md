# Scene Plan: First Glitches

## Summary

Elena's first patient of the day is a routine diabetes management visit - until the electronic health records system freezes mid-entry. She waits, restarts, tries again. The IT department is unreachable. She pulls out the paper backup forms they keep for exactly this situation, forms they've used perhaps twice in the five years since the clinic went fully digital. The first patient is processed by hand. Then the second patient arrives with a story: the pharmacy couldn't fill her prescription because their system is down too. A third patient was dropped mid-ride by a rideshare whose app crashed. The pattern is not yet visible, but the accumulation of small failures creates unease. Elena tells herself it's a coincidence. The backup generator hums on.

## Key Elements

- The EHR freeze: a specific technical failure Elena knows how to work around
- Paper backup procedures: the drills they've done, rarely used
- Accumulating patient stories: each brings news of another failed system
- The shift from annoyance to unease: Elena's professional calm masking growing concern
- Rosario's observations: she's fielding calls that aren't going through
- Dr. Samira Okonkwo's first appearance: she's skeptical, thinks it's a local outage
- The generator: already on, a background hum that will become significant
- The gap between "technical difficulties" and something larger

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Nurse practitioner switching to backup procedures, maintaining professional calm
- **Dr. Samira Okonkwo:** Supervising physician, initially dismissive of the scope
- **Rosario Mendez:** Receptionist noting the communications problems, fielding dead calls
- **Mrs. Gutierrez:** First patient, diabetic management, calm while Elena works around the system
- **Pharmacy patient:** Arrives with news her prescription couldn't be filled
- **Rideshare patient:** Dropped mid-trip when the app died

## Setting

- **Community health center:** Exam rooms, front desk, the paper forms pulled from storage
- **Through patient stories:** Glimpses of pharmacy, rideshare, the wider Phoenix

## Stylistic Notes

Transitional scene: still largely Knausgaard in its attention to procedural detail (how does one actually process a patient without digital records?), but Carson elements begin to appear in the fragmented information arriving via patients. Short declarative sentences for the glitch reports. Longer flowing passages for Elena's internal processing. The rhythm should feel slightly off-kilter, reality beginning to stutter.
