# Scene Plan: Before the Day Changed

## Summary

Elena Varga's morning routine on Tuesday, March 17, 2036 - the last ordinary morning before everything breaks. She wakes at 4:45 AM, before anyone else in the house, in the darkness that has become her private time. Checks on Abuela's blood sugar readings from the overnight monitor. Makes coffee in the quiet kitchen while the rest of the world sleeps. Drives to the clinic through pre-dawn Phoenix, the desert sky turning from black to purple to orange, radio playing the same news she always half-listens to. Arrives at the clinic, greets Rosario who is already there, reviews the day's patient schedule. The world as it was, captured in exhaustive detail that will become precious in retrospect.

## Key Elements

- Elena's private morning ritual: the quiet before the family wakes
- Abuela's health: checking the overnight glucose monitor, the routine of managing diabetes
- The drive: Phoenix geography, the specific route, the way light changes over the desert
- Small domestic details elevated to significance by what's coming
- The clinic's rhythms: familiar procedures, known faces, ordinary complaints
- Knausgaard-style immersion in mundane routine
- No foreshadowing - the ordinariness is the point
- Elena's competence and care established through small professional details

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Nurse practitioner in her element, moving through a morning she has lived hundreds of times
- **Abuela Varga (sleeping):** Elena checks on her; the overnight monitor shows stable glucose
- **Sofia and Mateo Varga (sleeping):** Elena looks in on them briefly; their sleeping faces
- **Rosario Mendez:** Longtime clinic receptionist, first person Elena speaks to, their easy morning rapport

## Setting

- **Varga home in Phoenix:** Small, multigenerational, lived-in
- **Elena's car:** The drive, the radio, the specific streets
- **Community health center:** The familiar institutional space before the crisis transforms it

## Timeline

- **Start:** 4:45 AM, Tuesday, March 17, 2036
- **End:** 6:30 AM
- **Duration:** Approximately 1 hour 45 minutes

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Pure Knausgaard mode: exhaustive attention to the ordinary. Long sentences that follow Elena's movements and thoughts without urgency. The rhythm of routine as meditation. No Carson compression here - this is the baseline of normality against which the coming crisis will register. Let the reader settle into Elena's world before it changes.
