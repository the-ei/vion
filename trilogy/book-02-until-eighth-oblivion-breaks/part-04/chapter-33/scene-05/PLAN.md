# Scene Plan: The Door

## Summary

Yusuf leaves. He doesn't look back because looking back would stop him. Nathaniel is waiting on the street with a borrowed bicycle and directions memorized. Sixty miles. Cold March. No guarantee the enclave will let them in, no guarantee they have the equipment that could save Habiba, no guarantee Yusuf will be able to return. But staying means watching his mother die. Leaving means the chance she won't. Yusuf walks out the door, closes it behind him, and starts walking toward the bicycle, toward Nathaniel, toward the unknown. The chapter ends in motion, in cold, in uncertainty. He has chosen not to watch his mother die. Whether he's saved her or abandoned her, he won't know until he returns. If he returns.

## Key Elements

- The departure: the door closing behind him
- Not looking back: the discipline of leaving
- Nathaniel waiting: the unlikely partner
- Sixty miles, cold March: the physical journey ahead
- No guarantees: every outcome uncertain
- The choice crystallized: stay and witness death, or leave and seek help
- Motion as faith: moving toward hope you can't see
- Carson compression for the ending: movement, cold, uncertainty

## Stylistic Notes

Shorter, moving prose. The door closing as central image. Yusuf's internal state rendered through what he notices: the temperature, the sound of his footsteps, Nathaniel's face. No looking back - this discipline should be present in the prose itself, which moves forward only. End the chapter on movement: the first steps of a journey whose outcome is unknown. Carson's compression for the uncertainty: short sentences, no false comfort, the reader left in motion with Yusuf.
