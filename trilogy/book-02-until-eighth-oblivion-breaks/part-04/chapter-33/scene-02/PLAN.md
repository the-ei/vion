# Scene Plan: Nathaniel's Map

## Summary

Nathaniel Burke arrives at the apartment with information. He has been working his contacts - people from the company, from the tech world, from networks Yusuf doesn't have access to. There is a facility outside the Twin Cities, about sixty miles northwest. It was built by a consortium of tech executives who saw this coming - not this specifically, but something like it. They prepared. They have generators, medical equipment, supplies. Nathaniel shows Yusuf a hand-drawn map, routes that avoid main roads, contact protocols. The facility might have dialysis equipment. It might be able to help Habiba. But there's a catch: the people who built it built it for themselves. Yusuf and Habiba aren't on any list. They'll be asking for entry to a lifeboat they weren't meant to be on.

## Key Elements

- Nathaniel's information: the prepared enclave exists
- The map: hand-drawn, specific routes, contact protocols
- Tech executives who prepared: the class dimension of survival
- Sixty miles northwest: the distance to possible salvation
- The catch: they weren't invited; they'll be asking for mercy
- Yusuf's distrust: these are the people who caused this
- Nathaniel's complicated position: he's one of them, sort of
- The lifeboat metaphor: who gets on, who decides

## Characters

- **Habiba Hassan (present, listening):** The reason this matters

## Stylistic Notes

Dialogue-heavy scene: the information must be exchanged. Nathaniel's speech should reveal his class position - educated, privileged, uncomfortable with his privilege now that it matters. Yusuf's responses are short, practical, barely containing his anger. The map as physical object: describe it, let Yusuf trace the route with his finger. The class politics rendered through specific details, not abstract commentary: the tech executives who prepared while everyone else was told the systems were safe.
