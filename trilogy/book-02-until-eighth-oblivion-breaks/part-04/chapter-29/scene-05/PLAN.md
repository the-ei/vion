# Scene Plan: The Stranded Engineer

## Summary

The next morning, Yusuf goes out to gather information and find any remaining medication sources. At a community gathering point - a church that has become an information hub - he encounters Nathaniel Burke, a tech worker whose company-provided rental car stopped when the fleet management system died. Nathaniel is out of his depth: he has money in accounts he can't access, a job at a company whose campus is now dark, and no practical survival skills. But he works for a startup Yusuf recognizes from news clips - one connected to Kevin Zhou. Nathaniel may know things about what happened. More importantly, he mentions that his company had contingency protocols, locations that were prepared for exactly this kind of failure. An unlikely alliance forms: Yusuf has survival skills, Nathaniel may have information about where help might be found.

## Key Elements

- The church as information hub: community self-organization
- Nathaniel Burke: stranded tech worker, fish out of water
- Class contrast: Yusuf's survival skills vs. Nathaniel's helplessness
- Kevin Zhou's company: Nathaniel works there, connection to larger plot
- The prepared enclaves: Nathaniel mentions contingency locations
- Unlikely alliance: each has what the other needs
- Distrust and necessity: Yusuf doesn't trust tech workers, but his mother needs help
- Setup for Chapter 33: the journey to find the enclave

## Stylistic Notes

Shorter scene closing the chapter. The church as space of practical grace - not about religion, just about the building being open and people organizing. Nathaniel rendered with complexity: he's not evil, he's just adapted to a world that no longer exists. His revelation about prepared enclaves should feel both hopeful and suspicious - who prepared, and for whom? Dialogue carries more weight here as information exchanges. End on the alliance forming: Yusuf will work with this man he doesn't trust because his mother's life depends on it.
