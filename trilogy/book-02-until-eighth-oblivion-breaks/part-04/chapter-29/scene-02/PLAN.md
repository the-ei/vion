# Scene Plan: His Father's Hands

## Summary

Memory scene: Yusuf is twelve, standing in the hallway of a hospital. His father Malik has been injured at the warehouse where he worked - a forklift accident, the company said, though the safety equipment was known to be faulty. Yusuf remembers his father's hands, always rough from work, now still on the hospital sheet. The company sent a representative who spoke to his mother in the hallway, using words Yusuf didn't understand then but understands now: liability, negligence, settlement. His father died the next day. The company's insurance fought the claim for two years. Yusuf learned, at twelve, that institutions do not catch you. This memory is why he isn't surprised by the crisis - he's been living in its precursor his whole life.

- Formative trauma: the loss that shaped Yusuf's worldview
- Father's hands: specific sensory detail anchoring the memory
- Institutional failure: the company's response to death
