# Scene Plan: The Offer

## Summary

Amina has received a full scholarship to a prestigious summer STEM program—funded by Prometheus Systems' education philanthropy. The irony is vicious: the company automating their family into poverty offers to elevate the exceptional sister. Family dinner becomes debate. Amina wants to take it—opportunity is opportunity, she argues. Yusuf sees the trap: co-optation, reputation laundering, the way philanthropy sanitizes extraction. Halima doesn't fully understand the politics but understands her daughter's chance. The debate reveals the impossible choice that poverty forces: accept help from those who harmed you, or maintain purity while your family suffers. No resolution emerges, just the weight of impossible choices.

## Key Elements

- The scholarship offer: Prometheus-funded, full ride, prestigious program
- The irony: the company destroying their livelihood offering to elevate one
- Family dinner as debate: food, love, and political argument intertwined
- Amina's position: opportunity is opportunity, pragmatism over principle
- Yusuf's position: co-optation, reputation laundering, principled refusal
- Halima's position: her daughter's future, not fully grasping the politics
- No resolution: the scene doesn't solve, just presents the weight
- Impossible choice: the trap poverty creates, no clean options
- Love under pressure: the family's bonds visible even in disagreement

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Brother whose political awakening meets family reality, trapped between principle and love
- **Amina Hassan:** Sister, 19, brilliant, pragmatic, wants the opportunity regardless of source
- **Halima Hassan:** Mother, wants her daughter's future, confused by the political dimensions

## Setting

The Hassan family apartment, dinner table. The same space as Chapter 21's home scene, now charged with this new pressure. The modest kitchen, the food they share, the family gathered in conflict.

## Timeline

- **Date:** June 2035
- **Time:** Evening dinner, 6 PM to 9 PM
- **Duration:** Three hours of conversation that doesn't end
- **Context:** Days after the coalition meeting; Yusuf brings new political consciousness home
