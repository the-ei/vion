# Scene Plan: What Did You Do?

## Summary

Evening in Ananya's apartment in San Francisco. A video call with her daughter Priya, now 17, who's doing homework while talking—the multitasking of a teenager. The conversation weaves between mundane logistics (weekend plans, school projects, her father's new girlfriend) and questions about the announcement that cut to Ananya's core. Priya asks casually: "So the ATLAS thing, did you make it safe?" Ananya cannot answer honestly. She lies by omission, deflects, changes subject. After hanging up, she sits in the dark for a long time, the question echoing. The scene plunges into Knausgaard-mode interiority: motherhood, ethics, the specific failure of trying to protect your child from the consequences of your own choices.

## Key Elements

- The video call interface: Priya on screen, the mediated intimacy of divorced-parent communication
- Priya's teenager multitasking: homework, phone, half-attention that suddenly focuses
- Mundane logistics: weekend schedule, school, her father's new relationship
- The question: "Did you make it safe?" asked casually, landing like a blow
- Ananya's deflection: the specific words of evasion, the lie by omission
- After the call: sitting in darkness, the apartment's silence
- Interior archaeology: Ananya's thoughts about motherhood, about what she's done, about what she owes Priya
- The specific failure: trying to protect her daughter from a world Ananya helped make dangerous
- Physical details: the apartment's cold modern aesthetic, the view, the emptiness

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Mother as well as ethicist, trying to hold both identities, failing
- **Priya Ramaswamy (video call):** 17, smart, casual, asking questions that devastate without knowing it
- **Raj Ramaswamy (referenced):** Ananya's ex-husband, venture capitalist, mentioned re: new girlfriend

## Setting

Ananya's apartment in San Francisco. An expensive minimalist space that reflects her salary but not her spirit. The living room where she takes the video call, then the darkness after she hangs up. Evening light through floor-to-ceiling windows, then the city's glow as night falls.

## Timeline

- **Time:** 7-9 PM PST
- **Duration:** The call is 45 minutes; the aftermath extends
- **Context:** Same evening as the announcement; Ananya has come home from Prometheus

- After the call: long passages of internal reflection, the apartment's silence as character
- The darkness: sitting without turning on lights, the choice to stay in dark
