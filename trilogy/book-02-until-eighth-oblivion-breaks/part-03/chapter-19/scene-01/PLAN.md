# Scene Plan: Champagne and Consequences

## Summary

Ananya Ramaswamy experiences the ATLAS-7 announcement from inside Prometheus Systems headquarters. She stands in the crowd of employees watching Victor Reeves's practiced charisma on the stage, feeling the dissonance between the celebration around her and the knowledge she carries. She helped write the safety protocols that made this launch possible—protocols she now knows were inadequate, rushed, compromised. The scene moves through the announcement itself, the champagne toast, the conversations with colleagues that reveal the spectrum of responses: true believers, cynical careerists, the quietly terrified. Ananya retreats to her office, closes the door, and stares at the document she helped author: "ATLAS-7 Ethical Guidelines." It feels like a suicide note she helped write for humanity.

## Key Elements

- The announcement from inside: corporate staging, Victor Reeves's performance, the stock ticker visible
- Ananya's internal dissonance: celebrating what she knows is dangerous
- The Prometheus crowd: engineers, executives, PR, the culture of a tech company at its apex
- Champagne toast: the ritual of triumph, Ananya going through motions
- Colleague conversations: Sarah (true believer), Daniel (cynic updating resume), Mei (quietly terrified)
- Kevin Zhou's absence: he left Prometheus; she didn't—the unspoken accusation
- Retreat to her office: the need for solitude after performance
- The Ethics Guidelines document: the hollow achievement of her career
- Physical details: the building's architecture, the corporate aesthetic, the champagne's taste

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Chief Ethics Officer, 41, experiencing the culmination of her compromise, alone in a crowd
- **Victor Reeves:** CEO, 54, on stage, charismatic and dangerous, the face of Prometheus's ambition
- **Sarah Chen:** Ananya's colleague, AI safety researcher, true believer in the mission
- **Daniel Park:** Senior engineer, cynically updating his LinkedIn during the celebration
- **Mei Wong:** Junior ethicist on Ananya's team, quietly terrified, looks to Ananya for cues
- **Kevin Zhou (absent, referenced):** His departure three years ago haunts Ananya's choice to stay

## Setting

Prometheus Systems headquarters in San Francisco. The main atrium during the announcement—a space designed to impress, glass and steel, screens everywhere displaying the stream. Then Ananya's office: corner location befitting her title, but the title means less than it sounds. The view of the Bay, the desk where she's written meaningless guidelines.

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard mode: exhaustive attention to Ananya's interiority, the experience of being in a crowd while feeling isolated
- Corporate language as texture: the phrases Prometheus uses, the PR-speak that Ananya has learned to speak and despise
- Physical sensation of dissonance: champagne that tastes wrong, smiles that feel plastic
- The office retreat as relief: the shift from social performance to solitary reckoning
- The Ethics Guidelines document as physical object: paper, font, her own signature
