# Scene Plan: The Files

## Summary

Late night. Ananya uses her credentials to access documents she's technically not authorized to see. The chapter's earlier guilt crystallizes into action—though action that's still private, still deniable. She navigates the internal servers, using access she has but shouldn't use this way. What she finds is worse than she feared: internal projections showing 20 million jobs displaced in the first wave, potentially 100 million by 2040. Capability curves steeper than public statements suggest. A safety testing memo with redactions she can read between. She copies files to a personal drive—a fireable offense, possibly a crime. The chapter ends with her staring at the drive in her hand, not yet knowing what she'll do with it, but knowing she's crossed a line.

## Key Elements

- The decision to look: the moment Ananya moves from passive guilt to active investigation
- Corporate servers at night: the bureaucratic interface of dangerous information
- Internal projections: the economic displacement numbers, the scale of disruption Prometheus expects
- Capability curves: what ATLAS-7 can actually do versus what they've told the public
- Safety testing memo: gaps, rushing, the specific compromises that enabled launch
- The act of copying: the physical and digital reality of moving files to a personal drive
- Legal exposure: Ananya's awareness that she's committing crimes
- The drive in her hand: physical object containing explosive information
- Not knowing yet: the ending is uncertainty, not resolution

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Crossing from complicity to something else, alone with her screens and her choice
- **Victor Reeves (implied):** The person she's betraying, present through the documents he authorized
- **Prometheus Security (imagined):** Ananya's paranoid awareness of surveillance, monitoring, the possibility of being caught

## Setting

Ananya's apartment, her home office space. Late night, the apartment dark except for her screens. The intimacy of dangerous action in a domestic setting. The computer interface as portal to Prometheus's secrets.

## Timeline

- **Date:** March 2035, overnight
- **Time:** 11 PM to 2 AM
- **Duration:** Approximately three hours of searching, reading, deciding
- **Context:** Same night as the announcement; Ananya has moved from grief to action

## Page Target

7 pages (~1,925 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Tension through process: the step-by-step nature of accessing, finding, copying
- Document language as revelation: the corporate prose that contains horror (projections, percentages, timelines)
- Carson mode in flashes: the fragmentary experience of reading terrible truths, the compression of realization
- Physical details: keystrokes, the drive's small weight, screen light on Ananya's face
- Paranoia as texture: the awareness of possible surveillance, the risk in each click
- The ending as suspension: not knowing what she'll do is the state, not a transition to be resolved quickly
