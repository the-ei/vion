# Scene Plan: How Did We Get Here

## Summary

An extensive memory sequence as Ananya prepares for the transfer. The scene travels backward through her career at Prometheus: the day she joined, believing she could shape the company from within; the first compromise, a report softened at executive request; the accumulation of small betrayals that made each subsequent one easier. Kevin Zhou's departure three years ago—his implicit accusation that by staying, she was choosing complicity. The conversation with Priya that broke something loose: "Did you make it safe, Mom?" The memories aren't chronological but associative, circling the question of how someone who meant to do good ended up with stolen files on her laptop, preparing to betray everything she built.
