# Scene Plan: The Documents

## Summary

Ananya Ramaswamy in her apartment, early morning, the day she decides to act. She reviews the files she copied weeks ago from Prometheus's servers. The internal projections are worse than public statements: 20 million jobs displaced in the first wave, potentially 100 million by 2040. Capability curves steeper than acknowledged. Safety testing gaps wider than admitted. She reads through them again, looking for a reason not to act—or confirmation that she must. A text from Jerome: "Can we talk?" She responds: "Not on this channel." The scene establishes the stakes of what she's about to do and her mindset: terrified, certain, alone. The files in her possession feel like live ammunition.

## Key Elements

- Early morning review: Ananya alone with dangerous information
- The documents' content: economic projections, capability curves, safety gaps
- The scale of harm: numbers that represent millions of lives
- Looking for reasons: the internal debate between action and safety
- Jerome's text: the outside world making contact
- "Not on this channel": the first step toward secure communication
- The apartment's morning light: the ordinary setting for extraordinary decision
- The physical files: a drive, a laptop, the tangibility of evidence
- Terrified and certain: the emotional state of someone about to cross a line

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** On the threshold of action, reviewing the evidence that will define her choice
- **Jerome Washington (text):** Present through message, the recipient of what she'll share
- **Victor Reeves (implicit):** Present through the documents he authorized, the person she's betraying

## Setting

Ananya's apartment in San Francisco. Early morning light, coffee, the laptop open to files that could destroy her career and perhaps save lives. The space where private decision becomes public consequence.

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late May 2035
- **Time:** 6 AM to 10 AM
- **Duration:** Four hours of reading, deciding, initiating
- **Context:** Weeks after Chapter 19; the files have been sitting, waiting for this moment

## Page Target

7 pages (~1,925 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- The documents' language: corporate prose containing horror, percentages and projections
- Ananya's reading: close attention, the search for something she might have missed
- Internal debate: the reasons for and against, the calculus of risk
- Morning light: the ordinary made extraordinary by context
- The text exchange: brief, coded, the beginning of conspiracy
- Physical sensation: the body's response to danger, cortisol, alertness
