# Scene Plan: The Transfer

## Summary

Ananya executes the leak. Following protocols Jerome has sent—secure apps, encrypted channels, the craft of source protection—she transfers the Prometheus files. The physical act is both monumental and banal: keystrokes, a progress bar, confirmation. Jerome receives them. They have a brief exchange about what happens next: he'll verify, corroborate, build the story carefully. Ananya will continue at Prometheus as if nothing happened—the hardest part. The chapter ends with her staring at her reflection in the dark window, trying to recognize the person who just changed everything. The scene is Carson-mode fragmented: the self in pieces, the irreversibility of the moment.

## Key Elements

- The protocols: secure communication, the craft of leaking safely
- The physical act: transfer, progress bar, confirmation
- Jerome's receipt: the moment the files leave Ananya's control
- The brief exchange: what happens next, Jerome's plans for the story
- Maintaining cover: Ananya must return to Prometheus, pretend normalcy
- The reflection: the self seen after transformation
- Carson-mode fragmentation: the prose matching Ananya's shattered state
- Irreversibility: once done, cannot be undone
- The threshold crossed: from complicity to something else, unnamed

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Completing the action, crossing the threshold, fragmenting
- **Jerome Washington (encrypted communication):** Receiving, confirming, planning
- **Ananya's reflection:** The self seen from outside, the person she's become
