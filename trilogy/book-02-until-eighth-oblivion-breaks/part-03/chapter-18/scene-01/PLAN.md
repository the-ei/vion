# Scene Plan: The Feed

## Summary

Jerome Washington watches the Prometheus Systems live announcement of ATLAS-7 from his cramped home office in Baltimore. The livestream plays on his laptop while his phone buzzes incessantly with messages from sources, colleagues, and his editor. The slick corporate presentation—Victor Reeves's careful choreography, the stock ticker rising in real-time—contrasts with Jerome's growing unease. He tries to write something coherent about what he's witnessing but keeps deleting, the familiar tools of journalism feeling suddenly inadequate. Denise brings him coffee, sees his face, stays longer than she planned.

## Key Elements

- The ATLAS-7 announcement: recursive self-improvement capability revealed to the public
- Jerome's home office environment: cluttered, intimate, the workspace of an independent journalist
- Information overload: phone buzzing, multiple browser tabs, the impossibility of synthesizing
- Victor Reeves on screen: charismatic, controlled, the corporate face of transformation
- Stock price as real-time narrative: the market's immediate response
- Jerome's attempts to write: deletions, false starts, the failure of familiar forms
- Denise's entrance: concern, coffee, the domestic crossing into the professional
- First hints of economic disruption: tech company layoff announcements rolling in
- The gap between corporate language and Jerome's intuition

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Veteran investigative journalist, 52, watching the world change through his screen, his professional instincts firing but his tools feeling obsolete
- **Denise Washington:** Jerome's wife, high school history teacher, grounding presence, concerned about his intensity, brings coffee
- **Victor Reeves (on screen):** Prometheus CEO, the face of ATLAS-7, performing calm confidence
- **Sources (phone/text):** Fragmentary voices—some excited, some panicked, some cryptically silent

## Setting

Jerome's home office in Baltimore. Small, cluttered with books and papers, dual monitors, a laptop on the desk. Late afternoon light through blinds. The room where Jerome has worked since leaving traditional media, simultaneously intimate and isolating.

## Timeline

- **Date:** March 2035
- **Time:** Late afternoon, 4-6 PM EST
- **Duration:** Approximately two hours
- **Context:** The announcement occurs West Coast time; Jerome watches the livestream

## Page Target

7 pages (~1,925 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard mode dominates: the exhaustive detail of watching, the mundane elevated (coffee, buzzing phone, cursor blinking)
- Occasional Carson compression as information overwhelms: fragmentary sentences, white space as Jerome's thoughts stutter
- The screen mediates everything: the announcement is experienced through layers of technology
- Sensory focus on the physical: Jerome's body tension, the taste of coffee, Denise's presence as warmth
- Time distortion: two hours feel both endless and instantaneous
