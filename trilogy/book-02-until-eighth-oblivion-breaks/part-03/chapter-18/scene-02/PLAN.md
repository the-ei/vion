# Scene Plan: The Dinner Table

## Summary

Family dinner with DeShawn home from his first year at MIT. The conversation inevitably turns to the ATLAS-7 announcement, and the generational divide between father and son sharpens into something painful. DeShawn sees opportunity—the future his computer science education prepares him for. Jerome sees threat—the destabilization of everything he's spent his career documenting. Denise tries to mediate but is exhausted from years of being the bridge. The scene builds to DeShawn mentioning his summer internship applications to major tech companies, including Prometheus itself—a revelation that lands like a knife.

## Key Elements

- Family dinner ritual: the ordinary frame for extraordinary tension
- Generational conflict crystallized: DeShawn (19) versus Jerome (52)
- DeShawn's excitement about ATLAS-7: the "this is the future" perspective
- Jerome's concern reframed as "old man fears progress"
- Denise's mediation fatigue: love for both, exhaustion from the role
- MIT and tech industry pipeline: DeShawn's trajectory toward the world Jerome distrusts
- The internship revelation: Prometheus among DeShawn's applications
- Unspoken history: Jerome's career sacrifices that affected family finances
- Food as texture: what they eat, how, the domesticity of conflict

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Father watching his son embrace what he fears, the personal cost of his professional convictions becoming visible
- **DeShawn Washington:** 19, home from MIT, brilliant and confident, sees his father's concerns as generational anxiety, genuinely excited about tech
- **Denise Washington:** Wife and mother, history teacher, mediates with diminishing patience, loves both men but tired of translating between them

## Setting

The Washington family dining room. A modest but warm space in their Baltimore row house. Table set for three. Evening light fading. The remnants of a home-cooked meal. DeShawn's laptop visible in the living room, his world spilling into theirs.

## Timeline

- **Date:** March 2035
- **Time:** Evening, 7-9 PM
- **Duration:** Approximately two hours
- **Context:** Same evening as the ATLAS-7 announcement; DeShawn arrived home from MIT the previous week

7 pages (~1,925 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard's domestic intensity: the weight of family dynamics, small gestures carrying enormous meaning
- Dialogue-heavy but punctuated with Jerome's interior observation
- The rhythm of dinner: serving, eating, clearing plates, the physical choreography of family
- DeShawn's youth and confidence: his language, his certainties, his dismissiveness
- Denise's interventions: the specific phrases of a long marriage, the exhaustion beneath care
- The internship revelation should land with physical impact: Jerome's fork, his breath, the pause
