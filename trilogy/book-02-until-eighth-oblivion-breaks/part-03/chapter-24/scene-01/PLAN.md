# Scene Plan: The Call

## Summary

Ruth Abramson in her DC apartment, evening. Jerome calls on an encrypted line—she's learning new protocols at 64, the technology of secrecy uncomfortable but necessary. Jerome describes the documents without showing them: Prometheus's internal projections, safety compromises, economic displacement modeling. Ruth's legal mind categorizes: what's actionable, what's protected, what falls into gray zones. Her conclusion is devastating: most of this is legal. The system allows for this level of harm. Jerome asks what can be done legally. Ruth doesn't have a good answer. She starts to offer something else—not legal advice but the beginning of their collaboration, two people who've decided to act despite institutional limits.
