# Scene Plan: The Story Drops

## Summary

Jerome in his home office as the story goes live. His phone explodes—colleagues, sources, his editor, strangers. The story spreads faster than he can track: picked up by cable news, misrepresented, spectacularized, reduced to sound bites. DeShawn texts a single question mark—his son processing that his father's work implicates the industry DeShawn aspires to enter. Jerome watches his careful investigation become content, become noise. Denise brings him dinner he doesn't eat. The scene captures the strange vertigo of a journalist watching their work enter the world, transformed by the attention economy into something other than what they made. Jerome feels both vindicated and defeated. The truth is out. Nothing has changed.

## Key Elements

- The story going live: the moment of publication, the release
- Information cascade: phone, social media, cable news, the explosion of attention
- Misrepresentation: how the story is reduced, distorted, spectacularized
- DeShawn's text: the question mark, father and son divided by the implications
- Denise's care: dinner brought, not eaten, the domestic in crisis
- Vindication and defeat: the contradictory feeling of successful truth-telling
- The attention economy: how journalism becomes content
- Jerome's home office: the intimate space from which he watches global reaction
- "The truth is out. Nothing has changed.": the chapter's dark revelation

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Journalist watching his work transform, feeling contradictory success
- **Denise Washington:** Wife, bringing dinner, the domestic grounding
- **DeShawn Washington (text):** Son, the question mark that speaks volumes
- **Colleagues, sources, strangers (phone/text):** The chorus of response

## Setting

Jerome's home office in Baltimore. The same space as Chapter 18, Scene 1—now the place where he watches his work meet the world. Evening, screens showing coverage, phone constantly buzzing.

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late June 2035
- **Time:** Evening of publication day

- **Context:** The story Jerome has built since Chapter 18 finally publishes
