# Scene Plan: The Source

## Summary

Ananya in her San Francisco apartment, watching the coverage. Jerome has protected her identity perfectly—she's not named, not hinted at, not identifiable in the story. Prometheus's PR response is already live: the documents are "taken out of context," the projections were "internal scenarios, not predictions," the safety protocols are "industry-leading." Victor Reeves appears on CNBC, calm and confident, the corporate machine absorbing the blow. Ananya watches the man she betrayed perform normalcy while her heart pounds with the fear of discovery. Priya texts about something unrelated—homework, weekend plans—and Ananya doesn't respond. She's waiting for the knock on the door that doesn't come, at least not yet.

## Key Elements

- Watching coverage: Ananya as spectator to the story she enabled
- Source protection: Jerome's craft, her identity invisible
- Prometheus PR response: the corporate machine's denial and deflection
- Victor Reeves on screen: the man she betrayed, performing confidence
- Fear of discovery: the terror beneath the surface
- Priya's text: ordinary life continuing, Ananya unable to engage
- The knock that doesn't come: waiting for consequences
- Isolation: alone with what she's done
- The gap between truth revealed and consequences felt

## Characters

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Source watching her secret work enter the world, terrified and suspended
- **Victor Reeves (on screen):** CEO performing denial, the target of her betrayal
- **Priya Ramaswamy (text):** Daughter's ordinary life, contrast to Ananya's crisis
- **PR spokesperson (on screen):** Prometheus's corporate voice, deflecting

## Setting

Ananya's apartment in San Francisco. Night. The living room where she watches coverage on multiple screens. The phone with Priya's unanswered text. The door she watches, waiting.

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late June 2035
- **Time:** Same evening as Scene 1, different time zone
- **Duration:** 3-4 hours of watching, waiting
- **Context:** The story she enabled is now public
