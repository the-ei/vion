# Scene Plan: The Ground

## Summary

Yusuf in Minneapolis, the same 72 hours. He's aware of the news—it's everywhere, unavoidable—but his mother is recovering from the hospital visit, his sister is deciding about the scholarship, and his gig app rating is still dropping. The breaking story that consumes elite attention is background noise for survival. At a coalition meeting, Fatima mentions the Prometheus leak: "Confirmation of what we already knew." The organizing continues regardless—mutual aid, pressure campaigns, the slow work of building power. Yusuf walks home through a neighborhood where the acceleration isn't news; it's lived reality. The chapter ends with him looking up at the Minneapolis skyline, imagining the distance between where the story matters and where it doesn't. Something has broken. But for him, it broke long ago.
