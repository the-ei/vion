# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Afternoon break at the Hassan family apartment. Yusuf comes home exhausted to find his mother Halima resting on the couch—her chronic health issues are worsening, and the medication she needs is increasingly unaffordable. His sister Amina, 19, is studying at the kitchen table, brilliant and driven, just accepted to a summer program that could change her trajectory—but the cost is prohibitive. Yusuf's frustration at the impossible math of their lives comes out as sharpness with Amina, then immediate guilt. He retreats to his room and tries to work on music—his private self, the thing that makes him more than a delivery vector—but the exhaustion is total. A text from the app: surge pricing in an hour, be ready. He leaves without saying goodbye.

## Key Elements

- The apartment: small, loved, containing three people's lives and struggles
- Halima's health: chronic illness, inadequate insurance, medication costs
- Amina's opportunity: summer program acceptance, prohibitive cost, the cruel arithmetic
- Yusuf's frustration: directed at Amina unfairly, then guilt
- The music: Yusuf's private passion, the thing the algorithm can't touch
- The attempt to rest: impossible, the body refusing, the mind refusing
- The text: surge pricing summons, the algorithm's demand
- Leaving without goodbye: the relationship cost of gig work's schedule
- Family love under pressure: the bonds that hold and strain

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Son and brother, trying to provide, failing to rest, carrying more than he can hold
- **Halima Hassan:** Yusuf's mother, early 50s, chronic health issues, dependent on his income, loving and worried
- **Amina Hassan:** Yusuf's sister, 19, brilliant, academic hope of family, accepted to summer program she can't afford

## Setting

The Hassan family apartment in Minneapolis. A two-bedroom space shared by three people—Yusuf in the living room, Halima and Amina in the bedrooms. Modest but maintained with care. The kitchen table where Amina studies, the couch where Halima rests, Yusuf's corner with his music equipment.

## Timeline

- **Date:** May 2035
- **Time:** 1 PM to 4 PM
- **Duration:** Three hours of afternoon
- **Context:** Mid-day break between morning and evening gig shifts

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard's domestic attention: the specific objects of a family's life, the weight of small interactions
- The apartment as character: its sounds, its light, its containment of struggle and love
- Family dynamics: the shorthand of long relationship, the tensions and tenderness
- Halima's illness: not melodramatic, just present, the constant pressure
- Amina's presence: her intelligence visible, her opportunities constrained
- The music attempt: the instruments, the software, the inability to create when survival demands everything
- The departure: the text that summons, the leaving that hurts
