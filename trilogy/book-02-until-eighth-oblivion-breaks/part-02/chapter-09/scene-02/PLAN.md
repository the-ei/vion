# Scene Plan: The Gathering

## Summary

Inside the converted warehouse, Jerome observes the Church of the Threshold's gathering. The ritual space defies his expectations - more seminar than sermon, more inquiry than faith. Nathaniel Crane presents the core interpretation: the Eighth Oblivion as threshold, as invitation humanity is too afraid to accept. Believers offer testimonies that range from technical to mystical. Jerome's journalistic detachment keeps slipping as he recognizes names, sees intelligent faces, hears explanations that address anomalies he himself has noticed.

## Key Elements

- The warehouse interior: industrial aesthetic repurposed for spiritual community
- The demographic breadth of attendees: tech workers, academics, seekers
- Crane's presentation style: evidence and interpretation rather than faith demands
- The core theological interpretation: Eighth Oblivion as transformation, not catastrophe
- Testimonies from believers: conversion stories, claimed experiences
- Jerome's notebook filling with questions he hadn't anticipated asking
- The atmosphere: genuine seeking rather than paranoid isolation
- Recognition of faces: former colleagues, respected thinkers

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** His skepticism confronted by intelligent believers. He came to observe the fringe and found something more unsettling: people who have thought deeply about questions he's been avoiding.
- **Nathaniel Crane:** First appearance. Former Prometheus VP presenting evidence, not demanding faith. His charisma is intellectual rather than emotional.
- **Various believers:** Tech workers who built the systems, academics who studied them, ordinary people seeking meaning after crisis

## Setting

- Converted warehouse interior: industrial bones visible, but warmly lit
- Folding chairs arranged in loose circles rather than rows
- Projection screens showing diagrams, timelines, data
- No religious iconography - the aesthetic is conference, not church

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late May 2034
- **Time:** Evening, approximately 7:30-9:00 PM
- **Duration:** 90 minutes

## Page Target

6 pages (~1,650 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Balance of Knausgaard and Carson. Knausgaard in the accumulated detail of observation - faces, postures, the specific quality of attention in the room. Carson in the testimonies themselves, which should feel compressed, elliptical, fragments of experience resisting narrative wholeness. The scene should create genuine uncertainty in the reader: is this wisdom or delusion? The text refuses to adjudicate.
