# Scene Plan: The Approach

## Summary

Jerome Washington drives to Oakland at dusk, his mind wrestling with his decision to finally investigate the Church of the Threshold. The drive becomes a meditation on his career, his principles, and the strange tips that have accumulated in his inbox over recent months. As the converted warehouse comes into view, unmarked except for the gathering crowd, Jerome confronts his own reluctance and recognizes it might be its own form of bias.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's drive across the Bay Bridge at twilight
- Internal reflection on why he resisted covering this story for so long
- The accumulating tips from readers: former colleagues, respected thinkers, unexpected converts
- His journalistic principles in tension with the story's unconventional terrain
- First glimpse of the warehouse and the crowd outside
- The quality of the gathering: not fringe paranoia but genuine seeking
- Jerome's preparatory rituals: checking recorder, reviewing notes, settling his mind

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Wrestling with his own assumptions about what constitutes legitimate investigation. His Pulitzer-winning instincts tell him this is fringe material, but something in the pattern of conversions troubles him.

## Setting

- Jerome's car, then the streets approaching the Oakland warehouse
- Bay Bridge crossing at dusk, San Francisco skyline behind
- Industrial Oakland neighborhood, gentrification visible at edges
- The warehouse exterior: no signage, but unmistakable purpose from the crowd

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late May 2034
- **Time:** Early evening, approximately 6:30-7:15 PM
- **Duration:** 45 minutes

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-dominant: long interior monologue as Jerome drives, the rhythm of thought matching the rhythm of traffic. The mundane act of driving elevated to meditation on epistemology and professional ethics. Carson compression in the moment of arrival - the warehouse appearing as almost visionary image, language tightening as anticipation builds.

## Connections

- **Parent (Chapter 09):** Establishes Jerome's investigation and his reluctance to cover the religious counter-narrative
- **Next Scene:** The gathering itself, where his journalistic distance will be tested
- **Part 2 Arc:** First major exploration of religious counter-narrative to the Eighth Oblivion
