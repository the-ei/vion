# Scene Plan: The Offer

## Summary

The interview concludes with Crane producing a drive containing documents and recordings from his Prometheus days. He offers it to Jerome - not for publication, just for investigation. The offer is framed as a test: is Jerome willing to look where truth might lead? Jerome takes the drive, uncertain whether he's pursuing a story or being manipulated into one. The scene captures the moment of commitment, the crossing of a threshold that mirrors the church's theology.

## Key Elements

- Crane producing the drive: physical object weighted with possibility
- His framing: not demanding publication, just investigation
- The claimed contents: internal memos, recordings, data from Prometheus
- Jerome's hesitation: journalistic ethics vs. genuine curiosity
- The question of provenance: are these documents real or sophisticated fabrication?
- Crane's challenge: "You're looking for truth. I'm offering you the chance to find it."
- Jerome's decision to take the drive
- The awareness that accepting changes something, commits him somehow
- The departure from the warehouse into night

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Confronting the moment where investigation becomes involvement. Taking the drive feels like crossing a line, though he can't articulate what line.
- **Nathaniel Crane:** Patient, not pressuring. His confidence suggests either genuine belief or masterful manipulation - Jerome can't determine which.

## Setting

- The same office, late night
- The drive on the desk between them
- The warehouse now quiet, congregation gone
- The weight of the decision in the small, enclosed space

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late May 2034
- **Time:** Approximately 10:30-11:00 PM
- **Duration:** 30 minutes

3 pages (~825 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-dominant: compressed, charged, the space between words carrying meaning. The drive as object, the exchange as ritual. Short paragraphs, white space, the decision rendered through what's left unsaid. This is a threshold moment and the prose should feel like crossing something.
