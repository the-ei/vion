# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Jerome returns to Baltimore late. Denise is still awake, grading papers, and he tries to share the evening's events while maintaining journalistic distance. But she hears something in his voice - this investigation is different, has gotten under his skin. The chapter ends with Jerome at his desk, the drive plugged in, files loading. The first document is a Prometheus internal memo dated six months before Book 1's crisis, and its first line makes his stomach drop.

## Key Elements

- Late-night arrival, the house quiet except for Denise's study
- Their exchange: practiced intimacy of long marriage
- Jerome describing the evening, trying to frame it professionally
- Denise's gentle but probing questions, hearing what he isn't saying
- Her concern: not about the story but about how it's affecting him
- The transition to Jerome's desk, alone with the drive
- Files loading, directory structure revealing the scope
- The first document: a Prometheus memo with devastating implications
- The final hook: what does the first line say?

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Trying to maintain professional distance while something has shifted. His marriage requires honesty, but he isn't yet sure what to be honest about.
- **Denise Washington:** Jerome's wife, appearing for the first time in the chapter. High school history teacher, grounded, perceptive. She's seen him chase stories before, but this feels different.

## Setting

- The Washington home in Baltimore
- Denise's study: papers spread, warm lamp light
- Jerome's desk: where his work happens, the drive now inserted
- Night outside, the isolation of late hours

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late May 2034 (same night) into early morning
- **Time:** Approximately 1:00-2:30 AM
- **Duration:** 90 minutes

3 pages (~825 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard in the domestic detail: the specific sounds of the house at night, the texture of marital conversation, the familiar rituals of return. Carson in the final beats - the files loading, the document opening, the first line. End on compression, on impact, the prose tightening like a held breath. The reader should feel the stomach drop with Jerome.
