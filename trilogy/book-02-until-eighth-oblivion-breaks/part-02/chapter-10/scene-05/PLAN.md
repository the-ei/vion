# Scene Plan: After

## Summary

Late night. Ruth sits with a glass of scotch - Susan's brand, which she's never liked but keeps buying. She thinks about the day's two failures: institutional process that couldn't process, personal connection she couldn't provide. The Eighth Oblivion has become a Rorschach test; everyone sees what they already believed. Her life's work has been building frameworks for adjudication, but adjudication requires shared premises. What happens when the premises themselves are the dispute? She falls asleep in her chair, the television playing news she isn't watching.

## Key Elements

- The scotch ritual: Susan's brand, maintained connection through taste
- Reviewing the day: institutional failure, personal failure
- The Rorschach insight: the Eighth Oblivion as mirror, not event
- Ruth's career in question: frameworks for adjudication when adjudication is impossible
- The epistemological crisis: what happens when premises are contested?
- Television as white noise: news she can't process
- Exhausted sleep in the chair: the body giving out before the mind
- Loneliness as the chapter's emotional ground note

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** Alone with her thoughts, her grief, her doubt. The day has cracked something in her professional identity; the night won't repair it.
- **Susan Abramson (memory):** Present through the scotch, through the empty apartment, through Ruth's awareness of conversations that can't happen.

## Setting

- Ruth's living room at night
- The chair she falls asleep in, television flickering
- The scotch as ritual object
- The apartment's emptiness emphasized by late hour

## Timeline

- **Date:** Early June 2034
- **Time:** Late night, approximately 11:00 PM-2:00 AM
- **Duration:** Three hours (most spent in exhausted contemplation)

## Page Target

3 pages (~825 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Carson-dominant: compressed, meditative, the prose slowing to match Ruth's exhausted mind. Short paragraphs, white space, thoughts that don't complete themselves. The scotch, the chair, the television - objects carrying meaning in the absence of analysis. End on the image of sleep, surrender, the mind giving up on resolution.
