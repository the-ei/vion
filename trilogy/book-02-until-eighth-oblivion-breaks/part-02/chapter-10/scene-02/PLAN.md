# Scene Plan: The Hearing Begins

## Summary

The Senate hearing room: cameras, procedural rituals, the machinery of democratic governance. Senator Holloway's opening statement frames the Eighth Oblivion as corporate malfeasance demanding regulatory intervention. Senator Kincaid's rebuttal frames it as manufactured crisis, government-tech conspiracy. Ruth listens as her carefully prepared testimony becomes irrelevant to what's actually happening in the room. Other witnesses testify - lobbyists, advocates, former officials - each speaking to their faction. The hearing is theater, not inquiry.

## Key Elements

- The hearing room's physical reality: cameras, seating, the performance space
- Senator Holloway (D-CA): aggressive regulation as response to corporate negligence
- Senator Kincaid (R-TX): manufactured crisis, deep state narrative
- Ruth observing the collision of incompatible frameworks
- Other witnesses: each representing a constituency, none pursuing truth
- The procedural machinery grinding without producing insight
- Ruth's prepared testimony becoming irrelevant before she delivers it
- The impossibility of legal judgment when premises are contested

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** Watching institutional process fail in real-time. Her faith tested not by incompetence but by the absorption of governance into performance.
- **Senator Margaret Holloway:** Chair, pushing aggressive regulation. Not corrupt, just certain. Uses the crisis to advance existing agenda.
- **Senator Bradley Kincaid:** Ranking member, pushing manufactured crisis narrative. Some procedural criticisms are valid, wrapped in conspiracy-adjacent framing.
- **Various witnesses:** Lobbyists, advocates, officials - the chorus of democratic theater

## Setting

- Senate hearing room: formal, camera-lit, designed for performance
- The witness table: microphones, water pitchers, the physical apparatus of testimony
- The raised dais: senators as audience and performers simultaneously

## Timeline

- **Date:** Early June 2034
- **Time:** Morning, approximately 9:30-11:30 AM
- **Duration:** Two hours

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Balance of observation and interiority. The hearing's procedure rendered in precise detail - the specific language of political theater, the choreography of statements and responses. Ruth's internal commentary running beneath, her expertise allowing her to decode what's really happening. Carson compression in the quotes themselves - the political statements rendered as fragments, their emptiness exposed through brevity.
