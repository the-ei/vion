# Scene Plan: Preparation

## Summary

Early morning in Ruth Abramson's DC apartment. She reviews her testimony for the Senate subcommittee hearing, carefully crafted over weeks to thread constitutional needles around AI governance. The apartment feels too quiet, too large since Susan's death. Ruth rehearses alone, missing her wife's probing questions, her ability to find the weak points. The car service arrives, and on the drive to Capitol Hill, Ruth watches the city that has been her professional home for four decades, wondering if the frameworks she's dedicated her life to building can hold.

## Key Elements

- Ruth's morning ritual: early rising, coffee, final preparation
- The testimony itself: constitutional implications of AI governance
- Susan's absence: the ghost of partnership, the missing rehearsal partner
- The apartment as space of absence: too quiet, too orderly
- Ruth's professional identity: decades of building legal frameworks
- Doubt emerging: can law adapt to what's coming?
- The car ride to Capitol Hill: the city as familiar territory becoming strange
- Anticipation of institutional process she still believes in (barely)

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** 61, semi-retired federal judge, called to advise on unprecedented legal territory. Her institutional faith is her anchor, but anchors can become weights.
- **Susan Abramson (memory):** Ruth's late wife, a doctor who died six years ago. Her absence shapes Ruth's morning, her questions still echoing.

## Setting

- Ruth's DC apartment: tasteful, professional, ordered, empty
- The car service: transition space, city passing by windows
- Capitol Hill approaching: the physical seat of institutional power

## Timeline

- **Date:** Early June 2034
- **Time:** Early morning, approximately 6:00-8:30 AM
- **Duration:** Two and a half hours

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard-dominant: the accumulation of morning detail, the specific textures of solitary ritual. Ruth's thoughts layered across time - the testimony she's preparing, the marriage she's lost, the career she's built. Carson in the moments of Susan's absence, the spaces where another person should be. Grief rendered through domestic detail rather than direct statement.

- **Parent (Chapter 10):** Establishes Ruth's expertise and her emotional context before the hearing
- **Next Scene:** The hearing begins, her preparation meets political reality
