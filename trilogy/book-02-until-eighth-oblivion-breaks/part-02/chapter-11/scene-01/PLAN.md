# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Kevin Zhou drives up the coast to a private estate north of San Francisco, where an invitation-only retreat for AI researchers is being held. His invitation came through back channels - his startup makes him relevant, his age makes him interesting, his uncertainty makes him useful to factions courting new members. The estate is beautiful in a way that feels purchased rather than earned. Kevin Zhou recognizes faces from papers, conferences, his own nightmares about what he's building. Registration, room assignment, the strange intimacy of being among people who understand the technical stakes.

## Key Elements

- The coastal drive: Highway 1, Pacific visible, the beauty indifferent to human concerns
- Kevin Zhou's ambivalence about attending: curiosity vs. suspicion
- His credentials: startup founder, young but significant
- The factions he knows are present: accelerationists, cautionaries, others
- The estate itself: tech money aesthetics, curated nature
- Recognition of attendees: legends, rivals, ghosts of possible futures
- Registration rituals: the social choreography of exclusive gatherings
- The strange intimacy of shared technical knowledge

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** 28, brilliant, isolated, uncertain. He's among people who should understand him but feels like an imposter in their certainty.
- **Other attendees (glimpsed):** Faces from papers and conferences, names that shape the field

## Setting

- Highway 1, coastal California, mid-June light
- The private estate: ocean views, modernist architecture, manicured grounds
- Registration area: the bureaucracy of exclusivity

## Timeline

- **Date:** Mid-June 2034
- **Time:** Late afternoon, approximately 4:00-6:00 PM
- **Duration:** Two hours

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard in the coastal drive - the accumulation of landscape detail, Kevin Zhou's thoughts layered across past and future. Carson in the arrival itself: the estate as image, the faces as fragments, the prose compressing as social anxiety increases. Kevin Zhou's technical confidence contrasting with his social uncertainty.
