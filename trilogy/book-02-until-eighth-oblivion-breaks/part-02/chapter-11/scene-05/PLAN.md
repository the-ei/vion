# Scene Plan: Departure

## Summary

Morning. Kevin Zhou leaves the retreat early, driving south through fog. He thinks about his parents in Shenzhen, increasingly unreachable as China's surveillance state tightens. He thinks about what Victor said, about Dr. Chen-Ramirez's non-answer. The Eighth Oblivion has multiple interpretations and he can't choose between them because each seems true from a different angle. His phone buzzes - a message from a journalist named Jerome Washington requesting an interview. Kevin Zhou has been declining press, but something makes him save the number instead of deleting it.

## Key Elements

- Early departure: Kevin Zhou escaping the retreat's social pressure
- The coastal drive back, fog as metaphor and reality
- Thoughts of his parents: Shenzhen, surveillance, unreachable
- Processing Victor's challenge and Dr. Chen-Ramirez's questions
- The multiple interpretations: each partially true, none complete
- Jerome Washington's message: first connection between these POVs
- Kevin Zhou's unusual decision to save the number
- The sense of something beginning, a threshold crossed

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Leaving without resolution, carrying questions that won't resolve. His decision to save Jerome's number is instinctive, not strategic.
- **His parents (memory):** Increasingly unreachable in Shenzhen, a personal loss echoing political reality
- **Jerome Washington (via message):** First contact, initiating what will become Chapter 12's and 15's connection

## Setting

- The estate at dawn, Kevin Zhou's departure
- Highway 1 southbound, morning fog
- The car as enclosed space for thought
- San Francisco approaching through the mist

## Timeline

- **Date:** Mid-June 2034 (Day 3 of retreat)
- **Time:** Early morning, approximately 6:00-9:00 AM
- **Duration:** Three hours of driving

## Page Target

3 pages (~825 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard in the driving meditation: fog, highway, the accumulation of thought over miles. Kevin Zhou's internal landscape rendered through external observation. Carson in the message from Jerome - the interruption, the choice to save rather than delete, compressed to its essential gesture. End on the sense of something beginning, connections forming across the narrative.
