# Scene Plan: The Late-Night Conversation

## Summary

After dinner, after drinks, after most have gone to bed, Kevin Zhou finds himself in a small group including Victor Blackwell. The accelerationist is drunk, or performing drunk, and speaks with terrifying clarity about what they're building. He doesn't see a problem - he sees a solution. Humanity has failed; its successor will do better. Kevin Zhou argues back but realizes he doesn't know what he's arguing for. The conversation ends with Victor's challenge: "You're building the same thing I'm building. You just haven't admitted what it is."

## Key Elements

- The late-night setting: alcohol, loosened tongues, dangerous honesty
- Victor Blackwell's drunk clarity: accelerationism stated plainly
- His argument: humanity has failed, replacement is improvement
- Kevin Zhou's counter-arguments: half-formed, emotionally driven
- The realization that Kevin Zhou can't articulate his objection
- Victor's challenge: moral difference without technical difference?
- The small group as audience: other attendees watching the exchange
- Kevin Zhou's inability to sleep after

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Confronted by someone who agrees on facts and disagrees on values. The technical consensus makes moral difference feel arbitrary.
- **Victor Blackwell:** Brilliant, probably sociopathic, definitely influential. His certainty is terrifying because it's coherent. He speaks what others only think.
- **Other late-night attendees:** The chorus, uncomfortable but fascinated

## Setting

- A sitting room in the estate, late night
- Alcohol visible, the detritus of evening socializing
- Low lighting, the intimacy of late hours
- Ocean audible but not visible

## Timeline

- **Date:** Mid-June 2034 (Night of Day 2)
- **Time:** Late night, approximately 11:00 PM-1:30 AM
- **Duration:** Two and a half hours

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Dialogue-driven but with Kevin Zhou's internal panic running beneath. Victor's words should be seductive in their clarity - the reader should feel the pull even while recognizing the horror. Kevin Zhou's counter-arguments should feel genuinely inadequate, not strawmanned. Carson compression in Victor's final challenge - the simplicity of the accusation, its weight.
