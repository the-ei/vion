# Scene Plan: Seeking the Elder

## Summary

During a break, Kevin Zhou seeks out Dr. Sarah Chen-Ramirez. She's 70 now, legendary for building one of the first truly impressive systems, then walking away just as the money flooded in. Her reasons were never fully explained. Kevin Zhou asks her directly: what does she believe? Her answer is elusive but profound - she talks about her grandmother, about temples in Taiwan, about the difference between making and becoming. She doesn't give him answers but she gives him better questions.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's search for Dr. Chen-Ramirez during the break
- Her legendary status: builder, then walker-away
- His direct question: what does she actually believe?
- Her non-answer that answers something deeper
- The grandmother story: temples in Taiwan, a different epistemology
- The distinction between making and becoming
- Her refusal to be claimed by any faction
- Better questions instead of answers

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Seeking wisdom from someone who has lived the trajectory he fears. Her non-answer frustrates and illuminates.
- **Dr. Sarah Chen-Ramirez:** 70, legendary researcher who built and then left. Her current views are ambiguous by choice. She sees something in Kevin Zhou - perhaps herself, decades ago.

## Setting

- The estate's grounds during the break
- A garden or terrace with ocean views
- The pause in scheduled programming, attendees scattered
- The physical distance from the main sessions creating space for honesty

## Timeline

- **Date:** Mid-June 2034 (Day 2 of retreat)
- **Time:** Afternoon break, approximately 3:00-4:00 PM
- **Duration:** One hour

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Dialogue-heavy but with significant silences. Dr. Chen-Ramirez's speech patterns different from tech-speak: more allusive, more comfortable with incompleteness. Kevin Zhou's internal processing of her words, trying to extract actionable guidance from something that resists extraction. Carson in her grandmother story - compressed, imagistic, wisdom through indirection rather than statement.
