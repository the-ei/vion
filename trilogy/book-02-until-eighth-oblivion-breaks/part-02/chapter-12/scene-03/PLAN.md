# Scene Plan: Contact with Kevin Zhou

## Summary

Jerome sends another message to Kevin Zhou, more specific now - referencing the retreat, asking about internal debates at Prometheus. Kevin Zhou responds cautiously. They have a preliminary conversation - not an interview, just two people trying to determine if they can trust each other. Kevin Zhou is useful because he's close enough to confirm some things, far enough to not be directly implicated. The conversation ends with Kevin Zhou agreeing to think about a real interview.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's second outreach to Kevin Zhou: more specific, more informed
- Kevin Zhou's cautious response: interest tempered by wariness
- The preliminary exchange: testing trust, not yet committing
- What Kevin Zhou can and cannot confirm
- His position: close enough to know, far enough to be deniable
- The mutual assessment: each evaluating the other's reliability
- Kevin Zhou's agreement to consider a real interview
- The connection forming across the narrative

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Pursuing a source who might bridge counter-narratives. His approach is careful, professional, respectful of Kevin Zhou's position.
- **Kevin Zhou (via messages/call):** Cautious, intelligent, recognizing something in Jerome worth engaging with despite the risks.

## Setting

- Jerome's home office: screens, phones, the apparatus of remote communication
- The virtual space of text and voice: connection without presence

## Timeline

- **Date:** Early July 2034
- **Time:** Several days of message exchange, culminating in a phone call
- **Duration:** One week

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Dialogue-heavy but rendered through the mediation of technology: messages quoted, call described, the distance inherent in digital communication. Two intelligent people circling each other, each revealing and concealing strategically. The scene builds toward the in-person meeting of Chapter 15 while accomplishing its own work of establishing trust's fragility.
