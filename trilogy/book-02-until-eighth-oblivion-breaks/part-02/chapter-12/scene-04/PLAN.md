# Scene Plan: Family Dinner

## Summary

Sunday dinner at the Washington house. Jerome is distracted, checking his phone for source responses. DeShawn challenges his father's assumptions about tech - "You think you understand what's happening, but you're working with outdated models." Denise tries to mediate but ends up expressing her own concern. Jerome realizes he's become the kind of obsessed investigator he used to profile sympathetically in journalism pieces. The family conflict surfaces deeper tensions: what does truth-telling cost, and who pays?

## Key Elements

- Sunday dinner ritual: the family gathered, domestic normalcy attempted
- Jerome's distraction: phone-checking, mind elsewhere
- DeShawn's challenge: generational critique of Jerome's tech understanding
- Denise's mediation turning to direct concern
- Jerome's recognition: he's become the obsessed journalist archetype
- The deeper tension: truth-telling's cost to family
- DeShawn's knowledge: his generation understands the tech world differently
- The family as microcosm: personal costs of public investigation

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Confronted by his family with truths he's been avoiding. His investigation has costs beyond professional risk.
- **DeShawn Washington:** 17, smart, tech-savvy. He challenges his father not from ignorance but from different knowledge.
- **Denise Washington:** Mediator, then direct speaker. Her concern is love expressed as confrontation.

## Setting

- The Washington family dining room
- Sunday dinner: the specific food, the ritual of weekly gathering
- The domestic space as arena for conflict

## Timeline

- **Date:** Early July 2034
- **Time:** Sunday evening, approximately 6:00-8:00 PM
- **Duration:** Two hours

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard in the domestic detail: the food, the physical arrangement, the accumulated history of family dinners. Dialogue rendered with the specific textures of long-established relationships. The conflict escalating naturally through accumulated small tensions. Carson in the pauses, the things unsaid, the charged silences between exchanges.
