# Scene Plan: Verification Attempts

## Summary

Jerome works his source network. Former Prometheus employees who might recognize the documents. Technical experts who can assess authenticity. Legal contacts who might have seen similar materials in discovery. The responses are mixed: some confirm the documents look real, others point out potential red flags. One source tells him the Church of the Threshold has become sophisticated at producing compelling forgeries. Another says Prometheus produces fake leaks to discredit real ones. The epistemic hall of mirrors deepens.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's verification methodology: multiple independent sources
- Former Prometheus employees: recognition, caution, partial confirmation
- Technical experts: authenticity assessment, metadata analysis
- Legal contacts: what they've seen in discovery
- The mixed responses: neither full confirmation nor clear debunking
- The forgery warning: sophisticated production capabilities
- The counter-warning: companies produce fakes to discredit real leaks
- The epistemic crisis: how to verify when verification itself is contested

## Characters

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** His professional methodology tested by novel terrain. The tools of verification are themselves compromised.
- **Various sources (phone/email):** Former Prometheus employees, technical experts, legal contacts - each offering partial perspective

## Setting

- Jerome's home office and kitchen (phone calls)
- Coffee shops and neutral meeting spaces (in-person source meetings)
- The scattered geography of source-building

## Timeline

- **Date:** Late June to early July 2034
- **Time:** Overlapping with Scene 1's analysis
- **Duration:** Two weeks of verification work

## Stylistic Notes

The rhythm of investigation: calls, meetings, waiting, follow-ups. Each source rendered briefly but distinctly. Jerome's growing frustration as certainty refuses to coalesce. Carson in the responses themselves - fragmentary, contradictory, the impossibility of synthesis. The scene should feel like drowning in partial truths.
