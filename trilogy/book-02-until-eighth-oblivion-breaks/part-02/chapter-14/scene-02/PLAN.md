# Scene Plan: Research Immersion

## Summary

Delphine spends days consuming everything: Jerome's articles, academic papers, counter-narrative content, government statements, tech company PR, religious interpretations. She fills a whiteboard with frameworks. The more she learns, the less certain she becomes about what story can be told honestly. She recognizes the documentary-maker's temptation: smooth the contradictions, create narrative satisfaction, give audiences the closure they crave. Her best work has resisted this temptation. Her most successful work has embraced it.

## Key Elements

- Days of intensive research: the accumulation of sources
- Jerome's articles: one of her key starting points
- The competing narratives: religious, political, technological
- Whiteboard mapping: frameworks, connections, contradictions
- Growing uncertainty: the more she learns, the less she knows
- The documentary-maker's temptation: narrative satisfaction
- Her professional history: best work vs. most successful work
- The gap between honest and effective documentary

## Characters

- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes (POV):** Immersed in material that resists synthesis. Her professional skill becomes the problem - she can make anything into story, which means she must choose what story.

## Setting

- Delphine's home office: screens, whiteboards, printed materials
- The accumulation of research physically visible
- Days blurring together in the intensity of immersion
