# Scene Plan: Meeting with Jerome

## Summary

Delphine meets Jerome at a coffee shop in DC, where she's doing interviews. They know each other from a documentary she produced about financial journalism five years ago. The conversation is frank: Jerome respects Delphine's work but is skeptical about the documentary form's ability to convey genuine complexity. He tells her about the Prometheus documents, about the Church of the Threshold, about the epistemic hall of mirrors. "You're going to have to choose," he says. "Neutral isn't real. The only question is whether you choose consciously or let the format choose for you."

## Key Elements

- Meeting in DC: Delphine's research trip
- Their history: previous documentary collaboration
- Professional respect with mutual wariness
- Jerome's skepticism about documentary form
- His summary of the epistemic landscape: documents, church, mirrors
- "Neutral isn't real" - the chapter's key truth
- Choosing consciously vs. letting format choose
- Jerome as potential subject: his decision to participate unclear

## Characters

- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes (POV):** Seeking Jerome's perspective and receiving more than expected. His critique of documentary form challenges her professional identity.
- **Jerome Washington:** Skeptical but engaged. He wants the story told honestly and doubts it can be done in documentary form. His challenge is genuine, not dismissive.

## Setting

- Coffee shop in DC: neutral professional space
- The specific details of the meeting: drinks, tables, ambient noise
- The city outside: power geography
