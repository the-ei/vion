# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Ruth arrives at David's Upper East Side apartment, a space that suddenly feels precarious - all that could be lost. David meets her alone; Amanda is at her sister's, needing space. He looks older than she remembers, worn by weeks of legal uncertainty. They embrace awkwardly - physical affection has never been easy between them. She asks for the facts: what exactly did his firm do, what exactly is he accused of, what exactly does the evidence show? David starts explaining, and Ruth realizes the complexity will take hours to untangle.

## Key Elements

- Ruth's arrival in New York: the physical journey as emotional transition
- David's apartment: wealth that now feels vulnerable
- Amanda's absence: marriage strain visible through her retreat
- David's appearance: the physical toll of uncertainty
- The awkward embrace: physical affection never their language
- Ruth's professional instinct: demanding facts, timeline, evidence
- The complexity emerging: this won't be simple
- The beginning of a long conversation

## Characters

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** Arriving as both mother and jurist, struggling to separate the roles. Her demand for facts is how she shows love.
- **David Abramson:** 38, worn by weeks of investigation. His need for his mother is visible beneath his professional composure.
- **Amanda Abramson (absent):** Her absence speaks to marriage strain; we understand through what's not present.

## Setting

- David's Upper East Side apartment: tasteful, expensive, newly precarious
- The doorway, the entrance hall, the living room where they'll talk
- New York outside: the city that promised success now threatening

## Timeline

- **Date:** Mid-July 2034
- **Time:** Late afternoon, approximately 4:00-5:30 PM
- **Duration:** 90 minutes

## Stylistic Notes

Knausgaard in the physical detail: the specific textures of expensive anxiety, David's face, the apartment's furnishings rendered ominous by context. The embrace's awkwardness described precisely - the history of physical distance between them. Carson in the silences, what Amanda's absence means without stating it directly.
