# Scene Plan: The Mutual Aid Meeting

## Summary

The informal mutual aid network that formed during the crisis has called a meeting at a community center. Yusuf goes reluctantly, expecting nothing, but finds something unexpected: people organizing not just for immediate survival but for what comes next. The meeting is chaotic, under-resourced, full of disagreement - but alive in ways the formal institutions never were. Yusuf does not speak much, but he listens. Several neighbor figures emerge who will recur in his life: community members who became something more during the crisis. The scene shows that catastrophe has created new connections even as it damaged existing systems.

## Key Elements

- The community center setting - a concrete space of collective action
- Mutual aid as emergent response: informal, imperfect, genuine
- Yusuf's reluctance giving way to interest
- The meeting's chaos as sign of life rather than failure
- Several recurring neighbor characters introduced
- Discussions of what comes next: not just surviving but building
- Yusuf's usual distrust of collective action tested
- The difference between institutional response and community response

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Skeptical but curious; watching more than participating; surprised by what he finds
- **Denise Williams:** Middle-aged Black woman who organized the meeting; practical, sharp, uninterested in credit
- **Omar Farah:** Somali elder from the neighborhood; knows Yusuf's family; carries authority without demanding it
- **Jasmine Torres:** Young organizer, late twenties, impatient with meetings but committed to the work
- **Various attendees:** A cross-section of the neighborhood - different ages, backgrounds, all marked by crisis survival

## Setting

- **Location:** A community center in Minneapolis - multipurpose room with folding chairs
- **Time:** Evening, a few days after Scene 2
- **Physical details:** Fluorescent lights, folding chairs in uneven rows, a whiteboard with scrawled notes, someone's donated coffee in paper cups, children playing in a corner while parents attend
- **Atmosphere:** The particular energy of people trying to build something together; messy, contentious, alive

## Timeline

- **Duration:** A two-hour meeting
- **Period:** Early December 2036, first week after crisis
- **Position in chapter:** Third scene, expanding Yusuf's world from family to community

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Dialogue heavy for this scene - multiple voices, disagreements, the texture of collective deliberation
- Yusuf as observer: his perspective filters what we see of the meeting
- Brief sketches of several characters - efficiency in characterization
- The meeting's chaos rendered as vitality, not dysfunction
- Contrast with algorithmic gig work: human coordination vs. platform coordination
- Seeds of Yusuf's potential political awakening, planted but not yet sprouting
