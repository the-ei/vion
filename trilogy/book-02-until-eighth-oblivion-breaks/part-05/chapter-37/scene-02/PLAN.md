# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Yusuf returns home to find his mother Fatima awake when she should be resting. Her diabetes complicated the crisis - medication access disrupted, stress effects on blood sugar. They sit together in the kitchen and do not talk about what happened, but their silence is different now. Amina is at the table, practicing SAT vocabulary with fierce determination, preparing for exams that may or may not happen on schedule. The family's survival strategies are visible in this kitchen scene: Fatima's care, Amina's ambition, Yusuf's labor. The question of what else might be possible hovers unspoken.

## Key Elements

- Fatima awake, worrying, when she should be resting - her health concerns foregrounded
- The kitchen as heart of the home - domestic intimacy after crisis
- Silence as communication: what Yusuf and Fatima do not say
- Amina's fierce focus on SAT prep as coping mechanism and future-building
- The three family members' different survival strategies made visible
- Fatima's diabetes and how the crisis affected her health management
- Unspoken question: is survival enough, or is something more possible?
- The warmth of the apartment against the cold outside

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Returning from work, observing his mother and sister, feeling the weight of his role in the family
- **Fatima (mother):** Awake with worry despite needing rest; her health fragile but her spirit unbroken; watching her children with new intensity
- **Amina (sister):** Sixteen, focused on SAT vocabulary, using academic ambition to control what she can; the family's hope for upward mobility

## Setting

- **Location:** The Hassan family apartment - specifically the kitchen
- **Time:** Evening, after Yusuf's deliveries
- **Physical details:** The small kitchen table, Fatima's tea, Amina's vocabulary flashcards, the refrigerator covered in appointment reminders and Amina's school schedule, the warmth of the radiator
- **Atmosphere:** Domestic intimacy heightened by crisis survival; love expressed through presence and routine

## Timeline

- **Duration:** An hour or two of evening time
- **Period:** Early December 2036, first week after crisis
- **Position in chapter:** Second scene, shifting from public/economic to private/familial

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard's immersive domestic detail: the specific objects, textures, sounds of the apartment
- Dialogue sparse - much communicated through gesture and observation
- Fatima's health concerns shown rather than explained
- Amina's vocabulary words could be thematically resonant (words about change, survival, future)
- The family dynamics rendered through small moments of care
- Warmth and tension coexisting - love does not eliminate worry
