# Scene Plan: Preparation (Elena/Flagstaff)

## Summary

Elena helps prepare for a small New Year's Eve dinner - just family, nothing elaborate. Daniel is cooking; she is setting the table with Sofia's help. Mateo plays underfoot. Abuela watches from her chair, offering commentary. The domestic scene is heightened by everything behind it: the crisis they survived, the decisions they have made, the uncertainty ahead. But the uncertainty feels different now - chosen rather than imposed. Elena thinks about her resignation, about the mutual aid meeting, about the path opening before her. She does not know where it leads, but she knows why she is walking it.

## Key Elements

- New Year's Eve dinner preparation: small, family-only, intimate
- Daniel cooking, Elena setting table, Sofia helping, Mateo underfoot, Abuela watching
- Ordinary domestic scene heightened by extraordinary context
- The uncertainty ahead: chosen rather than imposed
- Elena's reflections: resignation, mutual aid, the path forming
- Direction without destination: knowing why without knowing where
- The family as unit: different from before, reconfigured
- The eve of a new year: temporal marker of transition

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Preparing for dinner, reflecting on her choices, feeling the chosen uncertainty
- **Daniel:** Cooking, his presence domestic and supportive
- **Sofia (6):** Helping set the table, recovered somewhat from crisis but changed
- **Mateo (3):** Playing underfoot, his energy a counterpoint to adult weight
- **Abuela:** Watching from her chair, offering commentary, the family's anchor

## Setting

- **Location:** Flagstaff - the house where they are now living (Daniel's sister's, or a new rental)
- **Time:** December 31, 2036, late afternoon
- **Physical details:** The kitchen and dining area, table being set, cooking smells, Sofia's careful placement of forks, Mateo's toy on the floor, abuela's chair by the window, winter light
- **Atmosphere:** Domestic warmth carrying extraordinary weight; the ordinary made significant by context

## Timeline
