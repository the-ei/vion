# Scene Plan: The Call (Kevin/San Francisco)

## Summary

Just after midnight, Kevin's phone rings with an international number. His parents - the call finally getting through after weeks of silence. The connection is poor, breaking up, but he hears his mother's voice and something cracks open. They exchange fragments: are you safe, yes, are you safe, yes. His father comes on briefly - more said in silences than words. The call lasts three minutes before it cuts out, but those three minutes hold more weight than years of distance. Kevin sits in the quiet after, breathing. The city lights blink through his window. The Eighth Oblivion came and went and came differently than anyone expected. He is still here. The threshold is behind him now. What is ahead has no name yet, but he is ready to find one.

## Key Elements

- The international call: finally getting through to Kevin's parents in China
- Poor connection: breaking up, fragments, brief
- Mother's voice: the crack opening in Kevin
- Brief exchange: "Are you safe, yes, are you safe, yes"
- Father coming on: more in silences than words
- Three minutes: weighted with years of distance
- The quiet after: Kevin sitting, breathing, present
- The threshold behind: the Eighth Oblivion crossed
- What is ahead: unnamed but ready to be found
