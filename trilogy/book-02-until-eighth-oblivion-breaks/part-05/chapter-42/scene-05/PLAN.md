# Scene Plan: Alone (Kevin/San Francisco)

## Summary

Kevin is in his apartment, alone. But the alone feels different - chosen rather than default. He has spent the evening working on something, not his old code but something new: rough notes for what he and Yusuf discussed, the beginning of a framework for a different kind of building. His phone shows the text exchange with Yusuf, the connection maintained across distance. At midnight, he watches fireworks from his window - the city celebrating around him, still wounded but alive. He sends the "Happy New Year" text, waits for the response, feels something when it comes.

## Key Elements

- Kevin alone in his apartment: but differently alone
- Working on something new: notes from the Yusuf conversation, a different framework
- Not his old code: the break from previous building
- The text exchange with Yusuf: connection across distance
- Watching fireworks from the window: the city celebrating
- San Francisco still wounded but alive: crisis aftermath visible
- Sending the text, waiting, receiving: the ritual of connection
- Feeling something: unnamed but real

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Alone but differently, working on something new, connected to Yusuf
- **Yusuf Hassan (via text):** The other end of the connection, responding
- **San Francisco (environmental):** The city outside, celebrating, wounded, alive
