# Scene Plan: The Gathering (Yusuf/Minneapolis)

## Summary

Yusuf is at a New Year's Eve party organized by the mutual aid network - not a fancy affair, just a community center, potluck food, someone's speakers playing music. His mother Fatima is there, healthier than she has been in years. His sister Amina is there, animated, talking to people about colleges and futures. Yusuf moves through the crowd, surprised by how many people he knows now - people he met during the crisis, people who became community instead of strangers. He gets a text from Kevin Zhou: "Happy New Year from SF. Thinking about what we talked about." Yusuf smiles at his phone, an expression his mother notices and files away.

## Key Elements

- Mutual aid New Year's party: community center, potluck, casual
- Fatima present and healthy: improvement since the crisis
- Amina animated about college: the future opening for her
- Yusuf knowing more people: crisis created community
- The text from Kevin Zhou: maintaining the connection
- "Thinking about what we talked about": the conversation echoing
- Yusuf smiling at his phone: unusual enough for Fatima to notice
- Community as something new: stranger to neighbor to something more

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Moving through the party, surprised by his own belonging
- **Fatima (mother):** Present, healthier, watching her son with new attention
- **Amina (sister):** Talking about college, animated, hopeful
- **Kevin Zhou (via text):** Reaching across distance, maintaining connection
- **Community members:** People from the mutual aid network, now familiar faces

## Setting

- **Location:** Minneapolis community center - the mutual aid network's gathering
- **Time:** December 31, 2036, late evening
- **Physical details:** Folding tables with potluck food, speakers playing music, people in winter clothes having taken off coats, the warmth of bodies gathered, paper decorations
- **Atmosphere:** The particular warmth of community gathering; celebration that is also affirmation of survival

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Several hours of the party
- **Period:** New Year's Eve, December 31, 2036
- **Position in chapter:** Third scene, first of Yusuf's two scenes (Section B)

- Fatima and Amina's presence as signs of family health

- Yusuf's smile as notable: his mother's observation marks the change
- Potluck details specific: what food, who brought it, the flavors of different households
- The party as evidence: the crisis created something, not only destroyed
