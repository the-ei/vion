# Scene Plan: Midnight (Elena/Flagstaff)

## Summary

The family marks midnight together - champagne for the adults, sparkling cider for the children, the television countdown felt rather than watched. When the moment comes, Elena holds Daniel and thinks about thresholds. The crisis tried to break them; instead, it broke something in them - a membrane of illusion, a pretense that the system was survivable unchanged. What grows now will be different. She makes no resolutions, only recognitions: the world is what it is, she sees it now, and she will act accordingly. The children fall asleep on the couch; the adults carry them to bed. Elena stands at the window watching the first moments of the new year, and for once her mind is quiet.

## Key Elements

- Midnight countdown: felt rather than watched
- Champagne and cider: the family's modest celebration
- Elena holding Daniel: physical intimacy marking the moment
- Thoughts about thresholds: the Eighth Oblivion as threshold crossed
- What the crisis broke: a membrane of illusion, not the family
- No resolutions, only recognitions: seeing the world as it is
- Children falling asleep: the innocence and weariness both
- Elena at the window: the first moments of the new year, mind quiet

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Marking midnight, thinking about thresholds, finding quiet
- **Daniel:** Held by Elena, present in the moment
- **Sofia and Mateo:** Falling asleep on the couch, carried to bed
- **Abuela:** Present but retiring early, her blessing on the household implicit
- **The new year:** The temporal threshold crossing

## Setting

- **Location:** The Flagstaff house - living room, then children's bedroom, then window
- **Time:** December 31, 2036, 11:30 PM through January 1, 2037, early hours
- **Physical details:** The television muted, champagne glasses, children's heads drooping, the couch, the bedroom where children are carried, the window looking out at the new year
- **Atmosphere:** The particular quality of New Year's midnight: arbitrary but meaningful, intimate in small gatherings

## Timeline

## Stylistic Notes

- The countdown felt rather than watched: the television's role minimal

- No resolutions: refusal of the conventional New Year's trope
- Carrying children to bed as domestic sacrament: tender without being sentimental

- Mind quiet: Elena's resolution is a kind of stillness, not a plan
