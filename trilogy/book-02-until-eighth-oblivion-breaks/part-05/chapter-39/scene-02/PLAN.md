# Scene Plan: What They Saw

## Summary

That night, after the children are asleep, Elena and Daniel finally talk. He tells her what the crisis was like from Flagstaff - the uncertainty, the evacuation routes that kept changing, Sofia asking every hour when Mommy was coming. Elena tells him fragments of the clinic experience, but she cannot make it cohere. They are both crying by the end, not dramatically but with the exhaustion of people who have been holding too much. The conversation does not resolve anything, but it opens a channel. They sleep in the same bed for the first time in weeks.

## Key Elements

- The first real conversation between Elena and Daniel since the crisis
- Daniel's experience: the uncertainty, managing the children's fear, his own fear for Elena
- Sofia asking when Mommy was coming - the child's refrain haunting him
- Elena's fragmented telling: she cannot make her experience into narrative
- Both crying - the release of what has been held
- The conversation as opening rather than resolution
- Sleeping together: the physical intimacy of simply being beside each other
- The marriage strained but not broken

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Trying to tell Daniel what she experienced, failing to make it cohere, releasing through tears
- **Daniel:** Sharing his own crisis experience, equally unable to fully convey it, meeting Elena in shared incomprehension
- **The children (sleeping):** Their presence in the next room, their needs shaping what Elena and Daniel can say

## Setting

- **Location:** The guest room at Daniel's sister's house - the bed where they will sleep
- **Time:** Late night, same day as Scene 1
- **Physical details:** Low light, the bed, sitting close together, tissues, the sounds of an unfamiliar house settling, children asleep down the hall
- **Atmosphere:** The particular intimacy of late-night crisis conversation; vulnerability without resolution

## Timeline

- **Duration:** 2-3 hours of conversation
- **Period:** Mid-to-late December 2036, first week of Elena's return
- **Position in chapter:** Second scene, moving from physical reunion to verbal processing

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Dialogue heavy but interrupted - neither can tell their story straight through
- Fragments and half-sentences - the failure of language to contain experience
- Crying rendered without sentimentality - physical release, not dramatic peak
- Daniel's experience given full weight - his crisis was real, different, equally hard
- The intimacy of shared incomprehension - not understanding each other but trying
- Ending with the simple act of sleeping beside each other - physical presence as comfort

## Connections

- **Siblings:** Follows physical reunion with verbal processing; sets up Scene 3's focus on children's trauma
- **Part:** The "questions about marriage to Daniel" from Part plan begin to find answers here
- **Book:** Elena and Daniel's relationship, strained throughout Book 2, reaches a new honesty
