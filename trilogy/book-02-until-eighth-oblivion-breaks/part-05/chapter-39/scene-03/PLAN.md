# Scene Plan: Sofia's Drawings

## Summary

Elena spends time with Sofia, trying to reconnect. Sofia shows her drawings from during the crisis - disturbing images that reveal more than a six-year-old should understand. Houses with red skies. Stick figures lying down. A picture of Mommy in a white coat with red spots on it. Elena does not know how to respond. She holds Sofia and promises things will be okay, but she does not believe it and Sofia knows. The scene shows trauma transmitted generationally, the limits of adult protection.

## Key Elements

- Elena's attempt to reconnect with Sofia one-on-one
- Sofia's drawings: visual records of her crisis experience
- Specific disturbing images: red skies, stick figures lying down, Mommy with red spots
- A six-year-old processing what she should not have had to understand
- Elena's inability to respond adequately
- The promise "things will be okay" and its hollowness
- Sofia's knowledge that her mother does not believe her own words
- The limits of parental protection - Elena cannot undo what Sofia now knows

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Trying to reconnect with her daughter, confronting evidence of trauma she cannot erase
- **Sofia (6):** Showing her mother what she saw, what she drew, what she understands; watching her mother's reaction
- **The drawings:** Objects that speak what Sofia cannot say in words

## Setting

- **Location:** A room in Daniel's sister's house - perhaps the living room, where Sofia has been drawing
- **Time:** The next day, afternoon
- **Physical details:** Scattered drawings, crayons, the paper's texture, Sofia's hands showing Elena each picture, a couch where they sit together
- **Atmosphere:** The particular weight of children's art that reveals too much

## Timeline

- **Duration:** An hour or so of looking at drawings, talking, holding
- **Period:** Mid-to-late December 2036, first week of Elena's return
- **Position in chapter:** Third scene, focusing on children's trauma after adult conversation

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- The drawings should be described specifically - what Sofia drew, what it reveals
- Elena's internal reaction: horror, guilt, the impossibility of protection
- Sofia's matter-of-fact presentation - children's directness about what they have seen
- The hollow promise rendered without irony but with weight
- The moment of recognition: Sofia knows Elena does not believe her own words
- Holding Sofia as inadequate but necessary response
- The transmission of trauma across generations - shown, not explained
