# Scene Plan: Night Walking

## Summary

Elena cannot sleep. She walks Flagstaff's night streets, the cold mountain air so different from Phoenix's heat. She passes closed businesses, open bars, a 24-hour diner where someone is crying in a booth. She thinks about her patients - specific faces, specific failures. She thinks about the system that put them in her care without giving her the resources to save them. Something is crystallizing in her mind: not yet political language, but the shape of structural critique. The walking is how she thinks.

## Key Elements

- Elena's insomnia driving her out into the night
- Flagstaff's cold mountain air: physical contrast to Phoenix
- The streets at night: closed businesses, open bars, signs of other people's crises
- Someone crying in a diner booth - a stranger's grief witnessed
- Elena's mind on her patients: specific faces, specific failures
- The structural critique beginning to form: the system, not individual failure
- Walking as thinking - motion enabling processing
- The beginning of Elena's radicalization: seeing structure, not just symptoms

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Walking through the night, processing, beginning to see the system rather than just patients
- **Stranger in the diner:** A person crying, noticed but not approached - evidence of widespread crisis
- **Patients (memory):** Specific faces and failures returning to Elena's mind
- **The system (conceptual):** The healthcare structure that failed Elena's patients, now becoming visible to her

## Setting

- **Location:** Flagstaff streets at night
- **Time:** Late night, 2-3 AM
- **Physical details:** The cold air, her breath visible, streetlights, closed storefronts, an open bar with muffled music, the 24-hour diner's harsh light, mountains visible in darkness at the end of streets
- **Atmosphere:** The particular clarity of late-night walking; solitude enabling thought

## Timeline

- **Duration:** 1-2 hours of walking
- **Period:** Mid-to-late December 2036, first week of Elena's return
- **Position in chapter:** Fourth scene, Elena's private processing after focus on family
