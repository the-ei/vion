# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Elena drives to Flagstaff in a borrowed car - hers is still in Phoenix, and she could not face going home first. The approach through mountains, the different air, the distance from the desert that defined her crisis experience. She arrives at Daniel's sister's house and the children rush out. Mateo will not let go; Sofia hangs back. Daniel embraces her and she feels how thin he has gotten. No one knows what to say. The scene captures reunion as overwhelming sensory experience - the smell of Sofia's hair, Daniel's hands, Mateo's weight on her hip.

## Key Elements

- The drive to Flagstaff: the changing landscape, the approach through mountains
- Elena's choice not to go to Phoenix first - unable to face home
- The moment of arrival: children rushing out, the physical reality of reunion
- Mateo's clinginess - his regression visible in how he holds her
- Sofia hanging back - the six-year-old's more complex processing
- Daniel's thinness - evidence of his own crisis experience
- The silence beneath the reunion - no one knowing what to say
- Sensory overwhelm: smell, touch, weight, the physical reality of family

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Arriving after separation, overwhelmed by the physical reality of reunion, unable to process
- **Daniel:** Her husband, thinner than she remembered, carrying his own crisis experience
- **Sofia (6):** Hanging back, watchful, her trauma showing in restraint rather than clinginess
- **Mateo (3):** Clinging to Elena, refusing to let go, regressed by the separation
- **Daniel's sister (peripheral):** Standing in the doorway, having sheltered the family during crisis

## Setting

- **Location:** The drive from Phoenix area to Flagstaff; then Daniel's sister's house
- **Time:** Mid-to-late December 2036, afternoon
- **Physical details:** The highway climbing through mountains, pine trees replacing desert, the cooler air, the sister's house (modest, warm), the front yard where reunion happens
- **Atmosphere:** The particular intensity of reunion after trauma; relief and strangeness coexisting

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Several hours of driving, then the arrival moment
- **Period:** Mid-to-late December 2036, first week of Elena's return
- **Position in chapter:** Opening scene, establishing the reunion as both relief and new challenge

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- The drive rendered with Knausgaard attention: the landscape changing, Elena's state of mind through what she notices
- The reunion moment as pure sensation before thought - physical reality prior to interpretation
- Each family member's state shown through physical detail
- Minimal dialogue - the silence more eloquent than speech
- The sister's house as temporary haven - not home but shelter
- Ending as Elena holds Mateo and looks at Sofia, caught between two children's different needs
