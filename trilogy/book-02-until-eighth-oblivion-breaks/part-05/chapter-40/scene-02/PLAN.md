# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Yusuf takes Kevin to his family's apartment - a decision that surprises them both. Fatima is polite but assessing; Amina peppers Kevin with questions about AI and tech careers. Kevin is uncomfortable in ways that reveal him: he does not know how to be in someone's home, how to accept hospitality without transactionalizing it. Yusuf sees Kevin's discomfort and something shifts - he recognizes Kevin's isolation as genuine, not chosen superiority. The scene humanizes both characters through domestic detail.

## Key Elements

- Yusuf's spontaneous decision to bring Kevin home
- The Hassan apartment: small but warm, full of family
- Fatima's assessment of Kevin: polite but watchful
- Amina's questions about tech and AI: her ambition, her curiosity
- Kevin's discomfort with hospitality he cannot transactionalize
- Yusuf's realization: Kevin's isolation is real, not superiority
- Domestic intimacy: the particular vulnerability of being in someone's home
- Class rendered through space: what the apartment reveals about the Hassans

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Watching Kevin in his family's space, surprised by his own decision to bring Kevin here, gaining new understanding
- **Kevin Zhou:** Uncomfortable with hospitality, unable to relax, revealing his isolation through his awkwardness
- **Fatima (mother):** Polite but assessing the stranger her son has brought home; her health improved since the crisis
- **Amina (sister):** Asking Kevin questions about tech careers, college, AI - her practical ambition on display

## Setting

- **Location:** The Hassan family apartment in Minneapolis
- **Time:** Late December 2036, late afternoon/early evening
- **Physical details:** The small apartment, Fatima's cooking smells, family photos on walls, Amina's college materials spread on a table, the warmth against the cold outside
- **Atmosphere:** Domestic warmth that makes Kevin's isolation visible; the particular intimacy of someone else's home

## Timeline

- **Duration:** 1-2 hours in the apartment
- **Period:** Late December 2036 (near end of year)
- **Position in chapter:** Second scene, moving from public space to domestic intimacy

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard's domestic attention: specific objects, smells, the texture of family life
- Kevin rendered through his discomfort: what he does not know how to do
- Fatima's assessment shown through her behavior, not stated
- Amina's dialogue should feel genuine: a smart sixteen-year-old asking smart questions
- Yusuf's shift in understanding shown through observation, not exposition
- The hospitality as test Kevin does not know he is taking
