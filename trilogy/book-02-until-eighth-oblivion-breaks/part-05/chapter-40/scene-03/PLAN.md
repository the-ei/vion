# Scene Plan: The Walk

## Summary

Yusuf takes Kevin for a walk through his neighborhood - showing him the route he drives for deliveries, the community center where mutual aid meets, the corner where someone died during the crisis. The cold is brutal; Kevin is underdressed. But as they walk, they begin to actually talk - about the crisis, about what they saw, about what it meant. The conversation is halting, circling, both of them testing what they can say. The Minneapolis winter strips pretense; survival in the cold creates honesty.

## Key Elements

- The walking tour: Yusuf showing Kevin his world
- Specific locations: delivery route, community center, the corner where someone died
- The cold as equalizer: Kevin underdressed, suffering, stripped of defenses
- Conversation beginning to happen: halting, testing, circling toward the real
- The crisis as shared reference: what they saw, what happened to them
- Minneapolis geography as character: working-class neighborhood, winter beauty
- The corner where someone died: a specific marker of what was lost
- Honesty emerging through physical discomfort

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Showing Kevin his neighborhood, testing how Kevin responds, beginning to talk honestly
- **Kevin Zhou:** Suffering in the cold, observing, beginning to reciprocate Yusuf's honesty
- **The neighborhood (environmental):** The streets, the community center, the corner - Yusuf's geography made visible

## Setting

- **Location:** Yusuf's Minneapolis neighborhood - walking through streets
- **Time:** Late December 2036, early evening (getting dark)
- **Physical details:** Snow on the ground, breath visible, Kevin's inadequate coat, the community center building, closed storefronts, the specific corner where someone died, streetlights coming on
- **Atmosphere:** The particular honesty that cold weather forces; physical discomfort enabling emotional openness

## Timeline

- **Duration:** An hour or more of walking
- **Period:** Late December 2036 (near end of year)
- **Position in chapter:** Third scene, deepening the connection through shared movement
