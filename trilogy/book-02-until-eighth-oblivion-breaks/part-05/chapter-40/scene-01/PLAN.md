# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Kevin lands in Minneapolis in late December - his first time in the city, his first time in winter this severe. Yusuf picks him up at the airport, and the awkwardness is immediate. They barely talked during the crisis except functionally; now they must navigate actual conversation. Kevin notices everything: how Yusuf drives, what music plays, the neighborhoods they pass through. Yusuf watches Kevin watching, waiting for the judgment that does not come. The scene establishes their dynamic through tension and curiosity, two people trying to find a language for their strange connection.

## Key Elements

- Kevin's arrival in Minneapolis: unfamiliar city, unfamiliar cold
- The airport pickup: awkward first minutes, neither knowing how to be
- Kevin's observations: Yusuf's car, driving style, music, the route
- Yusuf's defensive watchfulness: expecting judgment that does not come
- Minimal conversation: both trying and failing to break the ice
- Minneapolis in winter: the physical environment as third character
- Class dynamics present but not voiced: the expensive coat, the used car
- The journey from airport as transition into their encounter

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Picking up Kevin, defensive but curious, watching Kevin watch his world
- **Kevin Zhou:** Arriving in unfamiliar territory, observing everything, unable to make small talk
- **Minneapolis (environmental character):** The cold, the neighborhoods, the visual difference from San Francisco

## Setting

- **Location:** Minneapolis-Saint Paul International Airport, then Yusuf's car driving through the city
- **Time:** Late December 2036, afternoon
- **Physical details:** Airport arrivals area, Yusuf's car interior, the music on the stereo, Minneapolis visible through windows - snow, working-class neighborhoods, the cold visible in people's breath
- **Atmosphere:** The particular awkwardness of meeting someone you shared crisis with but don't actually know

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Airport pickup plus 30-40 minute drive
- **Period:** Late December 2036 (near end of year)
- **Position in chapter:** Opening scene, establishing the meeting and their initial dynamic

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Yusuf's POV gives us his defensive awareness: what he expects Kevin to think, what Kevin actually seems to do
- Kevin rendered through Yusuf's observation: what Kevin notices, how he looks at things
- The car as confined space forcing proximity
- Music as characterization: what Yusuf plays, whether Kevin comments
- Minimal dialogue - the scene is about observing each other
- The cold as physical reality: Kevin underdressed, Yusuf used to it
