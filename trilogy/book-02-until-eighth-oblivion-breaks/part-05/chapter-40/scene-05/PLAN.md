# Scene Plan: After

## Summary

Kevin stays for dinner - Fatima's food, Amina's ongoing questions, the strange warmth of belonging somewhere. Later, Yusuf drives Kevin back to his hotel. In the car, they are quiet but the silence is different now. Kevin says he wants to build something different. Yusuf says he does not trust builders. But there is a possibility hanging in the air - not partnership exactly, but the beginning of something neither can name yet. Kevin flies back to San Francisco in the morning; Yusuf goes home and, for the first time in weeks, writes a complete piece of music.

## Key Elements

- Dinner with the Hassan family: Kevin experiencing domestic belonging
- Fatima's food: hospitality completed, Kevin somewhat accepted
- Amina's continuing questions: her energy, her ambition
- The drive back: silence that means something different now
- Kevin's statement: he wants to build something different
- Yusuf's response: he does not trust builders
- The possibility unnamed: not agreement but opening
- Yusuf writing complete music: the scene's emotional resolution

## Characters

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Having dinner with his family and Kevin, driving Kevin to hotel, going home to write music
- **Kevin Zhou:** Experiencing family dinner, stating his intention, leaving with something changed
- **Fatima:** Hosting the dinner, her hospitality now warmer toward Kevin
- **Amina:** Continuing to ask questions, her presence lightening the evening
- **The music (Yusuf's creation):** What emerges when Yusuf gets home - complete for the first time

## Setting

- **Location:** Hassan apartment (dinner), then Yusuf's car, then Yusuf's bedroom
- **Time:** Late December 2036, evening into night
- **Physical details:** The dinner table crowded, Fatima's specific dishes, the car at night, Yusuf's keyboard glowing, the completed piece of music
- **Atmosphere:** Warmth giving way to possibility; the drive's meaningful silence; the creative release at the end

## Timeline

- **Period:** Late December 2036 (near end of year)
