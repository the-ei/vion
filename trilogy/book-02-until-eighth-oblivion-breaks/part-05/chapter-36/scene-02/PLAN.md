# Scene Plan: The Waiting

## Summary

Between patients, Elena tries to reach her family. The communication systems are partially restored but overwhelmed - calls drop, texts hang in limbo, recorded messages tell her nothing. She reaches a voicemail that may be hours or days old. The specific anguish of not knowing takes hold: her children are somewhere, Daniel is somewhere, but the systems that would confirm their safety have become unreliable. A colleague mentions that cell towers are prioritizing emergency services. Elena returns to treating patients, but the waiting has begun - a new kind of trauma layered over the crisis itself. A patient asks if it's really over; Elena says yes but hears the doubt in her own voice.

## Key Elements

- Communication infrastructure partially restored but unreliable
- The particular anguish of not knowing vs. knowing the worst
- Elena oscillating between attempts to call and treating patients
- The way hope and dread coexist in uncertainty
- Cell towers overwhelmed, prioritization systems creating new inequities
- A patient asking for confirmation that the crisis has passed
- Elena's voice betraying what her words deny
- The communal experience of waiting - others around her in the same state

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Desperate to reach family but professional duty pulling her back to patients; caught between roles
- **Clinic colleague (Dr. Reyes):** Fellow medical professional who mentions the cell tower prioritization; equally exhausted, equally waiting for word from someone
- **Questioning patient:** Middle-aged man being treated for dehydration; needs reassurance Elena cannot honestly give
- **Background presence:** Others in the clinic checking their phones, the collective tension of waiting

## Setting

- **Location:** Phoenix community health center - treatment area and the small alcove where staff take brief breaks
- **Time:** Morning, continuing from Scene 1 (approximately 8 AM - 10 AM)
- **Physical details:** Elena's phone with its dwindling battery, the posted emergency communications protocols now outdated, the staff break area cluttered with empty coffee cups and discarded PPE
- **Atmosphere:** Tension beneath exhaustion; the clinic functioning but everyone elsewhere in their minds

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Approximately 2 hours
- **Period:** Late November or early December 2036, first day after crisis
- **Position in chapter:** Second scene, introducing the family anxiety that will drive the chapter

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Shorter, more fragmented sentences as Elena's anxiety mounts
- The phone as object of obsession - its weight, its screen, its silence
- Dialogue sparse but significant - the patient's question hangs in the air
- Time distortion: minutes feel like hours when waiting for news
- Physical symptoms of anxiety described in clinical terms (Elena knowing what her body is doing, unable to stop it)

## Connections

- **Parent (Chapter 36):** Develops the chapter's emotional core - shell shock compounded by uncertainty about loved ones
- **Siblings:** Follows Scene 1's medical focus with emotional stakes; sets up Scene 3's memory fragments and Scene 4's eventual connection
- **Part:** Contributes to Part 5's "relationship reconfiguration" theme by stressing Elena's family bonds
- **Book:** Elena's role as mother and wife, established throughout Book 2, now tested by physical and communicative separation
