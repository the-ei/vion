## Chapter 36: Shell Shock

### Prose Characteristics
- Opening sentence structure captures dissociation: time registered "somewhere in the part of her mind"
- Fluorescent light vs. dawn light: "intrusion" of world's "indifferent persistence"
- Glove snapping sound "now seemed to echo" - familiar made strange through trauma
- Hallway patients with paper triage numbers (1-37) - bureaucracy improvised under crisis
- Smell as thing she would remember: "antiseptic and sweat and staleness"

- Ventilation system "running but not enough, never enough" - system metaphor
- Fragment/memory structure in Scene 3 achieves Carson compression
