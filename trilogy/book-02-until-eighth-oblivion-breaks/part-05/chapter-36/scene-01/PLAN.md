# Scene Plan: First Light

## Summary

Elena arrives at the Phoenix clinic at dawn, having lost track of how long she has been awake. The aftermath of the crisis fills every corner of the space - patients on gurneys in hallways, supplies scattered, the exhaustion written on every face. She moves through this landscape with mechanical precision, treating a patient with diabetic complications while her mind cycles involuntarily through fragmented memories of the break. The scene establishes the overwhelming scope of aftermath through specific medical details, showing how trauma manifests in the body's inability to stop working even when the emergency has passed.

## Key Elements

- The clinic transformed: overflow patients, depleted supplies, exhausted staff
- Elena's dissociation - present physically, absent psychologically
- Specific medical details grounding the scene (diabetic ketoacidosis treatment)
- First fragments of crisis memory surfacing unbidden
- The strange quality of emergency mode without active emergency
- Dawn light as metaphor - brightness revealing damage
- Staff moving with the mechanical efficiency of the traumatized
- The body's refusal to register that it's over

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Sleep-deprived beyond measure, moving on autopilot, treating patients while her mind fragments
- **Diabetic patient (unnamed):** Elderly woman whose insulin access was disrupted during the crisis; requires careful management
- **Clinic staff:** Faces Elena recognizes but cannot name in her current state; colleagues operating on similar autopilot
- **Background patients:** The walking wounded of the crisis - dehydration, medication lapses, untreated injuries

## Setting

- **Location:** Phoenix community health center - the main treatment floor and triage area
- **Time:** Dawn, early morning, first day after the crisis resolution
- **Physical details:** Fluorescent lights humming, the smell of antiseptic mixed with unwashed bodies, makeshift pallets on the floor, the emergency generator's background thrum even though main power is restored
- **Atmosphere:** The particular silence of a place that was recently very loud; shellshocked quiet punctuated by medical equipment beeps

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Approximately 2 hours (6 AM to 8 AM)
- **Period:** Late November or early December 2036
- **Position in chapter:** Opening scene, establishing immediate aftermath register

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Heavy Knausgaard influence: exhaustive physical and medical detail, the mundane work of care elevated through attention
- Elena's internal state shown through what she notices and what she fails to notice
- Long sentences for the routine of treatment; fragmented intrusions when crisis memories surface
- Sensory details prioritized - the body's experience of aftermath
- Avoid sentimentality; the horror is in the ordinariness of continuing

## Connections

- **Parent (Chapter 36):** Establishes the chapter's opening register of shell shock through clinical specificity
- **Siblings:** Scene 2 will shift from medical work to the anguish of unreachable family; this scene must build that need through Elena's robotic focus
- **Part:** Opens Part 5's exploration of aftermath through Elena's ground-level perspective
- **Book:** Continues Elena's development from Part 4's crisis into Part 5's processing
