# Scene Plan: The Letter

## Summary

Elena writes her resignation letter. Not an impulsive act - she has thought about this for weeks. The letter goes through several drafts as she finds the right tone: grateful but honest, leaving the door open but not pretending she will return. Her supervisor will understand; they saw the same things. As she writes, she remembers specific patients, specific failures, specific moments when the system showed its face. The letter becomes elegy and manifesto, though it reads as nothing more than professional courtesy.

## Key Elements

- The resignation letter as deliberate, considered act
- Multiple drafts: finding the right tone
- Gratitude and honesty balanced: professional courtesy hiding deeper meaning
- Memories surfacing as she writes: patients, failures, systemic moments
- The letter as both elegy (for who she was) and manifesto (for who she will become)
- The supervisor who will understand: shared witness to the system's failures
- Not impulsive: this has been forming for weeks, since abuela's question
- The gap between what the letter says and what it means

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Writing her resignation, finding words, remembering patients, making the decision real through the act of writing
- **Patients (memory):** Specific faces, specific failures returning as she writes
- **Supervisor (mentioned):** The person who will receive the letter, who saw what Elena saw
- **The system (conceptual):** What Elena is resigning from, visible through her memories

## Setting

- **Location:** Phoenix - Elena and Daniel's apartment, where they have returned to pack
- **Time:** Early January 2037, morning
- **Physical details:** Elena at a table, laptop or paper (her choice), coffee, the apartment half-packed around her, morning light through windows
- **Atmosphere:** The particular focus of an important writing task; clarity emerging through revision

## Timeline

- **Duration:** Several hours of writing and revising
- **Period:** Early January 2037 (new year, new direction)
- **Position in chapter:** Opening scene, establishing Elena's decisive turn
