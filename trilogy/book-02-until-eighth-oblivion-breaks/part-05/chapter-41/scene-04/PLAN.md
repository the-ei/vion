# Scene Plan: The Meeting

## Summary

Elena attends a gathering organized by mutual aid networks that formed during the crisis. These are people who saw what she saw: the failure of institutions, the power of collective care, the possibility of something different. The meeting is chaotic, underfunded, full of disagreement - but alive in ways the clinic never was. Elena listens more than she speaks, but when she does speak, people listen. She has found a different kind of clinic, where health is understood as political and care as resistance. She volunteers to help organize the health committee.

## Key Elements

- The mutual aid gathering: crisis-forged networks continuing
- People who saw what Elena saw: shared understanding without explanation needed
- The meeting's chaos: underfunded, full of disagreement, alive
- Elena listening more than speaking: observing before committing
- When Elena speaks, people listen: her expertise valued differently here
- Health as political, care as resistance: the reframing Elena needed
- Volunteering for the health committee: the first concrete step
- The contrast with the clinic: different kind of aliveness

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Attending the meeting, finding her people, taking the first step toward new work
- **Mutual aid organizers:** A mix of crisis veterans, longtime activists, newly radicalized professionals
- **Health committee members:** The group Elena will join - nurses, community health workers, healthcare refugees like herself
- **The meeting facilitator:** Someone keeping the chaos productive

## Setting

- **Location:** A community space in Phoenix - perhaps a church basement or community center
- **Time:** Early January 2037, evening
- **Physical details:** Folding chairs in a circle, handmade signs, donated coffee, clipboards for sign-ups, the particular energy of people organizing
- **Atmosphere:** The chaotic vitality of grassroots organizing; different from professional settings Elena knows

## Timeline

- **Duration:** A 2-3 hour meeting
- **Period:** Early January 2037
- **Position in chapter:** Fourth scene, showing where Elena is going after leaving

## Stylistic Notes

- The meeting's chaos as vitality: disagreement productive rather than dysfunctional
- Elena's observational mode: taking in before acting

- The health committee as concrete next step: specific, actionable
- Contrast with clinic meetings: different purpose, different energy
- Hope without naivety: these people have seen the system fail, they are not optimistic but committed
- The specific politics of care: how healthcare becomes resistance
