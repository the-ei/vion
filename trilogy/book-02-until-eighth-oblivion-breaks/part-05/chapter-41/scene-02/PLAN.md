# Scene Plan: The Apartment

## Summary

Elena and Daniel pack up their Phoenix apartment together. Every object is a decision: keep, donate, throw away, put in storage. Their pre-crisis life feels like artifact - the optimistic kitchen equipment, the children's toys from stages already passed, the wedding photos from a different universe. Daniel finds her nursing school notes; Elena finds his early construction drawings. They are gentler with each other than they have been in months, united by shared purpose if not shared understanding.

## Key Elements

- Packing as sorting through a former life
- Every object a decision: keep, donate, throw away, storage
- The pre-crisis life as artifact: optimistic purchases now strange
- Kitchen equipment, children's toys, wedding photos: specific objects
- Daniel finding Elena's nursing notes: their old selves encountered
- Elena finding Daniel's construction drawings: reciprocal discovery
- Gentleness between them: united in purpose despite different understandings
- The physical work of dismantling one life to build another

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Packing up her former life with Daniel, finding artifacts, making decisions
- **Daniel:** Partner in the packing, gentle with Elena, also encountering his own past
- **Past selves (through objects):** Elena's nursing student, Daniel's young builder, the optimistic couple who bought this kitchen equipment

## Setting

- **Location:** Elena and Daniel's Phoenix apartment
- **Time:** Early January 2037, continuing from Scene 1
- **Physical details:** Boxes, packing tape, the apartment's rooms progressively emptied, specific objects held and considered, natural light changing through the day
- **Atmosphere:** The particular melancholy and release of packing up a life; intimacy of shared work

## Timeline

- **Duration:** A full day of packing
- **Period:** Early January 2037
- **Position in chapter:** Second scene, physical action following the written decision

- Bittersweet register: loss and liberation together
