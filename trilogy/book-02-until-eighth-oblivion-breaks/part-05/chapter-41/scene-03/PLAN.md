# Scene Plan: The Goodbye

## Summary

Elena goes to the clinic one final time. The building that was her second home for years, the colleagues who became family. Some understand why she is leaving; others are hurt or baffled. One colleague pulls her aside and whispers: "I wish I could do the same." Elena sees the system's hold on people - mortgages, children, inertia. She does not judge; she was there too. The goodbye is harder than she expected and easier than she feared. She leaves through the same door she has used thousands of times, and it is just a door now.

## Key Elements

- The clinic as second home: the building, the people, the routines
- Mixed reactions from colleagues: understanding, hurt, bafflement
- The whispered confession: "I wish I could do the same"
- Elena's understanding of the system's hold: mortgages, children, inertia
- No judgment: Elena was there too, and still would be without the crisis
- The goodbye's difficulty and ease: harder and easier than expected
- The door as symbol: what was significant becomes ordinary through leaving
- The finality of departure from a life's work

## Characters

- **Elena Varga (POV):** Saying goodbye to the clinic, receiving different responses, understanding more than she judges
- **Colleagues (various):** Dr. Reyes (understanding), Nurse Kaplan (hurt), others (baffled)
- **The whispering colleague:** Someone who sees what Elena sees but cannot act - mortgages, children, inertia hold them
- **The clinic (environmental):** The building, the routines, the patients still there

## Setting

- **Location:** Phoenix community health center - final visit
- **Time:** Early January 2037, same day or next day after packing
- **Physical details:** The building Elena has entered thousands of times, the treatment areas, the break room, the door she will leave through, her colleagues' faces
- **Atmosphere:** The particular weight of final goodbyes to a workplace; professional relationships becoming personal in departure

## Timeline

- **Duration:** 2-3 hours of goodbyes
- **Period:** Early January 2037
- **Position in chapter:** Third scene, the workplace goodbye after home departure

## Page Target

4 pages (~1,100 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- The building rendered through accumulated familiarity: what Elena knows without thinking
- Different colleagues' reactions shown through specific moments, not catalogued
- The whispered confession as the scene's emotional center
- Elena's non-judgment important: she understands why others stay
- The door at the end: specific, material, transformed by meaning
- Walking out should feel both momentous and ordinary
- Professional relationships given weight: these people mattered
