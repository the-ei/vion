# Scene Plan: The Empty Office

## Summary

Kevin Zhou goes to his startup's former office - now mostly empty, equipment removed for the emergency deployment that transformed his company into something else. The few remaining employees have scattered. He walks through the space where he spent most of his waking hours for years, seeing it as if for the first time: the expensive ergonomic chairs, the optimized snack stations, the screens that once displayed metrics he lived by. None of it means what it meant. The scene inventories what he built through the lens of its dissolution, a tour of a kingdom that no longer exists.

## Key Elements

- The empty office as archaeological site of Kevin's former life
- Specific details of startup culture rendered strange: ergonomic chairs, snack stations, standing desks
- The absence of people - colleagues scattered, co-founder departed
- Equipment gone to emergency deployment - his technology repurposed
- Kevin seeing familiar space as if for the first time
- The metrics screens dark or displaying irrelevant data
- The contrast between what this space represented and what it now is
- Kevin's identity as builder confronting the dissolution of what he built

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Walking through the empty office, confronting the gap between who he was and who he must become
- **Absent colleagues:** Their former desks, their personal items left behind, their departures
- **The co-founder (memory):** Present through absence - the desk they used, the conversations they had in this space
- **A remaining employee (brief):** Someone picking up personal items, awkward exchange with Kevin

## Setting

- **Location:** The startup office in San Francisco - open plan, tech aesthetic
- **Time:** Mid-December 2036, daytime
- **Physical details:** Empty desks, abandoned equipment, the particular desolation of a space designed for collaboration now empty, natural light through floor-to-ceiling windows, a whiteboard with plans now obsolete
- **Atmosphere:** The particular melancholy of abandoned workspaces; optimism curdled into emptiness

## Timeline

- **Duration:** 1-2 hours of wandering the space
- **Period:** Mid-December 2036, second week after crisis
- **Position in chapter:** Opening scene, establishing Kevin's confrontation with what his work became

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- Knausgaard's exhaustive inventory: every object in the office becomes significant
- The startup aesthetics rendered as artifacts of a lost civilization
- Kevin's internal state shown through what he notices
- Minimal dialogue - the conversation with the remaining employee awkward and brief
- The space itself as character: what the office reveals about what Kevin believed
- Irony without heavy-handedness: the optimization obsession now absurd
