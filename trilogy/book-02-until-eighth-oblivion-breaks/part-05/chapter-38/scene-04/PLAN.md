# Scene Plan: The Call from Ananya

## Summary

Ananya Ramaswamy calls - a surprise, given their history of professional tension on the industry safety board. But the tension has been made trivial by what they have both been through. She asks how he is, and Kevin does not know how to answer. They talk about the industry's response, the regulatory changes coming, the reshuffling of power. It is the first real conversation Kevin has had in days. Ananya mentions that the ethics board they both served on is reconstituting. Kevin does not know if he wants anything to do with it. The conversation ends without resolution, but something has shifted.

## Key Elements

- The unexpected call from a professional rival turned... something else
- Ananya and Kevin's history: tension on the safety board, different approaches
- The crisis having rendered their rivalry trivial
- First genuine human conversation Kevin has had since the break
- Industry discussion: regulatory changes, power shifts, what comes next
- The ethics board reconstituting - Kevin's ambivalence about participation
- The awkward intimacy of former rivals checking on each other
- Something shifting but not named

## Characters

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Receiving a call he did not expect, having a conversation he did not know he needed
- **Ananya Ramaswamy (voice):** Professional rival who has also been through the crisis; her checking in is surprising but genuine
- **The industry (discussed):** The changes happening in tech, regulation, power structures

## Setting

- **Location:** Kevin's apartment - daytime the next day
- **Time:** Morning, the day after Scene 3
- **Physical details:** Kevin's phone, the apartment's silence broken by conversation, weak winter light through windows
- **Atmosphere:** The particular surprise of an unexpected connection; guards lowered by exhaustion

## Timeline

- **Duration:** A 20-30 minute phone call
- **Period:** Mid-December 2036, second week after crisis
- **Position in chapter:** Fourth scene, introducing human connection after isolation

- The industry talk should feel both distant and immediate - big changes they both feel
- Kevin's inability to answer "how are you?" honestly - the question revealing his state
- The ethics board question left open - Kevin's ambivalence genuine
- Warmth emerging through professional conversation
