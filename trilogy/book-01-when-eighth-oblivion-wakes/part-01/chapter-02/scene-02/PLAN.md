# Scene Plan: The Inheritance

## Summary

Evening in the Washington household. Jerome works on his newsletter at the kitchen table while DeShawn, 17, home from school, codes at the other end. Their physical proximity is also distance - same room, different screens, different worlds. Jerome attempts to engage his son with the housing discrimination story he's writing: algorithmic pricing that perpetuates redlining, AI systems that encode historical bias. DeShawn's responses are polite but carry the particular condescension of youth: Dad is fighting yesterday's battles; the patterns he describes are being fixed; technology is the solution, not the problem.

Jerome looks at what DeShawn is building - a machine learning tool for prediction markets, something about aggregating signals - and feels the vertigo of not understanding what his child creates. The generational divide is technical and philosophical. Denise arrives home, exhausted from teaching; the three orbit each other in the kitchen, conversation skimming surfaces. When DeShawn mentions a summer mentorship opportunity at a tech company - the application deadline approaching - Jerome's questions come out wrong. His concern sounds like criticism; his warnings sound like fear. The conversation ends badly, DeShawn retreating to his room, the distance greater than before.

## Key Elements

- The kitchen table as shared but divided space
- The housing discrimination story: substantive, important, struggling for audience
- DeShawn's project: technically sophisticated, ethically unexamined
- The generational divide: Jerome sees threat where DeShawn sees opportunity
- Denise's arrival: the exhaustion of teaching in 2032, the weight she carries
- The mentorship opportunity: the first mention of what will become the Prometheus thread
- The failed conversation: good intentions, poor delivery, the door closing

## Characters Present

- **Jerome Washington** (POV): Father trying to connect, journalist seeing patterns, both failing
- **DeShawn Washington** (17): Son building the future, dismissing the past, sharp and partially right
- **Denise Washington**: Wife and mother arriving into tension, mediating without energy

## Setting

**Location:** The Washington family home in Baltimore - kitchen and adjacent spaces

**Time of Day:** 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM, weekday evening

**Physical Details:**
- Kitchen table large enough for family dinners, now a shared workspace
- Jerome's laptop open to his newsletter draft
- DeShawn's setup: gaming laptop, external monitor, the tools of his world
- The sounds of Denise arriving: keys, door, the exhalation of someone off their feet
- Dinner half-prepared, the domestic logistics competing with other tensions
- DeShawn's closed door after the argument, the silence it creates

## Timeline

**Relationship to chapter:** The central family scene; establishes the domestic tension that will run through Jerome's arc

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard - the weight of family, the specificity of domestic tension

**Sentence rhythm:** Varies with mood - flowing during the attempt at connection, choppy during the argument

**Dialogue approach:** The conversation that happens and the one that doesn't; what Jerome means versus what he says

**Key technique:** The generational gap made specific through technical language DeShawn uses that Jerome doesn't fully understand

**Emotional register:** Frustration, love, incomprehension - all coexisting without resolution
