# Scene Plan: The Frequency

## Summary

Saturday family dinner - an effort to mark the weekend, to be a family in the ways they remember. Denise has cooked; the table is set; there's intention behind the gathering. Conversation attempts connection: Denise's students and the particular stresses of teaching history in 2032, Jerome's investigation into housing discrimination, DeShawn's college applications. The meal is almost working when the tech mentorship surfaces again.

The summer program is at Prometheus Systems in San Francisco - significant, competitive, the kind of opportunity that launches careers. DeShawn has advanced to the interview stage. Jerome's objections are principled - the company's role in surveillance capitalism, the ethics of working for systems he's spent years critiquing - but delivered badly. DeShawn's defense of the tech industry sounds like all the talking points Jerome has spent years debunking: innovation, opportunity, the promise of change from within. Denise mediates, but the meal ends early, everyone retreating to screens.

Later, in bed, Denise asleep beside him, Jerome's phone buzzes: a message from an old source, a journalist he knew in DC who now writes for a tech publication. Brief: "Something happening at Prometheus. Interested?" Jerome types back "Tell me more." The cursor blinks. No reply yet. But the thread is open. The chapter ends with Jerome in the dark, his family sleeping around him, the investigation that will consume the next years of his life just beginning.

## Key Elements

- The Saturday dinner: the effort to be family, the rituals that hold and don't hold
- Denise's teaching: the exhaustion of the work, the students more anxious each year
- DeShawn's interview: Prometheus Systems named, the stakes made clear
- Jerome's objections: principled, poorly delivered, pushing his son away
- The argument dynamics: DeShawn's rebuttals are sharp, partially right
- The retreat to screens: family dinner ending in isolation
- The late-night message: the source, the opportunity, the thread opening

## Characters Present

- **Jerome Washington** (POV): Father losing an argument, journalist gaining a story
- **Denise Washington**: Wife mediating, teacher exhausted, person with her own weight
- **DeShawn Washington**: Son defending his future, son fighting his father
- **The source** (via text): The messenger who changes everything

## Setting

**Location:** Washington family home in Baltimore - dining room, then bedroom

**Time of Day:** 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM, Saturday

**Physical Details:**
- The dining room table set with intention: cloth napkins, good dishes
- The meal Denise prepared: Jerome notices the effort, feels the pressure
- The silence after the argument, the sounds of doors closing
- The bedroom in darkness, Denise's breathing, Jerome's phone glowing
- The city outside the windows, quiet in a way Baltimore rarely is

## Timeline

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard for the dinner - the weight of family ritual; shifting toward something more urgent when the message arrives

**Sentence rhythm:** The dinner conversation with its stops and starts; the quiet intensity of the late-night texting

**Dialogue approach:** The argument as it happens - no one wins, but the damage is real

**Key technique:** Prometheus named for the first time in Jerome's narrative; the family story and the investigation story begin their convergence

**Closing register:** The chapter ends not with resolution but with opening - a door closing on family, a door opening on story
