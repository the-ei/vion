# Scene Plan: The Encounter

## Summary

Sunday night. Kevin Zhou returns to Prometheus to work - the weekend office, quieter, populated only by the truly devoted or the truly lonely. He needs to be productive; the exclusion from the strategy meeting still stings, and productivity is how he processes emotion. The campus at night has a different quality: security lights, empty corridors, the hum of servers that never sleep.

Leaving late, in the parking garage, he encounters Ananya doing the same. They know each other only by reputation - the engineer and the ethics officer occupy different universes within the same company. But something sparks in this chance meeting. She mentions the Clarity ethics review, using language that sounds like criticism. Kevin Zhou's defense is too quick, too sharp - "Ethics theater," he says, or something close to it. She doesn't back down. The exchange is brief but charged: she questions what he builds; he questions her right to question. They go to their respective cars. Kevin Zhou sits in his for a long moment before driving away, replaying the conversation, composing better responses he didn't deliver. The chapter ends with him at home, the encounter circling his mind, a disturbance in his clean architecture.

## Key Elements

- The weekend office: sanctuary for those who find home less comfortable
- Kevin Zhou's work session: productivity as emotional management
- The parking garage encounter: chance meeting, charged exchange
- Ananya's critique: the ethics review, her questions, her standing
- Kevin Zhou's defense: "ethics theater," the dismissal that reveals
- The brief argument: neither wins, both are affected
- The drive home: the replayed conversation, the better comebacks
- The disturbance: a crack in Kevin Zhou's defended self

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard for the office work and the aftermath; the encounter itself tighter, more present-tense in energy

**Sentence rhythm:** The encounter in quick exchanges; the aftermath in longer, recursive sentences as Kevin Zhou replays

**Key technique:** The brief argument as revelation - what Kevin Zhou says and what it exposes about his defended position

**Dialogue approach:** Direct, clipped, defensive on both sides; the conversation that ends too soon

**Closing register:** Not resolution but opening - a relationship established through friction
