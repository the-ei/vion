# Scene Plan: The Room

## Summary

Prometheus Systems campus during the workday. Kevin Zhou learns he's been excluded from a strategy meeting about Clarity's deployment parameters - a meeting he expected to attend, prepared for, assumed was his by right. His manager's explanation is plausible (scheduling conflict, overlapping commitments, he'll be briefed later) but insufficient. Kevin Zhou's reaction - controlled publicly, roiling privately - reveals how much his self-worth depends on access, on being considered essential. The architecture of his identity requires being in the room where decisions happen.

The scene then shifts to evening: a video call with his parents in Shenzhen. The connection is poor - whether for technical reasons or political ones remains ambiguous. The conversation is careful, navigating around topics that might draw attention. His mother mentions that a colleague's son was questioned about overseas contacts; the implication sits between them. Kevin Zhou's life in America has become impossible to explain to people who see only the distance, the absence, what he's traded. His father asks about work in a way that suggests caution about being overheard. The call is fifteen minutes of managed silence, love expressed through what isn't said.

## Key Elements

- The excluded meeting: Kevin Zhou's discovery, his manager's explanation, his contained reaction
- The gap between technical authority and strategic voice
- The colleagues who were included: what they have that he lacks
- The wound to identity: being essential to the code but not to the decisions
- The video call with parents: the poor connection, the careful words
- Political tension made personal: the colleague's son, the implication
- The distance that work was supposed to bridge, now widened

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou** (POV): Engineer excluded, son failing to connect
- **His manager** (in office scene): The bearer of plausible explanations
- **Parents** (via video): Mother's worry, father's caution, the love beneath
- **Colleagues** (background): The ones who were in the room

## Setting

**Location:** Prometheus Systems campus (San Francisco), then Kevin Zhou's apartment

**Time of Day:** 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (office), 7:00 PM - 8:00 PM (video call)

**Physical Details:**
- The Prometheus campus: open floor plans, glass walls, the designed transparency
- Kevin Zhou's workspace: surrounded by peers, isolated within the crowd
- The conference room where the meeting happened: visible, unreachable
- The apartment later: the video call setup, the small screen, the lag in the connection
- His parents' apartment visible behind them: the furniture he remembers, changed

## Timeline

**Duration within chapter:** Approximately 5 hours at office, 1 hour evening video call

**Relationship to chapter:** The central scene; establishes Kevin Zhou's professional insecurity and family disconnection

## Page Target

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard for the office scenes - the texture of workplace dynamics; shifting register for the video call

**Sentence rhythm:** Clipped during the professional scenes (Kevin Zhou's controlled affect); more halting during the video call (the gaps in communication)

**Key technique:** The excluded meeting as wound to identity; the video call as parallel exclusion - two kinds of rooms Kevin Zhou can't enter

**Emotional register:** Shame managed through competence; grief managed through distance

**Dialogue approach:** The manager's explanation heard through Kevin Zhou's filter; the parents' conversation as subtext
