# Scene Plan: The Frame

## Summary

Delphine Okafor-Barnes on set in downtown Los Angeles, directing a video for the "HealthBridge Initiative" - a tech-enabled healthcare access program. The production is substantial: multiple cameras, a lighting crew, locations ranging from a community clinic to the home of a participating family. The imagery is beautiful, composed with Delphine's practiced eye: diverse faces, warm lighting, the visual grammar of hope and access and dignity.

Between setups, her mind performs its familiar calculations: who funded this initiative, what they want from this content, how this footage will be deployed. She knows HealthBridge is real, does real good, helps real people access real healthcare. She also knows it's a branding exercise for interests she hasn't fully traced - foundations connected to pharmaceutical companies, tech firms looking to enter healthcare markets, the usual tangle of mission and money. Her creative team is talented and similarly unexamining; they focus on making it good, leaving "it" undefined.

A moment of genuine connection surfaces during the shoot: one of the patients being filmed, an older woman who received diabetes care through the program, shares something real about what the access has meant to her life. Delphine's instinct is to capture it, frame it, use it - and then she recognizes that instinct as predatory. The scene ends with wrap, the collective exhalation of a shoot completed, the beautiful footage safely captured for purposes yet to be determined.

## Key Elements

- The production set as space of beautiful falseness: real people, real stories, unreal framing
- Delphine's directorial skill: she makes good content, and this is the problem
- The HealthBridge Initiative: genuine program, unclear purposes, the usual ambiguity
- The creative team: colleagues she likes, complicit in the same ways
- The patient's genuine moment: real emotion Delphine's trained to capture and use
- The recognition of predatory instinct: the self-awareness that doesn't change behavior
- Wrap: the satisfaction of craft, the question of what it serves

## Characters Present
