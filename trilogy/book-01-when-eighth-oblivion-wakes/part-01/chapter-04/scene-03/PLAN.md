# Scene Plan: The Residue

## Summary

Theo asleep, Jessie on her laptop in bed working on script pages, Delphine preparing for her weekly video call with her mother in London. The time difference is awkward - always too late for one of them, too early for the other. The call connects: her mother's face on the screen, the Brixton apartment Delphine knows by heart, the visible absence of her father who died two years ago. His chair is still there. No one sits in it.

The conversation touches on surface things: her mother's social calendar, neighborhood changes in Brixton, updates on cousins and old friends. Beneath the small talk: loneliness, grief managed but not resolved, the particular ache of a daughter far away. After the call, Delphine can't sleep. She opens her work laptop, not because anything is due but because she wants to see what she made today. The HealthBridge footage is good. She watches her own work with a director's eye and sees the craft and the problem. Late, very late, an email arrives: a contact at Prometheus Systems, corporate tone but enthusiastic, something about an upcoming product announcement. Would Delphine's company be interested in the content contract?

The email is vague - mentions Clarity, mentions transformative technology, mentions significant budget. Delphine bookmarks it for Monday. Closes the laptop. Lies in the dark beside sleeping Jessie, the city humming outside, her thoughts refusing to settle. The chapter ends here: a professional opportunity that is also, she senses without knowing why, something else.

## Key Elements

- The video call preparation: the rituals of maintained connection across distance
- Her mother's face on screen: aging, lonely, resilient
- The father's absence: the empty chair, the grief that doesn't lift
- The conversation that's smaller than the feelings: surfaces covering depths
- The editing session afterward: Delphine watching her own work
- The recognition of craft and complicity: the footage is good, which is the problem
- The late-night email: Prometheus, Clarity, an opportunity
- The sleepless ending: thoughts that won't quiet, the city outside

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Shifting toward Carson during the mother's call - the fragmentary weight of grief; returning to Knausgaard for the editing session; something more uncertain at the close

**Sentence rhythm:** The video call in shorter, more halting rhythms (the gaps in transatlantic conversation); the editing in professional flow; the ending in the stuttered rhythm of a mind that can't rest

**Key technique:** The father's absence made visible through detail - the chair, the mention of his garden, the things that have changed

**Emotional register:** Grief managed, distance maintained, sleep refused

**Closing register:** Not resolution but suspension; the email as intrusion, the sleeplessness as response
