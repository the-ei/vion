# Scene Plan: Before the Surface

## Summary

Ananya wakes at 6:30 AM in her Palo Alto house - a space designed for a family that no longer lives there together. The scene follows her morning ritual with meticulous attention: the precise choreography of coffee-making, the shower where she rehearses talking points for the day's meetings, the moment of checking her phone and steeling herself against the day's communications. During her commute to Prometheus headquarters through early Silicon Valley traffic, her thoughts drift between registers - her parents' immigration story, Priya's text from last night expressing school stress, the ethics review meeting she's about to navigate.

The scene establishes Ananya's consciousness as the novel's first territory: a mind trained to optimize, now applying that training to the management of doubt. The AI assistant in her car suggests an alternate route; she takes it without thinking about what patterns it has learned from her. This small detail plants the surveillance-as-infrastructure theme without emphasis.

## Key Elements

- The too-large house as physical manifestation of loss and solitude
- Morning rituals as meditation and armor-building
- The weight of the phone - checking it as both compulsion and dread
- Immigration history present in details: the coffee her mother taught her to make, the morning mantras
- Priya's text from last night: school stress rendered in teenage syntax
- The commute as liminal space between private and professional selves
- AI assistant's route suggestion as unremarkable convenience (for now)

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (POV): The ethics officer preparing for a day of managed compromise
- **Priya** (via text message): The fourteen-year-old daughter whose anxiety reaches across the custody divide
- **Parents** (in memory): The immigration story that shadows Ananya's American success

## Setting

**Location:** Ananya's Palo Alto home, then her car commuting to Prometheus Systems HQ in San Francisco

**Time of Day:** 6:30 AM - 8:15 AM

**Physical Details:**
- A four-bedroom house with two bedrooms unused
- Kitchen with high-end appliances, the espresso machine her one indulgence
- Master bathroom with dual sinks, one perpetually dry
- Tesla in the garage, the car choosing routes she doesn't question
- Highway 101 traffic, the familiar crawl toward San Francisco

## Timeline

**Duration within chapter:** Approximately 1.75 hours of narrative time

**Relationship to chapter:** Opens the chapter and establishes Ananya's baseline state before the day's challenges

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Primarily Knausgaard - immersive, flowing, interested in the texture of consciousness

**Sentence rhythm:** Long, recursive sentences that mirror the circling of a mind preparing itself for performance

**Sensory emphasis:** Morning light through kitchen windows, the smell of coffee, the feel of hot water in the shower, the synthetic voice of the car's AI

**Interiority level:** Deep - we are inside Ananya's head, experiencing her thoughts as they form and reform

**Key technique:** The mundane elevated to profound through sustained attention; coffee-making becomes meditation on diaspora, commuting becomes meditation on complicity
