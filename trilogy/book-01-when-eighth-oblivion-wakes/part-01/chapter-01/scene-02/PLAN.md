# Scene Plan: The Permission Structure

## Summary

The ethics review meeting at Prometheus headquarters. Ananya presents her assessment of a new "personalization" feature - a system designed to tailor content delivery based on aggregated user data. Her concerns are substantive: the data collection exceeds user expectations, the aggregation enables predictions users haven't consented to, the "personalization" language obscures surveillance. She articulates these concerns clearly, professionally, with evidence.

The meeting demonstrates how institutional power absorbs critique. Her colleagues listen respectfully, ask questions, express appreciation for her thoroughness. Then they work around her objections through semantic repositioning: "surveillance" becomes "user modeling," "prediction" becomes "anticipation," "data harvesting" becomes "experience enhancement." A small victory emerges - a two-week delay for additional review. A larger defeat is implicit: the feature will launch regardless; the delay creates the appearance of process without changing the outcome. After the meeting, a conversation with her assistant confirms what everyone knows: the outcome was predetermined; Ananya's role is to make the process feel rigorous.

## Key Elements

- The meeting room as designed space: open, glass-walled, performing transparency while enabling surveillance
- Ananya's presentation: clear, evidence-based, professionally delivered
- The semantic repositioning: watching language transform objections into approvals
- Colleagues as individuals, not villains - people she respects performing the alchemy of corporate ethics
- The small victory (two-week delay) as both achievement and defeat
- Post-meeting conversation revealing the performative nature of her role
- The gap between access and power made visible

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (POV): Delivering her assessment, watching it be absorbed
- **Ethics Review Board members** (4-5 colleagues): Named individuals with positions, not a faceless group
  - The general counsel who finds legal pathways around ethical objections
  - The VP of Product who genuinely believes in user benefit
  - The data science lead who speaks in statistics
  - The communications director who handles framing
- **Ananya's assistant** (brief): The after-meeting reality check

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard with moments of sharp observation - the meeting as theater observed from within

**Sentence rhythm:** Shifts between flowing internal commentary and shorter, more clipped observations of the meeting's dynamics

**Dialogue approach:** Actual meeting dialogue, but filtered through Ananya's consciousness - we hear what people say and what she hears beneath it

**Key technique:** The gap between official language and actual meaning; the reader learns to decode corporate speech with Ananya as guide

**Tension source:** The tension is not dramatic confrontation but the slow recognition that the system is working exactly as designed
