# Scene Plan: Co-Parenting in the Attention Economy

## Summary

Lunch with James, Ananya's ex-husband, at a restaurant chosen for its geographic neutrality - equidistant from their workplaces, a manifestation of divorce logistics. The conversation centers on Priya: her anxiety about school, her struggling grades, the question of whether she needs more support or less pressure. The exchange is civil, careful, marked by the particular tension of two people who once knew each other completely and now navigate around areas they've agreed not to touch.

James's concern is genuine, but his implication is clear: Ananya's work schedule doesn't help Priya's anxiety. Ananya's suppressed response - that James's venture capital firm funds companies like Prometheus - remains unspoken. A moment of genuine connection emerges when they both acknowledge their worry for their daughter, the shared love that survives the divorce. Then the retreat into logistics: schedules, appointments, the administrative infrastructure of separated parenting. Texts from Priya arrive during Ananya's drive back to Prometheus: school drama, a meme, nothing about the anxiety they've been discussing. The gap between parental worry and teenage communication.

## Key Elements

- The restaurant as neutral territory - neither's neighborhood, designed for business lunches
- The choreography of civil divorce: who arrives first, how they greet, the careful maintenance of boundary
- Priya as absent center: discussed but not present, her voice arriving only through texts
- James's genuine concern and his implicit criticism
- Ananya's defensive awareness of hypocrisy on both sides
- The moment of shared love that cuts through the tension
- The logistics of co-parenting: calendars, therapist recommendations, school meetings
- Priya's texts as counterpoint: the teenage reality that doesn't match parental narrative

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy** (POV): Navigating ex-husband, parental guilt, professional defensiveness
- **James** (Ananya's ex-husband): Venture capitalist, concerned father, wielder of subtle critique
- **Priya** (via text): The absent presence, communicating in memes and fragments

## Setting

**Location:** An upscale restaurant in Redwood City - equidistant from Palo Alto and San Francisco

**Time of Day:** 12:30 PM - 1:45 PM

**Physical Details:**
- Natural light through large windows
- The table James has requested - not too intimate, not too exposed
- Their phones on the table, screens occasionally lighting with notifications
- The food neither of them fully tastes, ordered by habit
- Ananya's car afterward, the drive back to Prometheus

## Timeline

**Duration within chapter:** Approximately 1.25 hours, plus 30-minute drive

**Relationship to chapter:** The domestic/personal counterweight to the professional scene; shows a different arena where Ananya manages compromise

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard - the intimate textures of family even after family has restructured

**Sentence rhythm:** More compact during dialogue, expanding during Ananya's internal processing

**Dialogue approach:** The conversation that happens and the conversation underneath; subtext as language

**Key technique:** The wound beneath the civility; the love that coexists with the hurt

**Emotional register:** Melancholy without melodrama; two people who tried and failed, now trying to succeed at something smaller
