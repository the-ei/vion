# Scene Plan: Convergence

## Summary

Back in Baltimore. The trip has changed something - Jerome is softer, sadder, more present. He approaches DeShawn differently: not to argue but to understand. "I want to understand what you're building," he says, meaning it for the first time. DeShawn's surprise is visible - this is not the father of their last conversation. The tentative opening leads to them sitting together at DeShawn's computer, Jerome asking questions instead of raising objections.

DeShawn's project is sophisticated: a tool for aggregating prediction market signals, using machine learning to identify consensus before it forms. As he explains, Jerome hears echoes of what he's learning about Prometheus - the same logic of prediction and modeling, the same faith that data can reveal hidden patterns. But he doesn't argue. He listens. For the first time, he sees his son's skill not as betrayal but as inheritance: the analytical mind, the drive to understand systems, the belief that tools can improve the world. The moment isn't reconciliation - the underlying tensions remain - but it's truce. A door that was closing has paused.

Simultaneously, the investigation advances. Ananya has sent documents through the secure channel. The picture emerging is both worse and more complicated than Jerome expected: Prometheus's AI isn't just surveillance, it's prediction and modeling at a scale that exceeds public disclosure. He doesn't know what to do with this yet. The chapter ends with parallel screens: Jerome reading the documents late at night, DeShawn in his room preparing for the interview that might take him to the company his father is investigating. Neither knows what the other is doing. The house is full of secrets.
