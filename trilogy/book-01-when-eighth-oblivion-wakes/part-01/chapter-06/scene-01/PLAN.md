# Scene Plan: First Contact

## Summary

Jerome receives Ananya's message through secure channels. The scene follows his vetting process: Is this real? Is it a setup? Is the source who they claim to be? He researches her - Ananya Ramaswamy, Chief Ethics Officer at Prometheus Systems, Stanford PhD, published papers, public statements, a position that would give access to exactly what she's hinting at. The credentials check out. The access is plausible. The risk is real.

He responds carefully, establishing protocols: separate encrypted channels, voice calls on secure lines, no real names in writing. The exchange is brief, professional, the first moves in a relationship that will define the next years of their lives. After the source communication, Jerome begins the broader investigation: What is Prometheus working on? What are the industry rumors? He reaches out to other contacts, floats questions without revealing what he's learning, builds the infrastructure of a story without committing to its shape.

Late at night, Denise asks what he's working on. He's vague - not from secrecy protocols (though those matter) but from superstition. If he names this too early, it might vanish. He's been here before: the story that seemed real until it wasn't, the source that went silent, the investigation that collapsed. He protects this one by not claiming it.
