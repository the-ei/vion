# Scene Plan: Two Renewals

## Summary

Domestic evening, centered on Jessie's news. Her TV show has been renewed for another season, but with compromises the writing staff resisted: a new showrunner brought in by the network, plot directions that transform a character Jessie helped create, changes that feel like betrayals of the show's original vision. The renewal is good news - steady income, continued employment in an uncertain industry - and also something else.

Evening conversation as the couple navigates this: Jessie venting, then defending the decision, then acknowledging the same contradictions Delphine lives daily. "We tell ourselves stories about what we're making," Jessie says. "I don't know if the stories help or hurt." They're mirror images, both skilled at work they're not certain serves good ends - Delphine making content that shapes perception, Jessie writing narratives that the network shapes for ratings.

Meanwhile, Theo has started preschool full-time. The house is emptier during the day; the rhythms have changed. Both parents notice the shift - the relief of childcare coverage, the grief of the child needing them less. The scene traces the evening routines with this new shape: dinner together, a show they watch with Theo, the bedtime rituals that are his to do but also theirs. The domestic as ground beneath the professional; the family as anchor.
