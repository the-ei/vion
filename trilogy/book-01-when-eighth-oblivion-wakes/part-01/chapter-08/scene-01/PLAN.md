# Scene Plan: The Pitch

## Summary

Delphine's company pitches for the Prometheus content contract. The meeting takes place at Prometheus Systems headquarters - the same campus Ananya and Kevin Zhou navigate daily, seen now through visitor eyes. The architecture that feels like home to insiders reads differently to Delphine: the glass walls that perform transparency, the open spaces that enable surveillance, the designed casualness that is anything but casual.

Delphine presents the creative vision: Clarity as a tool for self-understanding, technology as mirror rather than manipulation, the user as beneficiary rather than product. Her pitch is beautiful - she's good at this - and she watches the Prometheus marketing team respond to her language, her visuals, her framing. The pitch is good because it makes something powerful feel helpful, something invasive feel empowering. This is exactly what they want. The contract is won.

After the meeting, celebration at her company's office - the victory mood, the congratulations. Delphine performs enthusiasm while privately researching Clarity more deeply. The press releases are optimistic; the technical documentation is vague; the implications aren't spelled out. She's agreed to make content for something she doesn't fully understand. The scene ends with her alone in her office, scrolling through search results, the assignment won, the questions beginning.

## Key Elements

- The Prometheus campus through visitor eyes: different from insider perspective
- The pitch meeting: Delphine presenting, performing, succeeding
- The creative vision: making powerful technology feel friendly
- The marketing team's response: exactly what they wanted
- The victory: contract won, congratulations circulating
- Delphine's private research: press releases, documentation, gaps
- The questions beginning: what is she actually selling?
