# Scene Plan: The Mirror

## Summary

Back in LA, deep in the Prometheus creative work. The London trip has created distance - Delphine sees her professional life differently after touching her earlier self. She builds the Clarity campaign: scripts, visual treatments, the grammar of aspiration she knows so well. The work is good. She's good at it. The problem is what it's for.

The scene follows her through the creative development, showing her craft at work: the choices about framing, the language that positions Clarity as empowering rather than invasive, the visual vocabulary of trust and self-understanding. As she works, she goes deeper into the materials Prometheus has provided - the technical documentation, the use cases, the fuller picture of what Clarity does. The system doesn't just help users understand themselves; it models behavior at a scale that makes "help" a complicated word. The gap between what she's making and what she's selling opens wider.

Late night, Theo asleep, Jessie in bed, Delphine alone with her laptop. She reviews the campaign concept she's developed - beautiful, effective, designed to make something very powerful feel very friendly - and sees her own fingerprints on a machine she doesn't trust. Part 1 ends here: four lives, four levels of complicity, the surface tension stretched to the point just before breaking.
