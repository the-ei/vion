# Scene Plan: Parameters

## Summary

Kevin Zhou at work in the days before a major presentation. Clarity has achieved a development milestone: the system's behavioral predictions are testing at accuracy rates that seemed theoretical months ago. The scene follows Kevin Zhou through the preparation - reviewing results, optimizing the demonstration, rehearsing the presentation to senior leadership. The work is consuming in the way that serves as Kevin Zhou's refuge: barely sleeping, eating at his desk, existing entirely within the problem.

The scene establishes what Clarity actually does, in enough technical detail to feel credible: the system builds models of individual decision-making by aggregating behavioral data, then uses those models to predict future choices with increasing accuracy. The applications presented to leadership are framed as "user empowerment" - helping people understand their own patterns. The applications Kevin Zhou glimpses in the broader roadmap are more complex.

Brief interactions with colleagues establish his position: respected for technical brilliance, slightly feared for his intensity, not quite liked. An email thread about the ethics review process appears in his inbox; he deletes it without reading. He knows Ananya and her team are asking questions. He dismisses them as "ethics theater" without examining the dismissal. The scene ends with the presentation looming, Kevin Zhou alone with his code, the system he's built more capable than he's fully reckoned with.

## Key Elements

- The milestone: Clarity's prediction accuracy exceeding expectations
- The preparation: reviewing results, optimizing demonstrations
- Kevin Zhou's work mode: consuming, isolating, total
- What Clarity does: technical explanation that serves the reader without condescending
- The applications presented vs. the applications possible: the gap
- Colleague interactions: respected, feared, not liked
- The ethics review email: deleted without reading
- The presentation looming: alone with the code
