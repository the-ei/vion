# Scene Plan: Access

## Summary

The presentation to Prometheus senior leadership. Kevin Zhou is nervous but prepared; the nervousness becomes fuel. He demonstrates Clarity's capabilities with the precision of someone who built every component: the prediction engine, the decision modeling, the applications for "user empowerment." The room's response is electric - not just approval but excitement, the recognition that this is significant. Questions from executives reveal engagement: How scalable? What's the timeline? How do we position this?

After the formal presentation, Kevin Zhou is invited to stay for the strategy discussion - the meeting he was excluded from in Chapter 3. He's in the room now, finally, the technical authority recognized with strategic voice. What he sees there: the gap between what Prometheus says publicly and plans privately. The language of optimization applied to human behavior at a scale that should give him pause. The discussion of market applications, government contracts, the monetization of prediction. He should be disturbed. He's mostly proud. The validation is what he's wanted; the content of that validation goes unexamined.

The meeting ends; congratulations circulate; Kevin Zhou floats home on something like happiness. But something nags - a question from an executive about "edge cases," about what happens when predictions shape the behavior they predict. Kevin Zhou answered smoothly. The question echoes anyway.

- The room's response: electric, validating

- Kevin Zhou's response: pride drowning disturbance

- The nagging question: edge cases, predictions shaping behavior
