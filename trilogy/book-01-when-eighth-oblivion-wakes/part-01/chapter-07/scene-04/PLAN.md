# Scene Plan: Recognition

## Summary

Late night. Sara has gone home after the second date; they've made plans for a third. Kevin Zhou should sleep - the social energy has been unusual, the connection genuine - but something from the strategy meeting is nagging at him. The question about edge cases. The discussion of applications he participated in without examining.

He goes to his home computer, opens Clarity's development environment. His new access level lets him see more than before - the full system, not just his component. He starts exploring, reading code and documentation he hasn't seen until now. What he finds: the prediction capabilities being developed for population-level application. The "user empowerment" framing is accurate at the individual level - users can understand their patterns - but it's incomplete. The same models that help individuals understand themselves can model demographics, predict collective behaviors, anticipate responses to interventions. The system can predict not just what individuals will choose but how populations will respond to specific stimuli.

Kevin Zhou stares at his code - his elegant, efficient code - and sees it as if for the first time. The architecture is still beautiful. What it enables is something else. He doesn't know what to do with this. He closes the laptop without making notes, without taking action. Lies in bed, unable to sleep. The ceiling offers no answers. The chapter ends with him in the dark, the question of what he's built finally forcing its way into consciousness, the clean architecture cracked.
