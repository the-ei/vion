# Scene Plan: Arrival

## Summary

Friday evening. Priya arrives for the custody weekend, delivered by James with the minimal interaction that has become their routine. The scene traces the rituals of transition: bag unpacking, the specific foods Ananya has stocked (the yogurt Priya likes, the chips she'll pretend are contraband), the mutual adjustment from separate lives to shared space. Two weeks have passed since Chapter 1; in that time, Ananya has received more messages from the anonymous source, each more specific, each harder to ignore.

Priya is tired, distracted, communicating in fragments - the particular exhaustion of being fourteen, of shuttling between homes, of carrying anxieties she can't fully name. They share a dinner that's more parallel than connected: Priya on her phone, Ananya performing presence while her mind returns to the messages. After dinner, Priya retreats to her room; Ananya, alone in the house's quiet, opens her secure email.

The scene ends with Ananya reading. The source has sent new documents, attached without explanation. Clarity's architecture is revealed in schematics and internal memos: the system builds psychological models that predict user behavior with uncomfortable accuracy, and the ethics review she conducted covered only a subset of features. The full system is something else entirely. She reads until late, the house silent, her daughter asleep down the hall, the ground shifting beneath her certainties.

## Key Elements

- Priya's arrival: the custody handoff, James's brief presence
- The rituals of transition: the foods, the spaces, the adjustment
- Priya's exhaustion: teenager tiredness, the weight she carries
- Dinner as parallel activity: together but not connecting
- The retreat to screens: Priya to her room, Ananya to her email
- The new documents: Clarity's full architecture revealed
- The gap between ethics review and reality: what Ananya wasn't shown
- Reading into the night: the mother divided, the professional shattered
