# Scene Plan: The Message

## Summary

Sunday. The household has found a pocket of peace - Priya working on her project with actual engagement, the fight not forgotten but set aside. Ananya uses this space to do what she's been avoiding: research journalists who cover tech, who've shown willingness to take on powerful companies, who might be able to do what she can't.

Using the privacy tools she's spent years recommending to others - encrypted communications, VPN, the infrastructure of careful paranoia - she searches. She finds Jerome Washington: former prestige outlet journalist, Pulitzer winner, now independent, author of the investigation that got killed by corporate pressure. His recent work covers algorithmic systems, financial surveillance, the architecture of tech power. His methods are rigorous. His position is outside.

She drafts a message. Deletes it. Drafts again. The message must be careful, deniable, but also unmistakable: she has information; she wants to talk; the channels must be secure. She sends it before she can reconsider. The afternoon passes normally - cooking with Priya, watching something together, the rituals of Sunday evening. James comes to pick up Priya. The goodbye is better than the greeting. Alone again, Ananya checks her secure email. No response yet. The cursor blinks. The chapter ends with her waiting, having crossed a line she can't uncross.
