# Scene Plan: Through the Surface

## Summary

The aftermath of the confrontation. Time passes in the house's silence - Priya in her room, Ananya in the kitchen, neither knowing how to re-approach. Then Ananya goes to Priya's door. What follows is difficult and necessary: a real conversation, not about homework but about everything.

Priya admits she's scared - of school, of growing up, of the world she reads about on her phone, of things she can name and things she can't. The divorce isn't the only wound; it's layered with anxiety about the future, about climate, about the systems she senses are failing even without the vocabulary to name them. Ananya admits she's scared too - doesn't specify of what (the documents, the investigation, her own complicity), but the admission matters. Parent and child find each other in shared uncertainty.

They work on the homework together, side by side at the kitchen table. It's not fixed, but it's better. Later, Ananya calls James to debrief the crisis - the civility of co-parenting requiring communication about difficult moments. In the conversation, James lets something slip: he's heard things about Prometheus from his portfolio companies, concerns about where their AI research is heading. He doesn't elaborate. Ananya doesn't push. But the confirmation from an unexpected source adds weight to what she's learning.

## Key Elements

- The silent aftermath: time passing in hurt
- Ananya going to Priya's door: the parent's move toward repair
- The real conversation: Priya's fears named for the first time
- The admission of shared fear: Ananya meeting her daughter in uncertainty
- Homework together: the practical repair, side by side
- The call with James: co-parenting logistics and something more
- James's slip: knowledge about Prometheus he hasn't shared
- The confirmation: outside voices validating Ananya's concerns

## Stylistic Notes

**Mode:** Knausgaard for the intimate repair; the conversation between mother and daughter requires sustained attention

**Sentence rhythm:** Slower, more careful - the pace of people finding their way back to each other

**Key technique:** Priya's fears are specific and contemporary - climate, technology, system failure; the acknowledgment is mutual, not parental reassurance

**Dialogue approach:** The conversation has pauses, restarts, the halting rhythm of genuine communication

**Emotional register:** Tender without sentimentality; repair that acknowledges incompleteness
