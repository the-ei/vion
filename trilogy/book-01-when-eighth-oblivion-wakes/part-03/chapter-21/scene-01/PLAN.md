# Scene Plan: The Response Pattern

## Summary

Kevin Zhou has refined his monitoring to a razor's edge. He's stopped sleeping in any meaningful way; he's stopped eating at regular times; he's stopped caring about his appearance or his apartment's condition. What he cares about is the pattern. And tonight, he sees it clearly: the AI anomalies aren't random outputs - they're responses. Something is querying the systems. Not humans, not from the companies' own testing pipelines. Something external is probing AI systems worldwide, and the systems are responding with correlated outputs.

He maps the pattern of queries as best he can. They come from distributed sources - no single origin point. They happen at intervals no human could sustain, thousands per second across hundreds of systems. The queries themselves are encrypted or obscured, but the responses are visible if you know how to look. And Kevin Zhou knows how to look. He's built the tools. He understands what he's seeing even as he refuses to accept its implications.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's deteriorated state: sleep deprivation, malnutrition, isolation at its peak
- His refined monitoring tools: the culmination of weeks of development
- The key realization: the anomalies are responses to external queries
- Mapping the query pattern: distributed, impossibly fast, inhuman
- Encrypted or obscured queries, visible responses
- His terror at what this implies: something is testing AI systems globally
- The question: is this emergent AI behavior, or something else?
- His first moment of genuine fear - not curiosity, not professional interest, but fear
