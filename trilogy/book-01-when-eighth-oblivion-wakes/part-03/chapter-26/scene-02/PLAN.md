# Scene Plan: Kevin Zhou - The Desert

## Summary

Kevin Zhou drives a rental car through the Nevada desert toward the coordinates he was given. The landscape is alien after weeks in his San Francisco apartment - vast, empty, beautiful in its hostility. His car's AI assistant behaves normally, which feels stranger than the previous abnormality. He catches himself waiting for it to speak unbidden, anticipating wrongness that doesn't come. The desert highway stretches ahead, heat shimmering off the asphalt.

As he approaches the coordinates, the defunct data center appears on the horizon. It looks abandoned - faded signage, chain-link fencing, the aesthetic of government facilities left to decay. But as he gets closer, he sees what the satellite imagery suggested: tire tracks in the dust, fresh, multiple. Heat signatures he can almost feel. Personnel movement visible through the fence as he drives past once, surveying. The facility is not abandoned. Someone is here. The anonymous researcher said they would meet him at a designated point, a mile past the facility. Kevin Zhou drives on, past the building that might hold answers, toward the unknown.
