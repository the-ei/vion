# Scene Plan: Delphine - The News

## Summary

Delphine is at the Nexus Digital office when the news breaks. It comes in fragments: an internal memo forwarded with alarm, a Slack message from legal, Cameron's face appearing at her door. The company is being acquired - by a larger media conglomerate with opaque ownership and a reputation for restructuring. Or: a major regulatory investigation has been announced, citing the company's role in amplifying the "Eighth Oblivion" content. Or: a platform change from one of their major distribution partners that will cut their reach by forty percent. Something fundamental has shifted.

Whatever the specific news, it changes Delphine's position entirely. The "Eighth Oblivion" coverage that went viral has drawn attention she didn't anticipate. Her recommendation - the meta-coverage, the "theory everyone is talking about" - is now cited in regulatory documents or acquisition discussions or platform decisions. The thing she helped create has created consequences. She sits in her office after receiving the news, thinking about her conversation with Jerome, about Jessie's question ("What's the ending?"), about the counter-content she was planning. The rules have changed. What she does next will be decided by forces she can't control.

## Key Elements

- The news breaking: fragmentary, alarming
- Multiple possible forms: acquisition, investigation, platform change
- The "Eighth Oblivion" coverage as cited factor
- Her role now visible in ways she didn't anticipate
- The meta-coverage cited: her recommendation has consequences
- Thinking of Jerome, Jessie, her unfinished plans
- The loss of agency: external forces now controlling
- Uncertainty about what comes next
- The office as suddenly unfamiliar
