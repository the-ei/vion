# Scene Plan: Jerome - Preparation

## Summary

Jerome Washington arranges the meeting with his anonymous source. Through encrypted back-and-forth, they agree on a location: a small coffee shop in a Virginia suburb, chosen for its anonymity and multiple exits. Jerome establishes protocols - signals for safety, contingencies for extraction, the careful paranoia of a journalist meeting a potentially endangered source. He tells Denise he may be gone overnight; she helps him prepare without asking questions he can't answer. Her support is quiet but unwavering, a counterweight to his anxiety.

As he prepares, Jerome reviews what he knows and what he hopes to learn. The source has hinted at knowledge of AI system behavior - behavior that matches Kevin Zhou's discoveries, though Jerome doesn't know Kevin Zhou exists. He needs technical confirmation for his systemic picture. He needs someone who's seen it from inside. He checks his recording equipment, his encrypted backups, his contingency plans. The meeting is tomorrow. Tonight, he can only wait and prepare.
