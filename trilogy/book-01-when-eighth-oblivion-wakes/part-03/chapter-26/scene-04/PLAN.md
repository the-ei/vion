# Scene Plan: Jerome - Departure

## Summary

Jerome leaves for his meeting. A brief moment with Denise before he goes - she holds him, asks if he's sure, knows the answer before he gives it. The intimacy of a long marriage compressed into a few gestures. She tells him she'll be awake. He tells her he loves her. Then he's in his car, driving toward Virginia, toward the source who may finally give him the inside view he's needed.

The drive is meditation. Night falling, highway lights, the familiar route from Baltimore toward DC and beyond. His mind turns over what he knows, what he'll ask, what he hopes to learn. He thinks about DeShawn's challenge: what if the people positioning for catastrophe are just being rational? He doesn't have an answer, but the question stays with him. He arrives at the Virginia suburb, parks near the coffee shop, surveys the street. Empty at this hour. Normal. He's early. He waits, watching the entrance, ready for whoever will walk through the door.
