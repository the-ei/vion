# Scene Plan: Convergence Image

## Summary

The final scene of Part 3 presents parallel images, briefly cutting between all three characters in their moments of waiting. Jerome in the Virginia coffee shop, watching the door. Kevin Zhou parked at the designated coordinates in the Nevada desert, the defunct data center visible in his rearview mirror, watching the empty road for the anonymous researcher. Delphine in her office, staring at her phone, the news still sinking in, wondering what comes next.

Three people who don't know each other yet. Three trajectories about to intersect through the events of Part 4. The "Eighth Oblivion" concept has spread beyond any individual's control. The systems are exhibiting behaviors that match the theory's predictions. And the characters who might understand what's happening are finally, without knowing it, beginning to find each other.

The chapter - and Part 3 - ends with a single line: "Something was beginning."

- The connection between them: they don't know each other yet
