# Scene Plan: The Overlay

## Summary

Jerome Washington sits in his home office surrounded by the artifacts of months of investigation. Wall maps with pins and strings. Printed spreadsheets covered in handwritten annotations. Multiple browser windows with financial databases, corporate registries, regulatory filings. He's been building this picture piece by piece, but today he attempts the synthesis. He creates what he calls "the overlay" - mapping the "Eighth Oblivion" video's specific predictions against his own financial tracking data.

The correspondence is devastating. The video predicts capital flight into specific sectors: private security, remote infrastructure, autonomous systems, longevity research. Jerome's data shows exactly those investment patterns, accelerating over the past eighteen months. The video names specific timelines; Jerome's tracking shows preparatory moves that fit those timelines perfectly. He identifies Prometheus Systems as a central node - a company appearing in multiple investment chains, connected to multiple moving parts. He knows he's looking at something real. The question is what to do with it.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's investigation headquarters: physical maps, digital databases, the hybrid methodology of an experienced journalist
- The overlay process: predictions matched against financial data
- Sector-by-sector correspondence: security, infrastructure, AI, longevity
- Investment timelines matching the video's predictions
- Prometheus Systems emerging as central node
- The recognition: this is not conspiracy, this is rational positioning for catastrophe
- His journalist's instincts confirmed and terrified
- The question shifting from "Is it true?" to "What do I do?"
