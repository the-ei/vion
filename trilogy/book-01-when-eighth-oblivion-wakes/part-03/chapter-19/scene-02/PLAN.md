# Scene Plan: Down the Rabbit Hole

## Summary

Jerome spends two days tracing the video's origins through the information ecosystem. He contacts Rachel, who can only tell him where she found it. He traces the video through platforms, finding it in different edits, different languages, different framings - some explicitly conspiratorial, some dressed in academic credibility. He identifies the researchers whose work was synthesized: a climate scientist, an AI safety researcher, a former hedge fund quantitative analyst. He interviews them by video call. All three are alarmed that their work was used but cannot say the synthesis is wrong. The climate scientist says, "It's like someone connected dots we've all been avoiding." The hedge fund analyst asks Jerome if he believes it. Jerome realizes he doesn't know how to answer.

The scene embodies the epistemological trap: the video may be true AND designed to manipulate. The truth is being weaponized, but that doesn't make it less true.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's forensic tracing of the video across platforms
- The multiple versions: conspiratorial, academic, populist, nihilistic
- Interviews with the original researchers: their alarm and their inability to debunk
- The hedge fund analyst's question that Jerome can't answer
- His methodology of verification failing: the usual tools don't apply
- Growing sense that the information environment itself has changed
- The impossibility of simple debunking
- Rachel's warning: "Be careful what you pull on here"
