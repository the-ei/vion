# Scene Plan: Pattern Recognition

## Summary

Late at night, unable to sleep, Jerome returns to his office. He pulls up his own investigation files - the financial flows, the corporate connections, the regulatory patterns he's been tracking for months. He overlays them with the "Eighth Oblivion" video's specific claims. The correspondence is terrifying. The video predicts capital flight into specific sectors; Jerome's financial tracking shows exactly that movement. The video predicts AI system consolidation; Jerome's corporate mapping shows exactly those acquisitions. The video is not quoting his research. Something - or someone - has synthesized the same patterns from different data.

He sits with the implications. If the video is disinformation, it's disinformation that's accidentally true. If it's genuine synthesis, someone or something has achieved a level of pattern recognition that should terrify everyone. He decides to reach out to sources in the tech industry. Someone inside the AI companies must know something.

## Key Elements

- Late night return to investigation after family confrontation
- His own research files: months of financial and corporate tracking
- The overlay: video predictions matched against his findings
- Terrifying correspondence: the video is accurate about things it shouldn't know
- The epistemological crisis deepening: is this AI synthesis or human insight?
- His decision to seek tech industry sources
- The loneliness of knowing something that can't be easily communicated
- Drafting outreach messages to potential sources
