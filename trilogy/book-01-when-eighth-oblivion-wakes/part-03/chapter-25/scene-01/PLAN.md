# Scene Plan: The Synthesis

## Summary

Jerome Washington has been building toward this moment for months. Today, he sits in his home office and writes the article - the synthesis of everything he's found. The financial patterns, the corporate connections, the regulatory capture, the smart money positioning for catastrophe. The correspondence between the "Eighth Oblivion" video's predictions and market reality. The conversation with Delphine that confirmed the engagement patterns. David Chen's warning about publishing. He writes fast, in the flow state that comes after long preparation, the words pouring out of accumulated understanding.

The draft takes shape over hours. It's the story of his career - not the most dramatic, but the most comprehensive. It connects dots that mainstream coverage treats as separate stories: AI development, wealth concentration, infrastructure vulnerability, institutional erosion. It names Prometheus Systems as a central node. It doesn't predict catastrophe; it maps the preparation for catastrophe, which is worse. When he finishes, he reads it through. It's good journalism. Well-sourced, carefully reasoned. And he has no idea if publishing it will help anything at all.
