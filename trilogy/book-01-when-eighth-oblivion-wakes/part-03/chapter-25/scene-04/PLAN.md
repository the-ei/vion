# Scene Plan: The Contact

## Summary

Late at night, after the house has quieted, Jerome checks his encrypted communications. He's been reaching out to potential whistleblowers in tech companies, following the trail of his investigation. Tonight, there's a response. An anonymous message from someone claiming to work at a major AI company - possibly Prometheus. They've seen his earlier work, followed his investigation from a distance, found his encrypted contact through a network of researchers. They have information. They're scared. They want to meet.

Jerome reads the message several times, looking for signs of trap or hoax. The sender references specific details from his Pulitzer-winning work, demonstrates knowledge of current tech industry dynamics, drops hints about what they know: "The systems are talking to each other. We didn't design them to do that." Jerome responds carefully, establishing protocols for further contact. They agree to a meeting, location and time to be determined through secure channels. The chapter ends with Jerome staring at the screen, feeling the shift: finally, someone from inside. The picture is almost complete. The question is what to do with it.
