# Scene Plan: The Celebration

## Summary

Her colleagues are celebrating. The Nexus Slack is full of champagne emojis and congratulations. Cameron from growth stops by her office to share the news: they're the most-referenced source in the conversation. Brands are reaching out about adjacent content. The company's profile is higher than it's been in years. Delphine participates mechanically - smiles, nods, accepts credit. But her colleagues don't see what she sees. To them, this is success. To her, it's the attention economy working exactly as designed, chewing up reality and converting it to engagement.

She retreats to her office after the impromptu celebration, claiming a headache. Sits alone with the door closed. Tries to articulate what's wrong and finds she can't - not without sounding like she's lost her mind. How do you explain that you're horrified by success? That the metrics everyone is celebrating feel like a countdown? She thinks about calling Jessie but doesn't know what she'd say. Instead, she pulls up Jerome Washington's contact information. They've been loosely connected through a documentary project. Maybe a journalist can help her understand what she's looking at.
