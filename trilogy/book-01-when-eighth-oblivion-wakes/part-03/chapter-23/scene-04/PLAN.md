# Scene Plan: The Choice

## Summary

Delphine comes home to Jessie and Theo. Theo is already in bed; it's late. She finds Jessie on the patio, waiting. She shares everything: the viral moment, the celebration she couldn't participate in, the call with Jerome. Jessie listens as she always does - not trying to fix, just holding space for the complexity. When Delphine finishes, Jessie asks the TV writer's question: "What's the ending?"

Delphine doesn't know. But she knows she can't undo what she's done. The viral moment is beyond anyone's control now - least of all hers. What she can do is influence what comes next. She starts to see a path: counter-content. Something that adds clarity instead of just attention. Something that helps people understand without feeding the panic. She knows it will probably go viral too, becoming part of the problem even as it tries to be the solution. But doing nothing is also a choice. She decides to try. The chapter ends with her beginning to plan something new - not a story about the story, but a story about what the story means.
