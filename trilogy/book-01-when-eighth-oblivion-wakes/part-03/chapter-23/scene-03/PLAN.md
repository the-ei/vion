# Scene Plan: The Call

## Summary

Delphine calls Jerome Washington. Their connection is the documentary - a project about his earlier investigative work that her company produced years ago. They haven't spoken in months, but she needs to talk to someone who understands media manipulation without being captured by it. The call begins awkwardly - she's not sure how to frame why she's reaching out. But as they talk, something shifts. They're both looking at the same phenomenon from different angles: she from inside the content machine, he from inside the investigation.

The conversation becomes the chapter's heart. Jerome shares some of what he's found - the financial patterns, the positioning of wealth, the impossible correspondence between the video's predictions and market reality. Delphine recognizes something in his data: patterns that match what she's seen in engagement metrics, in how certain stories get amplified while others disappear. They realize they're looking at coordinated behavior - but coordinated by what? Algorithms? Intent? Emergence? The call doesn't answer the question, but it gives both of them something they've lacked: a witness. Someone else who sees that the moment isn't just viral content - it might be something real.

## Key Elements

- The call initiated: Delphine reaching out, not entirely sure why
- The documentary connection as pretext
- Awkward beginning, then the shift into genuine exchange
- Jerome sharing financial patterns; Delphine recognizing engagement parallels
- The synthesis: two professionals seeing the same phenomenon differently
- The question of coordination: algorithms, intent, or emergence?
- Neither has answers, but both have witnesses
- The validation of shared perception
- The beginning of collaboration, unplanned
