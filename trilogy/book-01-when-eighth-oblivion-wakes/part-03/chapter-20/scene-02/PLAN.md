# Scene Plan: Analysis

## Summary

After the meeting, Delphine retreats to her office to analyze the "Eighth Oblivion" content with her professional tools. She breaks down its construction: the narrative pacing, the emotional triggers, the visual grammar, the algorithmic hooks. She recognizes the craftsmanship - whoever made this understands virality at a deep level. The content is designed to spread, designed to stick, designed to resist easy dismissal. But as she analyzes the how, she can't dismiss the what. The claims about AI system convergence, economic instability, infrastructure vulnerability - she doesn't have the expertise to evaluate them, but they echo things she's sensed in her own work.

Then she remembers: the "social impact" campaign her company produced for a community health center in Phoenix. Elena Varga's clinic. She pulls up that content, looking at it with new eyes. The campaign celebrated healthcare access, but the subtext was always about systems under strain. She wonders if she's been telling stories about collapse without understanding them.
