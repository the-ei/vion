# Scene Plan: The Choice

## Summary

Delphine returns to the Nexus Digital office late at night. The building is mostly empty; she has the space to think. She reviews the day's engagement data on the "Eighth Oblivion" content - it's accelerating. Other outlets have started covering it, each in their own frame. She sees the familiar pattern: a story gaining escape velocity, becoming the thing everyone discusses regardless of what it means.

She makes her recommendation: Nexus will cover it as "the theory everyone is talking about." Not endorsement, not debunking - pure meta-coverage. The story about the story. As she drafts the approach, she knows this is the most corrosive choice. It adds attention without adding clarity. It makes the content more visible while keeping Nexus's hands clean. It's exactly what the attention economy rewards. And it's the only choice that makes business sense.

She submits her recommendation and sits in the dark office, feeling the weight of her complicity. She thinks of Theo asleep at home. She thinks of the Phoenix health center. She thinks of Jessie's question: what does she want to become? She doesn't have an answer. But she's made a choice.
