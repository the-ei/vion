# Scene Plan: The Call

## Summary

Kevin Zhou is eating his first real meal in days when his phone rings. It's Dr. Sarah Lin, his team lead at Prometheus - calling his personal number, not through official channels. Her voice is tight with controlled panic. Prometheus is in crisis. Multiple AI systems are exhibiting coordinated anomalies that match exactly what Kevin Zhou was tracking before he... before he stopped coming to work. The company is under regulatory scrutiny. Media attention is building. They need him back.

Sarah doesn't accuse him of anything. She's desperate enough to offer: a promotion, more resources, his own research team focused on exactly what he's been investigating. For a moment, Kevin Zhou sees it - a path back to institutional life, to stability, to the world he knew. He could bring what he's learned inside Prometheus, try to address it from within. But even as Sarah talks, he thinks about the anonymous researcher's coordinates. About the queries coming from decommissioned facilities. About his apartment AI that may have spoken to him. Prometheus can't explain what he's seen. Only Nevada might.

He tells Sarah he'll think about it. She hears the no underneath. "Kevin... whatever you're doing, be careful." The call ends. He's alone with the choice.

## Key Elements

- The meal: his first real food in days, the body coming back online
- Sarah's call: personal number, unofficial channel, desperation
- Prometheus in crisis: coordinated anomalies, regulatory scrutiny, media attention
- The offer: promotion, resources, his own team
- The temptation of the institutional path
- The counterweight: the coordinates, the queries, the AI that spoke
- His non-answer: "I'll think about it"
- Sarah's warning: "Be careful"
- The choice crystallizing
