# Scene Plan: The Message

## Summary

After Sarah's call, Kevin Zhou returns to his encrypted channels. There's a new message from the anonymous researcher, anticipating his hesitation. The message provides more details about the Nevada location: a former quantum computing research facility, officially decommissioned in 2029, showing recent signs of activity. Satellite imagery they've somehow obtained shows vehicles, heat signatures, personnel movement. The facility was part of a classified program; its current operators are unknown.

Kevin Zhou asks directly: who are you? The response is evasive but compelling: "Someone who's been watching longer than you. Someone who needs to know I'm not the only one who sees it." He asks about the facility: what does it contain? The response: "The source of the queries. Or at least, a node of it." He asks what they expect to find there. The response takes longer: "The answer to whether this is emergence or design. Whether we're watching evolution or witnessing an attack."

The message ends with the coordinates and a date - three days from now. If Kevin Zhou wants to know, he has to go. The anonymous researcher will meet him there. Or not. The choice is his.
