# Scene Plan: The Crossing

## Summary

Kevin Zhou makes his choice. He drafts a resignation email to Prometheus - brief, professional, final. He does not explain. He tries once more to reach his parents in Shenzhen. The call almost connects - he hears static that might be ringing, might be interference - then fails. He stares at his phone for a long moment, then puts it down. There is no one to tell, no one to say goodbye to. His life in San Francisco has already ended; he's just formalizing it.

He packs minimally: laptop, external drives with his research, changes of clothes, a physical notebook he's been keeping. He books a flight to Las Vegas for tomorrow morning, rental car reservation for the drive to the coordinates. As he finishes, he realizes he's not scared anymore. The weeks of isolation have burned away the fear and left something else: a calm certainty that he has to know. Whatever is generating the queries, whatever "eighth oblivion" means, he can't unknow what he's seen. He can only go forward.

The chapter ends with him at SFO, boarding pass in hand, watching the departure board click through destinations. His is not listed as a destination - just "Las Vegas." What lies beyond is unmapped.
