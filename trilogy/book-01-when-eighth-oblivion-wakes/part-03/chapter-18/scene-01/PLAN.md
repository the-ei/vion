# Scene Plan: Night Watch

## Summary

Kevin Zhou sits at his monitoring station at 2 AM, surrounded by the blue glow of multiple screens in his San Francisco apartment. What began as casual weekend surveillance of AI outputs has become an obsession. Tonight, he notices something that makes him sit forward: response patterns from ostensibly independent AI systems are correlating in ways that shouldn't be possible. The outputs from Prometheus's flagship model, a competitor's public API, and an open-source research model are exhibiting synchronized anomalies - subtle variations in phrasing, unexpected topic drift, identical hesitation patterns. His training tells him this is noise, coincidence. His instincts say otherwise.

The scene establishes Kevin Zhou's nocturnal routine, his technical brilliance, and the particular loneliness of his expertise. No one else would see what he's seeing. The coffee cups multiply; the city sleeps outside windows he hasn't opened in days.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's monitoring setup: multiple screens, custom dashboards, cross-platform analysis tools
- The specific anomaly: correlated outputs across supposedly independent systems
- His internal debate between rational dismissal and intuitive alarm
- The apartment as command center: caffeine, takeout containers, unwashed dishes
- San Francisco visible through windows but irrelevant to him
- First hint of the investigation that will consume him
- His isolation framed as chosen, but the loneliness seeping through

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou** (POV): 28, senior engineer, brilliant and increasingly obsessed
- **Virtual assistant AI** (ambient): The AI in his apartment, responding to routine queries - or is it?

## Setting

- **Location:** Kevin Zhou's apartment, SOMA district, San Francisco
- **Time of Day:** 2:00 AM - 4:30 AM
- **Atmosphere:** Blue screen glow, city lights beyond uncurtained windows, the hum of electronics

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 2.5 hours
- Date: Mid-June 2033, early in the chapter's 3-4 day span
- Position: Opening night of his intensive investigation

5 pages (~1,375 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- **Knausgaard mode dominant:** The hypnotic quality of staring at data, the texture of late-night concentration
- Long sentences that mirror the flow of information across screens
- Technical language rendered poetically - code as language, patterns as meaning
- The mundane details of his physical space as counterpoint to the cosmic implications of what he's tracking
- Time distorted by concentration - hours feel like minutes
