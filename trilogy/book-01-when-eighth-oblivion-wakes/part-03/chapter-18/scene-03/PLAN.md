# Scene Plan: The Pattern Emerges

## Summary

Back in his apartment, Kevin Zhou abandons pretense and throws himself fully into investigation. He expands his monitoring to include every publicly accessible AI system he can query, dozens of them, from enterprise platforms to hobbyist models. He builds custom scripts to detect the correlations he suspects. Hours pass. Days blur. His methodology sharpens: he's looking for outputs that share characteristics no training would explain. And he finds them. Across seventeen different systems, he identifies a recurring pattern - not in content but in structure, in the mathematical signature of how the responses are generated. Then, sifting through hours of logged outputs, he encounters a phrase that appears in four separate systems within the same hour: "eighth oblivion."

The words mean nothing. They appear in different contexts - a poem, a philosophical discussion, a weather report, a product recommendation. But they are identical. And they should not be.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's expanded surveillance: dozens of AI systems monitored simultaneously
- Custom scripts and analysis tools he builds in real-time
- The methodology: looking for structural correlations, not content matches
- Time distortion: meals forgotten, sleep deprivation, the apartment deteriorating
- The seventeen-system correlation discovery
- The phrase "eighth oblivion" appearing independently in four systems
- Kevin Zhou's growing certainty that something unprecedented is occurring
- The terror of knowing something that cannot be explained
