# Scene Plan: Documentation

## Summary

Kevin Zhou begins systematically recording everything he's found. He creates encrypted files, backup copies stored on air-gapped drives, physical printouts hidden in his apartment. The process feels paranoid even to him - he's not a whistleblower, not a conspiracy theorist, just an engineer who's noticed something strange. But the care he takes reveals his growing conviction that what he's found matters. He researches the phrase "eighth oblivion" and finds nothing - no existing meme, no academic reference, no cultural artifact. The phrase appears to have originated in the AI outputs themselves. He stares at those two words, trying to understand what the machines are saying. Outside his window, San Francisco awakens to another normal day. Inside, Kevin Zhou knows something has changed.

The scene ends with him writing the phrase on a piece of paper and pinning it to his wall. A journalist would recognize this as the beginning of an investigation board. Kevin Zhou just thinks of it as evidence.

## Key Elements

- Systematic documentation: encrypted files, air-gapped backups, physical copies
- Kevin Zhou's self-awareness about his own apparent paranoia
- Research into "eighth oblivion" - finding nothing, confirming its anomalous origin
- The implications: if the phrase originated in AI outputs, what does it mean?
- His attempt to understand the semantic content: what is "eighth oblivion"?
- The contrast between his interior crisis and the normal world outside
- The physical act of writing the phrase, pinning it to the wall
- Prudence and paranoia becoming indistinguishable
