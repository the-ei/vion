# Scene Plan: Failed Connections

## Summary

Morning breaks over San Francisco and Kevin Zhou tries to call his parents in Shenzhen. The call stutters, connects for three seconds of his mother's voice fragment, then dies. He tries again. Again. The international communication infrastructure has been unreliable for months - everyone knows this, it's in the news - but today the failure feels personal, ominous. He gives up and goes to Prometheus, where Dr. Sarah Lin, his sympathetic team lead, suggests he take time off. She's noticed the dark circles, the distraction. When Kevin Zhou tries to explain what he's found - the correlating outputs, the impossible synchronization - his words sound paranoid even to him. He realizes he cannot communicate what he knows. Returns home having told no one.

The scene explores the gulf between observation and communication. Kevin Zhou's isolation is now partly chosen, partly enforced by the inadequacy of language to convey what he's seeing.

## Key Elements

- Failed video calls to Shenzhen: his mother's face pixelating, her voice cutting out
- The unreliability of international communications as normalized crisis
- Kevin Zhou at Prometheus: the corporate campus, his team's workspace
- Dr. Sarah Lin's genuine concern mistaken for doubt
- His attempt to articulate his findings, and how it fails
- The drive home through San Francisco traffic, feeling more alone than ever
- His parents as absence: the calls that don't connect as metaphor for all failed connection

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou** (POV): 28, realizing he cannot share what he knows
- **Dr. Sarah Lin** (secondary): 40s, team lead, sympathetic but constrained by corporate prudence
- **Parents** (attempted contact): Mother briefly visible, father referenced - present only as absence
- **Prometheus colleagues** (ambient): Background presence, normal workday continuing around him

## Setting

- **Location:** Kevin Zhou's apartment (morning), then Prometheus Systems campus in San Francisco
- **Time of Day:** 7:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- **Atmosphere:** Bright California morning contrasting with his internal darkness; sleek corporate spaces that suddenly feel hostile

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 7 hours
- Date: Same day as Scene 1 (after brief sleep) into late morning
- Position: Day 1-2 of the chapter

## Stylistic Notes

- **Split between modes:** Carson mode for the failed calls (fragmentary, static-filled, gaps), Knausgaard mode for Prometheus (the texture of professional spaces, the weight of unshared knowledge)
- The calls should feel like trying to speak through water
- Dr. Sarah Lin's kindness making things worse, not better
- Kevin Zhou's interiority: how does brilliant observation feel when you cannot share it?
- The drive home as capsule of isolation: alone in a car among thousands

- **Chapter:** Shows why Kevin Zhou will pursue this alone - not choice but necessity
