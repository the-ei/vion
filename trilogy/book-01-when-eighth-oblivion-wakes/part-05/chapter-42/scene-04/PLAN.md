# Scene Plan: Synthesis - Evening

## Summary

The day closing, each character in their place. This final scene weaves together brief glimpses of all four POV characters as they settle into the evening - the first day of what comes next, the threshold crossed. The multi-POV structure becomes explicit here: short sections for each character, connected by the shared date and shared awareness, creating a mosaic of positions as Book 1 ends.

Ruth at her window, the city below, thinking about institutions and their limits, Susan's absence still present, the work continuing. Jerome at dinner with Denise, ordinary and good, the world going on. Delphine and Jessie putting Theo to bed, the routine a blessing, the future uncertain but shared. Ananya alone at home but not lonely, the documents in her office now part of something, Priya coming to visit next week. The final paragraphs pull back: the season turning, the Eighth Oblivion still out there, the characters positioned for what comes next. The last image echoes the book's opening - a formal callback that creates closure without resolution.

## Key Elements

- Multi-POV structure: four perspectives woven through a single evening
- Each character in their chosen position: established, not triumphant
- Ruth: at her window, thinking about the work ahead, Susan's ghost
- Jerome: at dinner with Denise, the ordinary good of continued life
- Delphine: bedtime routine with Theo, the ground of family beneath uncertainty
- Ananya: alone but purposeful, Priya coming soon, the documents meaningful now
- Seasonal detail: spring arriving, the turn from winter, renewal without resolution
- The echo: final image connecting to the book's opening
- The threshold: not "The End" but "To Be Continued" in feeling
