# Scene Plan: Ruth and Ananya - Morning

## Summary

The first scene of Book 1's final chapter brings Ruth and Ananya together in Ruth's San Francisco apartment. Ruth has relocated from her East Coast teaching position to be closer to the project she's building. A morning meeting of the informal working group is underway - Ananya present along with others who may become significant in Book 2. They're reviewing something concrete: data, a report, a strategy for ongoing monitoring of the forces they've identified.

Ruth articulates the project's purpose: not to prevent the Eighth Oblivion (they can't), but to ensure someone is watching, thinking, preparing. Ananya contributes her insider perspective - knowledge of how Prometheus and similar companies operate, what the warning signs look like, how to read the gaps between public statements and internal reality. After the meeting, Ruth and Ananya walk to get coffee, talking about what they've learned, what's changed, what it means to be awake in a world that mostly sleeps. Their alliance - institutional elder and ethics insider - is established as central to whatever comes next.

## Key Elements

- Ruth's apartment: the new San Francisco base for the informal project
- The working group meeting: reviewing concrete materials, assigning tasks
- Ruth's articulation: the purpose clarified - watching, not preventing; preparing, not predicting
- Ananya's contribution: insider knowledge applied to ongoing monitoring
- Other group members: seeds for Book 2 characters, briefly present
- The post-meeting walk: Ruth and Ananya alone, deepening their connection
- The alliance formalized: two women with different institutional experiences, now working together
- Anniversary context: somewhere in the day, the date's significance (one year since crisis onset) is noted
