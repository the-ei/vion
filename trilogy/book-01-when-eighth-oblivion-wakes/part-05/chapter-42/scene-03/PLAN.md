# Scene Plan: Delphine - Afternoon

## Summary

Delphine at a screening of her completed video series - a small audience, perhaps a focus group or advance screening before wider release. She watches her own work play, seeing its compromises and its achievements. The series is better than it could have been, worse than she hoped - the gap between intention and execution that defines commercial creative work. Jessie is present, supportive, watching Delphine watch her work.

Afterward, a viewer approaches Delphine - someone who was affected by the crisis, who found the series helpful, who says it helped them understand what happened to them. A moment of connection that suggests the work matters, even within its constraints. Delphine thinking about what comes next - maybe something less constrained, maybe with the group Ruth and Ananya have formed. The complicity question not resolved but reframed: imperfect work that reaches people might be better than perfect work that doesn't.

## Key Elements

- The screening: Delphine watching her own series, critical eye trained on her work
- Compromises and achievements: what she sacrificed, what she preserved
- Jessie's presence: supportive spouse, sharing the moment
- The viewer connection: someone for whom the work mattered, despite its limits
- Personal impact: the series helped someone understand their experience
- What comes next: Delphine thinking about less constrained possibilities
- Ruth's group as option: a different kind of engagement, potentially
- Complicity reframed: imperfect reach versus perfect obscurity
