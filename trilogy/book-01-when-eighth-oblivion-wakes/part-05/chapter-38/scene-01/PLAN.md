# Scene Plan: The Commission

## Summary

Delphine Okafor-Barnes in a meeting at her digital media company - they've been hired to produce a video series explaining the crisis and its implications for a major streaming platform. This is significant work: substantial budget, massive reach, aggressive timeline. The commission lands in Delphine's lap as creative director, and she immediately sees both the opportunity (to shape how millions understand what happened) and the danger (simplification, false balance, premature closure).

The meeting introduces Delphine's professional world: the language of content creation, the dynamics of a creative team, the gap between what clients want and what truth requires. By the scene's end, she's accepted the project, but the acceptance is shadowed by the question she's been avoiding her entire career: what is she actually building?

## Key Elements

- The meeting itself: company conference room, creative team assembled, client brief reviewed
- The commission's scope: major streaming platform, multi-episode series, substantial budget
- Delphine's immediate perception: the ways this could go wrong, the ways it could matter
- Creative team dynamics: younger colleagues, veteran cynics, varying relationships to the work
- The client's framing: they want explanation, context, ultimately closure - not ongoing alarm
- Delphine's assignment as creative director: the responsibility is hers
- Industry language: the jargon of content production, engagement metrics, platform dynamics
- The complicity question crystallizing: she's been paid to make things like this before

## Characters Present

- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes (POV):** Accepting a project that forces confrontation with her career's meaning
- **Creative team colleagues:** Range of attitudes - idealists, pragmatists, cynics; some who see the opportunity, some who see the paycheck
- **Senior executive:** The one who landed the client, framing the project's importance and constraints
