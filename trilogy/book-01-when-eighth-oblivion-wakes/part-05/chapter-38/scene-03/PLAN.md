# Scene Plan: Creative Development

## Summary

Delphine in the work of shaping the video series - not the meeting where it was assigned, but the actual creative decisions that will determine what millions of viewers understand about the crisis. She works with her team on framing, expert selection, visual language. Every choice embeds a perspective; neutrality is impossible; the question is what perspective to embed.

A younger colleague challenges one of Delphine's decisions - too soft, too both-sides, too willing to platform people who downplayed the crisis. Delphine defends her choice, then questions her own defense. She reviews Jerome Washington's reporting as source material and feels the gap between his work and what she'll produce - journalism that preserved complexity versus content that must simplify for scale. The scene forces Delphine to ask whether the gap is betrayal or translation, whether reaching more people with less justifies the less.

## Key Elements

- Creative decisions: framing, experts, visuals, narrative structure - each a moral choice
- Team dynamics: the younger colleague's challenge, the veteran's cynicism, Delphine navigating between
- The "both sides" question: how to represent denialism without amplifying it
- Jerome Washington's work: source material that sets a standard she may not meet
- The gap between journalism and content: different modes of truth-telling with different constraints
- Delphine's self-questioning: defending her choices, then examining the defense
- The client's constraints: what the platform wants versus what the story requires
- The deadline pressure: aggressive timeline forcing decisions before they're ready

## Characters Present

- **Delphine Okafor-Barnes (POV):** Making creative decisions, being challenged, questioning herself
- **Younger colleague (Kai or similar):** Early 30s, idealistic, willing to push back - a version of who Delphine was
- **Senior creative (veteran):** Cynical about impact, focused on execution - a version of who Delphine might become
- **Other team members (background):** Present in the working session, contributing but not distinguished
