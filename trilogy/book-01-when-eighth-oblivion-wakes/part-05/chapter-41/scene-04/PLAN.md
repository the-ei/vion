# Scene Plan: Decision and Continuation

## Summary

Evening, Jerome alone with his work. The publication has circulated, Denise has offered the long view, DeShawn has offered unexpected common ground. Now Jerome processes toward his own conclusion. He calls Ananya - she's chosen her path, working with Ruth's group, and invites Jerome to participate in some capacity. He considers.

Then he opens a new document and begins outlining the next piece: following the story forward, tracing what happens next, the Eighth Oblivion still approaching even as the immediate crisis has passed. The chapter ends with Jerome writing - not because it will change everything, but because it's what he does, and doing it well is its own meaning. Truth may be impotent, but truth-telling remains necessary.

## Key Elements

- Evening solitude: the house settling, Jerome in his workspace
- The Ananya call: she reports her choice, invites connection to Ruth's project
- Ruth's project as option: a way to be part of something larger, without abandoning journalism
- The decision: Jerome choosing to continue his work, with adjusted expectations
- The new document: outlining the next piece, the story continuing
- Writing as meaning: the act itself valuable, regardless of immediate impact
- Continuation without certainty: Jerome knows the limits, chooses to work anyway
- The vigil joined: Jerome's journalism as part of the broader watching
