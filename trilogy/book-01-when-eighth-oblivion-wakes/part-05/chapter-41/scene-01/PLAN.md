# Scene Plan: Publication Day

## Summary

Jerome's summation piece goes live - his most comprehensive work on the crisis and its implications, published on a significant platform with real reach. The scene captures both the publication and its immediate aftermath: Jerome watching the response unfold, the metrics accumulating, the discourse forming. His work gets traction, is cited by mainstream outlets, attacked by denialists, absorbed into the ongoing conversation. He has been right, is recognized as right, and nothing fundamental shifts.

The dissonance between attention and impact crystallizes. Jerome scrolling, refreshing, the dopamine hit of engagement tangled with the disappointment of seeing that engagement doesn't translate to change. The world knows what he told them. The world continues. What, then, did truth achieve?

## Key Elements

- The publication: Jerome's summation piece goes live on a major platform
- Watching the response: shares, comments, citations, attacks - the full spectrum
- Engagement metrics: the numbers that measure attention but not impact
- Mainstream pickup: legacy outlets citing his work, amplifying without transforming
- Denialist attacks: the organized or organic opposition that comes with visibility
- The absorption: his work becoming part of discourse, losing its sharp edges
- Jerome's internal response: satisfaction and disappointment intertwined
- The question crystallized: attention is not impact; what did this accomplish?

## Characters Present

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Watching his work circulate, feeling the gap between attention and change
- **Commenters (abstract):** The voices of response - supporters, critics, denialists, the engaged and the angry
