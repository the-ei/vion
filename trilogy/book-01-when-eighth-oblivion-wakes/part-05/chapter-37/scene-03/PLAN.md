# Scene Plan: Professional Decision

## Summary

Jerome faces the offers and threats that have accumulated around his elevated profile. On one side: mainstream outlets wanting to hire him, but with conditions that would compromise his independence - an editorial board position that requires "balance," a column deal with content restrictions, a documentary project with corporate sponsors. On the other side: harassment that has escalated beyond typical online hostility - not just trolls but coordinated campaigns, hints that someone is tracking his family's movements, enough to require conversation about security.

The scene moves through several interactions - a call with an editor making an offer, a review of the threat documentation with Denise, a difficult conversation about what Jerome owes his family versus his calling. The question crystallizes: Can he afford to continue this work? Can he afford to stop?

## Key Elements

- The offers: specific, attractive, compromised - each representing a path away from independence
- The threats: escalating, possibly coordinated, serious enough to discuss security measures
- Denise's involvement: this is a family decision, not just Jerome's
- Financial pressure: independent journalism doesn't pay like mainstream positions
- The compromise question: what would Jerome have to give up to be safer/wealthier?
- Security considerations: practical steps versus paranoia versus genuine risk
- Jerome's resistance to institutional absorption: he left mainstream media for reasons
- The calling versus family: can both be served, or is he sacrificing one for the other?

## Characters Present

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Weighing professional paths; confronting the cost of independence
- **Denise Cole:** Involved in the security discussion; her perspective on risk and sacrifice matters
- **Media editor (phone):** Making an offer, professional and reasonable, representing institutional absorption
- **Security consultant (mentioned/brief):** Someone who's reviewed the threats - voice of professional risk assessment
