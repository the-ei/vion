# Scene Plan: Legal Consultation

## Summary

Ananya meets with an employment lawyer recommended through her professional network. The consultation covers terrain that's both practical and existential: her administrative leave terms, the documents she retained (technically company property?), potential whistleblower protections, the risk profile of different paths forward. The lawyer is competent but clearly more comfortable with normal cases - wrongful termination, non-compete disputes. Ananya's situation sits at the edge of legal categories.

The scene establishes the legal constraints on Ananya's choices while also revealing how inadequate legal frameworks are for her actual situation. She leaves with information but not reassurance. The law offers options, not clarity. What she does with the documents, with her knowledge, with her position - these decisions will have to be made on grounds the law cannot fully address.

## Key Elements

- The lawyer's office: professional, competent, the setting of normal legal practice
- Administrative leave terms: ambiguous, potentially defensible, legally uncertain
- The documents question: Ananya retained materials that may be company property; risk assessment required
- Whistleblower protections: potentially applicable, but the protections have limits
- Risk profiles: what happens if she goes public, stays quiet, cooperates with investigations
- The lawyer's competence and limitations: good at normal cases, uncertain with this one
- Information without clarity: Ananya learns her options but not her path
- The inadequacy of legal frameworks: law designed for slower-moving situations

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Seeking legal clarity, finding legal options instead
- **Employment lawyer (Sandra or similar):** Mid-40s, experienced, competent within limits; represents normal professional practice
