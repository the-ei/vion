# Scene Plan: The Offers and Invitations

## Summary

The professional paths laid out for Ananya to consider. A corporate safety consulting role - good money, using her expertise, but for the same companies she's questioning. An academic position - slower, more reflective, but removed from impact. And then Ruth Abramson's invitation: something unofficial, a working group or project that's trying to think beyond institutional frameworks.

The scene moves through these options - a meeting or call about the corporate role, a conversation with an academic contact, and then Ruth's approach (phone or in-person, depending on Ruth's timeline). Each path represents a different answer to the question of what Ananya's ethics career meant. Ruth doesn't promise impact, but she offers purpose - and a community of people asking the same questions Ananya is asking.

## Key Elements

- The corporate offer: a consulting firm wants her expertise on AI safety; lucrative, compromised
- The academic option: a policy institute or law school wants her to teach/research; slower, safer
- Ruth's invitation: something unofficial, outside institutional channels, purpose over position
- The contrast: each path represents a different relationship to power and impact
- Ruth's pitch: not promising results, but offering meaningful engagement
- Ananya's weighing: what each path says about who she is and wants to be
- Kevin Zhou as shadow: her former rival's trajectory as counterpoint to hers
- The week's context: these conversations happening while Priya is present, family informing professional

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Weighing professional paths; testing different possible futures
- **Corporate representative (call/meeting):** Professional, reasonable, representing the well-paid path
- **Academic contact (call):** Colleague from Stanford network; representing the reflective path
- **Ruth Abramson (phone or video):** The invitation to something different; two women with institutional experience comparing notes
