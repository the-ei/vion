# Scene Plan: Week with Priya

## Summary

Extended time with her daughter Priya, who's on winter break from school. More time together than their usual scheduled calls and occasional weekends - a full week of relearning each other in the aftermath of crisis. The scene captures the texture of parent-teenager time: meals prepared together, walks, a museum visit, homework help where Ananya can still be useful, the ordinary domestic fabric that's been scarce.

Priya asks real questions about what happened - what Ananya did, whether she's in trouble, what it all means. Ananya tries to answer honestly while protecting her daughter from the full weight of adult complexity. A moment of genuine connection emerges, different from the mediated distance of video calls. Ananya sees her choices reflected in her daughter's perception, and that reflection helps her understand what she believes and values.

## Key Elements

- Extended domestic time: a week together rather than scheduled hours
- The texture of daily life: meals, walks, homework, the ordinary made precious by scarcity
- Priya's questions: direct, seeking understanding, not accusation
- Ananya's honest answers: calibrated for a 14-year-old but not condescending
- The museum visit: a specific shared activity, time outside the house
- Homework help: a domain where Ananya's expertise is uncomplicated
- Genuine connection: a moment where mother and daughter actually meet
- Reflection and clarity: Priya's perception helping Ananya understand herself

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Reconnecting with her daughter; seeing herself through Priya's eyes
- **Priya Ramaswamy (14):** Winter break from school; curious, perceptive, forming her own opinions about her mother's choices
