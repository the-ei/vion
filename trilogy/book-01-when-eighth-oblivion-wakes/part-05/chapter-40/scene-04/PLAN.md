# Scene Plan: The Choice

## Summary

Priya has returned to her father's house. Ananya alone in her home, but different from the isolation of Chapter 36. The week with her daughter, the legal consultation, the professional options - everything has been digested. Now she acts. She calls Ruth to accept the invitation. She calls Jerome to offer continued collaboration. She drafts an email to Prometheus that's a resignation rather than waiting for their decision. The chapter ends with Ananya having chosen - not a complete plan, but a direction that feels true to what she's learned.

## Key Elements

- Post-Priya solitude: alone but not lonely; the house different from before
- The Ruth call: accepting the invitation, committing to unofficial engagement
- The Jerome call: offering to continue their collaboration, formalizing their alliance
- The Prometheus email: resignation rather than waiting; taking control of the narrative
- The documents: their status resolved or held in abeyance, part of her ongoing leverage
- Integration: the choice emerging from everything that's come before
- Direction without complete plan: she knows the path, not every step
- The vigil formalized: Ananya becoming a watcher, intentionally

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Making calls, writing emails, committing to a direction
- **Ruth Abramson (phone):** Receiving Ananya's acceptance; their alliance formalized
- **Jerome Washington (phone):** Hearing Ananya's offer of continued collaboration
- **Prometheus (email, abstract):** The institution Ananya is leaving, addressed but not responded to

## Setting

- **Location:** Ananya's house in Palo Alto - home office, the spaces she's reclaiming
- **Time of day:** Evening into night; a Saturday or Sunday after Priya's departure
- **Physical details:** The house quiet but not empty; the documents in her office now purposeful
- **Season/Weather:** Late February; a clearer day; late winter light softening toward spring

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 4 hours (evening)
- Position in chapter: Final scene, the resolution of Ananya's Book 1 arc
- Pacing: Decisive - not rushed, but with clear forward movement

- **Ending posture:** Ready, oriented, purposeful - the vigil beginning in earnest
