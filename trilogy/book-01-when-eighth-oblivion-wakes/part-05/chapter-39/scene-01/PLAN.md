# Scene Plan: Arrival in Washington

## Summary

Ruth Abramson returns to Washington D.C. after years away from regular engagement with federal power. Semi-retired from the 9th Circuit, teaching law in California, she has been summoned to consult on the legal and constitutional dimensions of the crisis response. The city feels both familiar and alien - she knows these buildings, these corridors, but the people have changed, the culture has shifted, and she herself is different at 61 than she was during her active years.

The scene tracks Ruth from airport arrival through settling into her hotel near Capitol Hill, establishing her internal landscape: skeptical of why they want her, uncertain whether she has anything useful to offer, privately angry about being considered obsolete exactly when her perspective might matter most. Memory of Susan threads through - conferences they attended together, arguments before the Supreme Court, a younger version of engagement now inaccessible.

## Key Elements

- Physical return: the airport, the car service, the hotel, the familiar geography estranged by time
- Ruth's internal skepticism: why does Washington want her now? What do they actually want to hear?
- The gap between summons and substance: being invited for credibility versus being invited for ideas
- Memory of Susan: her late wife as intellectual partner, the conversations Ruth can no longer have
- Washington's architectural weight: monuments, buildings, the built environment expressing power
- Ruth's age-awareness: 61, feeling both her experience and her marginalization
- Legal framework thinking: Ruth's characteristic mode, how she processes complexity
- The evening alone: hotel room, the particular solitude of travel, preparation for tomorrow's meetings

## Characters Present

- **Ruth Abramson (POV):** Returning to a city she knows, uncertain of her role
- **Susan (memory):** Ruth's late wife, dead six years, present in Ruth's thoughts throughout
- **Car service driver (minimal):** Brief interaction, the anonymous infrastructure of travel

## Setting

- **Location:** Reagan National Airport to Capitol Hill hotel
- **Time of day:** 4:00 PM arrival; settling in until evening
- **Physical journey:** Airport, car, hotel lobby, hotel room - the infrastructure of official Washington
- **Season/Weather:** Late January/early February; East Coast winter; gray, cold, damp
