# Scene Plan: Vikram's Visit

## Summary

Vikram, a former colleague still at Prometheus Systems, arrives at Ananya's house - announced only briefly, a text message an hour before. He represents the colleagues who stayed, who are still navigating Prometheus from inside, who may have made different choices during the crisis or simply weren't positioned to make the choices Ananya made. The visit exists in ambiguity: Is he here as a friend checking on her welfare? As an institutional emissary gathering intelligence? As someone wrestling with his own complicity? Perhaps all three.

The conversation dances around what Ananya knows and what she did with it, around the internal dynamics at Prometheus, around the question of who is complicit and who is heroic - if those categories even hold. Vikram's genuine concern is mixed with institutional self-protection; he cannot fully separate the person from the position. The visit ends ambiguously - has connection been maintained or surveillance been conducted? Ananya is left uncertain, and this uncertainty is itself revealing about the environment she inhabited for years.

## Key Elements

- The unexpected visit: Vikram's arrival disrupts Ananya's solitude, introduces external pressure
- Ambiguous motives: friendship, intelligence-gathering, and personal reckoning intertwined
- The Prometheus internal situation: what Vikram reveals (or conceals) about the company's response
- The ethics role examined: Vikram's presence forces Ananya to articulate what she believes her role meant
- Physical presence after screens: the intimacy and awkwardness of in-person conversation after weeks of isolation
- What Ananya reveals and withholds: she has reasons to be careful, even with former friends
- The documents' shadow: their presence in the house, unmentioned but relevant
- Tea or coffee as social ritual: the domestic frame around difficult conversation

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Navigating an ambiguous encounter; testing her own judgments about former colleagues
- **Vikram Patel:** Early 40s, still at Prometheus, someone Ananya considered a friend; his loyalties divided between personal affection and institutional belonging

## Setting

- **Time of day:** 3:00 PM - 5:30 PM
- **Physical details:** The house that Vikram may have visited before socially, now feeling different; furniture arrangements, sight lines, the subtle staging of conversation
- **Season/Weather:** Late afternoon January gray; lights on against the dimness; rain beginning outside

## Stylistic Notes

- **Mode:** Primarily Knausgaard - the extended social interaction, attention to body language and domestic detail
- **Carson moments:** Brief intrusions when Ananya perceives the conversation's stakes sharply
- **Dialogue:** Layered, with subtext; what's not said as important as what is
- **Physical awareness:** How they sit, where they look, the choreography of bodies in space
- **Uncertainty:** The scene should feel unresolved; Ananya (and reader) should not be certain of Vikram's full agenda

- Has Vikram made his own ethical compromises that he's not sharing?
