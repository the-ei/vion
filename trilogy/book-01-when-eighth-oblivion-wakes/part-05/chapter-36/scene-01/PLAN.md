# Scene Plan: Morning Alone

## Summary

Ananya moves through her morning routines in her Palo Alto house, two weeks after the crisis that ended Part 4. The house she bought with Prometheus stock options now feels like evidence - every object carries a question about what she enabled, what she believed, what her ethics role actually meant. This opening scene establishes the post-crisis atmosphere of Part 5: the emergency has passed but its residue coats everything.

The scene tracks her from waking to late morning, moving through coffee ritual, deliberate news avoidance, the body's memory of crisis-mode adrenaline now fading into something heavier. She enters her home office where she keeps the documents she retained from Prometheus - a choice that may have legal consequences, but which she cannot bring herself to regret. The administrative leave that keeps her suspended between inside and outside the company feels less like limbo and more like a threshold.

## Key Elements

- Physical environment of the Palo Alto house: purchased with tech money, now feeling provisional
- Coffee ritual performed automatically while mind races elsewhere
- News avoidance as conscious strategy - she knows what they're saying and cannot bear to watch the simplification
- Body memory of crisis: the adrenaline fade, sleep still disrupted, appetite unreliable
- The documents in her home office: their presence a weight, a responsibility, potentially a weapon
- Administrative leave ambiguity: did she choose it or was it forced? Even she's unsure
- Weather and light: Bay Area winter gray, the quality of January light through windows
- First genuine moments of stillness since the crisis began

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Processing the aftermath; her relationship with her professional identity fundamentally disrupted; alone but not lonely - solitude as recovery

## Setting

- **Location:** Ananya's house in Palo Alto, California
- **Specific spaces:** Bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, home office
- **Time of day:** 6:30 AM - 11:00 AM
- **Season/Weather:** Mid-January 2034; overcast Bay Area winter; gray light, occasional drizzle

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 4.5 hours
- Position in chapter: Opening scene, establishing the post-crisis normal
- Pacing: Slow, deliberate - the first scene where time isn't compressed by emergency

## Page Target

- **Target:** 5 pages
- **Range:** 4-6 pages
- **Rationale:** Needs room for domestic detail and interiority that establishes Part 5's different rhythm from Part 4

## Stylistic Notes

- **Mode:** Predominantly Knausgaard - long domestic sequences, attention to mundane detail that now feels different
- **Carson intrusions:** Brief fragments when crisis memories surface unbidden - these should feel like interruptions, not sustained passages
- **Sentence structure:** Long, flowing sentences for routine activities; shorter, more fractured for memory intrusions
- **Attention to:** Light, texture, the material weight of objects, the silence of an empty house

## Connections

- **Next scene:** The video call with Priya will break the solitude and introduce family consequences of public action
- **Parent chapter:** Establishes the "morning after" tone that Chapter 36 extends across a full day
- **Thematic links:** Complicity and clean hands; what does her house, her career, her ethics role mean in retrospect?
