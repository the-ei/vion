# Scene Plan: Video Call with Priya

## Summary

Ananya's weekly video call with her daughter Priya (14) - the first since the crisis media coverage peaked. Priya lives primarily with her father, Ananya's ex-husband, and has witnessed her mother's sudden public visibility through news coverage and social media. The conversation must navigate what can be said and what cannot, what Priya understands and what she's projecting, what it means for a teenager to see her mother as a public figure in a contested narrative.

The scene captures the specific texture of video call communication - the slight lag, the frame's constraints, the way intimacy must work through a screen. Priya oscillates between pride and concern, curiosity and self-protection. She has questions she wants to ask and questions she's afraid to hear answered. Ananya tries to be honest while protecting her daughter from the full weight of adult complexity. The ex-husband's presence is felt just off-screen - audible occasionally, a reminder of the household Ananya is no longer part of.

## Key Elements

- Video call technology: the medium as character, its particular intimacies and distances
- Priya's mixed feelings: Is her mother a hero or in trouble? Both feel true
- What Priya has seen: news coverage, social media posts, perhaps classmates' comments
- The ex-husband off-screen: his venture capitalist worldview implicit in Priya's questions
- Mother-daughter negotiation: what to share, what to shield, the instinct to protect meeting the need for honesty
- Priya's growing autonomy: at 14, she's forming her own opinions about her mother's choices
- The scheduled nature of the call: this is their ritual, but today it carries different weight
- Physical distance bridged imperfectly: Priya in one house, Ananya in another, both in the Bay Area but worlds apart

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Navigating motherhood through the screen; seeing herself through her daughter's perception
- **Priya Ramaswamy (via video):** 14 years old; intelligent, perceptive, protective of herself; processing her mother's sudden visibility
- **Ex-husband (audible off-screen):** Present in the background; his reactions to Ananya's actions implicit in the conversation's subtext

## Setting

- **Location:** Ananya's home office (her side); Priya's bedroom in her father's house (Priya's side)
- **Time of day:** 12:30 PM - 1:45 PM
- **Technology:** Video call on laptop; the interface as part of the scene's texture
- **Season/Weather:** January gray continuing; Ananya's office lit by desk lamp against the overcast

## Stylistic Notes

- **Mode:** Balanced Knausgaard/Carson - Knausgaard for the conversation's flow and Ananya's observations; Carson for moments of emotional intensity
- **Dialogue:** Should feel naturalistic, with interruptions, half-finished thoughts, the way people actually talk
- **Video call texture:** Include the medium's idiosyncrasies - frozen frames, lag, the way faces look on screens
- **Interiority:** Ananya's internal responses to what Priya says, the gap between what she thinks and what she says
