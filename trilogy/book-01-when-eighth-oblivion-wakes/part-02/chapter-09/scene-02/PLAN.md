# Scene Plan: Family Dinner

## Summary

Evening in the Cole-Washington household. Jerome has emerged from his office for dinner, his mind still occupied by the documents he's been analyzing. Denise has made something simple after her own long day teaching. DeShawn is animated about a coding project - something involving AI APIs that makes Jerome's attention sharpen despite himself. The dinner becomes a study in disconnection: Jerome present in body but absent in mind, DeShawn speaking a technological language his father regards with suspicion, Denise managing the space between them.

The scene renders domestic life with Knausgaard specificity - what they eat, how they move around each other, the rhythms of a family that has been together long enough to have settled into comfortable grooves and worn-out paths. DeShawn's excitement about his project using Prometheus APIs creates dramatic irony: the reader will connect this to Jerome's investigation, though neither father nor son yet understands the link. The generational rift surfaces in their conversation about technology - DeShawn sees opportunity where Jerome sees threat - but remains civil. These are people who love each other across a gap they don't know how to close.

## Key Elements

- The kitchen and dining room as domestic stage - well-worn but loved
- Denise's exhaustion visible in small gestures
- DeShawn's animation about his coding project - genuine excitement
- First mention of Prometheus APIs in DeShawn's context (dramatic irony)
- Jerome's questions veering toward interrogation before he catches himself
- Food as ritual and connection - what they eat, who cooked, who cleans
- The television off (Denise's rule), but phones visible, attention fragmented
- Brief discussion of DeShawn's college plans - the future pressing
- Jerome's distraction visible to Denise, who notes but doesn't confront
- Post-dinner rhythms: DeShawn to his room, Denise grading, Jerome back to office

## Characters Present

- **Jerome Washington (POV):** Struggling to be present. His investigative mind keeps turning to the documents; his parental mind worries about DeShawn's tech enthusiasm; his husband mind knows he's failing Denise.
- **Denise Cole:** High school history teacher, 49. Her patience is real but finite. She carries the family's emotional weight while Jerome chases stories.
- **DeShawn Cole:** 17, talented coder. His voice is the voice of his generation - tech-native, opportunity-focused, impatient with his father's suspicion.

## Setting

- **Location:** Kitchen and dining area, Cole-Washington rowhouse, Baltimore
- **Time:** Same day as Scene 01, evening (6:30 PM - 8:30 PM)
- **Atmosphere:** Warm domestic interior against cold winter night outside

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 2 hours
- Dinner preparation, meal, cleanup
- The conversation ebbs and flows naturally
- Scene ends with family dispersing to separate activities

## Page Target

5-6 pages (~1,400-1,650 words)

## Stylistic Notes

- **Mode:** Knausgaard throughout - domestic detail elevated to significance
- **Dialogue:** Natural family rhythms, interruptions, half-finished thoughts
- **Physical detail:** Bodies in space - how they pass dishes, where they sit, who clears
- **Interior access:** Jerome's divided attention, his self-awareness about his failure to be present
- **Subtext:** What isn't said - Denise's concerns, DeShawn's distance from his father

## Connections to Adjacent Scenes

- **Previous (Scene 01):** Jerome's morning absorption in the investigation makes his evening distraction inevitable.
- **Next (Scene 03):** The late-night work session will show Jerome fully committed to the investigation, the domestic scene becoming what he escapes.
- **Chapter arc:** Establishes the family stakes - what Jerome risks when he pursues a story, who pays when he succeeds or fails.
