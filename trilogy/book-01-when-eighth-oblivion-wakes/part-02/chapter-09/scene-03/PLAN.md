# Scene Plan: Night Work and Sister

## Summary

Late night. Jerome has returned to his office after Denise went to bed. The house is dark except for his screens. He's deep in the documents now, mapping connections, following threads. The work has the quality of obsession - he hasn't looked at the time in hours. The scene alternates between his investigative process and a phone call with his sister Patricia about their mother's worsening dementia.

The Patricia call pulls Jerome out of abstraction and into the immediacy of family obligation. Their mother, living in Chicago, is declining. Patricia bears the primary burden of care; Jerome sends money when he can, visits when he can't afford not to, carries guilt like a stone in his chest. The conversation is loving but weighted with the things siblings can't say - resentment, exhaustion, the unfair distribution of care work. After the call, Jerome returns to his documents with new urgency. The systems he's investigating feel connected to his mother's vulnerability, though he couldn't articulate how.

As the night deepens, Jerome experiences a breakthrough: he realizes the algorithms aren't separate systems but implementations of common infrastructure. The hiring software, the insurance assessments, the lending decisions - they share architecture. They share data. They share a logic of sorting humans into categories. The scene ends in Carson mode as the scope becomes clear: fragmented sentences, white space, the vertiginous feeling of seeing something vast.

## Key Elements

- The home office at night - screens glowing, house silent
- Jerome's mapping process: physical (whiteboard) and digital (spreadsheets)
- Phone call with Patricia about their mother - interrupting but not breaking focus
- Mother's dementia details: what she forgets, what she remembers, who she still recognizes
- Patricia's exhaustion, her implicit accusation, their mutual love
- The guilt Jerome carries about his distance from care responsibilities
- Return to documents with sharpened attention
- The breakthrough: recognizing common infrastructure beneath separate applications
- First explicit connection to Prometheus Systems (named in a buried document)
- The emotional/intellectual shift as Jerome grasps the scale

## Characters Present

- **Patricia Cole (phone):** Jerome's sister, slightly younger. She manages their mother's care in Chicago, works full-time, carries resentment she's too kind to fully express.
- **Loretta Cole (mentioned):** Jerome's mother, early-stage dementia. Present through Patricia's reports and Jerome's memories.
