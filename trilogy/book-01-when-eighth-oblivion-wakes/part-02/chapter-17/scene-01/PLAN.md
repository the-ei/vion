# Scene Plan: The Synthesis

## Summary

Jerome's home office has transformed into a war room. Documents cover every surface. Multiple screens display different threads of investigation. A whiteboard - nearly full now - maps connections with dry-erase marker in three colors. Jerome has received Elena's documentation, Yusuf's testimony, his original source's documents, and his own financial mapping. Now, in the quiet of late night, he sees the shape of the system.

The scene follows Jerome through his synthesis process. He's not writing yet - he's understanding. Elena's MedAssist logs confirm what he suspected about healthcare triage. They match the algorithmic architecture he traced through corporate filings. Yusuf's testimony about gig work algorithms describes the same underlying logic: sorting humans, predicting behavior, optimizing extraction. The hiring algorithms he started with are just one application of a foundational infrastructure. And all of it traces back to Prometheus Systems.

Denise appears briefly, bringing coffee, her concern visible but restrained. Their marriage continues its tense negotiation - she supports his work without endorsing its costs. After she leaves, Jerome returns to his synthesis. By scene's end, he has a map that terrifies him: separate industries, separate applications, separate harms, all connected through shared AI infrastructure, all optimized to sort humans by perceived value. He doesn't yet have a story that can hold all this. But he has the shape of the truth.
