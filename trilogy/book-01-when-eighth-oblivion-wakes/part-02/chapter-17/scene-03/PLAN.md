# Scene Plan: The Inside

## Summary

Kevin Zhou POV. At Prometheus, his clean room access in full effect. A day of work that feels different now that he's documenting. He attends meetings where deployment metrics are discussed - the same systems Jerome is investigating from outside, seen here from inside, with the casual language of engineers who don't ask what their code does after it leaves their hands.

The scene renders Kevin Zhou's double consciousness: the competent engineer doing excellent work, the secret witness recording what he sees. In a deployment review meeting, he hears discussion of "resource allocation optimization" improvements for healthcare systems - what Elena calls MedAssist. He hears discussion of "behavioral prediction refinements" for labor platforms - what Yusuf experiences as algorithmic management. The internal language is clean, technical, divorced from consequence: "prediction accuracy," "engagement optimization," "churn modeling." Kevin Zhou translates in his head: sorting, surveillance, control.

After the meeting, he passes Ananya Ramaswamy in a hallway. Their eyes meet. Something passes between them - not recognition exactly, but question. She's asking something with her gaze: Are you seeing what I'm seeing? Kevin Zhou doesn't know how to answer. He adds the meeting notes to his private documentation. The scene ends with him understanding that inside and outside are just positions, and the truth they're seeing is the same truth.

## Key Elements

- Clean room access: Kevin Zhou's new position
- The work itself: competent, valuable, complicit
- Deployment review meeting: the language of internal optimization
- Healthcare systems discussed: MedAssist
- Labor platforms discussed: what Yusuf knows from below
- The translation: technical language to human consequence
- "Prediction accuracy" = sorting humans
- "Engagement optimization" = extracting behavior
- Ananya in the hallway: eyes meeting, question unspoken
- Documentation continuing: Kevin Zhou's private record
- Inside and outside seeing the same truth
