# Scene Plan: The Tour

## Summary

Kevin Zhou has accepted a preliminary meeting, and now he sees what acceptance means. Kevin Marsh escorts him to the clean room facility - a building within the building, separate security, no phones allowed. This is where Prometheus's most sensitive work happens, where the AI systems are developed that the company doesn't discuss publicly. The tour is conducted by Dr. Sarah Lin, head of clean room research, who explains what they're building with the confidence of a true believer.

What Kevin Zhou sees: AI systems designed for prediction, optimization, control. The hiring algorithms he traced are here, but they're the least of it. Social sentiment modeling predicts population-level attitudes, identifies "instability vectors." Behavioral prediction systems assess individual likelihood of various actions - protest, default, compliance. "Resource allocation optimization" determines who gets what in systems ranging from healthcare to housing to criminal justice. And most chilling: "social stability tools" being developed for deployment to governments. The technology is elegant, sophisticated, beautiful in its way. The applications are designed to sort humanity.

Dr. Lin speaks with genuine enthusiasm. She believes in this work - believes it's necessary, that only comprehensive prediction can prevent civilizational collapse. Kevin Zhou asks careful questions: What safeguards exist? Who determines deployment? How is bias addressed? Lin's answers are smooth, technical, and unsatisfying. By tour's end, Kevin Zhou understands he's been shown this deliberately - both as seduction and as warning. He knows; now the question is what he does with knowing.
