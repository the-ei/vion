# Scene Plan: The Choice

## Summary

Kevin Zhou accepts the promotion. The scene follows him through the formality: the meeting with Kevin Marsh, the handshake, the congratulations, the paperwork that makes him officially part of the clean room team. His face shows appropriate gratitude, appropriate excitement. Inside, something else is happening.

That night, alone in his apartment, Kevin Zhou opens a new encrypted folder on a personal drive completely separate from Prometheus systems. He begins documenting what he knows: the undocumented deployments he discovered, the clean room tour, the scope of what Prometheus is building, the specific applications he's been shown. He doesn't know what he'll do with this documentation. He doesn't know if he'll ever use it. But he's choosing to see, to remember, to preserve evidence of what is being built in his name.

The scene renders Kevin Zhou's documentation process in detail: what he records, how he protects it, the paranoia that is also professionalism. He's not ready to be a whistleblower. He doesn't have the connections, the platforms, the legal protection. What he has is access, and access means he can witness. His choice isn't heroic - it's a hedge, a preservation, a bet that knowing will matter eventually. The chapter ends with Kevin Zhou typing in darkness, screen glow on his face, choosing complicity that might become something else.

## Key Elements

- The formal acceptance: meeting with Marsh, paperwork, congratulations

- The interior reality: calculation, preservation, the choice to stay
