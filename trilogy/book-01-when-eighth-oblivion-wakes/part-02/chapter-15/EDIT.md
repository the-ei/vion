## Editorial Decisions

- **Cafeteria setting:** Natural light and living walls described as "productivity including the appearance of wellness" - the designed-for-optimization environment Kevin Zhou has "never once felt well" in
- **Performance detection:** Kevin Zhou reads Marsh's "executive presence" course training - both men performing roles
- **Architecture as narrative:** The transition from corporate warm to surgical cool marks threshold crossing
- **Phone as phantom limb:** The device separation creates physical/psychological discomfort that signals the stakes
- **"Was seen":** The passive voice for the retinal scan implies loss of agency at the moment of access
- **Clean room as literal/metaphorical:** The facility name operates on multiple levels - sterile environment, plausible deniability, containment
