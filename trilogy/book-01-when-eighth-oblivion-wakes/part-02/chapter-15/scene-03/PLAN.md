# Scene Plan: The Lonely Hours

## Summary

Night. Kevin Zhou games with James, their regular session, but tonight the banter feels hollow. Kevin Zhou is carrying knowledge he can't share - not with James who knows him only as WeiCode, not with colleagues who are either complicit or oblivious. The gaming session is rendered in its specific detail: the game they play, their competitive rhythms, the jokes that land and don't. Kevin Zhou makes excuses, logs off early. The void of his apartment swallows him.

After gaming, he tries to call his parents. This time the connection works - partially. His mother's face appears on screen, older than in his memory, worried in a way she's trying to hide. His father's voice comes from off-camera, gruff and brief. The conversation is surface-level: health, weather, his mother's garden, his father's retirement projects. Nothing about politics, nothing about the tensions between their countries, nothing about the fact that Kevin Zhou hasn't been home in six years and doesn't know when he can return. The call ends with I love you said in Mandarin, and Kevin Zhou sits in his apartment wondering when his parents became strangers.

The scene ends with Kevin Zhou thinking about Ananya Ramaswamy. He's seen her in meetings, dismissed her as ethics theater. But she's inside the system too, asking questions, navigating constraints. He doesn't know what she knows, but he wonders for the first time if his contempt for her was defensiveness - easier to mock ethics than to have them.

## Key Elements

- Gaming session with James: the surface normalcy, the hollow feeling
- The game itself: specific, competitive, the last remnant of Kevin Zhou's social life
- Making excuses, logging off early
- The empty apartment, the void of solitary existence
- Phone call to parents: connection working but not connecting
- Mother's face, father's voice, the surface conversation
- What's not said: politics, distance, the impossibility of return
- The Mandarin "I love you" as both connection and loss
- Post-call silence, Kevin Zhou alone with everything
- Ananya entering his thoughts: is his contempt actually fear?

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Isolated, carrying knowledge, questioning his defenses.
- **James (voice):** Gaming friend, present as voice and avatar, absent as person.
- **Kevin Zhou's mother:** Seen briefly via video, her worry visible, her distance palpable.
- **Kevin Zhou's father:** Heard only, brief, the gulf between them audible.
- **Ananya Ramaswamy (thought):** Present in Kevin Zhou's mind, his contempt becoming question.

## Setting

- **Location:** Kevin Zhou's apartment - gaming setup, then quiet darkness
- **Time:** Evening (8:00 PM - 12:00 AM)
- **Atmosphere:** The specific loneliness of a life organized around work, the void where life should be

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 4 hours
- 8:00 PM: Gaming session begins
- 10:00 PM: Logs off early
- 10:30 PM: Video call with parents
- 11:00 PM: Post-call silence, Ananya in his thoughts
