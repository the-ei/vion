# Scene Plan: The Approach

## Summary

Kevin Marsh finds Kevin Zhou in the Prometheus cafeteria. The setting is deliberate - public but not formal, collegial but not accidental. Marsh is Kevin Zhou's manager, a product director in his forties who has risen through the company by understanding what shouldn't be said. Their conversation begins with pleasantries: How's the project going? Getting enough sleep? The usual managerial performance of concern.

Then Marsh shifts. Senior leadership has noticed Kevin Zhou's work - his queries, his questions, his late-night access patterns. The company values curiosity, Marsh says. The company appreciates people who see what others miss. Would Kevin Zhou be interested in projects where his skills would be most valuable? The offer isn't explicit yet - Marsh is too skilled for that - but Kevin Zhou hears what's being offered: access, in exchange for something he can't yet name.

The scene renders the corporate dance in precise detail. Marsh's language is calibrated, his affect warm, his implications clear. Kevin Zhou listens with the analytical attention he brings to code, parsing subtext, identifying variables. He's being recruited for something - something connected to what he's discovered - and the recruitment is a test. His response will determine what happens next. He expresses measured interest, asks questions Marsh deflects, agrees to a preliminary meeting. The scene ends with Kevin Zhou returning to his desk, his mind racing, understanding that his investigation has been noticed and is being redirected.

## Key Elements

- The cafeteria as deliberate choice: public, corporate, controlled
- Kevin Marsh as company man: smooth, intelligent, opaque
- The conversation's structure: pleasantries, pivot, offer
- Marsh acknowledging Kevin Zhou's "curiosity" - code for his investigation
- The offer: "expanded responsibility," access to higher-level projects
- What's unsaid: we know what you've been looking at, we'd rather you looked with us
- Kevin Zhou's analytical listening, parsing corporate speech
- His measured response: interest without commitment
- The handshake, the return to desk, the racing mind
- Understanding: his investigation led to this, question is where this leads

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Navigating a recruitment that is also a warning.
- **Kevin Marsh:** Product director, mid-40s. His smoothness is his expertise; his opacity is his value to the company. He was once Kevin Zhou - ambitious, brilliant, willing not to ask questions.
