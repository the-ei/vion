# Scene Plan: The Architecture of Extraction

## Summary

Jerome's investigation has shifted from documenting to mapping. His home office has transformed - papers covering every surface, multiple browser windows open, a physical whiteboard accumulating connections in dry-erase marker. The scene follows Jerome through several days of financial analysis, compressed into investigative rhythm. He's following the money: who owns Vertex Analytics, where the investment comes from, how the corporate structure obscures accountability.

The mapping reveals a constellation. Vertex Analytics is owned by a holding company called Meridian Capital Partners. Meridian receives investment from a venture fund that includes partners from Prometheus Systems. The fund also holds stakes in healthcare AI companies (one of which licenses MedAssist), insurance risk modeling firms, and criminal justice analytics vendors. The separate stories Jerome has been tracking - hiring algorithms, healthcare systems, financial discrimination - are not separate at all. They share architecture. They share investors. They share logic.

The scene renders Jerome's investigative methodology: the corporate filings he pulls, the SEC documents he searches, the database subscriptions that eat into his already-thin budget. His whiteboard fills with names and arrows. By scene's end, he's identified Prometheus Systems as the architectural center - the company whose foundational AI models power the specific applications. He doesn't know about Ananya or Kevin Zhou; he only knows there's a there there. He emails a congressional staffer he's identified as potentially sympathetic, asking for a meeting in DC.

## Key Elements

- Jerome's office as investigation headquarters - papers, screens, whiteboard
