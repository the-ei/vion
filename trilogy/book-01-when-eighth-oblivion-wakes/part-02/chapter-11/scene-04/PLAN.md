# Scene Plan: The Shape of It

## Summary

The breakthrough scene. Late at night, Kevin Zhou sits at his workstation - home this time, not office - with everything he's found spread across multiple screens. He's been mapping connections: Sieve's deployment architecture, the external partners, the data flows. What began as technical anomaly has become something else. Now, in the quiet hours, he sees what it means.

Kevin Zhou traces Sieve to specific applications. Vertex Analytics uses Prometheus models to power hiring software that determines who gets job interviews. MedAssist - deployed in healthcare systems across the country - uses Prometheus infrastructure to triage patients and recommend treatments. Insurance companies use it for risk scoring. Criminal justice systems use it for sentencing recommendations. All undocumented. All designed to be invisible. All tracing back to the foundational AI models Kevin Zhou helped build.

For the first time, Kevin Zhou understands that his code has addresses. The abstractions he works in - tensors, gradients, optimization functions - terminate in bodies, in decisions about human lives. He built the infrastructure; he didn't ask what it would be used for. Now he knows. The scene ends with Kevin Zhou at his desk, past midnight, laptop still open, understanding for the first time what it means to have built something. He closes the laptop. He opens it again. He doesn't know what to do with what he knows.

## Key Elements

- Late night at home, multiple screens, the final mapping
- Sieve's deployment architecture made visible
- Connection to specific applications: Vertex (hiring), MedAssist (healthcare), insurance, criminal justice
- Kevin Zhou's realization: his code affects bodies
- The abstraction of engineering confronting concrete human stakes
- Ananya Ramaswamy's name appearing in ethics review documents - her signature on assessments that now feel inadequate
- Kevin Zhou's contempt for ethics-washing complicated by new knowledge
- The question: what does he do now?
- No resolution - the chapter ends in suspended uncertainty
- The glowing screen, the dark room, the weight of knowledge

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Alone with revelation. His identity as builder confronted by what has been built.
- **Ananya Ramaswamy (documents only):** Her name appears in ethics reviews for some of the systems Kevin Zhou is tracing. She becomes real to him as a question.
- **The system:** Present as map, as architecture, as the shape of consequences.
