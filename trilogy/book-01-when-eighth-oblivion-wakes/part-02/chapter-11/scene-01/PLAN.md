# Scene Plan: The Machine's Servant

## Summary

Kevin Zhou's morning begins in his San Francisco apartment - sparse, expensive, barely lived in. He's 28, one of Prometheus Systems' most valuable engineers, and his living space reflects everything about his life: high cost, low presence, surfaces that never accumulate the residue of living. He wakes to an alarm he doesn't need, makes coffee he drinks while coding, eats a protein bar that serves as both breakfast and proof he's taking care of himself. The routine is efficient because efficiency is how Kevin Zhou understands value.

The commute is equally sterile: a self-driving company shuttle from his building to the Prometheus campus, during which Kevin Zhou reviews code on his tablet. The campus is its own city - glass and steel and cultivated greenery, designed to feel like the future has already arrived. Kevin Zhou barely notices its beauty anymore; it's just where the work happens. He arrives at his building, passes through security, settles into his workstation with the particular relief of someone who is most himself when facing code.

The day proceeds normally until it doesn't. Kevin Zhou notices an anomaly: computational resources - significant ones - allocated to a project that doesn't appear in any documentation he has access to. He notes it, files it, continues working. But the anomaly nags at him. He runs queries that reveal more questions than answers. By day's end, what began as routine curiosity has become something else - the first thread of an investigation he didn't intend to begin.

## Key Elements

- Kevin Zhou's apartment rendered in precise, empty detail
- Morning routine as efficiency ritual
- The self-driving shuttle as transition space
- Prometheus campus as corporate utopia - beautiful, controlled, slightly unreal
- Kevin Zhou's workstation as natural habitat
- The anomaly: computational resources without documented allocation
- Kevin Zhou's technical methodology - the queries he runs, the patterns he notices
- His relationship to code as relationship to self
- Brief interactions with colleagues - cordial, professional, distant
- First mention of undocumented project (later revealed as "Sieve")

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** 28, senior engineer. His brilliance is his identity; his isolation is its cost. This scene shows his competence and his loneliness as aspects of the same life.
- **Prometheus colleagues (background):** Seen but not known - Kevin Zhou's relationship to work is to work itself, not to coworkers.
- **The anomaly:** Present as data, as question, as the first crack in Kevin Zhou's comfortable ignorance.

## Setting

- **Location:** Kevin Zhou's apartment (SOMA, San Francisco), then company shuttle, then Prometheus Systems campus
- **Time:** Mid-March 2033, morning (7:00 AM - 6:00 PM)
- **Atmosphere:** The sleek emptiness of high-end tech life - everything designed, nothing personal

## Timeline

- 7:30 AM: Shuttle to campus
- 8:00 AM: Arrive, begin work
- 4:00 PM: Notice the anomaly
