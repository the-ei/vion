# Scene Plan: Following the Thread

## Summary

Several days compressed. Kevin Zhou has become obsessed with the anomaly, though he'd never use that word. He tells himself it's technical curiosity, professional diligence, the instinct that made him one of Prometheus's best engineers. The scene follows his investigation through late nights and stolen moments - queries run during lunch, logs examined when he should be sleeping, a pattern emerging from noise.

Kevin Zhou's methodology is rendered in detail: the specific queries, the trace routes, the logs he accesses. He explains to himself (and thus to the reader) what he's looking for and why. The technical details should feel real without requiring expertise - we understand through his competence. What he discovers: API endpoints that shouldn't exist, serving systems he's never been briefed on. References in metadata to "Sieve" - a project name that appears nowhere in his official documentation but recurs in system logs like a ghost.

He traces Sieve's connections carefully. The endpoints link to external partners: a data analytics firm (Vertex Analytics, though he doesn't yet know Jerome's investigation started there), healthcare applications, insurance systems, criminal justice databases. The Prometheus models he helped build are being deployed in ways no one told him about. Kevin Zhou doesn't yet grasp the human consequences - he sees architecture, not bodies - but he understands he's looking at something deliberately hidden. The question is why.

## Key Elements

- Time compression: several days of investigation
- Kevin Zhou's technical methodology in precise detail
- Late nights at the office and at home, the boundary blurring
- Discovery of undocumented API endpoints
- The "Sieve" project name appearing in traces
- External connections: Vertex Analytics, healthcare, insurance, criminal justice
- Kevin Zhou's realization that Prometheus's reach exceeds its public claims
- His isolation as both obstacle and advantage - no one to talk to, no one monitoring him
- The ethical implications glimpsed but not yet felt
- Brief interaction with his manager, Kevin Marsh - cordial, unrevealing

## Characters Present

- **Kevin Zhou (POV):** Deep in technical problem-solving mode, increasingly disturbed by what he finds.
- **Kevin Marsh (brief):** Kevin Zhou's manager. Appears briefly, asks about Kevin Zhou's hours, encourages work-life balance in the way managers do when they don't mean it.
- **The system itself:** Present as character - the infrastructure Kevin Zhou is tracing, its architecture and its secrets.

## Setting

- **Location:** Prometheus campus (office, server rooms), Kevin Zhou's apartment
- **Time:** Several days in mid-March 2033
- **Atmosphere:** The timeless quality of deep technical work - day and night losing distinction

## Timeline
