# Scene Plan: 2 AM Frequencies

## Summary

Night. The household sleeps - Halima exhausted from the clinic visit, Amina finished with homework - but Yusuf is awake. This is his time, the hours he's carved out for the self that doesn't show up on any app, that doesn't get rated, that isn't optimized for anything. He sits at a small desk in the corner of the room he shares with no one (Amina has the bedroom, Halima the other, Yusuf sleeps on a futon in what's supposed to be a dining area), headphones on, making music.

The scene enters Yusuf's creative process: the DAW on his phone, the beats he builds layer by layer, the sounds he samples and shapes. His music is good - he knows it's good - and knowing feels dangerous, like hope that could cut him. Tonight he's working on something that feels different: a track built on the sounds of gig work - notification chimes, the GPS voice, the synthetic sounds that structure his labor. He's turning the algorithm's language against itself, making art from the machinery of his oppression.

The scene should feel different from the rest of the chapter: slower, more internal, the closest thing Yusuf has to church. He doesn't share his music with anyone. He saves the track, titles it something only he'll understand ("INVISIBLE EMPLOYER"), and finally sleeps. The alarm will come in a few hours. But for this moment, in the dark with his headphones, he's neither worker nor son nor statistic. He's an artist. The dream that sustains, even when he can't afford to believe in it.

## Key Elements

- Late night, household asleep, Yusuf's private hours
- His makeshift workspace - phone, headphones, a corner that's his
- The DAW and his process - building beats, sampling, layering
- This track specifically: built from gig work sounds, turning surveillance into art
- Yusuf's relationship to his music: pride, secrecy, fear of hope
- No audience - he's never shared these tracks
- The title "INVISIBLE EMPLOYER" - bitter, funny, true
- His body finally relaxing, the performance of survival paused
- Sleep coming, knowing the alarm will shatter this
- The futon, the dark, the brief peace before morning
