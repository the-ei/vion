## Editorial Decisions

- **Futon in dining area:** The specific living arrangement - sleeping in "what the lease optimistically calls a dining area" - establishes class position without editorializing
- **Navigation by feel:** Yusuf's knowledge of "every creak, every footstep" shows long habitation and consideration for sleeping family
- **Algorithm as extraction:** The apps "already churning, already deciding" - Yusuf names what the system calls "efficiency" as "something closer to extraction"
- **Halima's defiance:** Her cooking despite doctor's orders is presented as "both love and defiance in equal measure" - dignity preserved
- **Refugee history:** The suugo smell connects to "the kitchen of the refugee camp where she learned to make much from little, the kitchen in Mogadishu that exists now only in her stories"
- **Knausgaard mode for domestic detail:** The home scene slows down for sensory immersion (smell, language, light)
