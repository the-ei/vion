## Summary

Late afternoon. No, wait - per the chapter plan, this is meant to be Elena's clinic. Let me reconsider: Yusuf is in Minneapolis, Elena is in Phoenix. Revision: Halima travels to Phoenix periodically to see a specialist, and Elena Varga at Desert Sage has become her regular NP there - perhaps Halima has family in Phoenix, or the specialist requires quarterly visits. Alternatively, the clinic is a Minneapolis clinic also using MedAssist. Let me align with the cleaner interpretation: Halima sees Elena during visits to family in Phoenix, and this scene takes place during one such visit.

Yusuf accompanies his mother on her Phoenix trip to see relatives and get her specialized care. The waiting room scene takes place at Desert Sage, where Elena Varga calls Halima's name. Yusuf observes the waiting room as a microcosm: other patients whose bodies bear similar marks of labor and precarity. The fluorescent lights, the worn chairs, the MedAssist screen visible at the nursing station. He watches Elena - competent, tired, kind - and notes that his mother trusts her. After the appointment, Halima emerges with prescriptions, controlled optimism, the familiar rhythm of chronic disease management.
