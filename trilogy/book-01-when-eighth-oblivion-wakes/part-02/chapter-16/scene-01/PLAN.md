# Scene Plan: The Reach

## Summary

Jerome's email arrives through Keyana Wright, an organizer at a workers' advocacy group Yusuf has connected with. Keyana has been collecting testimonies from gig workers, documenting algorithmic injustices, building a case that hasn't yet found its audience. When Jerome contacted the organization looking for sources, Keyana thought of Yusuf - articulate, angry, and active on social media in ways that caught attention.

Yusuf's initial response is dismissal. He's seen journalists before - the ones who parachute in for a quote, who write their stories and win their awards and change nothing. He's cynical about media the way he's cynical about everything that promises change without delivering it. But something makes him look up Jerome Washington. He reads the newsletter, finds the old investigations, sees the Pulitzer from 2023 for financial crisis reporting. He recognizes something in Jerome's writing: an anger that persisted past the point where anger was profitable.

The scene follows Yusuf through the decision: Keyana's introduction, the research, the internal debate. A conversation with Amina crystallizes his thinking. She asks why he'd talk to a reporter. His answer surprises him: because maybe someone with a platform should hear what it's like, and because he's tired of being invisible. By scene's end, Yusuf has responded to Jerome, agreeing to meet.

## Key Elements

- Keyana Wright's email: introduction, context, Jerome's request
- Yusuf's immediate dismissal - another journalist, another nothing
- Research into Jerome Washington: newsletter, old investigations, Pulitzer
- Recognition of kindred anger in Jerome's writing
- Yusuf's cynicism about media, about change, about being heard
- Conversation with Amina about why he'd participate
- Her question sharpening his thinking
- His answer: tired of invisibility, willing to try
- Response email sent, interview agreed
- Anticipation and wariness mixed

## Characters Present

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Deciding whether to trust a stranger with his story.
- **Keyana Wright (email, then brief call):** Worker advocacy organizer, late 20s. Connected Yusuf and Jerome, vouches for the journalist's intentions.
- **Amina Hassan:** Present for conversation about the decision. Her questions illuminate Yusuf's motivations.
