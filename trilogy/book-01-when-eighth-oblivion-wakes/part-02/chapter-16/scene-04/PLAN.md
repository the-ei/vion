# Scene Plan: Aftermath

## Summary

The days after the interview. Yusuf replays the conversation in his mind, analyzing what he said, what he should have said, what he revealed that he hadn't meant to. The interview gave him something unexpected: permission. Not from Jerome, but from himself - permission to take his anger seriously, to believe it might matter, to stop performing carelessness as protection against disappointment.

Then Halima has a health scare. Blood sugar crash at work, the cleaning job at the hotel. Yusuf gets the call, picks her up, takes her to urgent care. Not Elena's clinic - they're in Minneapolis - but a similar space: fluorescent lights, plastic chairs, the particular purgatory of the uninsured. While waiting, Yusuf texts Jerome with a follow-up thought, something he wished he'd said in the interview about how the apps know when you're desperate. Jerome responds immediately. A connection is forming.

Halima emerges from the urgent care with adjusted medications, instructions to reduce stress (as if that were possible), and the controlled optimism of chronic disease management. In the car ride home, she thanks Yusuf for being there. He doesn't know what to say. The chapter ends with Yusuf at home, after Halima and Amina are asleep, working on the political tracks. The interview gave him something else, too: confidence that what he's making deserves to exist.
