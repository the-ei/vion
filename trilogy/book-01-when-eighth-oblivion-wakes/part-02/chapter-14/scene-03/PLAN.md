# Scene Plan: What It Does

## Summary

Elena alone at night, investigating. The children are asleep, Abuela retired to her room, the house quiet. Elena sits at the kitchen table with her tablet, doing what she's technically allowed but practically discouraged: reviewing patient records, comparing MedAssist recommendations, looking for the pattern she's sensed. What she finds confirms her worst suspicions.

The scene renders Elena's investigation in detail. She pulls Roberto's full case file, including the algorithmic output that determined his triage priority. The factors that weighted his assessment: employment classification (gig/irregular - flagged), insurance status (none - flagged), address (zip code associated with low follow-up compliance - flagged), prior visit history (sparse - flagged as "low engagement"). His actual symptoms - the chest pain, the shortness of breath, the diaphoresis - were weighted less than his demographics in the final priority score.

Elena compares Roberto's case to others. A patient with similar symptoms but different demographics was seen within fifteen minutes. The pattern is clear: MedAssist doesn't just recommend treatment, it sorts patients into categories of deserving and undeserving. The system's efficiency is measured in throughput, not outcomes. Certain patients are optimized out of urgency.

By scene's end, Elena has documented what she's found - screenshots, analysis, timestamps. She hides the files in a folder of recipes, feeling like a criminal. The irony is not lost on her: she's doing her job, protecting patients, and it feels like betrayal. The system has made care itself subversive.

## Key Elements

- Night, kitchen table, the solitude of investigation
- Elena accessing records she's technically allowed to see
- Roberto's full algorithmic profile revealed
- The factors that determined his priority: employment, insurance, address, engagement history
- Symptoms weighted less than demographics
- Comparison to similar patients with different outcomes
- The pattern crystallizing: sorting humans by perceived value
- Elena's documentation process: screenshots, analysis, hiding evidence
- The feeling of transgression while doing the right thing
- Recipes folder as hiding place - the domestic made covert
