# Scene Plan: The Waiting Room

## Summary

The chapter opens with the death that will change everything, rendered in fragments rather than chronology. Roberto Delgado-Fuentes - 52, construction worker, uninsured, undocumented status suspected but never confirmed - came to the clinic with chest pain. Elena saw him briefly during triage, noted concerning symptoms, flagged him for urgent attention. But MedAssist had already categorized him as low-priority based on factors Elena couldn't see in the system's black box: his employment classification, his lack of insurance, his home address in a zip code associated with "low follow-up compliance."

The scene moves through time non-linearly: Roberto in the waiting room, then Elena with another patient, then the code called, then Elena running, then CPR that doesn't work, then the body. The waiting room where it happened is the same room where Elena sees patients every day. The fluorescent lights. The plastic chairs. The MedAssist terminal humming in the corner. The death feels both extraordinary and ordinary - another failure of a system designed to fail certain people.

Days later, Maria Delgado-Fuentes appears at the clinic, asking questions Elena cannot answer. Why didn't they help him faster? Was there something they could have done? Elena faces the widow with nothing to offer but presence. She cannot explain that an algorithm decided her husband could wait. She doesn't yet have the language for what happened. She only knows that something is broken, and Roberto's body is the proof.

## Key Elements

- Roberto Delgado-Fuentes as a presence: his symptoms, his waiting, his death
- Non-linear structure: fragments of that day assembled into meaning
- Elena's triage interaction - she saw something wrong, couldn't override the system

- The code called, the CPR, the failure
- The waiting room afterward - same space, different forever
- Maria Delgado-Fuentes appearing days later, needing answers
- Elena's inability to explain, her guilt
- The specific medical details: what Roberto presented with, what killed him
- The first crystallization of Elena's anger
