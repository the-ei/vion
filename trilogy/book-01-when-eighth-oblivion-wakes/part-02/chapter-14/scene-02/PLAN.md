# Scene Plan: The Meeting

## Summary

The institutional response to Roberto's death. Elena meets with Dr. Katherine Reyes, the clinic director, in a conversation that reveals how systems protect themselves. Dr. Reyes is concerned - genuinely, as far as Elena can tell - but her concern is bounded by institutional imperatives. The meeting is framed as support for Elena, acknowledgment of trauma, but its true function is containment.

Dr. Reyes reviews the incident: patient presented with symptoms, was triaged according to protocol, waited for care, experienced cardiac event, could not be resuscitated. The language is careful, clinical, designed to describe without assigning cause. Elena asks about MedAssist's role in the triage decision. Dr. Reyes responds smoothly: MedAssist provides recommendations, clinicians make decisions, the system is a tool not an authority. But Elena knows the reality - there's never enough time to second-guess every recommendation, the system's suggestions become the truth by default.

The meeting's subtext emerges: Dr. Reyes is advising Elena to process her trauma, take time if needed, and not pursue questions that might complicate the clinic's position. She's not threatening - she's too skilled for that - but Elena hears what's being said. The institutional response to Roberto's death is to absorb it, document it, move past it. Elena leaves the meeting understanding that whatever she does next, she'll do alone.

## Key Elements

- Dr. Reyes's office: the administrative space, the distance from patient care
- The meeting framed as support, functioning as containment
- Dr. Reyes's genuine concern bounded by institutional loyalty
- Review of the incident in clinical language
- Elena's questions about MedAssist met with careful answers
- The reality of algorithmic authority vs. the fiction of clinical autonomy
- Subtle warning: don't make waves, don't complicate things
- Dr. Reyes's character: not villainous, trapped by role
- Elena's isolation becoming clear
- The decision forming: to investigate on her own

## Characters Present
