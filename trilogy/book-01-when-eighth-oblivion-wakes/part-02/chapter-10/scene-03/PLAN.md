# Scene Plan: The Pattern

## Summary

Midday. Elena's lunch break is interrupted by a call from Daniel - brief, loving, logistical. He's doing okay in Flagstaff, the project is on schedule, he'll be home in two weeks. She can hear the construction site behind his voice, the world of physical labor that is his domain. The call is comfort and distance both - she misses his body, his presence, the way he grounds her. After the call, she eats a protein bar at her desk, documents morning patients, prepares for the afternoon.

The scene pivots to a staff meeting where Dr. Katherine Reyes discusses MedAssist implementation metrics. The system is working well, Dr. Reyes reports. Efficiency up, documentation time down, diagnostic accuracy within acceptable ranges. Elena half-listens until something catches her attention: the "resource optimization" metrics. She asks a question about how MedAssist prioritizes patient recommendations. Dr. Reyes's answer is smooth, technical, reassuring. But Elena notices that the AI's suggestions seem to cluster - certain patients consistently recommended for specialist referrals, others not. She can't articulate the pattern yet, but something is forming in her mind.

The afternoon blurs. More patients, more MedAssist interactions. Elena begins paying attention differently - watching the recommendations, noting which patients receive them. By day's end, she's noticed something: the system's suggestions seem to correlate with factors that shouldn't matter medically. Employment status. Insurance type. Address. She doesn't have proof, only an accumulating unease.

## Key Elements

- Phone call with Daniel - intimacy across distance
- The protein bar lunch, the documentation, the exhaustion that never quite lifts
- Staff meeting as institutional ritual
- Dr. Reyes presenting MedAssist metrics with corporate smoothness
- Elena's question and the answer that doesn't satisfy
- Afternoon patients as accelerating blur
- Elena's attention sharpening - watching MedAssist differently
- The pattern emerging: non-medical factors correlating with recommendations
- Other staff accepting MedAssist uncritically
- Elena's isolation in her concern
