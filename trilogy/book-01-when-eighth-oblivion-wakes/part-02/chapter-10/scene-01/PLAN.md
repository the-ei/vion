# Scene Plan: Before the Day

## Summary

Elena Varga's morning begins at 5:30 AM in darkness. Her alarm is a soft tone, nothing that would wake the children. She moves through the small house like someone who has memorized its geography in the dark - bathroom, kitchen, the ritual of caffeine. Abuela Carmen is already awake, as she always is, sitting with coffee and Spanish-language news on the tablet, keeping her voice low. A brief exchange between them: Abuela will get Sofia and Mateo ready for school/daycare, as she does every day Elena works, which is most days.

The scene follows Elena through her morning preparations and drive to the Desert Sage Community Health Center. We ride with her through South Phoenix neighborhoods she knows intimately - the dollar stores and payday lenders, the apartment complexes where her patients live, the strip malls that speak to an economy of survival rather than prosperity. She parks in the clinic lot as the sun rises, orange light on stucco walls. Inside, she exchanges greetings with colleagues, checks the day's patient load, prepares for what she knows will be too much compressed into too little time.

## Key Elements

- Elena's dark morning routine - efficiency born of repetition
- Abuela Carmen as silent partner in family survival
- The children sleeping, their presence felt but not seen
- Brief text exchange with Daniel in Flagstaff - good morning, love you, be safe
- The drive through South Phoenix - geography as social text
- Desert landscape at dawn - sparse beauty against economic harshness
- Arrival at clinic - the building's inadequacy visible
- Morning interactions with staff: receptionist Maria, Dr. Reyes glimpsed
- The patient roster pulled up on screen - 18 patients scheduled, more will walk in
- Elena's internal preparation - the small pill she takes with cold coffee, unnamed

## Characters Present

- **Elena Varga (POV):** 34, nurse practitioner. Her competence is physical - she moves with practiced efficiency. Her exhaustion is also physical, carried in her shoulders, managed through routine.
- **Abuela Carmen:** Elena's grandmother, early 70s. Diabetic, helping raise her great-grandchildren. Her presence is quiet strength.
- **Daniel Varga (text):** Elena's husband, construction worker currently in Flagstaff. Present through brief phone contact.
- **Clinic staff (background):** Maria at reception, a medical assistant, the shape of a typical morning.

## Setting

- **Location:** Elena's home (South Phoenix), then commute, then Desert Sage Community Health Center
- **Time:** Early March 2033, dawn (5:30 AM - 8:00 AM)
- **Atmosphere:** Pre-dawn quiet giving way to the harsh light and heat of Arizona morning

## Timeline

- Duration: Approximately 2.5 hours
- 5:30 AM: Wake, prepare
- 6:15 AM: Leave house, brief exchange with Abuela
- 6:30 AM: Drive through South Phoenix
- 7:00 AM: Arrive at clinic, prepare for day
- 8:00 AM: First patients

- **Interior access:** Elena's thoughts about her family, her work, her quiet anxieties
- **Geographic detail:** South Phoenix as place with history and meaning
