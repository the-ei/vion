# Scene Plan: Discovery

## Summary

Yusuf is scrolling through his phone when he sees it. Jerome's investigation has gone viral, and buried in the documentation is his mother's name: Halima Hassan, 52, diabetes complications exacerbated by AI monitoring failure, deceased. His mother, who died three days after the initial crisis, whose death he's still processing, is now evidence in someone else's story. The scene opens with the specific, physical shock of seeing private grief made public.

Halima died in the days following the initial crisis - the damage from the monitoring failure compounded, she was admitted to the hospital, she didn't recover. Yusuf has barely had time to grieve, and now he finds her name on thousands of screens. His first reaction is rage: who gave them the right? His mother's life, reduced to a data point proving a thesis. The scene follows his immediate reaction - the phone thrown, the pacing, the tears - before he begins to read more carefully and sees Elena Varga's name attached to the documentation. The nurse who treated his mother. The person who was there.

## Key Elements

- The discovery: scrolling, then stopping, the name on screen
- Halima's death: what happened, briefly rendered (three days after the crisis, complications)
- The immediate rage: privacy violated, grief exploited
- The physical reaction: phone thrown, pacing, the body's protest
- Reading more carefully: finding Elena's name, understanding who documented this
- The context: Jerome's story, the Prometheus cover-up, the "Eighth Oblivion" framing
- Amina's reaction: she's been reading too, she found it first, she was going to tell him
- The family's private grief now public: what this means for them
- The decision forming: should he reach out? To Elena? To the journalist?
- The scene's close: Yusuf holding his phone, his mother's name still on screen
