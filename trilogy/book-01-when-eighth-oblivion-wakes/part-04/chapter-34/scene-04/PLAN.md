# Scene Plan: Voice

## Summary

Late night. Amina is asleep, the apartment quiet. Yusuf is awake with his phone and his guitar, working on the song he's been writing for months. The lyrics have always been about precarity, about algorithmic control, about the performance of productivity - but now they have his mother's death woven into them. The song is becoming testimony.

The scene follows Yusuf's decision to record something - not just participate in threads, but create content that's unmistakably his. Maybe it's a video of himself playing the song. Maybe it's a spoken-word piece over his music. Maybe it's just him talking to the camera about what happened, his own story, his own voice. Whatever form it takes, it's a commitment to visibility. He's choosing to be a participant in the story, not just a victim of it. By scene's end, Yusuf has recorded something and is about to share it. The chapter closes with his thumb hovering over "post" - not heroism, just the ordinary courage of speaking when you've been told to be quiet.
