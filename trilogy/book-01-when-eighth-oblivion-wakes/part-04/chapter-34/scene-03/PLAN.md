# Scene Plan: Community

## Summary

Yusuf reaches out. First to Elena Varga, the nurse whose name was attached to his mother's documentation. The conversation is brief, awkward, painful - Elena is kind but busy, Yusuf is grateful but guarded. She explains what happened, how she tried to help, why she documented. She doesn't apologize for making his mother's case public because she believes it was right, but she acknowledges his grief. Yusuf finds himself thanking her before he knows he's going to.

Then other connections form. Other gig workers who experienced the crisis have been reaching out on social media, forming ad-hoc communities. Yusuf finds a thread where people are sharing their stories: the delivery driver whose app stranded her in a dangerous neighborhood, the rideshare worker who couldn't get paid for a week, the warehouse worker whose AI manager logged her for absences she couldn't avoid. These aren't famous or powerful people; they're people like him. And they're talking, organizing, supporting each other. Yusuf starts participating - first reading, then commenting, then sharing his own story.

## Key Elements

- The call with Elena: brief, awkward, real
- Elena's explanation: what happened, why she documented
- Yusuf's unexpected gratitude: thanking her before he plans to
- Social media threads: other gig workers sharing stories
- The community forming: people like him, finding each other
- Specific stories: the delivery driver, the rideshare worker, the warehouse worker
- Yusuf participating: first reading, then commenting, then sharing
- His story: what happened to his family, what happened to him
- The response: others recognizing themselves in his words
- Organizers reaching out: people who've been fighting algorithmic exploitation

## Characters Present
