# Scene Plan: The Decision

## Summary

Late night, Day 5. Ananya has made her decision: she will share her evidence, but through multiple channels simultaneously. Jerome gets the story, but she'll also provide documentation to regulatory bodies and Congressional staff she trusts. If any single channel is suppressed, the others continue. This is not heroism; it's strategy. She knows the system well enough to game it.

The scene follows Ananya's night of commitment: creating secure copies of her evidence, establishing encrypted channels, drafting communications for each recipient. She contacts Dr. Elise Thornton, who agrees to corroborate from inside the company. She sends a message to Elena, establishing direct contact: they now share documentation, can support each other's accounts. By dawn, the network is in place. Ananya sends the final message to Jerome: she's ready. The chapter closes with her watching the sunrise from her apartment window, knowing she's crossed a line that can't be uncrossed.
