# Scene Plan: The Offer

## Summary

Day 5. Ananya has been in contact with Jerome Washington - encrypted messages, careful language, the dance of source and journalist. Now he makes a direct offer: he has the platform, the audience, and the investigative infrastructure to tell this story properly. He's also in touch with Elena Varga, whose ground-level documentation complements whatever Ananya might share. If Ananya is willing to provide her evidence, Jerome can publish. But first, they need to talk honestly about what this means.

The scene follows their secure video call - Ananya in her apartment, Jerome in his DC home office. They discuss not just what Ananya has, but what publishing it would mean. Jerome is honest: the story will matter, but it won't change everything; it might not even change much. The information ecosystem is hostile; Prometheus will deny and delay; there may be legal consequences. What he can offer is accuracy, reach, and protection - he won't burn her if she doesn't want to go public, but the story is stronger if she does. Ananya weighs his offer against other options she's considering: regulatory bodies, Congress, other journalists. By scene's end, she's leaning toward Jerome, but hasn't fully committed.

## Key Elements

- The encrypted contact: the previous days' careful messaging
- Jerome's offer: his platform, his audience, his investigative capacity
- The video call: two strangers becoming collaborators across screens
- Jerome's honesty: the story matters, but limits exist; this won't save the world
- The information ecosystem: why truth is necessary but not sufficient
- Elena's involvement: Jerome mentions her documentation, the ground-level evidence
- Ananya's other options: regulatory bodies, Congress, other media - each with drawbacks
- Legal exposure: what Ananya risks by going public, by even talking to Jerome
- The question of identity: anonymous source or public whistleblower?
- Ananya's decision-in-process: leaning toward Jerome, not yet committed
- The alliance forming: three strangers connected by crisis and conscience
