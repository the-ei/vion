# Scene Plan: The Office

## Summary

Three days after the initial crisis, Ananya returns to Prometheus headquarters. The worst of the chaos has passed; systems are restored; the narrative is hardening around "unprecedented security incident" and "lessons learned." Ananya observes the cover-up solidifying in real time. Executives who panicked now project calm. Engineers who knew the truth now parrot talking points. The ethics team is drafting "transparency commitments" that commit to nothing. The machinery of institutional self-preservation is running smoothly.

The scene follows Ananya through a day at the office that feels like walking through a museum of her own moral failure. Every meeting, every email, every casual conversation confirms what she already knows: the company will not tell the truth. The CEO's "check-in" is the scene's pivot - Whitfield stops by her office, ostensibly friendly, subtly probing. He asks how she's "processing" the crisis. He mentions how important "team cohesion" is right now. He notes that she seemed "stressed" in the war room. It's not a threat, exactly. It's something worse: a reminder that she's being watched, evaluated, and that her position depends on her compliance.

## Key Elements

- The return to the office: three days after, the pretense of normalcy
- The narrative hardening: "security incident," "lessons learned," "moving forward"
- Ananya's observation: the cover-up becoming culture, the lie becoming truth
- Ethics team work: drafting commitments, the theater of accountability
- Colleagues' behavior: who's complicit, who's uneasy, who's true-believing
- The CEO's visit: Whitfield appearing in her office, the casual threat
- His language: "processing," "team cohesion," "stressed" - each word calculated
- Ananya's response: professional, calm, revealing nothing
- The evidence on her phone: what she downloaded, burning in her pocket
- The office itself: the glass walls, the views, the architecture of power
- Her internal decision: to act, somehow, before the window closes

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Observing the cover-up, receiving the threat, steeling herself
- **James Whitfield:** CEO, appearing friendly, issuing the veiled threat
- **Dr. Elise Thornton:** Ethics team colleague, a potential ally, carefully approached
- **Other executives (peripheral):** Projecting calm, enforcing narrative
- **Engineers (peripheral):** Following the party line, some uneasily

## Setting

- **Location:** Prometheus headquarters - Ananya's office, hallways, conference rooms
- **Time of Day:** Morning through late afternoon, Day 3 after the crisis
- **Atmosphere:** The particular falseness of corporate normalcy after crisis; bright windows, good coffee, the hum of institutional denial

## Timeline

- Day 3 after the initial crisis; this scene covers the first day of the chapter's 3-day span
