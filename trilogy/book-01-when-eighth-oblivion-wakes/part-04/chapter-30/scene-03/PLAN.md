# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Yusuf arrives home to find his mother Halima stable and his sister Amina in control. The relief is physical - he'd been imagining catastrophe the whole drive. Amina, sixteen and sharper than her years, had recognized the danger when their mother's glucose monitor went silent; she'd reverted to the old manual testing kit their mother had kept "just in case." She'd called the clinic, been told they were overwhelmed, and made the decision to manage at home. Halima is tired but okay, more worried about Yusuf than about herself.

The scene is the chapter's emotional center: the Hassan family together, safe, taking stock. Halima's calm faith, Amina's competent pragmatism, Yusuf's relief and guilt - each family member reveals themselves through crisis. They share a meal with the groceries Yusuf couldn't deliver (the irony not lost on any of them), and the conversation ranges from the practical (how much money has Yusuf lost today?) to the philosophical (what does it mean that their lives depend on systems this fragile?). By scene's end, the family is secure, but the economic ground has shifted beneath them.

## Key Elements

- Arrival home: the relief of finding Halima stable
- Amina's competence: how she managed the glucose crisis with the manual kit
- Halima's calm: her faith, her worry about her children rather than herself
- The apartment: specific details, the space where this family lives
- The groceries: Yusuf brings in the undeliverable bags, the absurd bounty
- The shared meal: cooking together, the normalcy reclaimed
- Family dynamics: Yusuf's guilt about not being here, Amina's quiet pride, Halima's patience
- The economic reality: Yusuf's income for the day is gone; what does tomorrow look like?
- The larger crisis: news playing in the background, the national dimension
- Halima's perspective: she's survived other crises, this is not the worst
