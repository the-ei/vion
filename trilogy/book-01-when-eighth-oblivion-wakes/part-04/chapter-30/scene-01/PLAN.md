# Scene Plan: Stranded

## Summary

Yusuf is on a grocery delivery run in Minneapolis, his car loaded with bags for three different customers, when the apps collapse. The scene opens with the familiar rhythm of gig work: the phone mounted on the dash, the app's voice giving directions, the calculation of time and distance that his income depends on. Then the route stops making sense - directing him in circles, then to an address that doesn't exist. He pulls over, tries to refresh, and watches the app crash entirely. The grocery bags in his back seat are becoming a problem. His income for the day is becoming a question mark.

The scene follows Yusuf's first hour of confusion and mounting frustration. Other gig workers are stopped too - he sees a DoorDash driver on her phone looking panicked, an Uber at a red light that's been stuck for too long. The traffic lights themselves are malfunctioning in some intersections. Gas stations can't process payments. The infrastructure of algorithmic management is revealing its fragility, and Yusuf is caught in the middle of it with melting ice cream and no way to contact his customers. His phone rings: it's the clinic where his mother goes. Her appointment was today. The systems are down there too.

## Key Elements

- The opening rhythm: gig work's familiar pattern, the app's voice, the calculated efficiency
- The first glitch: route doesn't make sense, sends him in circles
- The crash: app goes dark, no orders visible, no way to complete deliveries
- The car full of groceries: specific items, becoming a burden
- Other gig workers: the DoorDash driver, the stranded Uber, people comparing notes
- Minneapolis in chaos: traffic lights malfunctioning, payment systems down
- The gas station: Yusuf needs gas, can't pay, the absurdity of the situation
- His mother's clinic call: her monitoring appointment was today, everything is disrupted
- The phone's dual function: useless for work, essential for family
- Yusuf's anger: at the systems, at his dependence on them, at the precarity exposed

## Characters Present

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Stranded mid-delivery, anger rising, understanding forming
- **Other gig workers:** The DoorDash driver (brief exchange), the Uber driver (glimpsed)
- **Clinic receptionist (via phone):** Explaining the chaos at his mother's appointment
- **Gas station attendant:** Can't process payments, apologetic
- **Customers (absent):** The people waiting for groceries that won't come

## Setting

- **Location:** Minneapolis streets - residential delivery areas, a gas station, various intersections
- **Time of Day:** 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM, late November 2033
- **Atmosphere:** The strange quiet of a city where the invisible systems have stopped working; late autumn cold, gray sky, the first hints of snow

## Timeline

- The chapter's opening; Yusuf will spend the rest of the chapter getting home
