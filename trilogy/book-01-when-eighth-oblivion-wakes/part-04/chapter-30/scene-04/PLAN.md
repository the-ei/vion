# Scene Plan: Taking Stock

## Summary

Evening. The family has eaten, Halima is resting, and Yusuf is on the couch with his phone, trying to calculate what this day has cost him. The gig apps are flickering back online, but his account shows no credit for the morning's work, no way to reclaim the hours lost. He scrolls through social media and finds a discourse forming: "Eighth Oblivion" is trending, people seeking explanations for what just happened. Some blame hackers, some blame corporations, some blame the AI systems themselves. Yusuf finds himself nodding at takes from people he's never met - other gig workers, nurses, teachers, anyone who experienced today from below.

The scene traces Yusuf's emerging understanding. He's been living inside the algorithm's logic for years, performing for rating systems and surge pricing, his income a function of variables he doesn't control. Today, the logic failed, and in its failure, became visible. He starts to see his position differently - not as individual precarity but as collective condition. Amina sits down next to him, asks what he's reading, and they talk about the future - hers, his, theirs. By scene's end, Yusuf hasn't resolved anything, but he's looking at his life with new eyes. The song in his head has new words forming.

- The song: new words forming, the melody becoming political
- The chapter's closing image: Yusuf at the window, the city outside, the night
