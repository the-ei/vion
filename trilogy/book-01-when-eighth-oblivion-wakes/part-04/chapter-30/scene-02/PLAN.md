# Scene Plan: The Journey Home

## Summary

Yusuf abandons the delivery attempt and heads home. The scene follows his journey across Minneapolis, a trip that would normally take twenty minutes but now takes over two hours. Traffic lights are out at major intersections; people are directing each other with hand signals. Some neighborhoods have backup power and seem almost normal; others are in chaos. The class geography of the city becomes visible: the wealthy areas with generators and alternatives, the poor areas with nothing but each other.

The journey becomes a meditation on the city Yusuf knows intimately from his gig work routes. He's delivered to the mansions in Kenwood and the apartments in Phillips, knows which buildings have doormen and which have broken buzzers. Now, driving through the crisis, he sees the same geography from a different angle: who's protected and who's exposed. The groceries in his back seat become a kind of absurdist symbol - he pulls over at one point and gives a bag to a woman at a bus stop whose phone is dead and who doesn't know how to get home. By scene's end, he's finally approaching his neighborhood, the familiar streets, his mother and sister somewhere inside their apartment.

## Key Elements

- The decision to head home: abandoning the deliveries, choosing family
- The drive: normally 20 minutes, now 2+ hours
- Traffic chaos: signals out, intersections jammed, people self-organizing
- The class geography: wealthy neighborhoods with backup systems, poor ones without
- Yusuf's knowledge of the city: his routes, his customers, the mental map of inequality
- The grocery giveaway: a bag to a stranded woman, the absurd generosity of crisis
- Other encounters: brief moments with people in the same situation
- The song in his head: the melody he's been working on, surfacing amid chaos
- The cold: late November, the car's heater fighting the open windows for air
- Approaching home: the familiar streets, the apartment complex, the relief

## Characters Present

- **Yusuf Hassan (POV):** Driving home, observing, understanding
- **The stranded woman:** Receives a bag of groceries, gratitude and confusion
- **Other drivers/pedestrians:** The informal economy of crisis, people helping each other
- **The city itself:** Minneapolis as character, its geography revealed

## Setting

- **Location:** Minneapolis streets - from commercial/residential delivery areas through downtown, various neighborhoods, to Yusuf's neighborhood
- **Time of Day:** 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM
- **Atmosphere:** The strange intimacy of a city in crisis, people reliant on each other, the cold of late November

## Timeline

- Mid-chapter, the journey home

## Stylistic Notes

- **Mode:** Balanced - Knausgaard for the journey's texture, Carson for the observations
- The drive as accumulating observations: what Yusuf sees, what it means
- The class geography: rendered specifically, without didacticism
- Yusuf's knowledge: he knows these streets intimately, sees them newly
- The grocery giveaway: a moment of connection, possibly absurd, definitely human
- The song: lyrics threading through his thoughts, the melody as refuge
- Physical details: the feel of the steering wheel, the cold, the car's sounds
- The relief of familiar streets: home approaching, the world narrowing to what matters
