# Scene Plan: Home

## Summary

Evening, Day 7 or Day 8. Elena is home, the children in bed, and she's on the couch with Daniel. The day's weight settles: the professional threat, the colleagues' mixed responses, the patients' gratitude, the Hassan family's request. She's spoken with Yusuf Hassan on the phone - a brief, raw conversation with a young man who lost his mother and now sees her case in public documentation. It was painful, but he thanked her for trying, for documenting, for caring. The connection was human, not strategic.

The scene closes the chapter with Elena and Daniel processing together what this week has meant. She's made her sacrifice - not a single dramatic gesture, but the accumulated choice to keep speaking - and she's accepted that it will cost her. Maybe her job, certainly some peace. But she's also drawn her line: she remains a nurse practitioner who speaks the truth, not an activist who happens to have medical training. The children are sleeping. The house is quiet. The work continues tomorrow. The chapter ends with Elena's last thought: she misses Halima Hassan, a patient she barely knew, whose death she couldn't prevent, whose life she chose to document.
