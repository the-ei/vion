# Scene Plan: The Aftermath

## Summary

The story has broken. Elena wakes on Day 7 to find that Jerome's publication - which includes her documentation, her cases, her name (with permission) - has gone viral. Her phone is full of messages: interview requests from journalists, thank-you notes from patients' families, and one message from her clinic director asking her to "come in early to discuss." The abstract idea of bearing witness has become the concrete reality of public exposure.

The scene follows Elena's morning as her life transforms. She reads the coverage, sees her own words quoted, sees the cases she documented now public. Some of the reporting is accurate; some distorts what she said. The "Eighth Oblivion" discourse now includes her name, her clinic, her patients. Social media has opinions about her: heroic truth-teller to some, HIPAA-violating attention-seeker to others. By the time she leaves for the clinic, Elena understands that she's stepped into a different world. She knew this was coming. She didn't fully know what it would feel like.

## Key Elements

- Waking to the news: the phone full of messages, the story everywhere
- Jerome's publication: what it says, how it uses her documentation
- The coverage: mainstream outlets picking it up, the variations in framing
- Interview requests: multiple outlets, different approaches, overwhelming
- The thank-you messages: from patients' families, the gratitude that matters
- The clinic director's message: ominous, the professional consequence arriving
- Social media discourse: the praise and the attacks, her name a battleground
- Daniel's reaction: he knew this was coming, but the reality shocks him
- The children: Sofia asking why Mama's name is on the news
- Leaving for the clinic: steeling herself, the public person leaving her private home
