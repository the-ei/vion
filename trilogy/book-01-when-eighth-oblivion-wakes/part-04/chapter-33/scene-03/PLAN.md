# Scene Plan: The Clinic

## Summary

Elena arrives at the community health center to find it transformed. Some colleagues greet her with warmth and admiration; others avoid eye contact. Dr. Patricia Okonkwo, the clinic director, is waiting. Their meeting is tense but not theatrical - Patricia is not a villain, just an administrator protecting an institution. She asks Elena to issue a "clarification" that distances herself from the more inflammatory interpretations of her documentation. Elena refuses. Patricia warns of consequences but doesn't specify them. The threat is real but undefined.

The scene follows Elena through her workday at the newly strange clinic. She sees patients, does her job, but everything feels different. Some patients have seen the news and thank her; most haven't and don't know. The work itself hasn't changed - the bodies, the needs, the care required - but the context has. By scene's end, Elena has received no formal discipline but knows it's coming. She's also received word that Halima Hassan's family wants to speak with her. The connection to Yusuf is forming, though she doesn't yet know what it will become.

## Key Elements

- Arriving at the clinic: the changed atmosphere, the divided reception
- Colleagues' responses: some supportive, some avoiding, the social texture of institutional controversy
- Dr. Okonkwo's meeting: the pressure to "clarify," Elena's refusal
- Patricia's position: not villainous, just institutional, protecting the clinic
- The threat undefined: consequences coming, specifics withheld
- The workday: seeing patients, the continuity of care amid personal crisis
- Patients' responses: those who know, those who don't, the variety
- Miguel Santos: the supportive colleague, solidarity expressed
- The Hassan family message: they want to speak with Elena about Halima's case
- The day ending: no resolution, just continuation
