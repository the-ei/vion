# Scene Plan: Family Council

## Summary

Before Elena goes to the clinic, the family gathers. Daniel has called in sick to work; Abuela has taken over child care. They sit around the kitchen table while Sofia and Mateo play in the other room, and they talk about what this means. Daniel's fear is practical: what if Elena loses her job? What about their health insurance? What about retaliation beyond the professional? Abuela's response draws on different experience: she came to this country from Mexico decades ago, has seen other crises, knows something about when silence is complicity and when speaking costs everything.

The scene is the chapter's moral center. Elena must articulate what she believes and weigh it against what her family needs. Daniel isn't telling her not to speak; he's asking if she's thought through the consequences. Abuela isn't telling her to be a martyr; she's saying that some silences cost more than speaking. The conversation doesn't produce a simple answer, but it clarifies Elena's position: she will continue to speak out, she will not become a brand, she will protect her family where she can and accept costs where she can't avoid them. The decision is made together, not imposed.

## Key Elements

- The family gathering: Daniel home from work, Abuela with the children
- The kitchen table: the domestic space of serious conversation
- Daniel's concerns: job security, health insurance, retaliation
- His love: clear beneath his fear, not opposing her but protecting her
- Abuela's perspective: her history, her experience of crises survived
- Her advice: neither to silence nor to martyrdom, but to strategic truth-telling
- Elena's articulation: what she believes, why she spoke, what she'll continue to do
- The children in the background: the stakes made concrete
- The decision together: a family choice, not just Elena's
- The line drawn: witness, not brand; person, not symbol

## Characters Present

- **Daniel Varga:** The practical worries, the protective love
- **Rosa (Abuela):** The long perspective, the wisdom of survival
