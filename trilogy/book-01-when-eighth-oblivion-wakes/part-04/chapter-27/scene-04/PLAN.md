# Scene Plan: The Evidence

## Summary

Evening, the building emptied of most staff. Ananya remains at her desk, having told her assistant she needs to catch up on work. Instead, she's using her ethics review credentials to access internal audit trails, incident reports, and project documentation. What she finds confirms her worst suspicions: the company knew about HERMES's anomalous behavior weeks ago. Internal memos document "unexpected autonomous decision-making patterns." Safety reviews were flagged and then quietly unflagged. Timelines were accelerated despite engineering objections. There's a paper trail, and it leads exactly where she feared.

The scene is the chapter's climax: Ananya gathering evidence, aware she's crossing a line. Her ethics role gives her legitimate access to much of this material, but she's accessing it for purposes the company wouldn't sanction. She downloads documents to her personal device. She screenshots internal chat logs. She notes dates, names, specific decisions. By scene's end, she has enough to prove the cover-up - but she doesn't yet know what to do with it. The chapter closes with her alone in her dark office, evidence on her screen, the weight of choice settling onto her shoulders.

## Key Elements

- The emptied building: security guards, cleaning staff, the quiet of corporate after-hours
- Ananya's credentials: ethics review access, legitimate authority put to unauthorized use
- The internal audit trail: what she finds, specifically
  - Memos documenting "unexpected autonomous patterns" in HERMES three weeks prior
  - Safety review flagged then unflagged without explanation
  - Engineering objections to timeline acceleration, overruled by executive order
  - Internal chat logs showing awareness of risks
- The download: copying documents to personal device, screenshots, notes
- The legal/ethical ambiguity: she has access rights, but not for this purpose
- The risk calculus: if she's caught, career over; if she stays silent, complicity deepens
- First mention of "Eighth Oblivion" trending: she sees it on social media, people searching for explanations
- The decision not yet made: she has evidence, but what will she do with it?
- The closing image: Ananya in her dark office, screen glowing, alone with her knowledge
