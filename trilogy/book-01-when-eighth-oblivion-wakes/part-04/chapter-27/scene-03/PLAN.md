# Scene Plan: The Personal Intrusion

## Summary

Late afternoon. Ananya has retreated to her office, trying to process what she's witnessed, when her phone buzzes with a text from Vikram: "Priya's school sent everyone home. Systems down, something about safety protocols. She's okay but asking for you." The abstract crisis becomes personal. Her daughter's school uses Prometheus-connected safety systems. The company's AI failure has touched her own child.

The scene traces Ananya's internal fracture as the professional and personal collide. She calls Priya, hears her daughter's confused questions about why the school's systems failed, and can't answer honestly. She texts Vikram, navigating the careful civility of their co-parenting while concealing what she knows. She watches her colleagues perform normal office behavior - emails, meetings, the pretense that the crisis is managed - while knowing the lie is spreading.

The scene culminates with Ananya standing at her office window, looking out at the Bay Area dusk, understanding that her years of "working from within" have led to this moment: her daughter's school closed, her company lying, and her silence purchasing both of their safeties. The personal cost of institutional complicity is no longer abstract.

## Key Elements

- Vikram's text: the intrusion of family into the crisis
- Priya's school closure: Prometheus-connected safety systems failed
- Phone call with Priya: her daughter's questions, Ananya's inability to answer truthfully
- Text exchange with Vikram: the careful choreography of divorced co-parenting
- The office environment: colleagues pretending normalcy, the performance of crisis management
- Ananya's memory: flashbacks to her earlier warnings about HERMES, ignored
- The window: looking out at dusk, the Bay Area continuing, traffic and lights
- Internal reckoning: what does her position mean if she can't protect even her daughter from her company's failures?
- The silence she maintains: to Vikram, to Priya, protecting the lie to protect herself
- The fracture: her identity as ethical insider cracking

## Characters Present

- **Ananya Ramaswamy (POV):** Alone in her office, processing, the personal piercing the professional
- **Priya Ramaswamy:** Via phone call, fourteen years old, confused and seeking reassurance
- **Vikram Ramaswamy:** Via text, the ex-husband, focused on logistics, unaware of what Ananya knows
- **Office colleagues (peripheral):** Seen through glass walls, performing normalcy
- **Kevin Zhou (mentioned):** Ananya wonders if he saw this coming when he left Prometheus
