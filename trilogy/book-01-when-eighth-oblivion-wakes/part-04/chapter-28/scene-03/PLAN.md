# Scene Plan: Ground Truth

## Summary

Amid the information chaos, Jerome reaches out to Elena Varga. He'd found her name in his earlier research - a healthcare worker in Phoenix who'd posted about AI diagnostic failures. Now, with hospitals reporting system crashes nationwide, her perspective becomes essential. The scene follows their video interview: Jerome in the coffee shop, Elena exhausted between patients at her clinic. For the first time, the crisis gains human specificity. Elena describes what she's seeing: the insulin pumps, the diagnostic failures, the patients suffering from systems designed to help them.

This is Jerome's first direct contact with Elena, a relationship that will deepen across the trilogy. Their conversation serves multiple functions: it gives Jerome ground-level evidence for his story, it shows him the human stakes beyond tech industry drama, and it connects two characters who will become unlikely allies. Elena is wary at first - she's busy, she doesn't fully trust journalists - but Jerome's questions are specific, informed, and focused on the people affected rather than the technology. By scene's end, she's agreed to stay in touch, to share more documentation if she can. Jerome has found ground truth.

## Key Elements

- Jerome finding Elena: her social media posts, her professional profile, reaching out
- Elena's initial wariness: she's busy, she doesn't know him, journalists have burned her before
- The video interview: the visual of Elena exhausted, still in scrubs, the clinic behind her
- Elena's testimony: specific cases - the insulin pump failure, the glucose monitor, the diagnostic AI
- The human stakes: patients suffering, the technology that was supposed to help them failing
- Jerome's questions: specific, informed, focused on people rather than tech
- The connection forming: mutual recognition of shared purpose
- Elena's request: if he writes about this, center the patients, not the technology
- Her agreement to stay in contact: she'll share documentation, maybe more interviews
- Jerome's realization: this story isn't about AI, it's about what happens to people when systems fail
