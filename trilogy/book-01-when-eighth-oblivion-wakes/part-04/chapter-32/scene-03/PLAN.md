# Scene Plan: Denise

## Summary

Evening, Day 5. After DeShawn has retreated to his room, Jerome and Denise have the conversation that matters. She's a high school history teacher; she knows something about how information shapes understanding. She's also been married to Jerome for twenty years; she knows his tendency to martyr himself and his tendency to second-guess himself. This conversation isn't about strategy; it's about what he actually believes and whether he's willing to act on it.

The scene follows their evening together - cooking dinner, eating, the ordinary intimacy of marriage. Denise doesn't tell Jerome what to do; she asks questions that clarify. What is he afraid of? What would he regret more - publishing and being ignored, or not publishing and never knowing? What does he owe his sources? What does he owe his family? The conversation becomes a kind of inventory: what matters, what he's willing to risk, what he's not. By scene's end, Jerome knows he's going to publish through his independent platform, coordinated with Ananya's multi-channel release. The decision feels less like courage and more like inevitability.

## Key Elements

- The aftermath of the argument: DeShawn upstairs, the house settling
- Cooking dinner: the ordinary activity during extraordinary conversation
- Denise's questions: not telling, asking; the clarity of good questions
- Their history: twenty years, what they've weathered, what journalism has cost
- His mother Dorothy: the parallel between caring for the vulnerable and telling truth for them
- The risk calculation: career, money, safety - what's actually at stake
- What he owes sources: Ananya trusting him, Elena sharing her documentation
- What he owes family: the years of sacrifice, the costs they've borne
- The decision crystallizing: not heroic, just necessary
- Their partnership: the practical planning after the emotional clarity
