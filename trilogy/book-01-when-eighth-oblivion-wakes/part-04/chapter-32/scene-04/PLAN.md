# Scene Plan: Publish

## Summary

Day 6, evening. The story goes live. Jerome has spent the day in final preparation: fact-checking, legal review (his own, limited), coordination with Ananya, integration of Elena's documentation. Now, at 7 PM Eastern, he publishes. The scene follows the hour surrounding publication: the final checks, the button pressed, the first responses, and the immediate uncertainty about whether any of it will matter.

The story is comprehensive: Prometheus's HERMES system failure, the evidence of prior knowledge, the corporate cover-up, the human cost documented through Elena's cases. Ananya remains anonymous in the piece but has gone public through other channels simultaneously. Jerome watches his platform's numbers: readers, shares, reactions. The initial response is strong, but he knows Day 1 numbers mean nothing. The real question - will this change anything? - won't be answered for weeks or months. The chapter closes with Jerome in his home office, the story out, his wife bringing him coffee, the strange anticlimax of having done what he could.

## Key Elements

- Final preparation: the day's work of checking, coordinating, preparing
- Ananya coordination: her multi-channel release timed with his publication
- Elena's integration: her documentation woven into the story, her voice present
- The publication moment: the button pressed, the irreversibility
- The story itself: what it says, how it's structured, its claims and evidence
- First responses: social media reactions, early comments, the initial reception
- The numbers: what they show, what they mean, what they don't
- The uncertainty: whether this will matter, whether truth has power left
- Denise's presence: the coffee, the support, the partnership
- The anticlimax: having done what he could, and not knowing if it's enough
