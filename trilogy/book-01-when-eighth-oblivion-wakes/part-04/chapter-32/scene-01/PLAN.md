# Scene Plan: The Offer

## Summary

Day 5, morning. Jerome receives an offer he's been half-expecting: a major outlet - let's call it the Tribune, one of his former employers - wants him back. The crisis is the story of the year, they need experienced hands, and they're willing to overlook the way things ended before. The offer comes via a former colleague, now an editor, who calls with genuine warmth and subtle conditions. Come back, tell this story, but with editorial oversight and legal review. The institution will protect you, but the institution will also shape the story.

The scene follows Jerome as he genuinely considers the offer. His independent platform has reach but no protection; the Tribune has protection but constraints. He knows his AI investigation from earlier parts connects to the crisis, but the Tribune might not let him make those connections - too speculative, too likely to draw lawsuits. The editor offers resources: research assistants, legal counsel, the old newsroom hum. Jerome feels the pull. Then he looks at Ananya's latest message, at Elena's documentation, and knows that what he's building is different from what the Tribune wants to publish.

## Key Elements

- The phone call: the editor's offer, warmth and conditions intertwined
- Jerome's history with the Tribune: why he left, what it cost, the unfinished business
- The offer's appeal: resources, protection, the old infrastructure
- The offer's constraints: editorial oversight, legal review, institutional caution
- Jerome's investigation: what he's built, how it connects to the crisis
- Ananya's messages: the evidence he has access to, what it could prove
- Elena's documentation: the ground-level truth that complements the inside view
- The calculation: independence versus institution, reach versus protection
- His home office: the space where independent journalism happens
- The decision not yet made: Jerome genuinely weighing, not just rejecting
