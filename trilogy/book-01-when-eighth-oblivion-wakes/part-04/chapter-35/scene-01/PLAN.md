# Scene Plan: Ananya - The Cost

## Summary

Two weeks after the initial crisis. Ananya has been placed on "administrative leave pending review" - the consequence she expected, delivered with HR precision. The scene opens with her at home, not in the office where she spent eight years building a career that now means nothing. She's reviewing the fallout: Prometheus's official response to her whistleblowing (denial, character attacks, legal threats), the regulatory investigations she helped trigger (slow-moving but real), the coverage of her story (mixed, contested, some supportive).

Ananya processes what she's lost and what she's gained. Her career at Prometheus is over; her reputation in the tech industry is damaged; she may face legal action. But her evidence is out, her testimony matters, and she's connected to people she never expected to know. Priya texts: "I'm proud of you." Jerome sends a message about the congressional hearing where her evidence will be cited. Elena has become an unlikely ally. The scene ends with Ananya looking at the San Francisco skyline from her apartment, knowing she's crossed a threshold she can't un-cross, not sure what comes next.

## Key Elements

- Administrative leave: the professional consequence, the corporate language
- Being home: the disorientation of not working, the empty days
- The fallout: Prometheus's response, the attacks, the threats
- The support: regulatory interest, congressional attention, media coverage
- Priya's text: the daughter's pride, the personal validation
- Jerome's message: the work continuing, the evidence mattering
- Elena's connection: the alliance solidified, the unlikely friendship
- The losses: career, reputation, certainty
- The gains: integrity, connection, truth told
- The view: San Francisco, the city where she built everything now lost
