# Scene Plan: Jerome - The Impact

## Summary

Jerome is assessing what his investigation accomplished - and what it didn't. The story went viral, then faded into the discourse; the evidence is undeniable, yet the denials continue; the congressional hearing is scheduled, but so is the news cycle's move to the next thing. The "Eighth Oblivion" concept has become common language, but its meaning is contested - co-opted by doomsayers, debunkers, and corporate apologists alike. The truth was told. He's not sure it mattered.

The scene follows Jerome through a day of reckoning. He talks to Denise about what comes next; he checks in on his mother in Baltimore (stable, confused, unchanging); he reads the coverage and the critiques. DeShawn, still skeptical, points out a viral TikTok making fun of "Eighth Oblivion" discourse. The gap between importance and impact yawns wide. But then Jerome sees Yusuf Hassan's video - the young man whose mother died, now speaking publicly - and something shifts. The story isn't just what he published; it's what people do with it. His work seeded the ground. Others are planting.
