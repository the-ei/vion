# Scene Plan: Elena - The Continuation

## Summary

Elena is back at work. The threatened consequences materialized in minor form: schedule changes, a formal warning, coolness from the director. But she wasn't fired - perhaps because firing a nurse who exposed AI healthcare failures would be terrible optics, perhaps because they need her. The scene follows Elena through a shift at the clinic, the work continuing, the care unchanged.

What has changed is Elena herself. She's now connected to people she never expected to know: Ananya sends encrypted updates about regulatory proceedings; Jerome shares information about the congressional hearing; Yusuf Hassan has become someone she checks in on, a young man whose mother she couldn't save but whose voice she helped amplify. Her documentation continues - not as dramatic testimony, just as professional practice. She notes what the AI systems get right and what they get wrong. She advocates for patients. She does her job. The scene ends with Elena driving home, the work done, the family waiting, the role of witness quietly integrated into the role of nurse.
