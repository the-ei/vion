# Scene Plan: Coda - The Convergence

## Summary

A brief scene, the chapter's closing movement. A congressional hearing on AI safety is scheduled for January. All four characters will be present: Ananya as a witness, Jerome as covering journalist, Elena as invited testimony, Yusuf as public figure whose story embodies the human cost. They haven't all met in person yet, but they will.

The scene cross-cuts between all four, a visual/temporal convergence. Ananya receives the formal summons. Jerome gets his press credential. Elena's clinic receives an invitation letter. Yusuf gets a DM from an organizer saying they want him there. Each, in their own space, reads or receives the news. Each knows the others will be there. The "Eighth Oblivion" that began as a trending topic has become the subject of institutional attention. Whether that attention will change anything is Part 5's question. Part 4 ends with four people, connected by crisis, about to enter the same room.

## Key Elements

- The congressional hearing: scheduled for January, the institutional response forming
- The cross-cutting: four spaces, four notifications, four preparations
- Ananya's summons: formal, legal, the weight of testimony
- Jerome's credential: the journalist's access, the work continuing
- Elena's invitation: the clinic's letter, her expertise valued
- Yusuf's DM: the organizer's message, the voice wanted
- Each reading/receiving: the moment rendered for each character
- The knowledge of convergence: they'll all be there
- The "Eighth Oblivion" as institutional topic: the concept legitimized
- The open question: will it change anything?
