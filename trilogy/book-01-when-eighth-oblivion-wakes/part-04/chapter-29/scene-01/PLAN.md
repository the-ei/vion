# Scene Plan: Systems Fail

## Summary

Elena is mid-shift when the diagnostic AI systems begin their cascade failure. The scene opens with routine - a patient with chest pain, the familiar rhythm of assessment, the diagnostic AI suggesting tests to run. Then the screen glitches. Then it displays results that don't match what Elena is seeing with her own eyes. Then it goes dark entirely. What follows is controlled chaos: the clinic's protocols for system failure activating, staff reverting to paper charts and manual processes, patients confused and frightened, and the slow realization that this isn't a local outage - it's something much larger.

The scene establishes Elena's expertise and the fragility of the systems built around it. She's a nurse practitioner who trained before full AI integration and can work without the systems, but many of her younger colleagues can't. The patients most affected are those most dependent on technology: diabetics whose insulin pumps lost connectivity, an elderly man whose medication reminders stopped, a child whose continuous glucose monitor went silent overnight. Elena moves from patient to patient, triaging human need against system absence, her anger building beneath her professional calm.

## Key Elements

- Opening routine: a normal patient encounter, the AI working as intended
- The first glitch: diagnostic AI suggesting tests that don't match the presentation
- The cascade: screens flickering, data disappearing, systems going dark
- Clinic protocols: the shift to paper, the reversion to pre-digital processes
- Staff responses: younger colleagues paralyzed, experienced staff adapting
- The patients: specific cases arriving in waves
  - Mrs. Okonkwo: elderly, medication reminders stopped, missed doses
  - The Reyes family: their diabetic daughter's glucose monitor failed overnight
  - A construction worker: insulin pump connectivity lost while on the job
- Elena's expertise: the value of training that preceded AI dependence
- Abuela's call: checking on Sofia and Mateo, the home front stable
- The larger crisis: news reports bleeding in, the realization this is national
- Elena's anger: the systems that were supposed to help are hurting people
