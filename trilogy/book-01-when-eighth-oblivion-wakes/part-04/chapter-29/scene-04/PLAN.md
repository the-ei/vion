# Scene Plan: Documentation

## Summary

Late night, the clinic finally quieting. Elena should go home - her shift ended hours ago, her children are asleep, her husband is worried - but she can't leave yet. Instead, she's at a computer in the staff area, documenting. Not the official patient records, which she's already completed, but something else: a personal account of what happened today. Photos of the diagnostic AI error screens before they were restored. Screenshots of the backlogged patient queue. Notes on each case that was affected by the system failures, with timestamps and outcomes. Halima Hassan's case, documented in full.

The scene is quiet and deliberate - the opposite of the chaos that preceded it. Elena is building a record. She doesn't know exactly what she'll do with it, but she knows that if this crisis is processed through official channels, it will be minimized, explained away, turned into lessons learned and improved protocols. She wants to preserve what actually happened. By scene's end, she's saved her documentation to personal storage, texted Jerome that she has more to share, and is finally walking to her car in the dark parking lot, exhausted but committed. The decision has been made: she will bear witness.

## Key Elements

- The quiet clinic: most staff gone, the emergency passed, the ordinary returning
- Elena staying: despite exhaustion, despite family waiting
- The documentation: what she's recording, why, how
  - Photos of error screens (taken earlier, now organized)
  - Screenshots of patient queues and wait times
  - Personal notes on each affected case
  - Halima Hassan's case in full detail
- The computer: institutional equipment, personal purpose
- Her awareness of risk: this could be seen as violating patient privacy, her job could be at stake
- Her justification: the greater violation is pretending this didn't happen
- The message to Jerome: "I have more. Can we talk tomorrow?"
- Daniel's text: worried, loving, waiting
- The walk to the car: the dark parking lot, the cold air, the exhaustion finally settling
- The chapter's closing image: Elena driving home through quiet streets, the documentation saved
