# Editing Guidelines for The Eighth Oblivion Trilogy

## Core Principles

**DO NOT:**
- Cut content for length
- Sand edges or soften difficult content
- Make language more generic or "accessible"
- Remove specificity in favor of vagueness
- Shorten sentences that benefit from their length
- Remove cultural, technical, or intellectual references
- Make characters more likeable or relatable
- Reduce complexity of themes or ideas
- Add explanations for things that don't need explaining

**DO:**
- Fix typos, grammatical errors, and unclear antecedents
- Strengthen specific word choices (replace weak verbs, remove unnecessary adverbs)
- Improve rhythm and cadence of prose
- Ensure character voice consistency across scenes
- Fix continuity errors (timeline, character details, setting details)
- Enhance sensory detail where thin
- Strengthen the Knausgaard elements: psychological interiority, mundane elevated to profound
- Strengthen the Carson elements: compression, white space, fragmentary intensity
- Ensure technical accuracy (AI, crypto, surveillance, medical, legal details)
- Deepen thematic resonance without being heavy-handed

## Style Preservation

### Knausgaard Elements to Preserve/Enhance:
- Long flowing sentences that accumulate detail
- Exhaustive attention to mundane moments
- Psychological interiority and self-examination
- Time dilation during significant moments
- The texture of memory and consciousness

### Carson Elements to Preserve/Enhance:
- Sudden compression after expansion
- Fragments that create meaning through juxtaposition
- White space and silence as structural elements
- Genre-bending formal choices
- Elliptical passages that require reader participation

## Editing Process

1. Read the scene for overall effect first
2. Check character voice against CHARACTERS.md
3. Line-edit for prose quality
4. Verify continuity with surrounding scenes
5. Strengthen sensory grounding
6. Preserve length - if you cut, you must add equivalent elsewhere

## Red Flags (Stop and Reconsider)

- If you're making the prose "smoother" - stop
- If you're cutting something that makes you uncomfortable - stop
- If you're adding explanation or context - stop
- If you're simplifying syntax - stop
- If you're removing repetition that creates rhythm - stop
- If the character suddenly sounds more reasonable - stop

## Quality Markers

Good edit: "She walked to the window" → "She crossed to the window, her reflection arriving before she did"

Bad edit: "The recursive loop of self-examination that had characterized her twenties, the constant monitoring of her own responses to determine if they were authentic or performed" → "She was always analyzing herself" (NO - this loses the specificity and rhythm)

## Commit Protocol

- Commit after each chapter edit with message: "Edit [book]-[part]-[chapter]: [brief description]"
- Never commit more than one chapter at a time
- If uncertain about an edit, leave a comment in the page file: `<!-- REVIEW: [concern] -->`
